### RABB: OWNER AND REARER

- `1:2.0` · language · Baghawī: rabb means owner, as one says "rabb of the house", or rearing and setting right, as in "so-and-so rabba the estate" — completed and improved it; God is the owner and the rearer of the worlds. Of a creature one says "rabb of such" only in construct, never "the Rabb", because the article means all and no creature owns all. [baghawi¶18]
