### LORDSHIP: GENERAL AND SPECIAL REARING

- `1:2.0` · language · Saʿdī: the Rabb is the one who rears all the worlds — everything other than God — by creating them, equipping them with faculties, and favouring them with great blessings without which they could not survive; every blessing they have is from Him. [saadi¶3]
- `1:2.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: His rearing is of two kinds — general: creating them, providing for them and guiding them to what serves their survival in this world; special: rearing His friends by faith, enabling them to it, perfecting them and warding off the diversions and obstacles between them and it — in truth the rearing of success toward every good and protection from every evil. [saadi¶3]
- `1:2.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: perhaps this is why most prophets' supplications use the word Rabb — all their requests fall under His special lordship; "Lord of the worlds" shows His sole creating, ordering and favouring, His perfect self-sufficiency and the worlds' complete need of Him in every way. [saadi¶3]
