### WHY ONLY THE DAY OF RECKONING IS NAMED

- `1:4.0` · lesson · Baghawī: God owns all days, but the Day is named because earthly kingdoms are gone then and no sovereignty or command remains but His — "the sovereignty that Day belongs to the All-Merciful" (25:26), "to whom belongs the kingdom today? To God, the One, the Subduer" (40:16), "the command that Day is God's" (82:19). [baghawi¶21]
