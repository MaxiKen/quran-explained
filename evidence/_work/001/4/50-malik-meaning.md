### THE SOVEREIGN OF THE DAY OF RECOMPENSE

- `1:4.0` · language · Saʿdī: the mālik is the one who has the attribute of sovereignty, whose effects include commanding and forbidding, rewarding and punishing, and disposing of his subjects in every way. [saadi¶4]
- `1:4.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: He tied sovereignty to the Day of Recompense, the Day of Resurrection, when people are repaid for their deeds good and bad, because that day shows creation in full His sovereignty, justice and wisdom and the end of creatures' ownership — kings and subjects, slaves and free are all alike submissive to His greatness, awaiting His recompense, hoping for His reward and fearing His punishment; so He singled it out, though He owns that day and the others. [saadi¶4]
