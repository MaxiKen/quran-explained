### THE MEANINGS OF DĪN

- `1:4.0` · athar · Mujāhid: dīn is reckoning — "that is the right religion" (9:36) means the right reckoning; Ibn ʿAbbās, Muqātil and al-Suddī: "malik yawm al-dīn" is the judge of the Day of Reckoning; Qatādah: dīn is recompense, for good and evil alike — "as you deal, you will be dealt with" (Baghawī). [baghawi¶20]
- `1:4.0` · athar · Muḥammad ibn Kaʿb al-Quraẓī: king of a day on which nothing avails but dīn; Yamān ibn Rabāb: dīn is subjugation (dintuhu fa-dāna, "I subdued him and he was humbled"); or obedience — "the day of obedience". [baghawi¶21]
