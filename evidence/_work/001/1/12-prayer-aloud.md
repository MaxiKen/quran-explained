### THE BASMALAH IN THE PRAYER

- `1:1.0` · hadith · The study draft: in most schools, except the Shāfiʿī and Jaʿfarī, the basmalah is not recited with the sūrahs in the daily prayers; Anas ibn Mālik is reported to have said, "I prayed behind the Prophet, Abū Bakr, ʿUmar and ʿUthmān, and they each began their recitation with 'Praise be to God, Lord of the worlds', not mentioning 'In the Name of God'" (it cites IK, Q, Sh, Z). [initial¶1]
- `1:1.0` · athar · The study draft: but Abū Hurayrah is reported to have recited the basmalah leading the prayer and said, "Among you I perform the prayer closest to the prayer of the Messenger of God" (IK). [initial¶1]
- `1:1.0` · ruling · Maʿārif: it is a sunnah to say the basmalah after aʿūdhu bi-llāh at the start of the first rakʿah, and views differ whether aloud or low; Abū Ḥanīfah and some other imams prefer low; there is consensus that it is said at the start of each later rakʿah too, unanimously a sunnah, though some narrations call it wājib. [maarif¶37]
- `1:1.0` · ruling · Maʿārif: in the prayer, aloud or silent, one does not say the basmalah before a sūrah that follows al-Fātiḥah — not reported from the Prophet or the first four caliphs; this is the view of Abū Ḥanīfah and Abū Yūsuf, preferred in Sharḥ al-Munyah, al-Durr al-Mukhtār and al-Burhān. [maarif¶38]
- `1:1.0` · ruling · Imam Muḥammad prefers saying it in a silent prayer, and some reports give this to Abū Ḥanīfah too (al-Shāmī quotes jurists for it; Thānawī's Bahishtī Zewar adopts it); all agree it is not makrūh to say it there. [maarif¶38]
