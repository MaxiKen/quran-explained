### IS THE ALIF-LĀM PART OF THE WORD "ALLĀH"

- `1:1.0` · language · Qurṭubī, continuing the twenty-first issue: a second group — al-Shāfiʿī, Abū l-Maʿālī, al-Khaṭṭābī, al-Ghazzālī, al-Mufaḍḍal and others, reported also from al-Khalīl and Sībawayh — hold that the alif and lām are always part of it and cannot be dropped; al-Khaṭṭābī: the proof that they belong to the word's structure and are not for definiteness is that one does not say "yā l-Raḥmān" or "yā l-Raḥīm", as one says "yā Allāh". [qurtubi¶27.3]
