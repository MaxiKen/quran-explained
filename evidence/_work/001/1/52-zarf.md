### THE PREPOSITION-PHRASE: "STABLE" OR "IDLE"

- `1:1.0` · language · Alūsī: the phrase is "stable" (mustaqirr) for some and "idle" (laghw) for others, and they define the terms differently — laghw: its governing verb is mentioned, mustaqirr: its governor is omitted; or mustaqirr: the governor is a general verb such as "ḥuṣūl" or "istiqrār" and is understood, laghw the opposite; or laghw: the governor lies outside it and is not understood from it, mentioned or not. All this is convention; he takes the first, so the phrase is "stable" here however the governor is estimated. [alusi¶37]
