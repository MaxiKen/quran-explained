### ISM: THE NAME IS THE NAMED

- `1:1.0` · language · Baghawī: the name is the named — its very self: God says "We give you good news of a boy whose name is Yaḥyā" (19:7) and then calls the name, "O Yaḥyā"; "You worship besides Him only names you have named" (12:40) means the persons worshipped; and "Glorify the name of your Lord" (87:1). [baghawi¶7]
- `1:1.0` · language · The word is also used for the naming itself, and more often so than for the named; asked what God's naming Himself means, the answer is that it teaches the servants how to open their recitation. [baghawi¶7]

- `1:1.0` · language · Maʿārif: the word "ism" has many lexical and intellectual nuances which an average reader need not know; it is rendered in English as "name". [maarif¶17]
