### THE TEN VIEWS ON WHERE THE BASMALAH STANDS

- `1:1.0` · ruling · Alūsī: outside al-Naml, where it is part of a verse by agreement, the basmalah's status is one of the mothers of issues — a group wrote separate works on it — and the views number ten: (1) not a verse of the sūrahs at all; (2) a verse of every sūrah but Barāʾah; (3) a verse of al-Fātiḥah only; (4) part of a verse of it only; (5) a unique verse sent down to mark the heads of the sūrahs for blessing and to separate them; (6) it may be counted a verse of them or not, since it was sent down repeatedly in both ways; (7) part of a verse of every sūrah; (8) a verse of al-Fātiḥah and part of a verse of the rest; (9) the reverse; (10) unique verses, if sent down repeatedly. [alusi¶2.2-2.3]
- `1:1.0` · history · Who holds which: the second view — Ibn ʿAbbās, Ibn al-Mubārak, the people of Makkah (such as Ibn Kathīr), the people of Kūfah (ʿĀṣim, al-Kisāʾī and others, not Ḥamzah), most of al-Shāfiʿī's followers and the Imāmiyyah; the third — some Shāfiʿīs, Ḥamzah, and, by ascription, Aḥmad; the fifth — the people of Madīnah (Mālik), Shām (al-Awzāʿī) and Baṣrah (Abū ʿAmr, Yaʿqūb), "the well-known view of our school", Alūsī being a Ḥanafī. [alusi¶2.3]
