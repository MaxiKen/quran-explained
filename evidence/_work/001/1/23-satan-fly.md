### WHAT THE BASMALAH DOES TO SATAN

- `1:1.0` · hadith · Aḥmad: a man riding behind the Prophet said "Cursed Satan" when the animal stumbled; the Prophet said, "Do not say 'cursed Satan' — he grows proud and says 'by my strength I threw him'; say 'bismillāh' and he shrinks until he is like a fly." [kathir¶1.40]
- `1:1.0` · hadith · Al-Nasāʾī (al-Yawm wa-l-Laylah) and Ibn Mardawayh (Tafsīr), Usāmah ibn ʿUmayr: the same, with "he grows until he is like a house; but say 'bismillāh' and he shrinks until he is like a fly" — Ibn Kathīr: "this is the blessing of reciting the basmalah". [kathir¶1.40]

- `1:1.0` · hadith · Al-Nasāʾī, from Abū l-Malīḥ, from the rider behind the Messenger of God: "When your mount stumbles do not say 'Satan perish' — he swells until he is like a house and says 'I made it by my strength'; but say 'bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm' and he shrinks until he is like a fly" (Qurṭubī). [qurtubi¶4-5.1]
