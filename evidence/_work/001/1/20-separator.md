### THE BASMALAH AS THE SEPARATOR OF SŪRAHS

- `1:1.0` · athar · Saʿīd ibn Jubayr, from Ibn ʿAbbās: the Prophet did not know where a sūrah ended until "bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm" came down — Abū Dāwūd in al-Marāsīl; al-Ḥākim calls it sound on the conditions of both shaykhs. [baghawi¶14]
- `1:1.0` · athar · Ibn Masʿūd: "We did not know the division between two sūrahs until 'bismillāh…' came down" (al-Wāḥidī; al-Bayhaqī in al-Shuʿab, per al-Suyūṭī). [baghawi¶15]

- `1:1.0` · athar · The study draft: Ibn ʿAbbās is reported to have said the Prophet did not know where to separate one sūrah from the next until "In the Name of God, the Compassionate, the Merciful" came down (Sh). [initial¶1]
