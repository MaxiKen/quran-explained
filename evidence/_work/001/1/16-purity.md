### TOUCHING AND SAYING THE BASMALAH IN IMPURITY

- `1:1.0` · ruling · Maʿārif: it is a verse of the Qurʾān, part of the verse in al-Naml and a verse in its own right between two sūrahs, so it is treated with the Qurʾān's respect: not touched without wuḍūʾ; in major impurity (after emission, in menstruation or after childbirth) not read as Qurʾān recitation before the bath; but it may be said as a prayer before a task such as eating or drinking, in any state. [maarif¶35]
