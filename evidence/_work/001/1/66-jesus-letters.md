### A FORGED REPORT ON THE LETTERS OF "BISM"

- `1:1.0` · hadith · Ṭabarī gives a report (no. 140) through Ismāʿīl ibn al-Faḍl, Ibrāhīm ibn al-ʿAlāʾ, Ismāʿīl ibn ʿAyyāsh and Ismāʿīl ibn Yaḥyā, from Ibn Abī Mulaykah from an unnamed man from Ibn Masʿūd, and from Misʿar ibn Kidām from ʿAṭiyyah from Abū Saʿīd: the Prophet said ʿĪsā's mother gave him to the scribe to teach him; told "write bism", ʿĪsā asked "what is bism?" and the teacher said he did not know; ʿĪsā said, "The bāʾ is God's splendour (bahāʾ), the sīn His brilliance (sanāʾ), the mīm His kingdom (mamlakah)". [tabari¶47-48]
- `1:1.0` · lesson · The editor's note: this report is forged and has no basis. Ibn Ḥibbān (al-Majrūḥīn) said of Ismāʿīl ibn Yaḥyā that he "related forged reports from the trustworthy, and what has no basis from the sure"; Ibn Kathīr, from Ibn Mardawayh, calls it "very strange … perhaps sound back to someone below the Messenger, or from the Isrāʾīliyyāt, not the raised reports", which the editor finds odd with the liar in the chain; al-Suyūṭī gives it to Ibn Jarīr, Ibn ʿAdī, Ibn Mardawayh, Abū Nuʿaym, Ibn ʿAsākir and al-Thaʿlabī and notes "a very weak chain"; al-Dhahabī and Ibn Ḥajar quote Ṣāliḥ Jazarah ("he forged hadith"), al-Azdī ("a pillar of lying"), and al-Dāraquṭnī and al-Ḥākim ("a liar"); Ibn Ḥajar: abandoned by agreement. [tabari¶48]
- `1:1.0` · lesson · Further, the first chain has an unknown narrator ("one who told him from Ibn Masʿūd") and the second has ʿAṭiyyah ibn Saʿd al-ʿAwfī, weakened by Aḥmad, Abū Ḥātim and others (the editor). [tabari¶49-50]
- `1:1.0` · lesson · Ṭabarī: he fears the narrator slipped — he meant the letters b-s-m as a beginner is taught the abjad letters at school, and wrongly joined them and said "bism" — since that reading makes no sense when "bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm" is recited as the reader recites it in God's Book, being impossible in meaning to all the Arabs and speakers of their tongue. [tabari¶50]
