### HOW THE FORMULA CAME TO BE WRITTEN

- `1:1.0` · athar · Al-Shāfiʿī's report from al-Shaʿbī: at first the Prophet wrote in the Quraysh manner "bismika Allāhumma", until "Embark in it; in the name of God be its course and mooring" (11:41) came down and he wrote "bismi Llāh"; then "call upon God or call upon al-Raḥmān" (17:110) and he wrote "bismi Llāhi l-Raḥmān"; then Sulaymān's letter, "it is: In the name of God, the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful" (27:30), and he wrote the same. [baghawi¶15]

- `1:1.0` · athar · Qurṭubī, third issue: al-Shaʿbī and al-Aʿmash — the Prophet wrote "bismika Allāhumma" until ordered to write "bismillāh"; when "call upon God or call upon al-Raḥmān" (17:110) came down he wrote "bismillāhi l-raḥmān", and when Sulaymān's letter (27:30) came down he wrote the full form. [qurtubi¶5.2]
- `1:1.0` · athar · In Abū Dāwūd's Muṣannaf, al-Shaʿbī, Abū Mālik, Qatādah and Thābit ibn ʿUmārah say the Prophet did not write "bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm" until Sūrat al-Naml came down (Qurṭubī). [qurtubi¶5.2]
