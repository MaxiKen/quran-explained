### SEEKING REFUGE BEFORE RECITING

- `1:1.0` · ruling · Maʿārif: before reciting the Qurʾān it is a sunnah to say "aʿūdhu bi-llāhi mina l-shayṭāni l-rajīm" and then the basmalah, and during recitation to say the basmalah at the start of each sūrah except al-Barāʾah (al-Tawbah). [maarif¶12-14]
- `1:1.0` · ruling · 16:98: "When you recite the Qurʾān, seek refuge in God from Satan the rejected". Maʿārif: by the consensus of the Ummah the taʿawwudh is a sunnah before recitation, in the prayer or outside it (Sharḥ al-Munyah), and is peculiar to Qurʾān recitation — for other tasks one says the basmalah only (ʿĀlamgīrī). [maarif¶32-33]
- `1:1.0` · ruling · Begin recitation with both; during it repeat the basmalah but not the taʿawwudh at the next sūrah, except al-Barāʾah — if met in the course of recitation, no basmalah before it; if one begins the Qurʾān with it, say both (ʿĀlamgīriyyah, from al-Muḥīṭ). [maarif¶34]
