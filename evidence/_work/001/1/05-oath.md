### THE BASMALAH AS GOD'S OATH AND WHAT IT HOLDS

- `1:1.0` · lesson · Qurṭubī, first issue: the scholars said "bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm" is an oath from our Lord, sent down at the head of every sūrah, by which He swears to His servants that what He has set down for them in the sūrah is true and that He will fulfil all He guaranteed in it of His promise, kindness and goodness; and it is among what God sent down in our Book and to this community in particular, after Sulaymān. [qurtubi¶4]
- `1:1.0` · lesson · Some scholars said the basmalah holds the whole sharīʿah, because it points to the Essence and to the attributes — Qurṭubī: "and this is correct". [qurtubi¶4]
