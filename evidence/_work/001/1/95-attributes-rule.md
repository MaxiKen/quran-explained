### THE SALAF'S RULE ON GOD'S NAMES AND ATTRIBUTES

- `1:1.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: it is agreed among the Salaf and the imams to believe in God's names, His attributes and the rulings of the attributes — they believe He is Raḥmān and Raḥīm, possessing the mercy He is described with, which relates to the one shown mercy, so every blessing is a trace of His mercy; and so for the other names — "ʿAlīm" is possessor of the knowledge by which He knows everything, "Qadīr" of the power by which He can do everything.
