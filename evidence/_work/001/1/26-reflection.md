### WHAT THE BASMALAH DOES TO A LIFE

- `1:1.0` · lesson · Maʿārif: by teaching man to begin everything with God's name, Islam turns the whole of his life toward God, so that at each step he renews the covenant that nothing he does, not his very being, comes about without God's will and help. [maarif¶9.1]
- `1:1.0` · lesson · Maʿārif: so all a man's worldly work and every movement become worship — a regular alchemy turning the profane into the sacred; the Muslim's "bismillāh" before food affirms that this morsel, which passed through countless stages from sowing to reaping and the labour of wind, rain, sun, sky, earth and a thousand men, is granted by God alone; likewise on going to sleep and on waking. [maarif¶9.2]
- `1:1.0` · lesson · Maʿārif: his needs and activities thus take the nature of remembrance and count as worship; saying it on mounting a carriage testifies that its wood, steel and metals, makers and driver were gathered by God's order of things, and even the coins that pay for it were not created by man — "bismillāh" is the philosopher's stone that turns dust to gold. [maarif¶9.3]
