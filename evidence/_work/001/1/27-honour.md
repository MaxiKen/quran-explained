### HONOURING THE WRITTEN BASMALAH

- `1:1.0` · athar · Qurṭubī, second issue: Saʿīd ibn Abī Sukaynah — "it reached me that ʿAlī ibn Abī Ṭālib saw a man writing 'bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm' and said, 'Make it fine, for a man made it fine and was forgiven'"; and "it reached me that a man saw a paper with the basmalah on it, kissed it and put it on his eyes and was forgiven". [qurtubi¶4]
- `1:1.0` · history · The story of Bishr al-Ḥāfī belongs here: when he picked up the scrap bearing God's name and perfumed it his own name was perfumed — al-Qushayrī mentions it; the editor's note gives it from Wafayāt al-Aʿyān and al-Risālah al-Qushayriyyah: he found on the road a paper with God's name, trodden by feet, and bought perfume with costly dirhams he had, set the paper in a crack in a wall, and saw in a dream a voice saying "O Bishr, you perfumed My name; I will perfume you in this world and the next", and on waking he repented. [qurtubi¶4]
