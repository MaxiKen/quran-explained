### AGAINST THOSE WHO SAY THEIR ACTS ARE THEIR OWN

- `1:1.0` · lesson · Qurṭubī, ninth issue: our scholars said the basmalah is an answer to the Qadariyyah and others who say their acts are within their own power; the ground is that God commanded us to open every act with it, so "bismillāh" means "bi-llāh", and "by God" means that by His creating and decreeing one reaches what one reaches — more to come; others said "bismillāh" means "I began by God's help, success and blessing", a teaching to His servants to mention His name at the opening of recitation and everything else, so that the opening is by His blessing. [qurtubi¶11]
