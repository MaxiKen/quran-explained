### WRITING THE BASMALAH BEFORE BOOKS, LETTERS AND POETRY

- `1:1.0` · ruling · Qurṭubī, sixth issue: the Ummah agree it may be written at the head of any book of knowledge and of letters; for a book of poetry, Mujālid reports from al-Shaʿbī that they agreed not to write the basmalah before poetry, and al-Zuhrī said the sunnah is not to write it in poetry; Saʿīd ibn Jubayr held to writing it at the head of books of poetry and most later scholars followed him — Abū Bakr al-Khaṭīb: "that is what we choose and like". [qurtubi¶8.3]
