### WHETHER THE FORMULA BELONGS TO THIS COMMUNITY ALONE

- `1:1.0` · language · Maʿārif: al-Suyūṭī says all the divine books begin with the basmalah; other scholars hold that "bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm" is peculiar to the Qurʾān and Muḥammad's followers. The two agree if all books share beginning with God's name while these exact words are the Qurʾān's: reports say the Prophet used to say "bismika Allāhumma" until the verse came down and then adopted these words, and the practice was fixed by his command, act or approval (Qurṭubī, Rūḥ al-Maʿānī).
