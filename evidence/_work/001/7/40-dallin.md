### AL-ḌĀLLĪN AND THE GRAMMAR OF "GHAYR … WA-LĀ"

- `1:7.0` · language · Baghawī: "wa-lā l-ḍāllīn" — those astray from guidance; ḍalāl originally means perishing and disappearing (water "lost" in milk). [baghawi¶30]
- `1:7.0` · language · Here "ghayr" has the sense of "lā" and "lā" of "ghayr", so the conjunction is allowed, as in "so-and-so is neither doing good nor acting well"; were ghayr "siwā" (other than), "lā" could not follow it — "ʿindī siwā ʿAbdillāh wa-lā Zayd" is not said. [baghawi¶30]
