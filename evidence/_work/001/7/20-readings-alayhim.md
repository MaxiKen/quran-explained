### THE HĀʾ AND MĪM OF ʿALAYHIM IN THE READINGS

- `1:7.0` · language · Baghawī: Ḥamzah reads ʿalayhum, ladayhum, ilayhum with a ḍamma on the hāʾ; Yaʿqūb does so for every hāʾ after a silent yāʾ (except "bayna aydīhinna wa-arjulihinna", 60:12); the rest read a kasra — the ḍamma returns the hāʾ to its origin, the kasra follows the silent yāʾ, kasra being yāʾ's sister. [baghawi¶28]
- `1:7.0` · language · Ibn Kathīr and Abū Jaʿfar lengthen every plural mīm when the word continues unless a silent letter follows; Nāfiʿ gives a choice; Warsh and the others differ over the hāʾ and mīm before a joining alif (Ḥamzah and al-Kisāʾī: ḍamma on both; Abū ʿAmr and Yaʿqūb: kasra; the rest: ḍamma on the mīm, kasra on the hāʾ) — Baghawī. [baghawi¶28]
