### THOSE WHOM GOD HAS FAVOURED, IN BAGHAWĪ

- `1:7.0` · language · Baghawī: "anʿamta ʿalayhim" — You were gracious to them with guidance and success; ʿIkrimah: with steadfastness in faith and uprightness — the prophets; or all whom God kept steadfast among the prophets and believers named in 4:69. [baghawi¶27]
- `1:7.0` · athar · Ibn ʿAbbās: the people of Mūsā and ʿĪsā before they changed their religion; ʿAbd al-Raḥmān ibn Zayd: the Prophet and those with him. [baghawi¶27]
- `1:7.0` · athar · Abū l-ʿĀliyah: the Messenger's family, Abū Bakr and ʿUmar and his household; Shahr ibn Ḥawshab: the Companions of the Messenger and his household. [baghawi¶27]
