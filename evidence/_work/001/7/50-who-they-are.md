### WHO THE ANGERED AND THE ASTRAY ARE

- `1:7.0` · athar · Baghawī: it was said the angered are the Jews and the astray the Christians, since God judged the Jews with anger ("whom God cursed and with whom He was angry", 5:60) and the Christians with straying ("do not follow the desires of a people who went astray before", 5:77). [baghawi¶31]
- `1:7.0` · athar · Sahl ibn ʿAbdillāh: "ghayr al-maghḍūb" — by innovation; "wa-lā l-ḍāllīn" — away from the Sunnah. [baghawi¶31]
- `1:7.0` · athar · ʿUmar read "ṣirāṭa man anʿamta ʿalayhim ghayri l-maghḍūbi ʿalayhim wa-ghayri l-ḍāllīn" (Baghawī). [baghawi¶31]
