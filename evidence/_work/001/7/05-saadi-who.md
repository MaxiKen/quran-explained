### THE THREE PATHS NAMED IN THE LAST VERSE

- `1:7.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: "the path of those You have favoured" — the prophets, the truthful, the martyrs and the righteous; not the path of "the angered" — those who knew the truth and left it, like the Jews and their like; nor of "the astray" — who left the truth in ignorance and error, like the Christians and their like.
