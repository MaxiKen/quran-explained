### WHAT THE PROPHET SAID OF ITS WORTH

- `1:0.0` · hadith · Ubayy ibn Kaʿb: the Prophet called him while he prayed; Ubayy hurried his prayer, then came; asked why he had not answered, given 8:24, Ubayy said he would answer from then on even when praying. The Prophet offered to teach him a sūrah the like of which was not sent down in the Torah, the Gospel, the Psalms or the Qurʾān; at the mosque door Ubayy reminded him; he asked how Ubayy recited in prayer, Ubayy recited Umm al-Qurʾān, and the Prophet swore that nothing like it was sent down — "it is the seven oft-repeated that God gave me". [baghawi¶35]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Baghawī gives this report from al-Tirmidhī (faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān) and Aḥmad, from Ubayy; Ibn Khuzaymah, Ibn Ḥibbān and al-Ḥākim have it from Abū Hurayrah from Ubayy; al-Bukhārī has a like report from Abū Saʿīd ibn al-Muʿallā. Baghawī: ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ. [baghawi¶35]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Ibn ʿAbbās: Jibrīl was with the Prophet when they heard a creaking from above; Jibrīl said a door had opened in heaven that never opened before; an angel came down: "Rejoice in two lights given to you and to no prophet before you — Fātiḥat al-Kitāb and the closing verses of al-Baqarah; you will not recite a letter of them but you will be given it." Muslim (806) and al-Nasāʾī; Baghawī: ṣaḥīḥ. [baghawi¶36]

- `1:0.0` · hadith · Aḥmad's Musnad, Abū Saʿīd ibn al-Muʿallā: the Prophet called him while he prayed and he did not answer until he finished; asked what stopped him, and told 8:24, "Answer God and the Messenger when he calls you to what gives you life", the Prophet said "I will teach you the greatest sūrah in the Qurʾān before you leave the mosque"; he held his hand, and when about to leave Abū Saʿīd reminded him, and the Prophet said "'al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn' — yes, it is the seven oft-repeated and the great Qurʾān that I was given"; also recorded by al-Bukhārī, Abū Dāwūd, al-Nasāʾī and Ibn Mājah. [kathir¶1.5-1.6]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Aḥmad, Abū Hurayrah: the Prophet called "O Ubayy!" twice while Ubayy prayed; Ubayy prayed faster and came, saying the greeting; asked what had stopped him, and reminded of 8:24, he said he would not do it again; the Prophet then offered a sūrah the like of which was not revealed in the Torah, Gospel, Psalms or Furqān; they walked toward the door, Ubayy slowing so that the Prophet would not reach it before finishing his talk; Ubayy recited Umm al-Qurʾān and the Prophet swore by Him in whose hand is his soul that God revealed none like it — "it is the seven oft-repeated that I was given". [kathir¶1.6-1.7]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Al-Tirmidhī records the same, with "it is of the seven oft-repeated and the great Qurʾān that I was given", and calls it ḥasan ṣaḥīḥ; a like report is from Anas ibn Mālik; ʿAbdullāh, Aḥmad's son, records a longer similar one from Abū Hurayrah from Ubayy; al-Tirmidhī and al-Nasāʾī have from Abū Hurayrah from Ubayy: "God has never revealed in the Torah or the Gospel anything like Umm al-Qurʾān; it is the seven oft-repeated and it is divided into two halves between God and His servant" — al-Nasāʾī's wording; al-Tirmidhī: ḥasan gharīb. [kathir¶1.7-1.8]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Aḥmad: ʿAbdullāh ibn Jābir came to the Prophet after he had poured water for purification and greeted him three times without an answer; later the Prophet came out and greeted him three times with God's mercy, then said "Shall I tell you the best sūrah in the Qurʾān?" and told him to read "al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn" to its end; Ibn Kathīr: a good chain. [kathir¶1.9]
- `1:0.0` · lesson · Some scholars took this hadith as evidence that some verses and sūrahs are more excellent than others (Ibn Kathīr). [kathir¶1.9]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Muslim (1:554) and al-Nasāʾī (al-Kubrā 5:12), Ibn ʿAbbās: Jibrīl was with the Prophet when he heard a noise from above and said a door in heaven had opened that had never opened; an angel came down: "Receive the glad tidings of two lights you have been given that no prophet before you was given — the Opening of the Book and the last verses of Sūrat al-Baqarah; you will not read a letter of them but you will gain its benefit." [kathir¶1.11]
