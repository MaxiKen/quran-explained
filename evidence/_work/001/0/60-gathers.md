### WHAT THE SŪRAH GATHERS

- `1:0.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: for all its brevity it holds what no other sūrah holds — the three kinds of tawḥīd: lordship, from "Lord of the worlds"; divinity, worship of God alone, from the word "Allāh" and "You alone we worship and You alone we ask for help"; and the names and attributes, affirming the attributes of perfection that God and His messenger affirmed, without denial, likening or comparison, which "al-ḥamd" shows. [saadi¶9]
- `1:0.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: it affirms prophethood in "guide us to the straight path", since that is impossible without a message; and recompense for deeds in "Master of the Day of Judgment", the recompense being just, dīn meaning just recompense. [saadi¶9]
- `1:0.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: it affirms the decree (qadar) and that the servant is a real doer, against the Qadariyyah and the Jabriyyah. [saadi¶9]
- `1:0.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: it answers all people of innovation and error in "guide us to the straight path", for that is knowing the truth and acting by it and every innovator and misguided person opposes it; and it affirms sincerity of religion to God in worship and seeking help, in "You alone we worship…". [saadi¶9]
