### WITHOUT IT THE PRAYER IS DEFICIENT

- `1:0.0` · hadith · Abū Hurayrah: "Whoever prays a prayer in which he does not recite Umm al-Qurʾān, it is khidāj, incomplete" — khidāj meaning deficient (some copies repeat it three times); Muslim (395), Baghawī: ṣaḥīḥ. [baghawi¶37]
- `1:0.0` · athar · Asked what to do when behind the imam, Abū Hurayrah nudged the questioner's arm: "Recite it to yourself, O Persian", for he had heard the Prophet say the following. [baghawi¶37]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · The ḥadīth qudsī: "I have divided the prayer between Me and My servant in two halves; half is Mine and half My servant's, and My servant shall have what he asks." When the servant says "al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn", God says "My servant has praised Me"; "al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm" — "My servant has extolled Me"; "mālik yawm al-dīn" — "My servant has glorified Me"; "iyyāka naʿbudu wa-iyyāka nastaʿīn" — "this verse is between Me and My servant, and My servant shall have what he asks"; "ihdinā … wa-lā l-ḍāllīn" — "these are for My servant, and My servant shall have what he asks." Muslim (395). [baghawi¶37]
