### WITHOUT IT THE PRAYER IS DEFICIENT

- `1:0.0` · hadith · Abū Hurayrah: "Whoever prays a prayer in which he does not recite Umm al-Qurʾān, it is khidāj, incomplete" — khidāj meaning deficient (some copies repeat it three times); Muslim (395), Baghawī: ṣaḥīḥ. [baghawi¶37]
- `1:0.0` · athar · Asked what to do when behind the imam, Abū Hurayrah nudged the questioner's arm: "Recite it to yourself, O Persian", for he had heard the Prophet say the following. [baghawi¶37]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · The ḥadīth qudsī: "I have divided the prayer between Me and My servant in two halves; half is Mine and half My servant's, and My servant shall have what he asks." When the servant says "al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn", God says "My servant has praised Me"; "al-Raḥmān al-Raḥīm" — "My servant has extolled Me"; "mālik yawm al-dīn" — "My servant has glorified Me"; "iyyāka naʿbudu wa-iyyāka nastaʿīn" — "this verse is between Me and My servant, and My servant shall have what he asks"; "ihdinā … wa-lā l-ḍāllīn" — "these are for My servant, and My servant shall have what he asks." Muslim (395). [baghawi¶37]

- `1:0.0` · hadith · Muslim, Abū Hurayrah: "Whoever performs a prayer in which he does not read Umm al-Qurʾān, it is khidāj — incomplete", said three times; asked "when we stand behind the imam?" Abū Hurayrah said "Read it to yourself", for he heard the Messenger report the ḥadīth qudsī dividing the prayer between God and His servant, in the wording that gives each verse's reply (praised Me, extolled Me, glorified Me — or once "entrusted to Me" — then "between Me and My servant", then "for My servant"). [kathir¶1.11-1.12]
- `1:0.0` · ruling · Ibn Kathīr: all this witnesses that reciting the Qurʾān — al-Fātiḥah — in the prayer is required, and the scholars agree on this ruling. [kathir¶1.15]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · The Two Ṣaḥīḥs, ʿUbādah ibn al-Ṣāmit: "There is no prayer for one who does not recite Fātiḥat al-Kitāb"; Ibn Khuzaymah's and Ibn Ḥibbān's Ṣaḥīḥs, Abū Hurayrah: "A prayer in which Umm al-Qurʾān is not recited does not suffice (lā tujziʾ)". [kathir¶1.15]
- `1:0.0` · ruling · Ibn Kathīr: with many other hadiths, reciting al-Fātiḥah in the prayer — by the imam and by those behind him — is required in every prayer and in every rakʿah. [kathir¶1.16]
