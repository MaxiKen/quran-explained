### AL-FĀTIḤAH AS A REMEDY

- `1:0.0` · hadith · Al-Bukhārī (faḍāʾil al-Qurʾān), Abū Saʿīd al-Khudrī: on a journey a female servant said the area's leader had been poisoned and their people were away — was there a healer? A man whose healing skill they had not valued stood, read a ruqyah and the chief recovered; he gave him thirty sheep and milk. The man said he had used only Umm al-Kitāb as a ruqyah; they told him to do nothing more until they asked the Prophet, who said "Who told him it is a ruqyah? Divide the sheep and reserve a share for me." [kathir¶1.2, kathir¶1.10]
