### WHERE IT CAME DOWN

- `1:0.0` · history · Baghawī: Makkan according to most; Mujāhid said Madinan; and it was said it came down twice, once at Makkah and once at Madīnah, which is why it is called mathānī. [baghawi¶4]
- `1:0.0` · history · Baghawī prefers Makkan: God reminds the Prophet of it as a favour in "We have given you seven of the oft-repeated" (15:87), meaning Fātiḥat al-Kitāb, and Sūrat al-Ḥijr is Makkan — so the favour could not be mentioned before it came down. [baghawi¶4]
- `1:0.0` · history · Saʿdī: "It is Makkan." [saadi¶1]

- `1:0.0` · history · Ibn Kathīr: it was revealed in Makkah, as Ibn ʿAbbās, Qatādah and Abū l-ʿĀliyah said, and God said "We have given you seven of the oft-repeated" (15:87) — "God knows best". [kathir¶1.1-1.2]
