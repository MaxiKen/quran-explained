### WHERE IT CAME DOWN

- `1:0.0` · history · Baghawī: Makkan according to most; Mujāhid said Madinan; and it was said it came down twice, once at Makkah and once at Madīnah, which is why it is called mathānī. [baghawi¶4]
- `1:0.0` · history · Baghawī prefers Makkan: God reminds the Prophet of it as a favour in "We have given you seven of the oft-repeated" (15:87), meaning Fātiḥat al-Kitāb, and Sūrat al-Ḥijr is Makkan — so the favour could not be mentioned before it came down. [baghawi¶4]
- `1:0.0` · history · Saʿdī: "It is Makkan." [saadi¶1]

- `1:0.0` · history · Ibn Kathīr: it was revealed in Makkah, as Ibn ʿAbbās, Qatādah and Abū l-ʿĀliyah said, and God said "We have given you seven of the oft-repeated" (15:87) — "God knows best". [kathir¶1.1-1.2]
- `1:0.0` · athar · Mujāhid (Ibn al-Anbārī, in al-Radd, through Qurṭubī): Iblīs wailed four times — when he was cursed, when he was sent down from Paradise, when Muḥammad was sent, and when al-Fātiḥah was sent down — and it was sent down at Madīnah. [qurtubi¶38.4]
- `1:0.0` · history · Qurṭubī, second issue: Ibn ʿAbbās, Qatādah, Abū l-ʿĀliyah al-Riyāḥī (Rufayʿ) and others — Makkan; Abū Hurayrah, Mujāhid, ʿAṭāʾ ibn Yasār, al-Zuhrī and others — Madinan; and it is said half came down at Makkah and half at Madīnah (Abū l-Layth al-Samarqandī, in his tafsīr). The first is sounder, because of "We have given you seven of the oft-repeated and the great Qurʾān" (15:87), al-Ḥijr being Makkan by consensus, and because there is no dispute that the obligation of prayer was at Makkah. [qurtubi¶59.2]
- `1:0.0` · lesson · It is not remembered that there was ever a prayer in Islam without "al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn", as "there is no prayer except with Fātiḥat al-Kitāb" shows — a report about the ruling, not about the beginning, Qurṭubī adds. [qurtubi¶59.3]
- `1:0.0` · history · Al-Qāḍī Ibn al-Ṭayyib mentioned the disagreement over what came down first — al-Muddaththir, "Read", or al-Fātiḥah (Qurṭubī). [qurtubi¶59.3]
- `1:0.0` · hadith · Al-Bayhaqī (Dalāʾil al-Nubuwwah), Abū Maysarah ʿAmr ibn Shuraḥbīl: the Prophet told Khadījah, "When I am alone I hear a call, and by God I fear it is something"; she answered "God forbid — God would not do that to you; you deliver trusts, keep kin and speak truth"; she sent Abū Bakr ("ʿAtīq") with him to Waraqah ibn Nawfal; the Prophet said he heard a voice behind him "O Muḥammad, O Muḥammad" and ran off; Waraqah told him to stay until he heard what it said and then come back; when alone he heard "O Muḥammad, say 'bismillāhi l-raḥmāni l-raḥīm, al-ḥamdu li-llāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn' … to 'wa-lā l-ḍāllīn'; say 'lā ilāha illā llāh'"; Waraqah: "Rejoice, then rejoice: I testify you are the one ʿĪsā ibn Maryam announced, on the like of Mūsā's Nāmūs, a sent prophet who will be ordered to fight after today — and if I live I will fight with you"; when Waraqah died the Prophet said "I saw the priest in Paradise in garments of silk, for he believed in me and confirmed me." Al-Bayhaqī: this is munqaṭiʿ. [qurtubi¶59.3-59.4]
