### HOW MANY VERSES, WORDS AND LETTERS

- `1:0.0` · ruling · Ibn Kathīr: there is no disagreement that al-Fātiḥah has seven verses; according to most Kūfan reciters, a group of the Companions, the Tābiʿūn and a number of later scholars, the basmalah is a separate verse at its beginning — he promises to return to this. [kathir¶1.3]
- `1:0.0` · history · The scholars say al-Fātiḥah has twenty-five words and one hundred and thirteen letters (Ibn Kathīr). [kathir¶1.3]
