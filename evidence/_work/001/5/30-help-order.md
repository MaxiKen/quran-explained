### SEEKING HELP, AND WHY WORSHIP IS NAMED FIRST

- `1:5.0` · language · Baghawī: "nastaʿīn" — we ask Your help for worshipping You and for all our affairs. [baghawi¶24]
- `1:5.0` · lesson · Objection: why is worship named before seeking help, when help comes before worship? Baghawī: that binds only one who puts capacity before the act; Ahl al-Sunnah put God's granting of success (tawfīq) and help with the act, so the order makes no difference; or seeking help is itself a kind of worship, so He named worship as a whole and then one of its parts. [baghawi¶24]
