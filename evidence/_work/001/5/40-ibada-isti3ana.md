### SINGLING GOD OUT, AND WHAT WORSHIP AND HELP ARE

- `1:5.0` · language · Saʿdī: "we single You alone out for worship and seeking help" — putting the object first gives restriction (ḥaṣr), affirming the rule for what is named and denying it for all else: "we worship You and none other; we seek help of You and none other". [saadi¶5]
- `1:5.0` · language · Saʿdī: worship is named before help as the general before the particular, and out of concern to put God's right before His servant's right. [saadi¶5]
- `1:5.0` · language · Saʿdī: ʿibādah is a comprehensive name for what God loves and is pleased with of deeds and words, outward and inward; istiʿānah is relying on God to bring benefits and ward off harms, trusting Him to achieve it. [saadi¶5]
- `1:5.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: observing worship and seeking His help is the way to everlasting happiness and safety from all evils; worship is worship only when taken from the Messenger and meant for God's face; help is named after worship though included in it, because the servant needs God's help in all his worship — if God does not help him he cannot do what he wants of obeying commands and avoiding prohibitions. [saadi¶5]
