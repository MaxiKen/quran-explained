### THE WORD ṢIRĀṬ AND ITS SPELLINGS

- `1:6.0` · language · Baghawī: ṣirāṭ is read with ṣād, with sīn (Uways from Yaʿqūb; the original, so called because it "swallows" — yasruṭ — the travellers) and with zāy; Ḥamzah gives the zāy an ishmām; all are valid dialects and most qurrāʾ prefer the ṣād for agreement with the codex.
