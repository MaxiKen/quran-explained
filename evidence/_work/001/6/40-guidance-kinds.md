### GUIDANCE TO THE PATH AND GUIDANCE IN IT

- `1:6.0` · language · Saʿdī: "ihdinā" — show us, direct us and give us success toward the straight path, the clear road that brings one to God and His Paradise: knowing the truth and acting by it. [saadi¶7]
- `1:6.0` · lesson · Saʿdī: guidance to the path is holding to Islam and leaving other religions; guidance in the path covers guidance to all the religious details in knowledge and action — so this prayer is among the most comprehensive and useful, and a person must make it in every rakʿah, out of his need of it. [saadi¶7]
