### GUIDE US: DIRECT US, OR KEEP US FIRM

- `1:6.0` · athar · Baghawī: "ihdinā" means direct us; ʿAlī and Ubayy ibn Kaʿb: "keep us firm", as one says to a man standing, "stand until I return" — remain as you are. So the believers' prayer, though they are already guided, means firmness and asking for more guidance, since God's gentle favours and guidance have no end on the Ahl al-Sunnah's view. [baghawi¶25]
