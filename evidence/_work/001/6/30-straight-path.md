### WHAT THE STRAIGHT PATH IS, IN BAGHAWĪ

- `1:6.0` · athar · Ibn ʿAbbās and Jābir: Islam (also Muqātil); Ibn Masʿūd: the Qurʾān; reported from ʿAlī as the Prophet's saying, "the straight path is the Book of God" — in Ṭabarī, where Aḥmad Shākir weakens it (Baghawī's note). [baghawi¶26]
- `1:6.0` · athar · Saʿīd ibn Jubayr: the way to Paradise; Sahl ibn ʿAbdillāh: the way of the Sunnah and the Community. [baghawi¶26]
- `1:6.0` · athar · Bakr ibn ʿAbdillāh al-Muzanī: the way of the Messenger; Abū l-ʿĀliyah and al-Ḥasan: the Messenger, his family and his two companions; in language the word means a clear road. [baghawi¶26]
