## 1:1 — بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

*[context]* In the name of Allah, the Beneficent, the Merciful.

### V1 · Qurʾānic text & translation

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** The verse is given only as the rendering *"In the name of Allah, the
Beneficent, the Merciful"*; the work passes on to the commentary of 1:2 without a separate exposition of
the basmalah.

_Coverage: V1 only._

## 1:2 — الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

*[context]* All praise belongs to Allah, Lord of the worlds.

### V2 · Running exposition (bayān)

**Ibn ʿAbbās** — *on his authority it is related that Ibn ʿAbbās said, regarding the saying of Allah,
Exalted is He, "Praise be to Allah":*

*On "Praise be to Allah" three reports are given:*

1. "He says: laudation is Allah's in that He is kind towards His created beings and, hence, they praise Him."
2. "It is also said that laudation is Allah's because of His excessive blessings upon His servants whom He has guided to believe in Him."
3. "It is also said: laudation, absolute unity and godhood all belong to Allah Who has neither progeny, partner, helper nor an assistant."

*On "Lord of the worlds" three reports are given:*

1. "Lord of any that has a spirit and walks about on the face of the earth and also of the dwellers of heaven."
2. "It is also said that this means: the Master of the jinn and human beings."
3. "It is also said that (Lord of the worlds) means the Creator of all created beings, their Provider and the One Who changes them from one state into another."

_Cross-heads: V11 — the third report names the exclusive divine prerogatives (absolute unity and godhood,
no progeny, partner, helper or assistant)._

### V8 · Language, lexicon & grammar (lughah · iʿrāb · balāghah)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** The phrase is glossed through the lexical range of *al-ḥamd*: in the
three reports it is rendered *laudation* — praise called out of kindness shown (report 1), praise called
out of abundant blessing and guidance (report 2), and praise bound together with *absolute unity* and
*godhood* (report 3). "Lord of the worlds" is likewise glossed through the range of *al-Rabb*: owner of
every living, walking creature and of the dwellers of heaven; master of jinn and humankind; and creator,
provider and transformer of states.

_Coverage: V1, V2, V8, V11 (cross-head)._

## 1:3 — الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ

*[context]* The Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful.

### V2 · Running exposition (bayān)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** "(The Beneficent) the Gentle. (The Merciful) the Gentle." — the two
divine names are both expounded as gentleness shown to creation.

### V8 · Language, lexicon & grammar (lughah · iʿrāb · balāghah)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** The rendering glosses both *al-Raḥmān* and *al-Raḥīm* by the same
English word, *the Gentle* — the distinction carried by the Arabic pair is not brought out in this
rendering. *(Quoted as given: "(The Beneficent) the Gentle. (The Merciful) the Gentle.")*

_Coverage: V1, V2, V8._

---

## 1:4 — مَالِكِ يَوْمِ الدِّينِ

*[context]* Master of the Day of Judgement.

### V2 · Running exposition (bayān)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** "Owner of the Day of Judgement" is "the Arbitrator on the Day of
Judgement which is the Day of Reckoning and settlement of disputes between created beings, i.e. the Day
when people are judged according to their deeds; there is no judge save Him."

_Cross-heads: V11 — the closing clause, "there is no judge save Him", is the exclusive divine prerogative
of judgement on that day._

_Coverage: V1, V2, V11 (cross-head)._

## 1:5 — إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ

*[context]* You alone we worship, and You alone we seek help.

### V2 · Running exposition (bayān)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** "(Thee (alone) we worship), we turn to you as the only One God and we
obey you; ((Thee alone) we ask for help), we ask for your help in worshipping You and from You we obtain
confidence in obeying You."

_Cross-heads: V11 — "we turn to you as the only One God" states the singling out of Allah in worship
(tawḥīd). V14 — "from You we obtain confidence in obeying You" states reliance on Allah for the strength
to obey._

_Coverage: V1, V2, V11 (cross-head), V14 (cross-head)._

---

## 1:6 — اهْدِنَا الصِّرَاطَ الْمُسْتَقِيمَ

*[context]* Guide us to the straight path.

### V2 · Running exposition (bayān)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** Three reports are given for "Guide us to the straight path":

1. "Guide us to the established Religion that You are pleased with, i.e. Islam."
2. "It is also said that this means: make us firm in holding fast to it."
3. "It is also said that the straight path refers to Allah's Book, and guidance to this means guidance to that which it prescribes as lawful or unlawful and to the exposition of its content."

_Coverage: V1, V2._

## 1:7 — صِرَاطَ الَّذِينَ أَنْعَمْتَ عَلَيْهِمْ غَيْرِ الْمَغْضُوبِ عَلَيْهِمْ وَلَا الضَّالِّينَ

*[context]* The path of those whom You have blessed — not of those who incurred wrath, nor of those who are astray.

### V2 · Running exposition (bayān)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** On "The path of those whom Thou hast favoured": "(the Religion of
those whom You have blessed with the Religion and who are the followers of Moses, before the blessings
of Allah deserted them … It is also said that (the path of those whom Thou hast favoured) refers to the
prophets." On "Not of those who earn Thine anger": "not the religion of the Jews who earned Your anger,
whom You forsook and whose hearts You did not protect until they became complaisant." On "Nor of those
who go astray": "nor the religion of the Christians who erred from Islam."

### V13 · History, sīrah & parables

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** Within the report on "those whom Thou hast favoured" stands a
wilderness narrative: the blessing of the followers of Moses is described as the time when
"*He shaded them with a white cloud and sent down on them honey and quails when they were in the
wilderness*".

### V17 · Specific virtues & supplications (faḍāʾil · duʿāʾ)

**Ibn ʿAbbās (as rendered here).** On *Āmīn*, with which the sūrah closes, three reports are given:
"thus shall be its custodians; it is also said that Amen means let it be so. It is also said that it
means: O our Lord! Do with us as we have requested You. And Allah knows best."

_Coverage: V1, V2, V13, V17._
