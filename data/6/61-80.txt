=61=

"He is the Supreme over His servants, and He sends guardians over you until when death comes to one of you, Our messengers take his soul, never neglecting."

This verse deepens Al-An'am's argument from creation to guardianship to Ibrahim's search. In simple words it reminds the reader that the Supreme sends angels to guard and to take souls, that only Allah saves from darkness, and that Ibrahim turned from setting stars to the Ever-Living. The Meccan listeners who demanded angels in 6:8 and mocked as in 6:10 heard this as answer: guardians are already over you, and the kingdom shown to Ibrahim is greater than any paper book.

It links to neighbours and to the whole Book: guardians over you as in 6:61 and 82:10, return to the True Master as in 6:62 and 22:6, darkness of land and sea as in 6:63 and 27:62, punishment from above/below as in 6:65 and 29:55, and Ibrahim's hanif turn as in 6:79 and 2:135. Revelation came when the Prophet was told not to drive away the morning rememberers in 6:52 and to be patient with deniers in 6:66-68. Hadith teaches that hearts are between fingers, that prayer is light, and that the Trumpet's blast is truth as in 6:73, and that Ibrahim is Khalil.

For today make it lived: remember the guardians over you in 6:61 when alone, call only Him in darkness as in 6:63-64 not idols, fear sectarian violence as in 6:65 and hold to the rope, and follow Ibrahim's method in 6:76-79 — test every setting thing, love only what does not set, and turn your face upright to the Originator.
