"And who is more unjust than one who invents a lie about Allah or denies His signs? Indeed, the wrongdoers will never succeed."

In simple words it shows that gathering on the Day, covering hearts, and standing before the Fire are one chain. The Meccan listeners who heard it — some who listened to the Prophet to mock, some who prevented others, some who said there is only this life — saw their own three responses named. The verse does not only inform, it diagnoses.

It links to its neighbours and to the wider Book: the gathering as in 6:22 and 6:12, the covers on hearts as in 6:25 and 17:46 and 36:9, the play of worldly life as in 6:32 and 29:64, and the grief of the Prophet as in 6:33 and 26:3. Revelation came when the Prophet grieved over mockery and was told messengers before were patient as in 6:34, and that only listeners respond as in 6:36 while dead hearts need resurrection. Hadith adds that hearts harden by sins until they are sealed, and that the Prophet taught that the Quran is a witness for or against you.

The Meccan listeners who heard it — some who listened to the Prophet to mock, some who prevented others, some who said there is only this life — saw their own three responses named. The verse does not only inform, it diagnoses.

It links to its neighbours and to the wider Book: the gathering as in 6:22 and 6:12, the covers on hearts as in 6:25 and 17:46 and 36:9, the play of worldly life as in 6:32 and 29:64, and the grief of the Prophet as in 6:33 and 26:3. Revelation came when the Prophet grieved over mockery and was told messengers before were patient as in 6:34, and that only listeners respond as in 6:36 while dead hearts need resurrection. Hadith adds that hearts harden by sins until they are sealed, and that the Prophet taught that the Quran is a witness for or against you.

For today turn the verse into a habit: do not be among those who invent a lie for gain as in 6:21, who listen to argue as in 6:25, or who prevent others as in 6:26. Instead, ask to be among those who listen and respond as in 6:36, who see worldly life as play but Hereafter as better as in 6:32, and who call only upon Allah when harm touches as in 6:40. That is how one verse becomes a daily check and twenty become a rope that holds Al-Baqarah's worship and Al-Imran's firmness together, simple for all. 
Simple step: today, listen to one ayah as if it is the first time, without cover, and let it change one choice.

For today, carry this verse as a question: where in my day am I listening without hearing, preventing without knowing, or calling other than Him? One honest answer is a step.
