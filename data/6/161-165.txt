=161=

"Say: Indeed, my Lord has guided me to a straight path — an upright religion — the creed of Abraham, hanif, and he was not among the polytheists."

This closing verse gathers the surah's long argument into a single act of devotion. In simple words it declares that guidance is not a private opinion but a straight path, an upright religion, the hanif millah of Ibrahim who turned from every setting thing. The Meccan listeners who first heard it — after demands for angels, after mocking the Quran as ancient tales, after assigning partners to Allah in crops and children — were told that the straight path is known, walked, and witnessed, not hidden.

It links to its neighbours and to the whole Quran: the straight path in 6:161 echoes 6:153 and 1:6, the prayer and sacrifice for Allah in 6:162 echoes 108:2, the hanif creed echoes 6:79 and 2:135, and the degrees in 6:165 echo 6:132 and 6:83. Revelation came when the Prophet was told to be first to submit as in 6:14 and 6:163, and to disavow what polytheists do. Hadith teaches that the best religion is the hanif, that deeds are by intentions, and that Allah is Swift in punishment yet Forgiving, Merciful — the two names that close the surah together as in 5:98 and 6:165.

For today make it lived: say 'my Lord has guided me' as in 6:161 with certainty, not as a wish; make your prayer, sacrifice, living and dying for Him alone as in 6:162, not for show; seek no lord beside Him as in 6:164; and remember you are a successor on earth as in 6:165 — raised in degrees not to boast but to be tested. That is how five closing verses become a life, simple for every home, blending Al-Baqarah's worship, Al-Imran's firmness, and Al-Ma'idah's Table into one upright path.

Simple step: today, live one verse — pray for Him alone, give for Him alone, live and die with His name on your tongue, and thank the One who made you a successor.

Remember: the path straight, the sacrifice for Him, and the successor's test are one story — Ibrahim's hanif turn, Muhammad's first submission, and your daily prayer are the same rope.
