=101=

"He is the Originator of the heavens and the earth — how can He have a child when He has no companion and He created all things? He is Knowing of all things."

This verse continues the surah's turn from Ibrahim's pure tawhid to the limits of human sight and the duty to follow revelation. In simple words it teaches that the Originator needs no companion to create, that vision cannot grasp Him but He grasps it, and that insight has come for whoever will see. The Meccan listeners who demanded to see Allah plainly as in 6:8 and swore strongest oaths as in 6:109 heard this as a gentle boundary: the unseen remains unseen, yet signs are diversified for those who know as in 6:105.

It links to neighbours and to the whole Book: the Originator in 6:101 and Creator in 6:102 echo the Creator in 6:1-2, the Subtle awareness in 6:103 echoes 6:3's knowledge of hidden, and the insight in 6:104 echoes the light in 6:15 and 6:82. Revelation came when polytheists insulted as in 6:108, when they swore as in 6:109, and when hearts were turned as in 6:110 after first rejection. Hadith teaches that Allah is Subtle with His servants, that hearts turn as eyes turn, and that devils inspire decorative speech as in 6:112. Other verses — 17:85, 42:51, 6:115 — echo the completed word in truth and justice.

For today make it lived: glorify the Originator who creates without partner as in 6:101, worship the Creator and Disposer as in 6:102, and remember vision will not perceive Him here as in 6:103, so follow insight as in 6:104. Diversify your recitation as in 6:105, follow only what was revealed as in 6:106 and do not insult as in 6:108 lest you provoke. When oaths are sworn as in 6:109, rely on Allah's ability, not on signs on demand, and eat only where His name is mentioned as in 6:118, leaving outward and inward sin as in 6:120. That is how twenty verses become one light that blends Al-Baqarah's guidance and Al-Imran's firmness, simple for every household to live.

Practical step: today, read one diversified sign with an open heart, follow the detailed Book as your judge, and eat only where His name is remembered — small obedience keeps the heart from wandering.
