=121=

"Those — their refuge is Hell, they will find no escape from it."

Ula'ika ma'wahum Jahannam wa la yajiduna anha mahisa — those who took Satan as wali in 4:119-120, whose promises were pure ghurur — delusion as thin as dust on a rock in 2:264 that a little rain washes bare — whose appointed share — nasib mafrud — was taken by the marid who said "I will take a fixed share of Your servants," their ma'wa — refuge, abode, home, final lodging, the place a traveller returns to — is Hell, and they find no mahis — escape, refuge, slant, crevice to slip away, side road, exit. Mahis from hayyasa — to slip, to turn aside, to find a gap. No mahis as skins were renewed to taste anew in 4:56 — "whenever skins mature We replace them so they taste" — and as no hadith was hidden from the Witness in 4:42 — "they will not hide any statement from Allah" — and as Muhit encompassed every night plot in 4:108 and 4:126 — "Allah encompasses what they do," and as Hasib reckoned every naqir. The verse after "Satan promises nothing but delusion" closes delusion with enclosure: delusion promised escape from hudud of inheritance, from four witnesses for chastity, from the need to refer news to those who extract, from the need to be just with one wife when fearing injustice, from the need to return the key. Hell gives no mahis, no side door, no discount, no reduction, no intercession without permission.

The surah now holds two ma'wa, two homes, for two kinds of followers, two refuges that mirror and teach. For those who believed and did righteous, Gardens beneath which rivers flow abiding forever and deep shade zalil in 4:57 and 4:122; for those who followed ghurur, Hell with no mahis. The believer who feared being levelled with dust in 4:42 yet worked, who gave dowry nihlah in 4:4, who returned trusts in 4:58, who stood for orphans, finds a refuge that never exposes his faults but covers them with Afuww; the deceiver who hid from people in 4:108 but not from the Encompassing, who hid orphan wealth by mixing in 4:2, who hid a wife's mahr behind buhtan in 4:20, who hid night plotting, finds a refuge that never releases him and never forgets. The Prophet described Hell dragged on the Day with seventy thousand reins, each rein pulled by seventy thousand angels; no mahis from that dragging, no rope cut. After Satan's claimed share, this says his share ends without share of escape, and the inheritance share of the Fire has no khiyar, no choice.

=122=

"But those who believe and do righteous deeds, We will admit them to Gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding forever. Promise of Allah in truth. Whose statement is truer than Allah's?"

Walladhina amanu wa amilu as-salihat sa-nudkhiluhum jannatin tajri min tahtiha al-anhar khalidina fiha abadan — but those who believe and do righteous deeds — amanu wa amilu as-salihat — the same twin condition as in 4:57, the same as 4:110's seeking with forgiveness, the same as 4:69's obedience that earns companionship, the same as 4:95's strivers, the same as 4:110 — enter Gardens beneath which rivers flow, abiding forever — abadan — the longest abiding in the surah, the word that will not end, opposite the wish to be dust in 4:42 and the no mahis enclosure in 4:121 and the skins' taste that never ends. Rivers flow, not stagnate; abundance moves, never bores, never dries.

Wa'da Allahi haqqan — promise of Allah in truth — haqq — the opposite of ghurur which is batil, delusion, false promise, as 3:185, as 31:33. As 3:9 "Allah does not break the promise of the Hour," and 30:60 "Allah's promise is true, let not those without certainty shake you," and 4:87 "whose hadith truer than Allah's?" and 10:55. The promise is not wishful amaniyy as in 4:123. Wa man asdaqu min Allahi qilan — whose qawl — speech, saying, statement, word, promise — is truer than Allah's? Qilan is more emphatic than hadith; even the form, the wording, the grammar of His speech is truest, no clause needed, no footnote, no correction. The promise after delusion heals the heart after night plotting, after throwing sin on the innocent in 4:112, after opposing the Messenger after guidance clear in 4:115, after taking Satan as wali, after tawalli. The believer who simply believed and did salih — prayer shortened yet kept as in 4:101 with acceptance of charity, trust returned as in 4:58 with Sami' Basir, justice with one wife as in 4:3 and 4:129 without inclining fully, salam returned better as in 4:86, intercession with good share as in 4:85, standing for qist even against himself in 4:135, verifying before branding in 4:94 — will be admitted not by lineage as in 4:49's claim "we purify ourselves," not by tribe as in 4:54's envy, but by promise that is haqq and by qawl that is asdaq.

The Prophet recited this verse to a Bedouin who asked, "Is it true, O Messenger of Allah? Has Allah promised this to us, we the sinners who believe?" and he said, "Yes, and it is haqq, and no one tells truer than Allah; are you not pleased? Should I not give you good tidings?" For today's reader surrounded by ghurur marketing that promises "keep riba and keep barakah, keep taghut and keep iman, keep night plotting and keep trust," haqq is anchor: Allah's promise is not a campaign slogan to be revised after election; it is true, verifiable by the Owner of heavens and earth who is Muhit, and no one speaks truer, no hadith is truer, and the believer's hope is built on rock.

=123=

"It is not by your wishful thinking nor by the wishful thinking of the People of the Book. Whoever does evil will be recompensed for it and will not find besides Allah any guardian or helper."

Laysa bi-amaniyyikum wa la amaniyyi Ahli al-Kitabi — it is not by your wishful thinking — amaniyy — nor theirs. Amaniyy is wish that floats without deed, from tamanna — to wish idly, to sing, to recite without acting, to assume — the same amaniyy that built "Fire will touch us only days" in 2:80, 3:24, "we are beloved sons of Allah" in 5:18, "we are the saved group so deeds do not matter" in 9:31, and modern "we are the chosen nation, we are Sunnis, we love the Prophet so deeds do not matter" or "if only we had been with them for great success" in 4:73, "our sheikh will intercede without deeds." The Jews said "we will enter Paradise, you will not," Christians said opposite, some Muslims in early Madinah thought being Muslim alone without deeds, without salah, suffices. The verse says not by your amaniyy nor theirs, not by identity, not by slogan, not by love alone, not by claim.

Man ya'mal su'an yujza bihi — whoever does su' — evil, as in 4:110 su' versus zulm nafs, as in 42:30, 2:284 — will be recompensed — yujza — exactly, by atom in 4:40, by fatil in 4:49, by naqir in 4:53, by dam, by worry, by loss. The recompense is not only Fire; it is also expiation. The Prophet said to Abu Bakr when this verse descended and Abu Bakr paled and said, "Who can escape after this? We all do evil, O Messenger, are we all recompensed, are we doomed?" — "O Abu Bakr, are you not saddened, are you not fatigued, are you not pricked by a thorn? Are you not worried? Every hardship is expiation, even a thorn that pricks you, even worry that touches you, even fatigue." Thus yujza is not only Fire for the insistently evil; it is also expiation in dunya for the believer who slips and seeks, as 4:110, and as 2:286.

Wa la yajid lahu min duni Allahi waliyyan wa la nasira — will find no guardian — waliy — nor helper — nasir — besides Allah — the same pair sufficient as Wali Nasir in 4:45 and in 4:75's du'a for a ladunni Wali Nasir and in 4:75's mustad'afin, and denied here. Amaniyy imagined waliy and nasir in tribe, in taghut court, in intercessor without permission, in saying "we will be with the righteous by love without deeds" and "our sheikh will carry us"; the verse says no waliy, no nasir to avert recompense, no wasta without permission. This after Gardens versus Hell reframes success: Gardens are not by claiming "we are Muslims" or "we are People of the Book" or "we love the Prophet," but by doing and avoiding su', by seeking, by standing. Wishful thinking is the ghurur Satan promised in 4:120; recompense is Haqq in 4:122. The balance is mercy with justice: su' not overlooked, but after it, 4:110's seeking finds Ghafur, and 4:103's prayer and 4:64's coming are paths, and even yujza itself is mercy when it is thorn and worry that cleans.

=124=

"Whoever does righteous deeds — male or female — while believing, will enter Paradise and will not be wronged a speck."

Wa man ya'mal min as-salihati min dhakarin aw untha wa huwa mu'min — whoever does righteous deeds — min as-salihat — from the righteous deeds, some, not necessarily all, but sincere, consistent — male or female — dhakarin aw untha — while believing — huwa mu'min — condition without which deeds are as dust scattered as in 4:38 spenders without belief in Last Day, as in 18:103-106, as in 25:23. Fa-ula'ika yadkhuluna al-jannata wa la yuzlamuna naqira — will enter Paradise — yadkhuluna al-jannata — and will not be wronged a naqir — speck on date stone's back where sprout emerges, the smallest point, the pit's back, as in 4:53 "would not give people even a naqir" and 4:49 fatil on the groove and 35:13 qitmir membrane and 4:40 dharrah atom and 4:77 fatil. Allah stacks the smallest images to say justice and reward are atomic: no good deed lost, no speck withheld, no intention hidden, no tear unseen, no early dawn wudu unseen.

Male or female while believing is equality of recompense after earlier equality of share — nasib for men and women in 4:7 "for men a share, for women a share," earning nasib in 4:32 "men have a share of what they earned, women have a share of what they earned," and "you are from one another" in 4:25, and 3:195 "I will not waste the deed of any doer among you male or female, you are from one another," and 16:97 "whoever does righteous, male or female, while believing, We will give a good life and great reward," and 4:124 repeats for An-Nisa's women. The verse answers any lingering claim that because women inherit half in some cases of 4:11 it means worth half: worth with Allah is by iman and salih, and Paradise entry, its rivers and shade and purified spouses, is not half. The slave woman whose hadd punishment was halved in 4:25 but whose reward is not half is included; the weak mustad'af who could not emigrate in 4:98-99 but wanted to is included; the traveller who prayed shortened is included; the sick who prayed with tayammum is included.

Wa la yuzlamuna naqira — not a speck of hasanah lost, not a grain of mercy withheld, not a tear unseen, not a salam returned better unseen. For the woman told her deeds count less because her testimony counts differently in some contracts, for the orphan girl told her share is less, for the mother told her du'a is less — no naqir lost. For today's girl told "your prayer in the corner is less, your charity is less, your fasting is less," this verse is direct: while believing, deeds count full, Paradise entry equal, no naqir of injustice, and the Appreciative Knower in 4:147 Shakir Alim will appreciate even a smile.

=125=

"Who is better in religion than one who submits his face to Allah while being a doer of good and follows the creed of Ibrahim, upright? Allah took Ibrahim as a close friend."

Wa man ahsanu dinan mimman aslama wajhahu lillahi wa huwa muhsinun — who is better in religion — ahsanu dinan — than one who aslama — submitted — wajhahu — his face — whole self, presence, direction, the noblest part that shows intention and honour, the part that prostrates — to Allah while he is muhsin — doer of good, excellence, ihsan — doing good as if he sees Allah, as the Prophet defined ihsan in Jibril hadith, as 2:112 "whoever submits his face while doing good will have reward, no fear, no grief," and as 31:22 "whoever submits his face and is muhsin has grasped the firm handle, the trustworthy." Wattaba'a millata Ibrahima hanifan — and follows the creed — millah — path, way, with its shade and law and rites and hajj — of Ibrahim, hanifan — upright, turning from crooked to pure, from crooked idols to pure tawhid, as Ibrahim smashed idols in the house his father Azar built for them, stood in fire and Allah made it coolness — bardan wa salama — left Hajar in the valley trusting "is it Allah who commanded? Then He will not waste us," and built the House on tawhid with Ismail saying "Our Lord, accept from us, You are Hearing, Knowing," and was told to follow.

Wattakhada Allahu Ibrahima khalila — and Allah took Ibrahim as khalil — intimate friend — khalil from khullah — deepest love that permeates and interweaves, the highest love after habib, as the Prophet Muhammad is Habibullah and also Khalil Ar-Rahman, but Ibrahim is named Khalil here exclusively. Khalil is not legal kinship but love after submitting face through fire, knife, valley, and building, and it is given, not claimed. The verse after "not by wishful thinking" and "not wronged a speck" defines best deen: not claim, not lineage, not slogan, not group, not era, but three together: submission of face, ihsan in deeds, and hanif followership, all together, as 4:125's three. Ibrahim's fire and knife and valley are aslama wajhahu; his hospitality to guests with a roasted calf, his prayer for his father, his prayer for the city to be safe, his building, his supplication, are huwa muhsin. Without submission, ihsan is riya as in 4:38; without hanif, submission is bid'ah as in 4:60.

For today's Muslim navigating competing authentic paths — spirituality without Sharia, law without mercy, modernity without revelation, activism without tawhid, culture without hanifiyyah, Sufism without Sunnah, Salafism without sulh — Ibrahim's hanif creed is the filter: submit face to One God, do good as if you see Him, follow hanif not hizb, not tribe, not trend, not the cult of personality. The best religion is not the loudest, not the harshest, but the most submitted and most beautified, and then Allah calls such a submitter khalil.

=126=

"To Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever on earth. Allah is ever Encompassing of all things."

Wa lillahi ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ardi — to Allah belongs whatever is in the heavens and whatever on earth — repeated as in 4:131-133, a refrain after creed that grounds khullah that might otherwise seem like peer friendship needing equality. The One who took Ibrahim as khalil owns all heavens and earth; friendship with Him is not need but honour and selection, as a king taking a poor man as friend is honour to the man, not need of the king. Wa kan Allahu bi-kulli shay'in Muhita — Allah is ever Encompassing — Muhit — as in 4:108 He encompasses what they plot by night, 3:120 He encompasses what they scheme and conceal, 4:40 no atom escapes His knowledge, 4:108 no secret escapes, 4:42 no hadith hidden, 4:148 Hearing. Muhit means ownership plus knowledge plus power that leaves no edge outside Him; no deed, no wish, no speck, no trick, no ransom relocates outside His kingdom, no refuge leaves His perimeter, no shafa'ah without permission.

After amaniyy that imagines seizing what belongs to Him, and khalil's nearness is nearness to the Owner who encompasses, the promise of Gardens and threat of no mahis both stand because the Owner is Muhit; no deed, no ransom, no intercession without permission exits His perimeter. For the believer, Muhit is comfort: his hidden charity in 4:114 and his suppressed anger in 3:134 and his verification before sharing in 4:83 are also encompassed, not lost. For the deceiver, Muhit is terror.

=127=

"They ask you for a ruling about women. Say: Allah gives you a ruling about them and about what is recited to you in the Book concerning orphan women you do not give them what is ordained for them though you desire to marry them, and about helpless children, and that you stand for orphans with justice. Whatever good you do, Allah is Knowing of it."

Wa yastaftunaka fin-nisa — they ask you for fatwa — yastaftunaka — about women, after hijrah, after jihad, after hypocrisy, after prayer, after Satan, after Gardens, after amaniyy, after Paradise for both sexes, after khalil. Qul Allahu yuftikum fihinna — say, Allah gives you ruling about them — the Mufti is Allah, not custom, not the loudest podcaster, not "women's liberation" as defined by those who worship inathan female idols made by men, not the market, not the tribe. Wa ma yutla alaykum fil-Kitabi fi yatama an-nisa allati la tu'tunahumma ma kutiba lahunna wa targhabuna an tankihuhunna — and what is recited to you in the Book about orphan women you do not give them what is ordained — ma kutiba — what is fixed — mahr and share — though you desire to marry them — to keep wealth without full mahr as in 4:3 "if you fear not to be just to orphans, marry other women." Wal-mustad'afina min al-wildani — and about helpless children — who were given no share in Jahiliyyah and stood at division in 4:8, and were unable to emigrate in 4:98-99, and will stand at mizan, and whose fathers worried in 4:9 about weak offspring.

Wa an taqumu lil-yatama bil-qist — and that you stand for orphans with justice — qist — upright, standing, as 4:58 "judge with justice" and 4:135 "stand firm for justice as witnesses for Allah even against yourselves" and 4:3 "be just to orphans" and 4:127's qist. Qist for orphans is not pity only; it is standing — qawwam — as in 4:34, as in 4:127. Revelation repeats the opening orphan-marriage question because after detailing prayer, jihad, shirk, Satan, the community still asked about women; the disease of withholding from women remains the last to leave a society, as seen when brothers still take houses and say "she will marry, she does not need." Allah answers: the recitation you already have answers it; stand with qist, do not marry the orphan to take her share, do not keep her waiting, do not make her mu'allaqah, do not eat.

Wa ma taf'alu min khayrin fa-inna Allaha kana bihi Alima — whatever good you do — standing for orphans with qist, giving charity in najwa in 4:114, interceding good share in 4:85, greeting better in 4:86, remembering much after fear, holding fast, verifying — Allah is Knowing of it — Alim as in 4:32, 4:12 Halim, 4:70 Kafa Alim, 3:92, 4:147. For today, ask about women to Allah, not to the trend that changes every season; His fatwa is recited; it gave orphan women full mahr, share nasib mafruda, and qist, and it gave helpless children a share and standing, and it gave you a path to stand and be known by Him and rewarded.

=128=

"If a woman fears from her husband defiance or desertion, there is no blame if they reconcile between them with settlement. Reconciliation is better. Souls are prone to stinginess. If you do good and are mindful, Allah is ever Aware of what you do."

Wa in imra'atun khafat min ba'liha nushuzan aw i'radan — if a woman fears from her husband — ba'l — master, husband — nushuzan — defiance, superiority, hardness, rising up, as in 4:34 where the husband fears nushuz from wife — or i'radan — turning away, desertion, leaving suspended, not divorcing nor fulfilling, not spending, not coming, preferring another. The verse gives the wife's fear as mirror to the husband's fear of nushuz in 4:34. There husbands fearing nushuz were given steps: advise, forsake bed within the house, strike lightly with siwak limits and never on face; here when the husband is the one showing nushuz — perhaps older, desiring a new marriage, withdrawing time, maintenance, or affection, as was case with Sawdah bint Zam'ah — the wife's path is sulh — settlement, not immediate court and not silent suffering alone.

Fa-la junaha alayhima an yusliha baynahuma sulhan — no blame upon them both if they reconcile between them with settlement — sulhan — the same sulh Islah as between quarrelling spouses with two arbiters in 4:35 and as najwa islah in 4:114. She may waive part of rights — a night, maintenance, housing form, a gift — to stay, as Sawdah bint Zam'ah, wife of the Prophet, did when she grew older and feared the Prophet might separate from her to ease him and to remain as Mother of the Believers: she gave her day and night to Aisha to remain, and he kept her with honour till death, visiting her still, and the Quran records her settlement as Sunnah, not as injustice, because it was with her willing, knowing, uncoerced consent — tib nafs as in 4:4, not forced.

Was-sulhu khayrun — reconciliation is better — khayrun — better than divorce's rupture, better than insisting on full rights with bitterness that poisons children, better than suspending as mu'allaqah in 4:129, better than cotton of rights that chokes. The Prophet said sulh is permitted among Muslims except a sulh that makes haram halal or halal haram. Wa uhdirat al-anfusu ash-shuhha — souls are made present with shuhh — stinginess, niggardliness, avarice that is stronger than bukhl; bukhl withholds, shuhh clings and withholds and fears loss and perceives giving as losing and watches. Husband wants to keep full favour while giving less time or support; wife wants full maintenance while giving no concession; both shuhh whispers "do not yield a night, a dirham, a word; you will be diminished, you will be stepped upon."

Wa in tuhsinu wa tattaqu fa-inna Allaha kana bima ta'maluna Khabira — if you do ihsan — as muhsin in 4:125 — and are mindful — taqwa — as 4:34 Aliyy Kabir and 4:131's wasiyyah of taqwa and 4:135's qawwamin — Allah is Aware — Khabir — of what you do, as in 4:35 Alim Khabir after arbitration. The sulh that confronts shuhh with ihsan and taqwa finds Khabir aware of every waived night and every kind word after, every extra maintenance given, every concealment of flaw, and that awareness is the believer's wage when worldly rights are adjusted for a greater khayr that keeps a family, a lineage, hearts, and a mizan intact. Without ihsan and taqwa, sulh is just pressure and shuhh wins.

=129=

"You will never be able to be just between women even if you strive. So do not incline completely and leave one suspended. If you reconcile and are mindful, Allah is Forgiving, Merciful."

Wa lan tastati'u an ta'dilu bayna an-nisa wa law harastum — you will never be able to be just — ta'dilu — between women even if you are eager — harastum — zealous, striving with effort, with schedule, with gifts, with words. The justice impossible here is love of the heart and its inclination — mayl qalbiya — not the justice of division that 4:3 commanded "if you fear you will not be just, then marry only one." 4:3 is justice of hand: nights, maintenance, housing, kind speech, division of time, which is wajib and measurable; 4:129 is justice of heart: love, attraction, natural inclination, which Allah does not burden beyond ability and does not ask.

The Prophet himself divided nights with scrupulous equality among his nine wives, counting, equalising, being just, then said, "O Allah, this is my division in what I control, do not blame me for what You control and I do not," meaning love and natural inclination. Allah exempts what hearts cannot control while holding firm what hands can control with mizan. The verse is not permission for injustice; it is relief from impossible perfection, coupled with warning against using impossibility as excuse for neglect.

Fa-la tamilu kulla al-mayli fa-tadharuha kal-mu'allaqah — do not incline wholly — kull al-mayl — leaving one mu'allaqah — suspended, hanging, neither having full rights as a wife nor being divorced so she may wed another; she dangles like a cloth on a line, moved by wind, neither worn nor stored, neither kept nor freed, neither married nor divorced. The same harm as 2:231 retaining wives to harm — "do not retain them to harm them, transgressing" — as not giving fatil, as i'rad. Suspension is zulm, and Allah is Aliyy Kabir in 4:34 over such height misused — High above the high husband who thinks height protects.

Wa in tuslihu wa tattaqu fa-inna Allaha kana Ghafuran Rahima — if you reconcile — islah, as in sulh khayr — and are mindful — taqwa — Allah is Forgiving, Merciful — Ghafur covers past inclines and mu'allaqah and missed nights, Rahim brings new mercy that makes future fairness possible and love perhaps grow. The verse after sulh khayr guards sulh from becoming a gentle name for mu'allaqah: sulh with taqwa, with time divided, with maintenance given, is Ghafur; sulh without taqwa, with one wife left hanging while the other enjoys all time and money, is just pressure to waive rights, not islah, not khayr.

=130=

"If they separate, Allah will enrich each from His abundance. Allah is ever Vast, Wise."

Wa in yatafarraqa yughni Allahu kullan min sa'atihi — if they separate — yatafarraqa — the couple whose sulh was attempted sincerely with ihsan and taqwa but failed, if divorce occurs — Allah will enrich each — yughni — will make free of need, independent, content — from His sa'ah — spaciousness, abundance, sa'ah. Sa'ah is from the root of wasi', spacious, vast, as in 4:100. The verse after "reconciliation is better" gently acknowledges divorce may still happen even after best sulh, and it is not doom or poverty sentence or shame that lasts, especially for the woman who fears i'rad and offers sulh. A woman who fears desertion and offers sulh is not threatened with poverty if sulh fails; a man who cannot be just and fears inclining is not threatened with ruin if he separates with ihsan and gives mut'ah.

Wa kan Allahu Wasi'an Hakima — Vast, Wise — Wasi' whose bounty encompasses as Muhit in 4:126 and whose provision is without account as in 4:37 and 4:40 and 4:130, Hakim who knows when separation is khayr and when staying with sulh is khayr, when enrichment is wealth and when it is tranquillity and a righteous new spouse and when it is closeness to Him. This removes the fear that prevents needed separation or needed patience: staying in mu'allaqah for fear of poverty or for fear of society's blame is shuhh; trusting Wasi' opens. The Prophet enriched companions after divorce: after separation, better spouses and provision came, and the community supported, and the verse's promise was seen. After fearing husband's i'rad and the stinginess of souls, the promise is that Allah's bounty does not narrow with divorce when done with taqwa; it widens, as earth was spacious in 4:100 and as His bounty is vast.

=131=

"To Allah belongs whatever is in heavens and on earth. We have enjoined those given the Book before you and you to be mindful of Allah. If you disbelieve, to Allah belongs whatever is in heavens and earth. Allah is Free of need, Praiseworthy."

Wa lillahi ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ardi — to Allah belongs whatever is in heavens and on earth — repeated for the third time in ten verses, a refrain after khullah and sa'ah, that grounds enrichment and divorce and sulh in ownership. Wa laqad wassayna alladhina utu al-Kitaba min qablikum wa iyyakum an ittaqu Allaha — We have enjoined those given the Book before you and you to be mindful — ittaqu — the same taqwa in 4:1 single soul created from one, 4:9 qawl sadid that reforms deeds, 4:128 ihsan and taqwa, 4:131 wasiyyah, 4:135 qawwamin. The wasiyyah is one across time: tawhid and taqwa, as Ibrahim enjoined his sons in 2:132-133 to submit and not die except as Muslims and as Luqman enjoined his son with tawhid and gratitude.

Wa in takfuru fa-inna lillahi ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ard — if you disbelieve, to Allah belongs all — He needs no taqwa; you need its benefit, as 14:8 Musa said if you disbelieve you and all on earth together, Allah is Rich, Praised, Free of need. Wa kan Allahu Ghaniyyan Hamida — Free of need — Ghani — Hamid — Praiseworthy — even without your thanks, as in 2:267 "Allah is Rich, Praised, does not need your charity" and 31:26. After divorce enrichment from sa'ah, this reminds that enrichment comes from Ghani who needs no bribe to give, and Hamid who is praised for giving even when you forget to thank, even when you disobey. The wasiyyah is not a tax for Him; it is a rope for you, and cutting it harms only you, not Him.

=132=

"To Allah belongs whatever is in heavens and on earth. Sufficient is Allah as Disposer."

Wa lillahi ma fis-samawati wa ma fil-ardi wa kafa billahi Wakila — to Allah belongs whatever is in heavens and earth — repeated again — sufficient as Wakil — disposer who manages enrichment after separation, knows ta'dil impossibility in 4:129, and encompasses souls prone to shuhh, and disposed the previous sulh and the next fear. After Ghaniy Hamid, Wakil says entrust your affairs to Him; He disposes better than you, better than your scheming, better than your withholding. The Prophet when worried would say, "HasbunAllahu wa ni'ma al-wakil" — Allah suffices, excellent disposer — the same word, and the Quran says in 3:173 when they said hasbunAllahu, Allah sufficed.

For today's divorcing couple, Wakil disposes children, housing, provision, and hearts better than rivalry, better than revenge, better than withholding a child's support to punish the ex. He disposes, so rely and do what Wasi' loves: ihsan, and He will dispose khayr.

=133=

"If He wills, He can take you away, O people, and bring others. Allah has power over that."

In yasha' yudhhibkum ayyuha an-nasu wa ya'ti bi-akharina — if He wills He can remove you — yudhhibkum — take you away, as He took Ad and Thamud and as He replaced Sabbath breakers who were made apes, and bring others — ya'ti bi-akharina — substitutes, better. The surah that warned "was not the earth spacious to emigrate?" in 4:97 and promised muraghaman refuges in 4:100 now warns the complacent after sulh and enrichment: if you make mu'allaqah your habit, withhold nasib, claim purity as in 4:49, devour riba as in 4:161, block with sudud, He can replace you as He replaced nations and as He can make faces turned. Wa kan Allahu ala dhalika Qadira — Capable — Qadir — over all things, as 3:26 "Owner of sovereignty gives and takes, You are over all things Capable," and as 4:30 easy for Him, and 4:133. After Wasi' who enriches, Ghani who needs nothing, Wakil who disposes, Qadir who can replace says do not take His patience as permission or His sa'ah as your trick or His Ghafur as your plan to incline.

=134=

"Whoever desires the reward of this world, with Allah are the rewards of this world and the Hereafter. Allah is Hearing, Seeing."

Man kana yuridu thawaba ad-dunya fa-inda Allahi thawabu ad-dunya wal-akhirah — whoever desires reward of the world — thawab dunya — like the lagger's fawz azim of spoils in 4:73, like the hypocrite's izzah with disbelievers in 4:139, like the crumb of desire in Satan's promise in 4:120 — with Allah are rewards of both worlds. Why ask the small portion from taghut or from mu'allaqah pressure or from riya or from hoarded share when the Owner of both is Allah? As 42:20 "whoever desires harvest of Hereafter We increase for him, who desires harvest of world We give some, but he has no share in Hereafter," and as 3:145, 11:15.

The verse after replacement threat offers the better bid: ask Allah for both hasanah, as Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanah wa fil-akhirati hasanah wa qina adhaban-nar in 2:201, and He is Wasi' who gives both, not one, and Ghani who loves to give. Wa kan Allahu Sami'an Basira — Hearing what you ask — thawab dunya only or both — Seeing what you do — islah or mu'allaqah, sadaqah or bukhl, qiyam or shuhh, qasr or abandonment. Sami' Basir after Wakala and Qadar says desire and deed are heard and seen before reward is given, and the gulf between wish and deed is known, and His hearing is near, His seeing is encompassing.

=135=

"O believers, stand firm for justice as witnesses for Allah even against yourselves or parents or kin. Whether rich or poor, Allah is more worthy of both. Do not follow desire so you deviate. If you twist or turn away, Allah is Aware of what you do."

Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu kunu qawwamin bil-qisti shuhada'a lillah — be qawwamin — maintainers, supporters, standers, pillars — with justice — bil-qist — the same qist as 4:58 "judge with justice" and 4:127 "stand for orphans with justice" and 5:8 "be qawwamin for Allah witnesses in justice." Qawwamin is more than "be just once"; it is to stand, to uphold, to be pillar as qiyam wealth in 4:5. Shuhada'a lillah — witnesses for Allah — not for tribe, party, family name, or profit, not for husband against wife, not for rich. Wa law ala anfusikum awi al-walidayni wal-aqrabin — even against yourselves, parents, kin — the closest wall that usually bends justice, as in 6:152 and as Ibrahim stood against his father Azar.

In yakun ghaniyyan aw faqiran Fallahu awla bihima — whether rich or poor, Allah is closer — awla — worthier — to both than your pity for poor or awe of rich; His right over them precedes yours, so do not favour rich fearing loss of patronage, nor poor out of misplaced pity that distorts testimony or share, as many think pity requires tilting qist for the poor. Fa-la tattabi'u al-hawa an ta'dilu — do not follow desire — hawa — so you deviate — as hawa made ra'ina twisting in 4:46 and taghut following in 4:60 and envy in 4:54 and maylan in 4:27. Hawa is the mother of sudud and of mudhabdhab.

Wa in talwu aw tu'ridu fa-inna Allaha kana bima ta'maluna Khabira — if you twist — talwu — distort testimony with tongue as yuharrifuna in 4:46 and layyan bi-alsinatihim — or turn away — tu'ridu — as i'rad in 4:128 and sudud in 4:61 — Allah is Aware — Khabir — of what you do, aware of a smirk that bends a word, of a silence that withholds a share, of a sulh that is mu'allaqah in disguise. Placed after sulh and mu'allaqah and sa'ah and Ghani and Wakil and Qadir, this is the court that checks sulh: settlement is better, but not by twisting justice for the wealthy husband nor by turning away from helpless wife's claim, nor by giving the rich what he does not deserve. Stand for qist even if it makes you suspended yourself with truth, even against your father, for Khabir sees and will enrich as Wasi', and Sami' hears.

=136=

"O believers, believe in Allah and His Messenger and the Book He sent down upon His Messenger and the Book He sent down before. Whoever disbelieves in Allah, His angels, His Books, His messengers, and the Last Day has strayed far."

Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu aminu billahi wa rasulihi wal-kitabi alladhi nazzala ala rasulihi wal-kitabi alladhi anzala min qabl — O believers, believe — aminu — command to increase, renew, confirm, deepen, as the Prophet taught renewal of faith by saying la ilaha illa Allah. Believe in Allah, Messenger — Muhammad, Book He sent down upon His Messenger — the Quran, nazzala — gradual, piecemeal over twenty-three years, and Book He sent down before — anzala — Tawrah and Injil in original light, Suhuf, Zabur. The believer is not to believe in some and disbelieve in some as 4:150 describes those who split — "we believe in some, disbelieve in some" — sabil between, as mudhabdhab.

Wa man yakfur billahi wa malaikatihi wa kutubihi wa rusulihi wal-yawmi al-akhir fa-qad dalla dalalan ba'idan — whoever disbelieves in these pillars — Allah, angels, Books, messengers, Last Day — has strayed far — dalalan ba'idan — the same far as shirk in 4:116 and 4:60's taghut far and 4:167's blockers far. The verse after qawwamin bil-qist shows iman is pillar of qist; without pillars, justice is hawa that twists. For today's Muslim who says "I follow Quran but not Sunnah," or "I believe in Allah but angels are metaphor," this says pillars are bundled; to deny one is to stray far, as to deny one witness in 4:41 is to deny the gathering.

=137=

"Those who believed then disbelieved, then believed then disbelieved, then increased in disbelief — Allah will not forgive them nor guide them to a path."

Inna alladhina amanu thumma kafaru thumma amanu thumma kafaru thumma izdadu kufran — those who oscillated: believed then disbelieved then believed then disbelieved then increased — izdadu — as in 3:90, the pattern of yatarabbasuna waiters and mudhabdhabin and the morning-evening tactic of 3:72. They use iman as costume. Lam yakun Allahu li-yaghfira lahum wa la li-yahdiyahum sabila — Allah will not forgive nor guide to a path — sabila — as in 4:68, 4:88, 4:143. Not because mercy closed arbitrarily, but because they made belief a game, increasing, sealing as 4:88's arkasa.

=138=

"Give tidings to the hypocrites that they will have a painful punishment —"

Bashshiri al-munafiqina bi-anna lahum adhaban alima — give tidings — bashshir — irony as in 3:21 — to hypocrites — alima — painful. Tidings they wanted — fawz azim, izzah — replaced with true tidings. Signs: lies, breaks promise, betrays trust.

=139=

"Those who take disbelievers as allies instead of believers. Do they seek honour with them? Indeed, all honour belongs to Allah entirely."

Alladhina yattakhidhuna al-kafirina awliya min duni al-mu'minina — fourth repetition. Ayabtaghuna indahum al-izzah — seek honour? Fa-inna al-izzata lillahi jami'an — all honour is Allah's wholly. As Aziz in 4:56, 4:158.

=140=

"He has already revealed to you in the Book that when you hear Allah's signs denied and mocked, do not sit with them until they engage in other talk, otherwise you are like them. Allah will gather hypocrites and disbelievers in Hell together."

Wa qad nazzala alaykum fil-Kitabi an idha sami'tum ayati Allahi yukfaru biha wa yustahza'u biha fa-la taq'udu ma'ahum hatta yakhudu fi hadithin ghayrihi — already revealed in 6:68. Do not sit where signs are mocked — mocking inheritance hudud, qawwam, four witnesses — until other talk. Innukum idhan mithluhum — then like them. Jami' — gather — in Hell together. Those who sought honour with them will be gathered with them.
