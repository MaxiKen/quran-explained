=1=

O humanity, be mindful of your Lord who created you from a single soul — from it He created its mate — and from both spread many men and women. And be mindful of Allah, by whom you ask one another, and of family ties — Allah is ever Watchful over you.

Ya ayyuha an-nas — O humanity — same universal call as earlier in al-Baqarah (2:21) worship your Lord. Here fear your Lord who created you from single soul — nafs wahidah — Adam — and from it created its mate — Hawwa from rib? From same essence, so equal origin.

Wa baththa minhuma rijalan kathiran wa nisa — and spread from both many men and women — population from one pair, bonding humanity as one family.

Wattaqu Allaha alladhi tasa'aluna bihi wal-arham — Be mindful Allah by whom you ask one another and family ties — In pre-Islam they would say As'aluka billah — I ask you by Allah — and ask by womb ties. Preserve both.

Inna Allaha kana alaykum Raqiba — Allah ever Watchful over you — Raqib — Observant, Guardian. Same as nothing hidden (3:5), knows what in chests.

This verse foundation of An-Nisa — Women — because it begins humanity from one soul and mate, establishing equality origin, spread, and sanctity wombs. Marriage, inheritance, orphan care all branch from family tie sanctity.

If single soul origin, then racism, tribalism collapse. And if Allah Watchful, orphan wealth (next verse) cannot be swallowed unseen.

It also answers those who think woman inferior creation. From same soul, created mate from it — together building.

So God-consciousness includes consciousness of womb ties.

Next orphan wealth.

=2=

Give orphans their wealth when they reach maturity, and do not exchange worthless of yours for their good, nor consume their wealth with your wealth — it is a great sin.

Wa atu al-yatama amwalahum — Give orphans their wealth — when rushd — maturity of mind, not just age.

Wa la tatabaddalu al-khabitha bit-tayyib — Do not exchange bad for good — al-khabith — worthless, bad — with tayyib — good, valuable. Guardians would take orphan's healthy sheep giving sick one, or fine date palm giving dead one, thinking orphan won't notice.

Wa la ta'kulu amwalahum ila amwalikum — Do not eat their wealth along with your wealth — mixing to confuse.

Innahu kana huban kabira — It is great sin — hub — sin.

Orphan care mentioned after womb ties because orphan most vulnerable family tie severed by death.

Maturing concept: physical puberty not enough; rushd — ability manage wealth responsibly. Until then protect, invest, test.

Do not exchange — khabith tayyib contrast earlier (2:267) do not aim for bad to spend while you would not take except closing eyes — same principle: do not give orphan bad taking good.

Great sin because orphan weak, no father to defend, but Allah Raqib Watchful earlier sees.

Modern application: managing orphan funds, charity funds, inheritance for minors — mixing personal business with orphan's account — prohibited.

Next if fear fail give orphan women due rights then marry other women.

=3=

If you fear you will not do justice to orphan women — then marry other women you like — two, three, four — but if fear you cannot be just, then one — or what your right hand possesses — that is more likely you will not be unfair.

Fe-orhan women — yatama — girls under guardianship whose wealth you manage — temptation to marry them without proper mahr to keep wealth, or to oppress. If fear injustice to them, then marry other women you like — ghayr — not from among them.

Mathna wa thulatha wa ruba — two three four — permission polygyny with limit four — pre-Islam unlimited. Permission not command.

Fa-in khiftum alla ta'dilu fa-wahidah — If fear not just — justice — then one. Justice here includes time, support, kindness, division nights housing? Not love feeling but equity.

Aw ma malakat aymanukum — or right hand possesses — slave women context where no polygyny justice required same? Historical.

Dhalika adna alla ta'ulu — This more likely you not be unfair — ta'ulu — deviate, be burdened with many dependents unjustly.

This verse often misread divorced from orphan context. It came to protect orphans, not to promote desire. Polygyny conditional justice, justice itself condition fears — then one.

Justice among wives great responsibility — Prophet said whoever has two wives and inclines to one will come Day Resurrection with one side drooping.

If unable justice then one — Quran sets monogamy as closest to fairness.

So verse: protect orphan women wealth rights — if fear injustice marrying them, marry others up to four; fear injustice among them — one.

Next give women dowries graciously.

=4=

Give women their dowries graciously — but if they willingly waive some, enjoy it freely with wholesome pleasure.

Wa atu an-nisa saduqatihinna nihlah — Give women saduqat — dowries — nihlah — graciously, gift, cheerfully — not as price purchase but as gift honour.

Orphan girls just mentioned often married without proper mahr; this general command gives women dowry as right, not father's property.

Fa-in tibna lakum an shay'in minhu nafsan fa-kuluhu hani'an mari'an — But if they willingly waive some of it — about part of mahr — with good will of self — then eat it — i.e., use it — hani'an mari'an — freely wholesome pleasant — pleasure without sin.

Nafs — willingness — important: coercion invalidates. Waiving must be happy heart, not pressured by family or guilt.

This transforms dowry from transaction to gift and then potentially shared gift if she chooses.

Shows woman's financial independence: mahr hers, can give back willingly if wants, husband may enjoy but only with her willing waiver.

Connects to earlier give orphans wealth (2) — both about returning property to rightful owners: orphans, women.

Modern: cultural practices where father or family takes mahr — prohibited; it's hers.

Next do not entrust incapable among dependants with wealth that Allah made support.

=5=

Do not give those weak-minded your wealth which Allah made as support for you — but feed and clothe them from it and speak to them kind words.

Wa la tu'tu as-sufaha amwalakum — Do not entrust incapable — sufaha — weak-minded, immature, prodigal, cannot manage wealth responsibly — your wealth which Allah made qiyam — support standing for you — qiyaman — means of support.

But feed clothe them from it — feed clothe — maintain — and speak kind words — qawlan ma'rufan — gentle.

Who are sufaha? Orphans not reached rushd, minors, those with mental incapacity, or spendthrift who would waste.

Wealth Allah made means support — qiyam — survival, not to be thrown away. Owner — your wealth? Actually orphan's wealth but called your wealth because community trust? Or guardian's own wealth should not be given to incapable who will waste, causing family collapse.

Balance: protect wealth but maintain compassion — feed clothe speak kind, not harshly refuse.

This verse precedes testing orphans (6) — do not give wealth yet, but care.

It also shows wealth responsibility: wealth is standing of community — if wasted society collapses.

Kind words — as earlier (2:263) kind words better than charity followed by injury — even refusing to hand large sum should be with kind words.

Next test orphans.

=6=

Test orphans until they reach marriageable age — if you perceive sound judgment in them, give them their wealth — do not consume it wastefully hastily before they grow — whoever rich should abstain, whoever poor may eat fairly — when you hand wealth, take witnesses — Allah suffices as Reckoner.

Wabtulu al-yatama hatta idha balaghu an-nikah — Test orphans until reach marriage age — test with small business, responsibility.

Fa-in anastum minhum rushdan — If perceive rushd — sound judgment — fa-dfa'u ilayhim amwalahum — give their wealth.

Wa la ta'kuluha israfan wa bidaran an yakbaru — Do not eat it wastefully hastily before they grow — israf — waste, bidar — hastening fear they will grow and take it, so spending quickly to prevent.

Wa man kana ghaniyyan fal-yastafif wa man kana faqiran fal-ya'kul bil-ma'ruf — Whoever rich among guardians should abstain — not take wage for managing; poor may eat fairly — reasonable fee for work.

Fa-idha dafa'tum ilayhim amwalahum fa-ashhidu alayhim — When handing wealth take witnesses — protect both.

Wa kafa billahi Hasiba — Allah suffices as Reckoner — Hasib — computes, knows how managed, will ask.

Sequence orphans: give when maturity (2), do not exchange bad good (2), fear justice orphan women (3), now test until rushd, do not hastily eat, rich abstain poor eat fairly, witnesses, Allah Hasib.

Complete system protecting vulnerable from guardian greed and from own immaturity.

For modern fund managers, trustees, will executors — perfect guidance.

Next shares of inheritance.

=7=

Men have share of what parents and close relatives leave — women have share of what parents and close relatives leave — whether little or much — ordained share.

Lir-rijali nasibun mimma taraka al-walidan wal-aqrabun wa lin-nisa nasibun... — Men have share what parents close relatives leave, women have share — both.

Pre-Islam women had no inheritance; some cultures said women lack intellect battle. Quran declares nasib — share — for women as for men, little or much.

Nasiban mafrudan — ordained obligatory share — mafrud — fixed, decreed. Not charity optional.

This verse foundation inheritance law detailed next.

It follows orphan wealth protection because inheritance often source orphan wealth. Ensuring women share prevents ancient injustice where male relatives took all.

Lineage closeness matters: parents, aqrabun.

Whether little much — qalilan aw kathiran — no minimum threshold; even small estate divided as per share.

Mafrudan — implies fixed by Allah not by whim, so no one can deprive.

For today where cultural practice deprives daughters — contradicts ordained share. Quran says share for women.

Next if relatives, orphans, poor present at division.

=8=

If at division relatives, orphans, poor are present, provide for them from it and speak to them kind words.

Wa idha hadara al-qismata ulu al-qurba wal-yatama wal-masakin farzuquhum minhu wa qulu lahum qawlan ma'rufan — If at division relatives non-heirs, orphans, poor present at inheritance distribution — give them something — farzuquhum minhu — provide from it.

Wa qulu qawlan ma'rufan — Speak kind words.

Division can be painful for those present who hoped share but not heir — distant relatives, orphan neighbours, poor who came.

Islam teaches not to ignore them heart broken watching. Give something from estate before fixed shares? Scholars: from third after bequests? Or from own generosity? Either, provide.

And kind words — explain, comfort, not harshly turn away — as earlier kind words better than charity followed by injury (2:263) and kind words before entrusting incapable (5).

This verse softens fixed inheritance with voluntary benevolence.

Encourages compassion: formal shares must be exact, but kindness beyond formal shares also needed.

Next let those fear leaving weak offspring behind.

=9=

Let those fear who, if they left behind weak offspring, would be concerned for them — so let them be mindful of Allah and speak right words.

Walyakhsha alladhina law taraku min khalfihim dhurriyyatan di'afan khafu alayhim — Let fear those who if leaving weak offspring would worry — dhurriyyah di'af — weak children — khafu — concerned.

Falyattaqu Allaha wal-yaqulu qawlan sadida — Be mindful Allah and speak right words — sadida — straight, correct, right.

Context: guardians managing orphan wealth or persons making bequests: imagine your own weak children left behind orphaned, how would you want them treated? Fear that scenario. Then treat orphans in your care as you would want your weak children treated.

Qawlan sadida — right words — honest, straight — as earlier kind words but here sadida — correct, not manipulative.

If you would not want your children deprived, do not deprive others.

Empathy principle: put yourself place orphan's father.

This verse moves from law to conscience — Raqib Watchful knows if you treat weak children as you treat your own.

Next those who consume orphans wealth unjustly.

=10=

Those who consume orphans' wealth unjustly — they only consume fire into bellies — they will burn in Blaze.

Inna alladhina ya'kuluna amwala al-yatama zulman innama ya'kuluna fi butunihim naran wa sa-yaslawna sa'ira — Those eat orphans wealth unjustly — zulman — wrongfully — innama ya'kuluna fi butunihim nar — only consume fire into bellies — what they think eat wealth is actually fire.

Wa sa-yaslawna sa'ira — Will burn Blaze — sa'ir — flaming fire.

Earlier (2:174) those conceal Book and exchange small price consume nothing but Fire into bellies — same consumption imagery linking wealth unjust consumption to eating Fire. Orphan wealth and hiding knowledge both considered fire in belly.

Preceding verses gave protection system: give when maturity, not exchange bad good, not hastily eat, rich abstain poor eat fairly, witnesses, Allah Hasib. Now consequence eating unjustly.

This warns guardian: transaction may look profitable in ledger but belly filled fire and final destination Blaze.

For modern charity worker who takes from orphan sponsorship fund — same threat.

After this severe warning, detailed inheritance shares come — to prevent disputes leading to such consumption.

Next Allah instructs you concerning children.

=11=

Allah instructs you concerning your children — for male like share of two females — if there are only daughters, two or more, two-thirds; if one, half; for parents each sixth if child left; if no child and parents only heirs, mother third; if siblings, mother sixth after bequest or debt — parents and children you don't know who more beneficial — ordained from Allah — Allah Knowing, Wise.

Yusikum Allahu fi awladikum li-dhakari mithlu hazzi al-unthayayin... — Central inheritance verse. For male like share two females — not because worth difference but responsibility: male qawwam — maintainer provides mahr, maintenance wife children, defence, blood money, etc. Female share often kept as pure savings.

If daughters only — two or more two-thirds; one daughter half.

Parents: if deceased left child, each parent sixth; if no child parents only heirs, mother third father remainder; if siblings exist mother sixth (due crowd).

Min ba'di wasiyyatin yusi biha aw dayn — After bequest he ordains or debt — bequest debt settled before division — shows rights of dead honoured before heirs.

Aba'ukum wa abna'ukum la tadruna ayyuhum aqrabu lakum naf'an — Fathers children you don't know who nearer benefit — you might think son more beneficial but daughter more caring, etc. — leave division to Knower.

Faridatan min Allah — ordained from Allah — as earlier share ordained (7).

InnAllaha kana Aliman Hakima — Knowing, Wise — same closing as earlier shaping wombs as wills (3:6) and providing without account — He knows benefit, places shares with wisdom.

This verse transforms Jahiliyyah deprivation into precise math mercy.

For today, calculate with care, use trustworthy scholar, not emotions.

Next shares spouses and calibration.

=12=

And for you half of what wives leave if no child — if child then quarter — for them quarter of what you leave if no child — if child then eighth — after bequest or debt — if man or woman leaves no parent nor child but has brother sister each sixth; if more than that they share third — after bequest or debt not harming — bequest from Allah — Allah Knowing, Forbearing.

Spouses shares: husband gets half wife's estate if no child, quarter if child; wife gets quarter husband's if no child, eighth if child — if multiple wives share quarter/eighth equally.

After bequest or debt — again.

Kalalah — one leaves no parent child but sibling — each sibling sixth, more than one share third equally male female — unique case siblings share equally.

Ghayra mudar — not harming — bequest should not intend harm heirs by exceeding third or favouritism.

Wasiyyatan min Allah — Bequest from Allah — as wasiyyah Allah gave you? Or instruction from Allah — Allah Knowing Forbearing — Halim — tolerant, not hastening punishment for mistakes inheritance but knows.

This verse completes obligatory shares: children, parents, spouses, siblings kalalah.

Shares intricate but purpose: preserve womb ties, protect weak, prevent hoarding, distribute.

Earlier (2:180) bequest parents close relatives — now detailed shares abrogate? Scholars say detailed shares replace earlier general bequest but bequest for non-heirs up to third remains.

Fear leaving weak offspring (9) addressed: fixed shares prevent leaving weak destitute?

Next those limits Allah.

=13=

These are limits of Allah — whoever obeys Allah and Messenger will be admitted to Gardens beneath rivers flow abiding forever — that is great success.

Tilka hududu Allah — These limits Allah — hudud — boundaries — inheritance shares are hudud not suggestion.

Wa man yuti'illaha wa rasulahu yudkhilhu jannatin... — Obeys Allah and Messenger admitted Gardens rivers forever — great success — fawz azim.

Obedience in dividing precisely as commanded, not manipulating with tricks, leads Gardens.

After detailed math, promise Gardens — shows importance.

Limits protect: if crossed, great sin.

Next whoever disobeys limits transgresses will enter Fire.

=14=

Whoever disobeys Allah and His Messenger and transgresses His limits — He will put him in Fire abiding therein — humiliating punishment.

Wa man ya'si Allaha wa rasulahu wa yata'adda hududahu yudkhilhu naran khalidan fiha wa lahu adhabun muhin — Disobeys Allah Messenger transgresses limits — enter Fire abiding — punishment humiliating — muhin — humiliating, as earlier disgrace struck (3:112).

If obedience Gardens great success, disobedience Fire humiliating.

Hudud Allah repeated many times around marriage divorce inheritance — boundaries protect family and society.

Transgression includes depriving female heirs, taking orphan wealth, making harmful bequest.

Humble adherence brings Gardens, arrogance crossing limits brings Fire humiliation.

After inheritance, next verse about immorality women.

=15=

Those who commit immorality among your women — bring four witnesses among you — if they testify confine them in houses until death takes them or Allah makes way for them.

Wallati ya'tina al-fahishata min nisa'ikum fastashhidu alayhinna arba'atan minkum — Women commit fahishah — adultery — bring four witnesses from you — four to prove, strict proof to avoid slander.

Fa-in shahidu fa-amsikuhunna fil-buyut hatta yatawaffahunna al-mawt aw yaj'ala Allahu lahunna sabila — If testify confine houses until death or Allah makes way — sabila — way out.

Early ruling confinement until later abrogated by flogging 100 lashes for unmarried and stoning married? Scholars differ; Quranic later verse (24:2) provides lashes, Sunnah adds.

"Allah makes way" — indicates early temporary ruling awaiting further legislation.

Four witnesses requirement protects chastity from accusation.

This verse after inheritance shows family protection not only wealth but chastity.

Next two men immorality.

=16=

And the two among you who commit it — punish them — if they repent and correct, leave them alone — Allah is Ever Accepting repentance, Merciful.

Walladhani ya'tiyaniha minkum fa-adhuhuma — Two among you commit — lesbians? Actually two men? Scholars said two men adultery? Or man woman? Adhuhuma — punish them — harm — ta'zir.

Fa-in taba wa aslahā fa-a'ridu anhuma — If repent and correct leave them.

Inna Allaha kana Tawwaba Rahima — Ever Accepting repentance Merciful.

Early ruling mild punishment then if repent leave.

Later detailed hadd punishments came.

Purpose: purify society while opening repentance door.

Verses 15-16 show gradual legislation: confinement, harm, then later fixed hadd.

Opening repentance door consistent pattern: except those repent correct (2:160, 3:89).

Next repentance time.

=17=

Repentance with Allah is only for those who do evil in ignorance then repent soon — they Allah will turn to — Allah Knowing, Wise.

Innama at-tawbatu alalLahi lilladhina ya'maluna as-su'a bi-jahalah thumma yatubuna min qaribin — Repentance with Allah is for those do evil in ignorance then repent soon — qarib — soon after, before death.

Fa-ula'ika yatubu Allahu alayhim wa kan Allahu Aliman Hakima — Will turn — Allah Knowing Wise.

Jahalah — ignorance — not lack knowledge that it's wrong, but ignorance of consequences? Or committing in state of overwhelming desire ignorance that soul forgets Allah — all sin done in jahalah sense.

Min qarib — soon — before death, before final rattle.

Allah Knowing Wise knows who sincere soon vs delay.

Next not for those continue evil deeds until death comes.

=18=

Not for those who continue doing evil until death approaches one of them and says now I repent — nor those who die disbelievers — painful punishment.

Wa laysat at-tawbatu lilladhina ya'maluna as-sayyi'at hatta idha hadara ahadahum al-mawtu qala inni tubtu al-ana wa la alladhina yamutuna wa hum kuffar — Not for those continue evil until death approaches one says now repent — nor those die disbelievers.

Ula'ika a'tadna lahum adhaban alima — Painful punishment.

Repentance at deathbed after life of persistence not accepted, as Pharaoh when drowning said believed.

Soon repentance accepted, death rattle repentance not.

Those die disbelievers not accepted even earth full gold earlier.

Thus time window for repentance limited.

Next prohibition inheriting women by force.

=19=

O you who believe, it is not lawful for you to inherit women by force — nor prevent them to take back part of what you gave them unless they commit clear immorality — live with them in kindness — if you dislike them perhaps you dislike something Allah placed much good in.

Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu la yahillu lakum an tarithu an-nisa karhan — Not lawful inherit women by force — pre-Islam when man died, his heir would inherit widow as property, marry without mahr or prevent marriage to take ransom.

Wa la ta'duluhunna li-tadhhabu bi-ba'di ma ataytumuhunna illa an ya'tina bi-fahishatin mubayyinah — Not prevent them to take back part dowry unless clear immorality.

Wa ashiruhunna bil-ma'ruf — Live with them in kindness — ma'ruf.

Fa-in karihtumuhunna fa-asa an takrahu shay'an wa yaj'ala Allahu fihi khayran kathira — If dislike them perhaps dislike something Allah placed much good — khayr kathir — much good — child, compassion later, etc.

This verse after repentance shows marriage respect: women not property inherited.

Live with kindness — includes spending beloved, restraining anger pardoning (3:134).

If dislike, patience may reveal much good — dislike may be temporary due to behaviour; patience reveals goodness.

Prophet said believing man should not hate believing woman if dislike one trait pleased with another.

Next tighten marriage prohibitions.

After inheritance and orphans, marriage kindness foundation.

=20=

If you wish to replace wife with another and you gave one of them qintar — heap — do not take anything from it — will you take it by slander and clear sin? And how will you take it after you were intimate and they took from you firm covenant?

Wa in aradtum istibdala zawjin makana zawjin wa ataytum ihdahunna qintaran fa-la ta'khudhu minhu shay'an — If replace wife with another and gave one qintar heap — large mahr — do not take anything.

A-ta'khudhunahu buhtanan wa ithman mubina — Take by slander clear sin? Accusing her immorality to justify taking mahr.

Wa kayfa ta'khudhuna wa qad afda ba'dukum ila ba'd wa akhadhna minkum mithaqan ghaliza — How take after intimate — afda — some to other — and they took from you firm covenant — mithaq ghaliz — solemn covenant.

Mithaq ghaliz used only for marriage and for prophets covenant — shows marriage serious.

Qintar — even huge mahr — once given cannot be reclaimed upon divorce to replace.

Earlier (2:229) not lawful take back anything gave except khul' case. Here emphasises no reclaim even heap.

Buhtan — slander — and ithm mubin — clear sin — double crime.

Firm covenant — marriage covenant — includes kindness living, protecting.

For today's divorces where husband demands return mahr or gifts — prohibited unless she initiates khul' and returns willingly.
