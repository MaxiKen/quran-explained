=81=

"They say, 'Obedience,' but when they leave your presence a group among them spends the night planning other than what you say. Allah records what they plot at night. So turn away from them and trust in Allah. Sufficient is Allah as Disposer."

Wa yaquluna ta'atun — they say obedience — a word easy in daylight, as 4:62's "we intended only good" and 4:46's "we hear" with twisting, and as modern "insha'Allah we will implement." Fa-idha barazu min indika bayyata ta'ifatun minhum ghayra alladhi taqul — when they go forth from you, a group spends the night — yubayyituna — planning other than what you said, what you commanded for inheritance or for march. Bayyata is to plot at night, literally to spend the night in counsel, where Raqib in 4:1 still watches, Hasib in 4:6 still records, Sami' in 4:148 still hears, and Muhit in 4:126 still encompasses. Darkness does not dim the pen.

Wallahu yaktubu ma yubayyitun — Allah writes — yaktubu — what they plot at night, as 4:108 "He is with them when they plot by night what He is not pleased with" and as pens write atom weight in 4:40 and fatil in 4:49. The record is not rumour; it is kitabah. Daylight obedience is not fooled by night ink; the ink of night is read in daylight by the One who writes.

Fa-a'rid anhum wa tawakkal ala Allahi — turn away from them — a'rid — and trust in Allah. The command after 4:63's turn away + admonish + baligh is repeated here as political distance plus tawakkul, not as surveillance state. The Prophet lived this: Abdullah ibn Ubayy withdrew three hundred before Uhud, and revelation ordered to turn away and rely on Allah alone; at the Trench the hypocrites said "our houses are exposed" in 33:13, and he turned away and trusted. Wa kafa billahi Wakila — sufficient as Wakil — disposer who manages affairs, as Wali in 4:45 and Nasir and Shahid and Hasib. Night planners plot in darkness; the Wakil manages in all hours, and what they plot is already written before it strikes. The believer's answer to double-faced obedience is not panic surveillance, not bugging, but referral and trust: what they plot at night is already written, and the Wakil suffices.

For today the same bayyata is the group chat after the meeting where "we obey the share" becomes "how to bypass the share with a loan paper," or "we will go forth" becomes "let us discourage others." The pen still writes. Turn away from their private counsel as in 4:140's sitting — do not sit where signs are mocked — and trust the Wakil who suffices for the orphan, the wife, and the traveller.

=82=

"Do they not reflect deeply on the Quran? Had it been from other than Allah they would have found much discrepancy."

Afala yatadabbaruna al-Qur'an — do they not do tadabbur — deliberate, deep, patient reflection that follows the text to its end, collects its parts, allows the seventh reading to clarify the first, as a gardener follows water to its root and as a diver follows a pearl? The question rebukes the night planners who said "obedience" by day and spent night inventing contradiction or seeking loopholes. The Quran's legislation was revealed over twenty-three years — gradual wine from hint in 2:219 to curb in 4:43 to prohibition in 5:90, inheritance precise after compassion in 4:7-12 and 4:176, prayer intact in tayammum in 4:43 and qasr in 4:101 and fear in 4:102, jihad for the weak after mercy in 4:75, marriage with chastity after prohibitions in 4:22-25, riba after trusts — with one Author it remains coherent; any human code edited that long, by many hands, collapses under its own amendments.

Wa law kana min indi ghayri Allahi la-wajadu fihi ikhtilafan kathira — had it been from other than Allah they would have found much discrepancy — ikhtilaf kathir — many incoherences, contradictions. Yet they found none that survives tadabbur; the coherence is not forced harmony but organic growth under one Root. Rasikhuna in 3:7 return mutashabih to muhkam and say "we believe, all from our Lord" — that is tadabbur. The night planners seek cracks to justify bayyata; tadabbur finds the rope that binds prayer to jihad to inheritance under tawhid to wombs.

The Prophet said the Quran does not contradict itself; the believer reads until the verse that seemed to clash explains the other, as 4:48 and 4:116 frame shirk, as 4:40 and 4:49 frame justice. For a modern reader bombarded with "contradiction" clips, where a verse about "fight the allies of Satan" is cut from "Satan's plot is weak" or "kill the polytheists" from its treaty context, tadabbur is vaccine: read the whole, in order, with Sunnah, with asbab, with scholars, and discrepancy dissolves. The verse after yubayyituna says if they had reflected instead of plotted at night, they would have found no crack to exploit and would have found guidance to join.

=83=

"When news of security or fear reaches them, they spread it. Had they referred it to the Messenger and to those in authority among them, those who derive it among them would have understood it. Were it not for Allah's bounty and mercy, you would have followed Satan except for a few."

Wa idha ja'ahum amrun min al-amni aw al-khawfi adha'u bihi — when an affair — amr — of security — amn — or fear — khawf — comes — victory, defeat, rumour of attack, treaty, price, plague — they spread it — adha'u bihi — broadcast hastily, forwarding before verifying, liking before understanding. The same impulse as night planners: speak before knowing; the same as lagger who believes rumour; the same as sudud who refuses referral. The verse after tadabbur shows reflection without referral breeds panic, and referral without reflection breeds blind following; both fail.

Wa law radduhu ila ar-rasuli wa ila uli al-amri minhum la-alimahu alladhina yastanbitunahu minhum — had they referred it — radduhu — to the Messenger and to those in authority among them — uli al-amr, scholars and wise leaders who are obeyed within obedience to Allah and Messenger as in 4:59 — those who yastanbituna — extract, derive — as water is drawn from a deep well with a bucket — would have known it, derived its ruling, its timing, its need to hide or to act. Yastanbituna is fiqh: drawing the hidden water of ruling from the source with roots, not scooping the surface foam.

Wa lawla fadlu Allahi alaykum wa rahmatuhu la-ittaba'tum ash-Shaytana illa qalila — were it not for bounty and mercy you would have followed Satan except a few. Fadl here is Quran and Sunnah and the uli al-amr who extract correctly; rahmah is the community that holds them and the discipline to refer. Without referral, even good intention follows Satan's wish to deviate greatly in 4:27 and his ghurur in 4:120. For today's feeds — instant news, market panic, battlefield footage, fatwa before verification — the ethic is sharp: do not forward security/fear news until those who yastanbitun have extracted its meaning. Forwarding before extraction is adha' — spread — the same sin as night plotting, only in daylight, and it creates the fear that 4:71's hizr was meant to manage. The Prophet said, "It suffices as a lie for a person to narrate everything he hears," and Umar would drag a man who spread without referral.

=84=

"So fight in Allah's path. You are responsible only for yourself, and encourage the believers. Perhaps Allah will restrain the force of disbelievers. Allah is stronger in force and more severe in deterrence."

Fa-qatil fi sabili Allahi la tukallafu illa nafsaka — fight in His path; you are tasked only for yourself — your nafs, not conditional on others joining, not excused because others lag as in 4:72, not postponed because news was mishandled as in 4:83. The Prophet at Badr stood with three hundred against a thousand, at Uhud wounded yet stood calling, at Hunayn stood when many turned, at Tabuk called while some lagged and some spread fear; the command stands on the individual as 4:29's prohibition and 4:40's atom reckoning stand on the individual, and as 4:135's standing for justice stands even against yourself.

Wa harrid al-mu'minina — encourage, urge, kindle believers — harrid is to make eager, to inflame hearts with hope, not to coerce with shame; the Prophet's harrid was stories of the weak's du'a in 4:75, of ajr azim in 4:74 regardless of killed or victor, of the weak plot in 4:76, of degrees in Paradise in 4:96.

Asa Allahu an yakuffa ba'sa alladhina kafaru — perhaps — asa — Allah will restrain — yakuffa — the force — ba's — of disbelievers — asa from Allah is not maybe but near certainty and promise, as His will to restrain. Wallahu ashaddu ba'san wa ashaddu tankila — Allah is stronger in force and more severe in deterrence and exemplary punishment — tankil — that deters repetition and makes an example. Stronger than any coalition that wished you to incline in 4:27, stronger than Satan's weak plot in 4:76, stronger than the fear in 4:77. The verse after referral culture says individual responsibility remains, communal encouragement remains, and result — kaff of enemy's ba's — is His, not your numbers, not your trending. After 4:71's precautions and 4:76's weak plot, the believer fights not because victory is guaranteed on paper but because his nafs is tasked and Allah's ba's is stronger and His tankil more severe than any man's.

=85=

"Whoever intercedes with a good intercession will have a share of it, and whoever intercedes with an evil intercession will have a share of it. Allah is over all things Watchful."

Man yashfa' shafa'atan hasanatan yakun lahu nasibun minha — whoever intercedes with good intercession — shafa'ah — mediation, advocacy, recommendation, encouragement, even a forwarded post that encourages verification, even a word to a commander to be gentle — will have a share — nasib — of its reward. Nasib here is blessed share, as in inheritance nasib in 4:7 and earning nasib in 4:32 "men and women have a share of what they earned." Wa man yashfa' shafa'atan sayyi'atan yakun lahu kiflun minha — evil intercession — his share is kifl — burden share that drags and weighs, as 16:25 "they will bear their burdens and burdens with their burdens." Nasib versus kifl: both portions, but kifl adds weight that pulls down and must be carried, as 4:112's ihtamala.

Intercession is not neutral chat; it is shareholding in outcome, partnership. Encouraging believers to fight for the weak in 4:75, verifying news before spreading in 4:83, mediating between spouses with qist in 4:35 and 4:128, enjoining charity in najwa in 4:114 are hasanah with nasib. Discouraging, lagging as in 4:72, spreading fear news as in 4:83, night plotting as in 4:81, taking taghut as court in 4:60 are sayyi'ah with kifl. Even a like is shafa'ah.

Wa kan Allahu ala kulli shay'in Muqita — Watchful, Keeper, Preserver, Witness — Muqit from quta — to give sustenance and to watch — He records intercession as He records dharrah in 4:40, fatil in 4:49, naqir in 4:53, qitmir. In a surah of shares — inheritance nasib mafruda, earning nasib, Satan's nasib mafrud — this verse says speech also has nasib. A forward without referral, a recommendation for a job with bribe, a mediating word that favours the rich over the poor in 4:135, even a sitting with mockers in 4:140 — all are shafa'ah with nasib or kifl that the Muqit writes and the Hasib reckons.

=86=

"When you are greeted with a greeting, greet with better than it or return it. Allah is over all things Reckoner."

Wa idha huyyitum bi-tahiyyatin fa-hayyu bi-ahsana minha aw rudduha — when you are greeted with tahiyyah — peace, security, long life, honour — greet with better — ahsan — or return it — equal. Tahiyyah in Islam's best form is as-salamu alaykum wa rahmatullahi wa barakatuh, but also any greeting of honour that carries peace. Better is to add "wa rahmatullah" or "wa barakatuh"; equality is "wa alaykum as-salam." The Prophet said, "Do not initiate salam to Jews and Christians seeking honour in that form, but if they greet you say wa alaykum," teaching adab of context without abandoning justice, yet the general principle remains: respond with increase, not decrease, with warmth, not coldness.

In a surah of trusts — return trusts to owners in 4:58 — and justice — stand for qist even against yourselves in 4:135 — and intercession — salam is a trust and a shafa'ah: a peace offered must be answered with more peace, a trust returned better. Inna Allaha kana ala kulli shay'in Hasiba — Reckoner — Hasiba as in 4:6 suffices as Reckoner over orphans' wealth, and in 4:86 over greetings; He reckons salam as He reckons atoms, fatil, naqir, qitmir. Even the smallest social act carries share and reckoning, and hasanah. The Prophet said, "Spread salam among you; you will not enter Paradise until you believe, and you will not believe until you love one another; shall I tell you what makes love? Spread salam." Returning better is da'wah, economics, and defence at once: it disarms the suspicious, it honours the host, it builds the community that can then take hizr and fight and judge.

=87=

"Allah — there is no god except Him. He will surely gather you to the Day of Resurrection about which there is no doubt. Whose statement is truer than Allah's?"

Allahu la ilaha illa Huwa — the shahada that is the pillar of the surah, as Ali Imran 3:18 "Allah bears witness there is no god except Him, and the angels and those of knowledge" anchored witnessing, and as 4:87 now anchors gathering. La yajma'annakum ila yawmi al-qiyamati la rayba fihi — He will surely gather — yajma' — you to the Day of Resurrection about which there is no doubt — la rayba fihi — the same certainty as 2:2 "this Book, no doubt in it" and 4:122's haqq promise and 3:9's promise. The Gatherer is the One who created from one soul in 4:1 and spread many; He can gather one soul's many back to One.

Wa man asdaqu min Allahi hadithan — whose statement — hadith — speech — is truer than Allah's? Hadith here is not the Prophet's hadith but Allah's speech. The question is rhetorical: none. This after greeting and intercession and disputation sets the scale: intercession shares, salam replies, orphan swaps, mahr heaps, night plots, fear news — all gathered where no hadith is hidden as 4:42 said no statement hidden. If He is truthful about tashri' of inheritance with sixths and quarters, He is truthful about jami' for reckoning, and the Truest Statement is the Book that taught tadabbur in 4:82 and referral in 4:83. For the believer, duas and deeds rest on the truest hadith; for the denier, blame shifting in 4:78 — "this is from you, O Muhammad" — collapses before the Truest who is also the Gatherer.

This verse recurs as 4:122 "whose qawl — saying — truer than Allah's?" to frame the whole surah with truthfulness: from hadith to qawl, from gathering to promise.

=88=

"What is wrong with you that you are two groups concerning the hypocrites while Allah has cast them back for what they earned? Do you wish to guide those whom Allah has let stray? Whoever Allah lets stray you will find no way for him."

Fa-ma lakum fil-munafiqina fi'atayni — what is wrong with you that you are two groups — fi'atayni — about hypocrites? After Uhud, companions divided: one group said kill the deserters who withdrew three hundred and left the flank exposed, another said excuse them — they are still of us, they have families. They had marched out with a thousand, then three hundred turned back with Abdullah ibn Ubayy. Wallahu arkasahum bima kasabu — Allah cast them back — arkasa — turned them over, made them regress, flipped them — for what they earned. Arkasa is like nakasa, to flip a vessel; earning flipped them, as 2:7 sealed hearts after refusal and 4:88's own earning and 61:5 when they deviated Allah made hearts deviate and 4:155 hearts sealed for disbelief.

Aturiduna an tahdu man adalla Allahu — do you want to guide whom Allah let stray after his earning and after clear proof, after 4:88's own division? Wa man yudlili Allahu fa-lan tajida lahu sabila — whoever Allah lets stray, you will find no way — sabila — the same no sabil as in 4:88 for cast-back and 4:115 for burning and 4:143 for wavering and 4:137 for increased disbelief. Guidance is not forced after earned deviation persisted and chosen; it is left to its way, as 6:110 "We will turn their hearts as they turned away at first."

Two groups about hypocrites reappear whenever the community faces double agents — those who say obedience by day and plot at night, who spread fear news, who lag and discourage. The verse orders clarity: they are not confused Muslims needing excuse, they are cast-back earners whose night plotting and fear-spreading and lag earned regression. Your debate will not reopen what their earning closed unless they come as in 4:64 seeking forgiveness and in 4:146 with four steps. Guidance after clear proof is with Allah; your two groups will not bridge what Allah cast back.

=89=

"They wish you would disbelieve as they disbelieved so you would be equal. So do not take them as allies until they emigrate in Allah's path. If they turn away, seize and kill them wherever you find them. Do not take from them any ally or helper."

Waddu law takfuruna kama kafaru fa-takununa sawa'an — they wish you would disbelieve as they disbelieved so you would become equal — sawa'an — level, even, same. The same wish as 2:109 "many of the People of the Book wish to return you to disbelief after you believed out of envy after truth became clear" and 3:69 "a group of the People of the Book wishes to mislead you" and 4:139 seeking honour by alliance, 4:51 calling polytheists more guided to please, 4:102 wishing you neglect weapons. Equality in kufr is the hypocrite's comfort; his isolation in nifaq is painful, your distinction in iman with fawz azim in 4:13 and ajr azim in 4:40 is a rebuke; making you sawa' soothes.

Fa-la tattakhidhu minhum awliya hatta yuhajiru fi sabili Allah — so do not take them as allies — awliya — intimate confidants, secret keepers, protectors — until they emigrate in His path — hijrah — the physical, public proof of sincerity, leaving the disbeliever camp for Medina, breaking ties that bind to nifaq and to taghut courts, as the weak in 4:97 were blamed for not emigrating when able and as hijrah in 4:100 promises many refuges. Emigration here is not mere travel; it is severance from the hostile camp and joining the community of Salat al-khawf and qist.

Fa-in tawallaw — if they turn away — tawallaw is not a slip of lagging but turning to hostile plot after emigration was offered and proof was clear and the Book was made plain in 4:26 — fa-khudhuhum waqtuluhum haythu wajadtumuhum — seize and kill them wherever you find them. This is not a licence for vigilantes or for anger; it is State law for combatants who after clarity become hostile conspirators and who wish you sawa' in kufr, as context of war with hostile desert Arabs shows and as 4:90-91 immediately qualify with treaty and constricted chests. Wa la tattakhidhu minhum waliyyan wa la nasira — do not take any ally or helper — because waddu to make you equal cannot be trusted with counsel as 3:118 warned "do not take bitanah — intimate — from outside you; they wish you ruin, hatred shows from mouths, what chests hide is greater" and as 4:144 will warn again.

=90=

"Except those who join a people you have a treaty with, or come to you with chests constricted from fighting you or fighting their own people. Had Allah willed He would have given them power over you and they would have fought you. If they withdraw, not fighting you, and offer you peace, Allah has not made a way for you against them."

Illa alladhina yasiluna ila qawmin baynakum wa baynahum mithaqun — except those who join — yasiluna — connect, attach — to a people between you and them is a treaty — mithaq — as with Banu Mudlaj, Khuza'ah, or other allies with whom the Prophet had pacts, or the treaty of Hudaybiyyah later. Covenant protects even disbelievers; the Prophet never broke a treaty first, as prophets keep. Aw ja'ukum hasirat suduruhum an yuqatilukum aw yuqatilu qawmahum — or come to you with chests constricted — hasirat — tight, pressed — suduruhum — their chests — from fighting you or fighting their own people. They dislike battle either side, hesitant, neutral, not the lagger in 4:72 who wanted spoils nor the double agent in 4:91 who plunges when fitna returns. Neutrality with tight chests is sincerity of aversion, not opportunism; they truly abhor war and find no good way.

Wa law sha'a Allahu la-sallatahum alaykum fa-laqatalukum — had Allah willed He would have given them power, authority, dominance — sallatahum — over you and they would have fought you. Power is His to give and restrain, as mulk in 3:26 "You give sovereignty to whom You will" and as He restrains ba's in 4:84. Their restraint is not weakness alone; it is His restraint. Fa-in i'tazalukum fa-lam yuqatilukum wa alqaw ilaykum as-salam fa-ma ja'ala Allahu lakum alayhim sabila — if they withdraw — i'tazalu — not fighting you, and throw to you peace — alqaw as-salam — offer salam, surrender, truce — Allah has made no way — sabila — authority, legal way, to harm them. Sabila is removed for neutral peace-offerers even if they remain disbelievers. Islamic war ethics after "fight for the oppressed" in 4:75 and "Satan's plot weak" in 4:76 and "take precautions" in 4:71 and "fear not but Me" now shows restraint and mercy: neutral peaceful people, even ideologically opposed, are protected if they offer salam and restrain hands. The Prophet kept treaties until the other side broke them, and he accepted the neutrality of tribes who withdrew.

=91=

"You will find others who wish to be safe from you and safe from their own people. Whenever they are returned to temptation they plunge into it. If they do not withdraw from you, offer you peace, and restrain their hands, seize and kill them wherever you overtake them. For them We have given you clear authority."

Sa-tajiduna akharina yuriduna an ya'manukum wa ya'manu qawmahum — you will find others who want safety — aman — from you and from their own people — double-faced opportunists, unlike the constricted neutral above whose chest was tight. These seek aman as insurance, not as principle. Kullama ruddu ila al-fitnati arkisu fiha — whenever they are returned to fitna — temptation, trial, invitation to betray, to attack, to join against you — they plunge — arkisu — fall headlong, invert. The root rakasa is to invert, to fall on the head; not hesitant but eager when chance suits, as opportunists do when spoils appear as in 4:73.

Fa-in lam ya'tazilukum wa yulqu ilaykum as-salam wa yakuffu aydiyahum fa-khudhuhum waqtuluhum haythu thaqiftumuhum — if they do not withdraw, offer peace, and restrain — yakuffu — hands, seize and kill wherever you overtake them — thaqiftumuhum — find them. The three conditions mirror the exception above: withdrawal, salam, restraint of hands. If all three absent, they are hostile double agents, not neutrals; they have chosen aman as trick, not as truce. Wa ula'ika ja'alna lakum alayhim sultanan mubina — for them We have given you clear authority — sultan mubin — manifest, plain proof to act, so no community will say you attacked the peaceful; you restrained for the constricted in 4:90, you acted against the plunger here. The distinction is fiqh that lives today: protect neutral peace-offerers even if ideologically opposed; confront opportunists who use neutrality as mask to plunge when safe, and whose yatarabbas waits for your weakness.

=92=

"It is not for a believer to kill a believer except by mistake. Whoever kills a believer by mistake, let him free a believing slave and pay blood money to his family unless they forgo as charity. If the victim was from an enemy people though he was a believer, let him free a believing slave. If from a people you have a treaty with, pay blood money to his family and free a believing slave. Whoever does not find, let him fast two months consecutively as repentance from Allah. Allah is Knowing, Wise."

Wa ma kana li-mu'min an yaqtula mu'minan illa khata'an — it is not for a believer — ma kana — it is not befitting, not imaginable in iman — to kill a believer except by mistake. The sanctity of the believer's blood is like the sanctity of the Ka'bah; the Prophet said the believer's blood is more sacred to Allah than the House, and "the first matters judged on the Day are blood." Illa khata'an — except by mistake — accident without intent, misidentification in the confusion of salat al-khawf, or arrow that went astray, or testimony error.

Consequence is both liberation and compensation: fa-tahriru raqabatin mu'minatin — freeing a believing slave — emancipation as worship, moving society toward freeing, as 4:25 moved marriage of the owned toward chastity with permission and dowry, and as freeing is expiation for zihar and for breaking oaths — and diya — blood money — musallamah — handed over, paid — to his family — ahlihi — unless they forgo as sadaqah — charity — that may heal and hide and be better for the killer's soul. The family may forgive entirely, as the Prophet encouraged forgiveness, but the community must not pressure.

Fa-in kana min qawmin aduwwwin lakum wa huwa mu'minun fa-tahriru raqabatin mu'minatin — if the victim was from an enemy people — dar al-harb — though he was a believer — perhaps he hid his faith — then freeing a believing slave, no diya to an enemy treasury that may use it to fight you, though scholars differ on whether diya to his Muslim family still. Wa in kana min qawmin baynakum wa baynahum mithaqun fa-diyatun musallamatun ila ahlihi wa tahriru raqabatin mu'minatin — if from a people between you and them is a treaty — mithaq — as in 4:90, then diya to his family — even if the victim was not Muslim, treaty protects life — and freeing. The treaty life is sacred; breaking it iszulm that even jizyah does not permit.

Faman lam yajid fa-siyamu shahrayni mutatabi'ayni tawbatan min Allah — whoever does not find a slave to free — because slavery was being ended and few remained, or he is poor — then fast two months consecutively — sixty days, continuous, no break except illness or travel, as expiation for zihar, as repentance — tawbatan min Allah — repentance from Allah, granted by Him. Wa kan Allahu Aliman Hakima — Knowing whether it was mistake or masked intent, whether the hand trembled or aimed, Wise in making expiation heavy enough that precautions in 4:71 and verification in 4:83 and 4:94 matter before shooting. After distinguishing neutrals and double agents, this verse says even in legitimate fighting precaution is required so believers not be killed by mistake, and community must free, pay, or fast.

=93=

"Whoever kills a believer intentionally, his recompense is Hell, abiding eternally, Allah is angry with him, cursed him, and has prepared a great punishment."

Wa man yaqtul mu'minan muta'ammidan fa-jaza'uhu Jahannamu khalidan fiha — whoever kills a believer intentionally — muta'ammidan — deliberate, premeditated, with enmity — his recompense is Hell abiding — khalidan — eternally, or a long abiding that scholars discuss with hope of Tawbah. Wa ghadiba Allahu alayhi — Allah is angry — ghadiba — His wrath, not only law; anger that is an attribute befitting His majesty. Wa la'anahu — cursed him — la'n — distance and expulsion, as in 4:46,52. Wa a'adda lahu adhaban azima — prepared — a'adda — a great punishment — azima — great, as ajr azim is great for obedience. Four afflictions after one deed, each heavier than the last, to match the sanctity violated.

The verse after mistake shows contrast: mistake has freeing, diya, fasting; intent has anger, curse, prepared great torture. The Prophet said the believer remains in spaciousness of his religion so long as he does not shed forbidden blood; if he does, spaciousness closes. He said, "The first cases judged on the Day are blood." Intentional murder cannot be washed by diya alone; it demands qisas — life for life — or pardon from heirs with diya, and sincere near repentance in 4:17 may still open before gargle, for less than shirk Allah forgives for whom He wills as in 4:48 and 4:116, but threat remains to deter hands that think privilege, tribe, or uniform permits. After "Allah does not wrong even a dharrah" in 4:40 and "not wronged a fatil" in 4:49 and "not wronged a naqir" in 4:124, shedding a believer's blood without right is the grossest zulm, the opposite of muqit, the opposite of Hasib.

=94=

"O believers, when you go forth in Allah's path verify, and do not say to one who offers you peace, 'You are not a believer,' seeking fleeting gains of worldly life. With Allah are many bounties. You were like that before and Allah favoured you. So verify. Allah is ever Aware of what you do."

Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu idha darabtum fi sabili Allahi fa-tabayyanu — when you strike forth — darabtum — in His path — travel, patrol, campaign, as in 4:101 darabtum fil-ard — verify — fa-tabayyanu — be clear, investigate, withhold judgment until proof, as yastanbituna in 4:83 and radduhu in 4:83. In the recitation fa-tathabbatu — be firm — same meaning: be fixed, not hasty. Wa la taqulu li-man alqa ilaykum as-salam lasta mu'minan — do not say to one who throws to you peace — as-salam — greeting "as-salamu alaykum" or surrender and peace — "you are not a believer" — tabtaghuna arad al-hayat ad-dunya — seeking fleeting chattel of worldly life — spoils, a flock of sheep, a reputation for zeal, a kill count. Fa-inda Allahi maghanimu kathirah — with Allah are many bounties — maghanim — spoils, rewards, provision beyond the one sheep you chase by hasty takfir, and better provision that is halal.

Kadhalika kuntum min qablu fa-manna Allahu alaykum — you were like that before — concealers of Islam in Makkah, weak, afraid to show, speaking salam quietly, even the killer was once like his victim — and Allah favoured you — manna — with guidance, safety, and belonging. So remember your origin when judging another's salam, and remember his present. Fa-tabayyanu — verify — repeated for emphasis after manna. Inna Allaha kana bima ta'maluna Khabira — Aware — Khabir — of what you do, whether verification with patience or hasty branding for gain, whether you remembered you were once like that. Revelation: a companion — Usamah ibn Zayd in one report, another companion in another — killed a man who had said as-salamu alaykum after a battle to take his sheep; the Prophet rebuked him severely, and this verse descended, as did the rebuke to Usamah: "Did you split his heart to see?" The lesson after intentional murder is to not slide from careful war ethics in 4:90-92 into labeling to justify killing for worldly gain; tabayyun and tathabbut are worship, and manna on you demands manna from you.

=95=

"Not equal are those believers who sit at home without excuse and those who strive in Allah's path with their wealth and selves. Allah has favoured those who strive with wealth and selves over those who sit by a degree. To both Allah has promised the good, but Allah has favoured those who strive over those who sit with a great reward —"

La yastawi al-qa'iduna min al-mu'minina ghayru uli ad-darar wal-mujahiduna fi sabili Allahi bi-amwalihim wa anfusihim — not equal — la yastawi — are sitters without injury — qa'idun — who remain at home, not guarding, not striving, and strivers with wealth and selves — mujahiduna. The phrase bi-amwalihim wa anfusihim repeats the sale in 4:74 who sell worldly for Hereafter and the batta' of lagger; striving is spending wealth and exposing selves, not only sentiment, not only dua though dua is jihad. Faddala Allahu al-mujahidina ala al-qa'idina darajatan — favoured strivers over sitters by a degree — darajatan — one rank. Wa kullan wa'ada Allahu al-husna — to both — sitters with excuse and sincere intention — He promised husna — the good, Paradise — as 4:40's multiplied good includes them, 4:96's maghfira, 4:95's own promise, and as the blind companion Abdullah ibn Umm Maktum worried and this exception ghayru uli ad-darar was revealed for him and those like him. Wa faddala Allahu al-mujahidina ala al-qa'idina ajran azima — and favoured strivers over sitters with a great reward — ajran azima — beyond the degree — the same ajr azim as in 4:40 and 4:114.

Ghayru uli ad-darar — except those with harm — injury, blindness, chronic illness, valid excuse — excuses the sitter without blame, as the Prophet left behind honest excused at Tabuk and they shared reward by intention. Not equal does not mean worthless; husna remains for the sincere sitter with excuse, but ajr azim is for strivers who fought taghut's allies in 4:76 for the mustad'afin in 4:75 and who verified in 4:94.

=96=

"Degrees from Him, and forgiveness and mercy. Allah is ever Forgiving, Merciful."

Darajatin minhu wa maghfiratan wa rahmah — degrees from Him — darajāt — distinct ranks, not one flat reward, as the Prophet said Paradise has a hundred levels, between each two like heaven and earth, prepared for mujahidin, and as the shuhada in 4:69 have degrees. Wa maghfiratan — forgiveness — covering shortcomings of the fighter who earlier feared people as in 4:77, or lagged as in 4:72, then repented and went. Wa rahmah — mercy — that makes striving possible despite weakness in 4:28 and despite fear and despite wounds.

Wa kan Allahu Ghafuran Rahima — Forgiving of lag and fear when turned and of sitting with darar, Merciful in multiplying a dharrah into ajr azim and degrees. The surah of atom justice and husna ends the comparison with forgiveness for both groups, but degrees for the strivers, to encourage without crushing the excused, and to show that husna is not the ceiling.

=97=

"Those whose souls the angels take while wronging themselves — the angels say, 'What was your condition?' They say, 'We were oppressed in the land.' They say, 'Was Allah's earth not spacious so you could emigrate?' Those — their refuge is Hell, an evil destination — except the helpless among men, women, and children who cannot devise a plan nor find a way; for them perhaps Allah will pardon."

Inna alladhina tawaffahum al-malaikatu zalimi anfusihim qalu fi-ma kuntum — angels at death take souls while they wronged themselves — zalimi anfusihim — by staying among oppressors where faith cannot be lived, prayer cannot be kept as in 4:43/101, hijab cannot be kept, children cannot be raised, yet not emigrating though able and earth is spacious, as 4:100 promises many refuges. Angels ask, "What were you in?" — fi-ma kuntum — what condition, what deen? They answer, "We were oppressed — mustad'afina — in the land." They claim mustad'af. Angels reply, "Was Allah's earth not spacious — wasi'ah — so you could emigrate?" Wasi'ah belongs to Allah as in 4:126 and 4:100 muraghaman wa sa'ah.

Fa-ula'ika ma'wahum Jahannam wa sa'at masira — their refuge is Hell, evil destination — sa'at masira — as 4:97, 4:115. Exception: illa al-mustad'afina min ar-rijali wan-nisa wal-wildani la yastati'una hilatan wa la yahtaduna sabila — truly helpless who cannot devise hilah — plan — nor find way — truly unable, not double agents in 4:91, not laggers in 4:72. Fa-ula'ika asa Allahu an ya'fu anhum — perhaps Allah will pardon — asa from Allah is sure hope — Afuww Ghafur — effacing, Forgiving.

Hijrah after Badr made this urgent: staying in Makkah while able to join the Prophet without excuse became self-wrong. For today earth spacious applies wherever hijrah to a place where deen can be lived is possible — to another city, to a community, to a different work — while excuses are measured by reality, not comfort or fear of losing status. The weak who cannot devise hilah are not blamed for staying; Afuww covers them.

=98=

"Except the helpless among men, women, and children who cannot devise a plan nor find a way."

Illa al-mustad'afina min ar-rijali wan-nisa wal-wildani la yastati'una hilatan wa la yahtaduna sabila — the exception detailed to prevent despair and to prevent false claims. Mustad'af is not the lagger who lagged by choice in 4:72 saying "Allah blessed me not to be there," not the double agent who plunges in 4:91, but the genuinely helpless: elderly parent, widowed mother with infants, child, prisoner with no map or money or guide, the ill who cannot be moved. Hilatan — trick, plan to exit — the Quran honours hilah for the weak as wisdom, not deceit; Sunnah permits hilah to escape oppression. La yahtaduna sabila — no way guided. For them, staying is not self-wrong; next verse says perhaps pardon, because excuses with Allah are precise.

=99=

"For them perhaps Allah will pardon, for Allah is Ever Pardoning, Forgiving."

Fa-ula'ika asa Allahu an ya'fu anhum — for them perhaps Allah will pardon — 'asa, perhaps, is divine courtesy meaning sure hope; when Allah says perhaps, He will. Wa kan Allahu Afuwwan Ghafura — Pardoning — Afuww — effaces sin as if never written, more intense than Ghafur which covers — Forgiving. After threat of Hell for able stayers and warning that earth is spacious, hope for the helpless, and after warnings of skins renewed and humiliating punishment, Afuww still effaces for the one who cannot move but whose heart wanted to emigrate, who did dhikr standing, sitting, lying while stuck. The Prophet said the excused at home at Tabuk shared reward with mujahidin by valid intention, as sitters with darar in 4:95-96 are promised husna and degrees to mujahidin.

=100=

"Whoever emigrates in Allah's path will find many refuges and abundance. Whoever leaves his home emigrating to Allah and His Messenger then death overtakes him, his reward has fallen upon Allah. Allah is Forgiving, Merciful."

Wa man yuhajir fi sabili Allahi yajid fil-ardi muraghaman kathiran wa sa'ah — whoever emigrates in His path will find many refuges — muragham — places to go despite the nose of the enemy being rubbed in dust — raghima anfuhu — and sa'ah — spaciousness, abundance, provision, company, prayer places, work. Muragham annoys taghut as night plotting annoys Allah; hijrah is worship that angers falsehood. Sa'ah answers "was not the earth spacious?" — yes, spacious, not narrow as Satan's ghurur in 4:120 promised security in staying.

Wa man yakhruj min baytihi muhajiran ila Allahi wa rasulihi thumma yudrikhu al-mawtu fa-qad waqa'a ajruhu ala Allah — whoever leaves his house emigrating to Allah and His Messenger then death overtakes him before arrival, his reward has fallen — waqa'a — upon Allah — secured, guaranteed, deposited. The story of an old sick man — Jundub ibn Damrah or Habib, or an unnamed companion — who said "carry me, I am not of those who cannot find a way" and died on the road to Medina occasioned this; companions worried his hijrah was incomplete, and this verse assured it completed with Allah. Intention plus departure secures wage even if feet stop, as 4:95's intention secures husna for the excused.

Wa kan Allahu Ghafuran Rahima — Forgiving of the hijrah's abandonment of home, past sins left behind, and Rahim in providing many refuges and in guaranteeing wage. Placed after pardon for the helpless, this promises openings for the able: many refuges, abundance, reward guaranteed even if death interrupts, spacious earth under a Wali who suffices in 4:45 and Wakil in 4:81. The surah that began with to Allah belongs whatever in heavens and earth in 4:126 ends this hijrah passage with a guarantee that His earth and His wage encompass the emigrant.
