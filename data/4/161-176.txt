"They ask you for a ruling. Say: Allah gives you a ruling about kalalah — one with no parent or child. If a man dies childless and has a sister, she has half what he left. He inherits her if she dies childless. If two sisters, two-thirds of what he left. If there are brothers and sisters, the male like two females. Allah makes clear so you do not stray. Allah knows all things."

Yastaftunaka qul Allahu yuftikum fil-kalalati — they ask you for fatwa — yastaftunaka — about kalalah — one with no surviving parent or child — as in 4:12's maternal siblings kalalah and now this verse for full and paternal siblings, the last verse revealed in An-Nisa, the closing of inheritance, the seal. In imru'un halaka laysa lahu waladun wa lahu ukhtun fa-laha nisfu ma taraka — if a man dies and has no child and has a sister — full or paternal, not maternal sisters of 4:12 — she has half what he left — as in 4:11 a single daughter half, but here sister half, because she stands in place of child. Wa huwa yarithuha in lam yakun laha walad — he — brother — inherits her entirely if she dies childless and without parent — complete, as husband's half in 4:12 and father's residue in 4:11, but here brother takes all when alone.

Fa-in kanata ithnatayni fa-lahuma ath-thuluthani mimma taraka — if two sisters, two-thirds, as two daughters two-thirds in 4:11. Wa in kanu ikhwatan rijalan wa nisa'an fa-lidh-dhakari mithlu hazzi al-unthayayn — if mixed brothers and sisters, male like two females — same qiwamah economics as 4:11, not a statement of worth but of responsibility and distribution, as mothers and wombs were protected earlier and as qist demands and as the Prophet explained.

Yubayyinu Allahu lakum an tadillu — Allah makes clear — yubayyinu — so you do not stray — an tadillu — lest you go far as in 4:60 far, 4:116 far, 4:60, and lest you stray as Satan loves in 4:119 yudillu, and as 2:282. Bayan closes the surah as it opened with bayan desire in 4:26 "Allah wants to make clear and guide you," and as 2:242 "He makes clear His signs so you may reason," and as 16:89. The surah that began with one soul spreading many, then detailed trusts, dowries, prohibitions, permissions, wisdoms, and warnings, ends with precise division of those many when one soul dies without parent or child, so wombs are not wronged a thread, so no mahis is needed, so clarity prevents deviation that makes religion heavy and makes riba seem light.

Wallahu bi-kulli shay'in Alim — Allah knows all things — Alim — the same Alim who knew hearts in 4:63, sins in 4:111, faith in 4:25, hearing in 4:148, and encompasses as Muhit in 4:126. The One Watchful — Raqib — at birth in 4:1, Witness — Shahid — in between in 4:41, Trustee — Wakil — through life, Sufficient — Hasib — in reckoning, Appreciative — Shakir — in gratitude, is Knowing — Alim — at death in 4:176, and after. The circle that began with "be mindful of your Lord who created you from one soul" closes with "Allah knows all things" — mindful of the One who knows, who created, who watches, who judges, who forgives.
