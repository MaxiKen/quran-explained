Revelation came when Christians from Abyssinia heard the Quran and tears flowed, when some companions vowed celibacy, when game came near hands in ihram to test. Hadith teaches that tears from fear of Allah extinguish fire, that intoxicants are mother of evils, that the Ka'bah is support as in 5:97, and that Allah is Severe and Forgiving as in 5:98.

For today apply it straight: love those whose hearts soften at Quran as in 5:82-83, not those who rush to sin; eat lawful and good without excess as in 5:87-88, keep deliberate oaths with expiation as in 5:89, avoid intoxicants and gambling as filth that blocks prayer as in 5:90-91, and obey as in 5:92.
