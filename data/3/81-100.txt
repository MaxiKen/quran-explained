"In it are clear signs: the standing place of Ibrahim. And whoever enters it shall be safe. And pilgrimage to the House is a duty owed to Allah upon the people — for whoever is able to find a way to it. And whoever disbelieves — then indeed, Allah is free of need of the worlds."

Clear signs include Maqam Ibrahim. Whoever enters is safe — the sanctuary's security, a historic and legal reality of the Haram. Hajj to the House is due to Allah upon people who can find a way — istita'ah of body, path, and means. Whoever refuses in disbelief — Allah is Ghani, free of need of all worlds. Hajj benefits you, not Him. The duty is honour; the independence of Allah is absolute.

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said hajj mabrur has no reward except Paradise, and he taught the rites in person: take your manasik from me. He performed the farewell hajj and removed Jahiliyyah practices from the House. Ability is a condition that scholars detail with care; inventing inability out of laziness is not the spirit of istita'ah. Disbelief here includes denying the obligation after knowledge.

If you are able and delay without excuse, the duty hangs over the years. If you enter the Haram, honour its safety — of people, of animals, of the sanctity of blood and hunting as legislated. If you stand at the Maqam, remember the builder who stood on it while raising walls with his son. Ghaniyyun 'an al-'alamin means your ritual does not feed Allah — it feeds your own salvation and polishes your submission.

Plan for hajj like you plan for major life events if the way is open. Save. Learn. Purify the intention for Allah alone. And if the way is truly closed, know that Ghani does not lose — and the sincere intention of the unable is not unseen with Him.

Maqam Ibrahim, safety of entry, hajj as duty on the able, Allah free of need if you refuse. Plan hajj when able; honour Haram; learn manasik from the Prophet's "take from me." Ghani means your rite feeds you, not Him. Delay without excuse is dangerous comfort.

"Say, 'O People of the Book, why do you disbelieve in the signs of Allah while Allah is Witness over what you do?'"

Why kufr in the signs while Allah is shahid over what you do? Witness as watcher of the refusal. The House, the Prophet, the clear verses, the covenant of the prophets — signs enough — and the Watcher is not absent. Shahid here is not a passive observer; it is the prelude to account.

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said Allah gives respite to the wrongdoer until He seizes him and he does not escape. Witness is patient, then decisive. He also said the feet of a servant will not move on the Day until he is asked about his life, knowledge, wealth, and body — all under the Witness who already knows the answers.

Do what you do knowing the Witness. Disbelief under witness is a choice made in an open courtroom. Close the case with iman before the session ends. The same Witness watches a Muslim who sees signs in his own life and still refuses a command — so the ayah's spirit is wider than its first address.

A practice: when you are alone about to sin, say aloud, "Allah is Witness." Many sins die in the throat of that sentence. When you are alone about to do good unseen, smile: the Witness is enough audience.

Why disbelieve while Allah is Witness? Open courtroom. Practice saying "Allah is Witness" when alone toward sin or toward secret good. Seizure comes after respite. Close your case with iman while the session of life continues.

"Say, 'O People of the Book, why do you avert from the way of Allah those who believe, seeking to make it seem crooked, while you are witnesses? And Allah is not unaware of what you do.'"

Not only personal kufr — active diversion of believers from Allah's way, seeking to twist it, i'waj, while they witness the truth. Allah is not ghafìl, unaware. Missionary corruption of the path is a watched crime. Turning people from the way is heavier than turning yourself alone, because the burden multiplies with those who follow the misleader (a principle the Prophet stated about sunnah of good and evil).

The Prophet, peace be upon him, warned of people who invite to the gates of Hell, well-spoken, and said whoever calls to misguidance carries a burden like those who follow him without theirs diminishing. Seeking to make the path look crooked is intentional redesign of deen — through doubt campaigns, moral inversion, or scholarly distortion.

If you influence others, fear this ayah. Do not sell a crooked version of the way to keep company, funding, or academic fashion. Unaware He is not. The believers you avert may recover; your file as averter remains. And if you have been averted by someone, return and do not become an averter in turn out of bitterness.

Media, education, and friendship are the modern fields of this ayah. Ask: does my content straighten the way or crook it? Does my joke about halal and haram plant i'waj in a teenager's chest? Allah is not unaware. That is either terror or comfort — depending on what you are doing when you remember it.

Averting believers from the way, seeking crooking, while witnesses — and Allah is not unaware. Influencers of doubt carry burdens of those who follow. Audit your content and jokes. Straighten, do not crook. Unawareness is not His attribute; make it your fear and your comfort according to your deeds.

=100=

"O you who have believed, if you obey a party of those who were given the Scripture, they will turn you back after your belief into disbelievers."

A warning slammed onto the ummah's door at the end of this hundred. If you obey a faction of those given the Book — of the type described: envying, mixing, concealing, wishing your misguidance — they will turn you after iman into kafirs. Obedience to the wrong religious authorities outside your deen will not end in polite dual citizenship of identity; it can end in inverted faith. The verse does not ban learning skills, trade, justice, or kind relations (60:8). It bans obedient following in deen of those whose project is your apostasy.

The Prophet, peace be upon him, said you will follow the ways of those before you span by span and cubit by cubit, even into a lizard's hole if they entered it — a warning against blind imitation of failed religious communities. He said he left us on a clear white path. Uhud-era and Madinan pressures included invitations to doubt after victory and after loss; this ayah arms the believers before the next pull.

Believers: choose whom you obey in deen carefully. A party would love your return to kufr. Do not hand them the reins of your creed, your children's education in faith, or your fatwa. Hold the Book of Allah and the Messenger, and let every other authority be measured against them — or dropped. Obedience is a form of worship; misdirected obedience can become a road out of Islam.

This is a fitting seal for 1–100 of Al Imran: from Alif Lam Mim and Hayy Qayyum, through Isa and the common word, to a final guardrail — do not obey those who want your disbelief. Stay submitted. Stay with the wali of the believers. Stay on the millah of Ibrahim and the path of the Messenger until you die upon it, if Allah wills. And seek His help, for hearts turn and He is the Turner of hearts.

If you obey that party of the Book, they will turn you back to kufr after iman. Guardrail for the ummah. Learn skills from anyone; take deen from Quran and sunnah. The Prophet warned of following earlier nations hole by hole. Measure every religious obedience against revelation. Stay muslim under the Wali of the believers until death, and ask the Turner of hearts to fix you on it.
