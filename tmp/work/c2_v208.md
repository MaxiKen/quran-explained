**ENTERING ISLAM ENTIRELY**

***“O believers! Enter into Islam wholeheartedly and do not”*** establishes entering islam entirely. The believers are commanded to enter into al-silm or al-islām completely—udkhulū fī al-silm kāffah. The phrase means embracing the religion as a whole rather than selecting only the beliefs, rituals, or ethics that fit personal preference. Islam is submission to Allah in creed, worship, character, family life, commerce, justice, and public responsibility. Completely does not mean that a believer becomes instantly perfect. Human beings learn gradually, sin, repent, and grow. It means that the direction of submission is comprehensive: no part of life is declared permanently independent of Allah’s guidance. A person may struggle with a command while believing it is true and working toward obedience; he must not make his struggle a principle that rejects revelation. A person cannot claim Islam while severing worship from ethics. Prayer with fraud, fasting with cruelty, charity with arrogance, or public religious speech with private abuse indicates that the message has been divided in practice. The Qur’an joins faith and action repeatedly, and the Prophet ﷺ embodied the connection between devotion and good character. The command also prevents selective reading. Some people accept verses about mercy but reject justice, or quote rules while ignoring the compassion and wisdom that govern them. Entering Islam completely means reading the Qur’an as a coherent revelation, returning disputed matters to Allah and His Messenger, and allowing clear principles to correct personal bias. The warning about Satan’s footsteps repeats 2:168–169. (2:168 — **“O humanity! Eat from what is lawful and good on the earth and do not follow Satan’s”**) Because entering islam entirely frames the whole revelation belongs together, the whole revelation belongs together returns attention to entering islam entirely.

**THE WHOLE REVELATION BELONGS TOGETHER**

***“follow Satan’s footsteps. Surely he is your sworn enemy”*** deepens the whole revelation belongs together. Satan rarely begins by asking a person to reject everything at once. He invites a small exception, a convenient reinterpretation, a private compromise, or a division between spiritual and practical life. The believer watches the direction of the path. A person may begin by delaying prayer for work, then treating prayer as optional, then deciding that worship has no relevance to public life. Another may begin by excusing a small lie and gradually build a business on deception. The cure is to return quickly, close the first door, and ask Allah for firmness. Entering Islam completely does not require every Muslim culture to look identical. The Sharīʿah contains fixed acts of worship and moral boundaries, while allowing diversity in language, food, clothing, art, architecture, and customary practice when these do not violate revelation. Complete submission means evaluating culture by Islam, not erasing all legitimate culture. Likewise, Muslims differ in knowledge and spiritual strength. The community should teach patiently and support gradual growth without turning gradual learning into permanent rejection. The Prophet ﷺ taught people according to their capacity while making the complete goal clear. Islam addresses the heart through tawḥīd, the body through worship, society through justice, and the soul through purification. The believer enters the whole path by accepting that Allah has the right to guide every sphere. This protects against the modern tendency to keep religion as a private identity while allowing desire or market power to govern everything else. **PARTIAL OBEDIENCE OPENS THE ENEMY’S ROAD**

It also protects against harshness. Complete Islam includes the Prophet’s mercy, his fulfilment of treaties, his patience with learners, his care for neighbours, and his protection of the weak. A person who applies isolated rules while abandoning the prophetic ethic has not entered the revelation completely. The verse addresses the community in the plural. Muslims must help one another enter Islam fully through education, good example, justice, and supportive institutions. A society that makes lawful living impossible through corruption or exploitation pushes people toward compromise. Community leaders should make worship accessible, protect the vulnerable, and teach without humiliation. Each individual remains responsible. No group can claim to have entered Islam completely while its members excuse injustice because a leader commands it. The whole community submits to Allah, and every leader remains a servant. Think of entering islam entirely through a family that moves into a house and boards up one room because it reminds them of the previous owner. The house is theirs, the room is theirs, and the damp in the wall behind their own bed comes from the door they never opened. Entering islam entirely confronts the whole revelation belongs together today in anyone who keeps the fasts, pays the alms and leaves a part of the law untouched because the people around him do not touch it. The verse gives him an order to enter the whole house and an enemy to watch at the threshold, and it asks him which room he has kept locked. **ISLAM AND HUMAN DIVERSITY**

Because the command is given to believers, entering islam entirely shows that the entry into the religion is not finished by a single act. The address is to people who had already believed, and the order is to hand over the rest of the household of the self after the door has been opened. The Book gives the same order to the whole of humanity in an earlier verse of the chapter, and repeats the warning that he is a sworn enemy. A further passage states the path that the feet were meant to walk, so that the reader has the road and the trail set side by side. The sentence ends with the reason: Surely he is your sworn enemy. Ibn ʿAbbās keeps the hostility as one that is plainly declared, so that no believer is excused for treating the steps as harmless. Because the enemy is named at the end of the command, entering islam entirely shows that the trail is not a path but a trap. Since the danger is attached to a step rather than to a doctrine, entering islam entirely teaches that a whole religion can be left one small habit at a time. The whole revelation belongs together makes entering islam entirely practical; partial obedience opens the enemy’s road therefore matters.
