**VICTORY BY ALLAH’S PERMISSION**

***“So they defeated them by Allah’s Will, and David killed Goliath. And”*** establishes victory by allah’s permission. The small believing group defeated the larger force by Allah’s permission. This phrase does not deny human effort. The believers obeyed, endured, planned, and fought under Ṭālūt’s leadership. It means that causes do not operate independently of Allah, and success is not proof that the victorious are entitled to pride or that every victorious cause was just. The same principle protects believers after defeat. A sincere community may lose worldly battles without losing Allah’s favour. The measure is obedience, justice, and patience, not numbers or immediate results. Victory can be a test of gratitude and defeat a test of perseverance. David—Dāwūd—killed Jālūt, the champion of the opposing army. The Qur’an mentions the decisive event without glorifying physical strength alone. David was part of a community under lawful leadership and a just cause. His victory illustrates how Allah can bring relief through a person whose apparent resources are small. The Qur’an later describes David as a prophet, worshipper, judge, and leader. His story therefore continues beyond battlefield success. A person who defeats an enemy but cannot control his own ego has not reached the wisdom Allah gave David. Allah gave David mulk, authority or kingship, and ḥikmah, wisdom. Authority without wisdom becomes oppression, while wisdom without the ability to protect people may be unable to restrain aggression. David’s example joins competence with moral judgement. (38:26 — **“˹We instructed him:˺ “O David! We have surely made you an authority in the land,”**) A report on victory by allah’s permission comes through Ibn ʿAbbās, who identified David as the one who killed Goliath before receiving kingship, wisdom, prophethood, and knowledge of armour-making.

**DAVID AND GOLIATH**

***“Allah blessed David with kingship and wisdom and taught him what He willed.”*** deepens david and goliath. He is later commanded to judge between people with truth and not follow desire. This is an important leadership principle. A ruler, judge, or community leader needs knowledge of revelation, understanding of circumstances, courage, consultation, and self-restraint. The possession of power is not a sign of personal holiness. It becomes a trust measured by justice. Allah taught David what He willed, including judgement, revelation, and skills useful for public welfare. The wording preserves humility: knowledge is granted according to Allah’s will, and no human being possesses all knowledge. A leader should continue learning and consult people with expertise rather than assuming that authority has made him infallible. The Prophet ﷺ praised useful skills and lawful work. The Qur’an later describes David making armour, and authentic hadith say that he ate from the work of his own hands. A righteous leader can combine worship, craftsmanship, administration, and service. If Allah did not repel some people by means of others, the earth would be corrupted. This is a general statement about Allah placing checks upon tyranny. He may protect the vulnerable through a just ruler, a courageous witness, a community organization, a court, or people who resist oppression. Without any restraint, powerful groups could consume the weak without consequence. The verse does not make all conflict beneficial. Human beings must seek peaceful solutions when possible, defend the innocent, and avoid transgression. (2:246 — **“Have you not seen those chiefs of the Children of Israel after Moses?”**)

**KINGSHIP AND WISDOM**

***“Had Allah not repelled a group of people by ˹the might of˺ another,”*** clarifies kingship and wisdom. The repelling can include law, mediation, public accountability, and nonviolent resistance as well as lawful defence. The principle is the preservation of justice, not a celebration of violence. Allah’s bounty is to all worlds—al-ʿālamīn. His gifts include life, guidance, opportunities for reform, and the means by which oppression is checked. A victorious community should recognize that its power is a gift and use it to establish justice, protect minorities, feed the poor, and end revenge. David’s later story contains warnings about judgement and desire. Even a prophet with authority must remain humble before Allah. Political success does not suspend accountability. The real victory is not merely killing the enemy champion. It is preserving faith, protecting people, and developing a just society afterward. A community that wins a battle and then loots, enslaves, or oppresses has converted victory into corruption. The believer asks Allah for victory over the ego as well as protection from external aggression. The Prophet ﷺ taught that true strength lies in controlling anger. This inner victory is essential for anyone given power over others. Think of victory by allah’s permission through a town that raises a small guard on the bridge because the river beyond it is full of rival traders, and that sleeps behind the guard for a hundred years. The bridge is guarded by men, and the peace it keeps is the mercy of a Lord who checks one people with another. (38:17 — **“Be patient ˹O Prophet˺ with what they say. And remember Our servant, David, the man of strength.”**)

**TAUGHT WHAT ALLAH WILLED**

***“corruption would have dominated the earth, but Allah is Gracious to all”*** closes with taught what allah willed. Victory by allah’s permission confronts david and goliath in a reader who believes that his own strength is the only wall between his house and the world. The verse gives him a small company, a sling that ended a giant, a crown given to a shepherd and a mercy that holds the nations apart. And it asks him who is keeping the world from falling while he sleeps. Because the close names the grace that reaches all, showing through victory by allah’s permission that the quiet street he lives in is one of the gifts he never paid for. The story of the two armies is closed with a sentence about the signs, where the Book calls the whole narrative a proof of the message. And the same law of the repelling of peoples is stated in the chapter of the pilgrimage. Because victory by allah’s permission opens into david and goliath, kingship and wisdom joins victory by allah’s permission to david and goliath. Since kingship and wisdom develops victory by allah’s permission, david and goliath carries the transition. Taught what allah willed matters, which means kingship and wisdom answers david and goliath. David and goliath gives practical form to victory by allah’s permission; taught what allah willed sets its horizon. Today, victory by allah’s permission shapes david and goliath in present decisions.
