**TWO VERBS AND A BANK**

The unit that answers the envy-campaign is an order to keep working: ***“Establish prayer, and pay alms-tax.”***. In the gloss of Ibn ʿAbbās, each verb carries its full instruction. The establishing is the perfection of the five daily prayers. The paying is the poor-due, the share owed from the wealth to the poor. The reason the pairing answers the campaign is structural. The pairing is the passage's answer to the strategists of the previous verse. The envy-campaign wants the believers' attention. The order sends the attention to the timetable and to the account. The structure is worth naming. Prayer governs the day. The alms govern the wealth. Between the two, the whole of the believer's estate is scheduled, and the schedule leaves no opening for the recruiters.

**WHAT THE ACCOUNT KEEPS**

The verse then opens the ledger of the sending: ***“Whatever good you send forth for yourselves, you will ˹certainly˺ find ˹its reward˺ with Allah.”***. The glosses hear the sending in the language of the market. Whatever of good, of righteous deeds and charitable works, the servant saves for himself by sending it ahead, he will find its reward stored with the owner. The tradition's gloss widens the good beyond the two verbs to include observing kinship and every obedience. The position of the verse in the passage is therefore deliberate. The envy of 2:109 wants the believers turned back. The bank of 2:110 keeps the believers turned forward. The goods sent ahead are goods nobody can envy away. The recipients of the poor-due are then described in the passage where the charter is given: (2:273 — **“˹Charity is˺ for the needy”**), and the needy are the people whose dignity the same passage protects. The vow and the charity are likewise inventoried in the passage that keeps the full books: (2:270 — **“Whatever charities you give or vows you make are surely known to Allah. And the wrongdoers will have no helpers”**). What follows from the ledger is a difference of time. The envy-campaign works in the present tense. The account works in the settlement tense.

**THE SEEING OF THE SPENDING**

The closing clause files the whole economy under one observation: ***“Surely Allah is All-Seeing of what you ”***. The companion's tradition turns the seeing into the knowing of the innermost thing: he knows your intentions, what you spend in charity and the rest of it. The seeing of the spending is the inspection that makes the bank trustworthy. The goods sent forward are recorded in their full weight, including the intention that gave them their weight. The discipline of the patience needed to keep working under fire was commanded in this surah's first page of practice: (2:45 — **“And seek help through patience and prayer. Indeed, it is a burden except for the humble—”**). The verse therefore answers both halves of the situation at once. The recruiters keep scheming. The worker keeps shipping. The one who sees the shipping keeps the books.

**THE WORK AS THE ANSWER**

For any reader who has been the target of a campaign — a household dispute, an office faction, a theological fight that runs on momentum — the order in this verse is the withdrawal of fuel. The campaign feeds on attention and reply. The prayer and the alms remove both from the table. The reason this is the passage's answer rather than a change of subject is structural. The envy wants a reversal of the truth. The prayer keeps the truth standing on the day's own feet. The alms keep the truth standing in the wealth. The reversal has nowhere to begin. The verse leaves the recruiters with the truth on its timetable and the reward in its bank, and the two together close the door the strategy was trying to open. Keep the schedule. Keep the account. The verdict arrives on its own hour. The spending that went forward keeps arriving too, at the hour when the books are opened and the goods are weighed. Nothing shipped to the owner's account is ever lost in the mail. The two verbs of the verse are the whole estate of that mail service. The prayer stamps the day. The alms stamps the wealth. The owner of the account sees the sending and keeps it. That is the full economy of the verse, and the economy runs without the permission of the envy-campaign.
