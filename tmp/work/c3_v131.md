**WARNING OF A PREPARED FIRE**

***“Guard yourselves against”*** Guard yourselves against the Fire prepared for the disbelievers attaches to the riba-prohibition its enforcement-horizon. The attachment's severity demands careful attention. The interest-consumers, addressed as believers one verse back (O believers. Do not consume interest), are commanded to fear the Fire prepared for the disbelievers. U'iddat lil-kafirin. Readied for the rejecters. The believer's sin set, by the warning's address, at the border of the rejecters' abode. Not as a sentence of disbelief upon the sinner but as the sin's trajectory published. Riba being the practice this Book alone confronts with war from Allah and His Messenger. The believer who walks its road walks toward the abode's border, and the verse posts the border's name. The address also places mercy inside its severity. The Fire is named prepared for the disbelievers. Its designed occupants the rejecters. So the believing consumer is warned off another people's abode. Turn back. This address was never built for you. (3:130 — **“O believers! Do not consume interest, multiplying it many times over.”**)

**MARKET MISCONDUCT BESIDE ETERNAL FIRE**

***“the Fire prepared”*** The verse's placement completes the bridge-passage's descent. The preceding command named the practice, and this warning names its terminus. The two-step the Book's constant warning-architecture. And the Fire-clause's deployment against a market-sin, between two battle-passages, carries the curriculum's deliberate proportion. The community learning Uhud's lessons is shown that the ledger's sins burn as the field's cowardices wound. No compartment of life exempt from the two abodes' pull, the purse's daily choices routing, as surely as the battle-hill's, toward one of the two prepared houses. (3:132 — **“Obey Allah and the Messenger, so you may be shown mercy”**) Market misconduct beside eternal fire bears on modern life because economic misconduct is not morally small merely because contracts and institutions normalize it. That placement joins private finance to public faith and denies any moral refuge in common custom.

**MINDFULNESS BEFORE CONSEQUENCE**

***“for the disbelievers”*** The guarding-command itself. Wattaqu n-nar. Fear the Fire. Carries the believer's operational discipline. The ittiqa of the Fire being taqwa's most concrete form. The shield-word's original picture (the guarded man placing a barrier between himself and harm) deployed at its ultimate object. And the Sunnah supplies the shield's material at every purse's scale. Protect yourself from the Fire even with half a date. The riba-consumer's exact counter-programme. The wealth that multiplied by devouring un-multiplied into shield by giving. And the passage's next verse names the shield's other half. Obedience to Allah and the Messenger, so you may be shown mercy. Before the counter-race opens at the surrounding passage. The community commanded off the Fire's road and onto forgiveness's, where the racing-verbs of mercy replace the multiplying-verbs of gain. Imagine a small spark beside a warehouse marked as highly flammable. In connection with warning of a prepared fire, Ibn ʿAbbās reads fear of the Fire as a warning against consuming usury and rejecting its prohibition. The command to guard against the Fire gives the previous financial rule its true scale; which means usury is not only poor social policy or an unkind bargain. Deliberate disobedience exposes the person to judgment; therefore the Fire being prepared for disbelievers does not make the warning irrelevant to believers; it makes imitation of disbelief’s path especially dangerous. A believer hears the destination and turns away from the road leading toward it; in other words this also prevents legal cleverness from replacing moral fear. A transaction may be dressed in respectable language while preserving the same oppressive substance; hence mindfulness asks what the arrangement truly does, not only what it is called. In present finance, people can become accustomed to harm because it is common, automated, or supported by powerful institutions; this is why the verse restores personal accountability. No custom can cool the Fire, and no profit can compensate for knowingly crossing a boundary set by Allah. Preparation means that judgment is not an improvised threat. The destination fits the settled rejection for which it was made, and believers are warned away from conduct that resembles that rejection. Fear becomes useful when it changes a transaction before the account is closed. A warning heard but repeatedly neutralised by excuses has not yet performed its protective work. Genuine caution leaves a visible mark on the choices made before danger arrives.
