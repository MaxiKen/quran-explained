**CREATION IN THE WOMB**

***“He is the One Who shapes you in the”*** establishes creation in the womb. The verse directs attention to one of the most hidden stages of human existence: formation in the womb. Before a person has a name, social position, wealth, or public reputation, Allah knows and shapes him. The body’s development, the distinguishing features of each individual, and the emergence of a living human being all occur by Allah’s creative will. The Qur’an elsewhere describes the stages of human creation in the womb. These passages are not invitations to replace careful medical study with speculation. Rather, they direct observation toward gratitude and humility: scientific description can examine processes, while revelation identifies the Creator, purpose, and moral responsibility of the life being formed. Allah’s name al-Muṣawwir means the One who gives form. Human beings share a common origin and basic bodily structure, yet every person has a distinct face, voice, fingerprint, temperament, and life story. This diversity is not evidence of divine disorder. It is a sign of creative wisdom and a foundation for recognizing one another. The verse also corrects the illusion that human worth is produced by appearance. Since Allah shapes every person, beauty, disability, sex, ethnicity, and physical difference cannot be grounds for arrogance or contempt. Honour before Allah is tied to faith and righteousness, not to features over which a person had no choice. Allah’s willing and shaping do not deny the ordinary means through which life develops. (23:12 — **“And indeed, We created humankind from an extract of clay”**) A reading attributed to Ibn ʿAbbās places every human form under Allah’s choice in the womb and joins that creative power to divine might and wisdom.

**AL-MUṢAWWIR: THE SHAPER**

***“wombs of your mothers as He wills. There is”*** deepens al-muṣawwir: the shaper. Parents, genetics, nutrition, medical care, and the environment are real created means. They do not operate independently of Allah, but studying them is not a denial of the Creator. The verse calls the believer to see both levels: the created process and the divine Lord who gives that process existence and order. This understanding also guards against fatalism. A person may not control every aspect of his origin or body, but he remains responsible for his choices. Allah judges people according to what they were given and what they were capable of doing; no one is blamed for a feature or condition outside his control. The verse closes with two names. Al-ʿAzīz is the Almighty, whose power cannot be defeated. Al-Ḥakīm is the All-Wise, whose acts and decrees contain wisdom, even when the servant does not immediately perceive it. The One who shapes human beings in hidden stages is both powerful and wise; creation is neither accidental nor abandoned. These names also answer the objections that will appear later in the sūrah concerning Jesus. Allah creates by His command and is not limited by ordinary human expectations. At the same time, every extraordinary act occurs with wisdom and does not make the created being divine. Remembering one’s formation in the womb produces humility toward Allah and compassion toward people. (22:5 — **“O humanity! If you are in doubt about the Resurrection,”**)

**FORMED BY DIVINE CHOICE**

***“no god ˹worthy of worship˺ except Him—the Almighty, All-Wise”*** clarifies formed by divine choice. It should lead parents to protect children, societies to care for the vulnerable, and individuals to treat their bodies as trusts. Life is not self-created property. It is a gift placed under moral responsibility. The forming child is like a work taking shape behind a closed curtain, except the Creator knows every stage and chooses every feature without uncertainty. Today, pregnancy, disability, appearance, and sex must never become grounds for contempt, because every human form exists by the Creator’s wisdom. Because creation in the womb opens into al-muṣawwir: the shaper, formed by divine choice joins creation in the womb to al-muṣawwir: the shaper. Since formed by divine choice develops creation in the womb, al-muṣawwir: the shaper carries the transition. Al-ʿazīz and al-ḥakīm matters, which means formed by divine choice answers al-muṣawwir: the shaper. Al-muṣawwir: the shaper gives practical form to creation in the womb; al-ʿazīz and al-ḥakīm sets its horizon. Creation in the womb stays bound to formed by divine choice because al-muṣawwir: the shaper supplies the human setting of creation in the womb. Al-ʿazīz and al-ḥakīm carries creation in the womb into conduct shaped by formed by divine choice. (49:13 — **“O humanity! Indeed, We created you from a male and a female,”**)
