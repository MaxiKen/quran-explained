**GRIEF AT ANOTHER’S GOOD**

***“When you ˹believers˺ are touched with good, they grieve; but when”*** When you ˹believers˺ are touched with good, they grieve. But when you are afflicted with evil, they rejoice completes the concealed-enmity file with its diagnostic instrument. The instrument's precision lies in its exact diagnosis. The two-clause schedule. Your good their grief, your harm their joy. Envy's exact fingerprint. And its diagnostic power being its involuntariness. Speeches are managed, greetings staged, but the flinch at the believers' good news and the light in the eye at their setback escape management. The schedule reads hearts through their reflexes. The verse thus handing the community the lining-law's field-test. The confidant-candidate is read not at his professions but at the arrival of your good and your evil. Whom your blessing quietly pains, the lining excludes. (3:119 — **“Here you are! You love them but they do not love you,”**)

**PATIENCE AND MINDFULNESS DEFEAT THE PLOT**

***“you are afflicted with evil, they rejoice. ˹Yet,˺ if you are”*** ˹Yet,˺ if you are patient and mindful ˹of Allah˺, their schemes will not harm you in the least delivers the file's closing equipment. And its two conditions are the argument's oldest pair at their most operational. In tasbiru wa tattaqu. If you are patient and God-fearing. Sabr and taqwa. The endurance that absorbs the campaigns' annoyance without breaking formation, and the mindfulness that keeps the community's own conduct inside Allah's bounds under provocation. And the conditions' yield stated as absolute insulation. La yadurrukum kayduhum shay'an. ***“patient and mindful ˹of Allah˺, their schemes will not harm you in”*** Their scheming will not harm you at all. The kayd. The whole apparatus. The reversion-operations, the crookedness-projection, the Bu'ath-poems, the bitten-knuckle councils. Rendered harmless not by the community's counter-scheming but by its character. Patience and taqwa as armour-plate. The doctrine this Book states as physics. The schemes' only entry-points into a believing community are its impatience (the provoked overreaction, the formation broken at the taunt) and its taqwa-lapses. Seal the two doors, and the apparatus spends itself on the armour. (3:121 — **“O Prophet,˺ when you left your home in the early morning to”**) Patience and mindfulness defeat the plot has present-day force because envy is exposed when another person’s success feels like loss and that person’s hardship feels like gain.

**EVERY SCHEME SURROUNDED**

***“the least. Surely Allah is Fully Aware of what they do”*** Surely Allah is Fully Aware of what they do seals the file with the encirclement-clause. Wallaha bima ya'maluna muhit. Allah encompasses what they do. The muhit-word of another Qur'anic passage's storm-scene (Allah encompasses the disbelievers). The schemers' whole theatre. The councils, the schedules of grief and joy, the operations planned and pending. Contained inside the Awareness they plot beneath. And the verse's placement closes the pre-Uhud file at the exact seam. The next verse steps directly onto Uhud's morning (when you left your home in the early morning to position the believers). The battle in which every clause of this record would be field-tested. The enemy's rejoicing at the believers' wound, the hypocrites' desertion, and the insulation-conditions' proof in both directions. Harmed where patience and taqwa lapsed, and preserved, through the wound itself, wherever the two held. The file thus standing as Uhud's issued briefing. Read before the march, and graded on the mountain. Imagine a rival cheering a damaged ambulance because it belongs to another team. Regarding grief at another’s good, Ibn ʿAbbās identifies joy at believers hardship and grief at their success, then reads patience and mindfulness as protection from the plot. Patience and mindfulness answer two different pressures in the scene; which means patience prevents pain from driving a believer into rash reaction, while mindfulness keeps that restraint tied to obedience rather than fear. Together they close the opening through which an enemy’s scheme seeks to enter; therefore this does not mean that no material injury can occur, for the passage itself recounts wounds. It means that hostile joy and planning cannot determine the believer’s final standing or force a moral collapse; in other words allah surrounds every act, so no part of the plot lies outside His knowledge and power. In modern life, another person’s envy may appear through gossip, blocked opportunities, or celebration of failure; hence the verse directs attention away from reading every hostile mind and toward guarding one’s own response. Schemes gain their deepest victory when they produce disobedience in the person targeted; this is why steadfast conduct denies them that victory even before their outward plan is defeated.
