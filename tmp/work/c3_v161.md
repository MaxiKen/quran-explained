**BETRAYAL UNFIT FOR A PROPHET**

***“It is not appropriate for a prophet to illegally withhold spoils of”*** It is not appropriate for a prophet to illegally withhold spoils of war guards, mid-audit, the community's inner trust at its most sensitive register. The recorded occasion shows why the trust required such protection. Murmurs after the field's confusions (a missing velvet cloak at Badr by one transmitted account. The spoils-anxieties Uhud's hill had just documented) touched the unthinkable. The answer declares that charge incompatible with the prophetic office. Wa ma kana li-nabiyyin an yaghull. It is not for a prophet to ghulul. The ghulul-word. The spoils' secret skimming. The taking before the lawful division. The office's very nature excluding the crime. Prophethood and embezzlement not merely un-combined but un-combinable. The bearers of revelation's whole trust (the amanah at its summit) incapable, by the office's constitution, of the pouch's petty treason. The verse thus simultaneously acquitting the Prophet of the murmur and educating the community in the office's nature (the incorruptibility not his personal virtue only but the sending's own requirement. The message's channel necessarily clean). (3:160 — **“So in Allah let the believers put their trust”**)

**FULL REPAYMENT WITHOUT LOSS**

***“war. And whoever does so, it will be held against them”*** And whoever does so, it will be held against them on the Day of Judgment turns the guard to the ranks. Wa man yaghlul ya'ti bima ghalla yawma l-qiyamah. And whoever skims will come, on the Day of Resurrection, with what he skimmed. And the clause's picture is the Sunnah's own gallery. The skimmer arriving at the gathering carrying his theft. The Prophet's fearsome exposition preserved entire. Let me not find one of you coming on the Day of Judgment with a camel on his neck bleating. With a horse on his neck neighing. With sheep on his neck bleating. ***“on the Day of Judgment. Then every soul will be paid”*** The embezzled things themselves borne, alive and crying, on their takers' necks before the assembly. And the file's field-case published at Khaybar. The servant Mid'am struck by a stray arrow, the ranks crying congratulations, Paradise. And the Prophet. No, by the One in whose Hand is my soul. The cloak he took from the spoils before their division is blazing upon him in fire. The ghulul's smallest units. A cloak, and in the parallel case a sandal-strap or two. Audited at the Day's full register. (3:162 — **“Are those who seek Allah’s pleasure like those who deserve Allah’s wrath?”**) Full repayment without loss bears on modern life because public funds, charitable property, and entrusted goods must be protected even when misuse appears impossible to detect.

**JUSTICE WITHOUT DIMINUTION**

***“in full for what it has done, and none will be wronged”*** Then every soul will be paid in full for what it has done, and none will be wronged seals the guard with the settlement-formula. And its deployment over the spoils-file completes the passage's trust-architecture. The community's wealth-integrity (the spoils the era's treasury) secured at both ends. The office incorruptible above (the prophet-clause), the ranks audited below (the Day's carried evidence), and the settlement exact for all (the full discharge, the no-wronging). The verse's permanent jurisdiction running, wherever public wealth passes believing hands. The treasuries, the trusts, the offices of every era standing under the ghulul-file's terms. The public purse's every skimmed unit scheduled, by this verse, for the neck of its taker at the hall the surrounding passage named. Imagine a thief arriving in court carrying the very object he believed was hidden. In connection with betrayal unfit for a prophet, Ibn ʿAbbās applies the verse to deception concerning booty and describes the deceiver carrying the stolen property into the final account. The opening protects the integrity of revelation by declaring secret theft incompatible with the prophetic office; which means the warning then broadens to every soul, so the Prophet’s innocence does not become an excuse for anyone beneath him. Property taken in secret will not remain secret at judgment; therefore carrying it there makes the hidden deed visible and joins the offender to the very object once concealed. Full repayment answers the thought that a small theft from a common store can disappear among many people; in other words no share is too slight for the account, and no soul loses what justice owes it. Present institutions also depend on people handling funds, supplies, data, or public trust when no owner is watching; hence the verse places that unseen access under final disclosure. Integrity means returning every trust before it becomes evidence carried against its keeper, because the practical demand of betrayal unfit for a prophet follows from it.
