**A WOUND ANSWERED BY A WOUND**

***“If you have suffered injuries ˹at Uḥud˺, they suffered similarly”*** If you have suffered injuries ˹at Uḥud˺, they suffered similarly ˹at Badr˺ opens the alternated-days law with the ledger equalised. And the equalisation's realism is its first medicine. In yamsaskum qarhun faqad massa l-qawma qarhun mithluh. If a wound has touched you, a like wound touched that people. The qarh-word doubled. Your seventy at Uhud, their seventy at Badr. The community's wound thus stripped, at one stroke, of its imagined uniqueness. The enemy bleeds too, has bled first, and bled equally. The war's pain distributed by the same clause that will now name the distribution's law. (3:139 — **“Do not falter or grieve, for you will have the upper hand,”**)

**BELIEVERS DISCLOSED UNDER PRESSURE**

***“˹at Badr˺. We alternate these days ˹of victory and defeat˺”*** We alternate these days ˹of victory and defeat˺ among people states the law itself. Wa tilka l-ayyamu nudawiluha bayna n-nas. And those days. We rotate them among the peoples. And the mudawalah-verb is history's mechanics published. The days. Victory's and defeat's. Circulated by divine administration. No community holding triumph as freehold, none leased defeat forever. The law's wisdom then itemised in the verse's own purpose-clauses. So that Allah may reveal the ˹true˺ believers. Wa liya'lama llahu lladhina amanu. ***“among people so that Allah may reveal the ˹true˺ believers, choose”*** The knowing-clause. Defeat as diagnostic. The day's turning exposing what victory's weather never could. Choose martyrs from among you. Wa yattakhidha minkum shuhada. And take from you witnesses. The martyrdom-clause at its full height. The fallen seventy not the day's waste but its selection. Shuhada. Witnesses. Taken. The verb of choosing. The community taught to read its graves as heaven's own drafting. (3:141 — **“and distinguish the ˹true˺ believers and destroy the disbelievers”**) Believers disclosed under pressure has present-day force because changing fortunes in public life should humble winners, steady the wounded, and reveal commitments hidden during ease.

**WITNESSES CHOSEN WITHOUT LOVE OF WRONG**

***“martyrs from among you—and Allah does not like the wrongdoers—”*** And Allah does not like the wrongdoers closes the law with the forfeited-love clause. And its placement inside the alternation-doctrine is the law's guard. The days rotate, but the rotation is not moral neutrality. The enemy's granted day at Uhud was administration, never endorsement. The wrongdoers unloved at their triumph's very noon. A reader shaped by a wound answered by a wound and the law carries its complete calibration. The wound expected (the rotation's terms), the ledger read whole (their qarh and yours), the purposes trusted (the revealing, the drafting). And the days' turning never misread in either direction. Victory no verdict of love, defeat none of abandonment. The verdicts residing, as this whole argument insists, in the two columns the next verse completes. The believers purified, the disbelievers effaced. Imagine a wheel turning so that yesterday’s upper point descends and another rises. Regarding a wound answered by a wound, Ibn ʿAbbās compares Uhud’s wound with Makkah’s wound at Badr and reads alternating days as occasions that disclose faith and grant martyrdom. Shared pain corrects the idea that a wound alone proves divine rejection; which means the opposing people had suffered as well, so injury could not by itself decide which cause was true. Days of advantage circulate because worldly victory is not handed permanently to one group; therefore this exposes claims, trains communities, and brings hidden qualities into action. The mention of martyrs also shows that death in a just struggle is not simple loss; in other words allah chooses witnesses from among the believers, while wrongdoing remains unloved even when it enjoys a day of success. Present observers often treat each headline as a final moral verdict; hence the verse refuses that short measure. A temporary rise may test the winner, and a temporary fall may purify the wounded; this is why what matters is the conduct each changing day reveals. Believers therefore study a defeat without worshipping it, and receive success without imagining that success has placed injustice beyond judgment. Alternation also prevents any army from reading victory as a permanent certificate of righteousness. An unjust side may receive a day without receiving Allah’s love. A faithful side may be wounded without losing His care. The longer account includes what each day produces in the heart and what witness it leaves for the final judgment.
