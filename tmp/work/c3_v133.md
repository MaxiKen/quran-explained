**FORGIVENESS PURSUED WITHOUT DELAY**

***“And hasten towards forgiveness from your Lord and”*** The command sends believers urgently toward their Lord’s forgiveness and a garden as vast as heaven and earth. It fires the counter-race the bridge-passage built toward. And the command's verb is the passage's whole inversion. Wa sari'u. And race. The hastening-verb of another Qur'anic passage's charter (race one another toward good deeds) and the surrounding passage's honoured racers. Deployed here against the just-prohibited multiplication. The community commanded out of wealth's compounding and into mercy's. The racing's first object being maghfirah. Forgiveness from your Lord. Pardon set before Paradise in the race's own order, by the believer's true sequence. The racer's first need the record's clearing. The repentance-hastening the Sunnah schedules at the sin's own heels. And the second object's measure being the verse's famous immensity. A Paradise whose width is the heavens and the earth. The ard. The breadth. If its width has this measure, its length lies beyond calculation. The image retiring, in one clause, every scarcity-instinct the market's multiplications fed on. The racers compete for an estate whose breadth is the cosmos. No rival's arrival crowds the track. (3:132 — **“Obey Allah and the Messenger, so you may be shown mercy”**)

**A GARDEN AS WIDE AS CREATION**

***“a Paradise as vast as the heavens and”*** Prepared for those mindful ˹of Allah˺ completes the race-announcement with the estate's readiness and its named heirs. And the clause's symmetry is the passage's sealed architecture. U'iddat lil-muttaqin. Prepared for the God-fearing. The same readiness-verb, word for word, as the surrounding passage's Fire prepared for the disbelievers. The two abodes standing built, doors open, each with its designed occupants published. The reader between them holding, in the two u'iddat-clauses, the whole stakes of every hastening and every delay. And the heirs' title. The muttaqin. The watchword's people. (3:134 — **“˹They are˺ those who donate in prosperity and adversity, control their anger,”**) A garden as wide as creation guides conduct today because repentance should be pursued with the urgency people usually reserve for scarce opportunities.

**A HOME PREPARED FOR THE MINDFUL**

***“the earth, prepared for those mindful ˹of Allah˺”*** Being the race's entry-criterion and the next verses' whole business. For the announcement's audience will immediately ask the racing's practical shape, and the surrounding passage answer with the muttaqin's four-fold portrait. The givers in ease and hardship, the anger-swallowers, the pardoners, and the sinners who remember and return. The estate's heirs drawn, with this Book's constant realism, not as the sinless but as the racing. Those whose falls are followed by faster returns. A reader formed by this command carries its permanent correction of tempo. The religion's gait is the race's. The forgiveness sought at speed, the good hastened before its hindrances (the familiar counsel to use opportunities before they pass), and the width of the heavens and the earth held before the eyes at every market where smaller multiplications glitter. Imagine runners moving when the gate opens instead of debating whether the prize is valuable. On racing toward forgiveness, Ibn ʿAbbās reads the race as urgent repentance from usury and every transgression, joined to righteous action and the garden prepared for the mindful. Hastening does not mean that forgiveness is earned by speed alone; which means it means that delay is dangerous because the heart can become comfortable with what first caused shame. The race begins with repentance and opens toward a garden whose vastness defeats narrow ideas of divine generosity; therefore heaven and earth are used to stretch the imagination, yet the garden remains prepared rather than merely possible. This gives hope a solid object; in other words it also identifies the people for whom that preparation has meaning: those who remain mindful of Allah. A person may race for promotion, property, or recognition while treating repentance as work for a later age; hence the verse reverses those priorities. The urgent opportunity is forgiveness, because worldly opportunities end at death while its result continues; this is why readiness for Paradise is built through present choices, not through admiration of its description. The command turns hope into movement before another day makes the same return harder. The size of the garden also corrects the scarcity that often governs competition. People race harshly when they think only one rival can win. Divine forgiveness is not diminished by another servant receiving it, so this race encourages rather than crushes fellow travellers. Each person can urge another forward while still hastening personally.
