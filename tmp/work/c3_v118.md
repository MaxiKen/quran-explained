**RELENTLESS EFFORT TO CAUSE RUIN**

***“O believers! Do not associate closely with others who would not miss a”*** The prohibition concerns close association with outsiders who would not miss a chance to cause harm. It legislates the community's inner-circle law. And its key term must be read at its original depth. Bitanah. The garment's lining. The word for the confidant worn against the skin. The counsellor admitted to secrets, the adviser inside the deliberations, the intimate before whom guards drop. The verse forbidding not acquaintance, trade, or kindness across communities but the lining-intimacy granted to those the clause then diagnoses. Min dunikum. From outside yourselves. Outside the faith's bond. (3:117 — **“destroying it ˹completely˺. Allah never wronged them, but they wronged themselves”**) ***“chance to harm you. Their only desire is to see you suffer. Their”*** Whose disqualifying mark is not their creed alone but their documented disposition. They would not miss a chance to harm you. La ya'lunakum khabalan. They spare you no ruin. The corruption-effort unstinting. Their only desire is to see you suffer. Waddu ma anittum. They wish your distress. The wish-verb of the campaign-file. The verse's occasion, being believers who kept their old confidencies with the hypocrites' circles and the hostile factions' men. The lining worn from before Islam, unexamined after it.

**WORDS REVEAL LESS THAN HEARTS**

***“prejudice has become evident from what they say—and what their hearts hide is far”*** Their prejudice has become evident from what they say. And what their hearts hide is far worse supplies the law's evidence-clause. And its intelligence-doctrine is the verse's gift. Qad badati l-baghda'u min afwahihim. The hatred has shown from their mouths. The tongue leaks. The slips of rage, the mockeries half-swallowed, the annoyance of the surrounding passage's campaigns. Enough surfaces to read the current. And what their chests conceal is greater. The visible leakage certified as the lesser fraction. The believer taught to price the iceberg by its tip, never to excuse the tip by the submerged mass's invisibility. We have made Our revelations clear to you, if only you understood closing the verse at the reason-summons. The law's clarity delivered. The understanding. The reading of linings by their leaks. The hearer's remaining share. (3:119 — **““We believe.” But when alone, they bite their fingertips in rage. Say,”**) Words reveal less than hearts guides conduct today because access to private plans, passwords, and vulnerabilities should be given according to proven trust rather than social charm.

**SIGNS MADE PLAIN**

***“worse. We have made Our revelations clear to you, if only you understood”*** The law's permanent jurisprudence, holds its boundaries as the plain wording defines them. The verse governs the lining, not the neighbourhood. The community's life with other communities remains open at every lawful register. Closed only at the counsel-chamber's door against the documented ill-wisher. And its mirror-clause, is the diagnostic the believer carries. The disqualifier was never the outsider's label but the harm-wish's evidence. Whence the law reads inward too. The labelled insider whose tongue leaks the same baghda' stands equally outside the lining's trust. The garment's lining chosen, in every era, by one criterion. Those who wish the wearer whole. While the next verse will hold the mirror to the believers' own unreciprocated warmth. Here you are. You love them but they do not love you. Imagine a company limiting access to its master key even while cooperating warmly with many partners. On confidence kept within the faithful, Ibn ʿAbbās reads the prohibition as protection against confidants who seek the believers moral ruin while concealing hatred greater than their speech reveals. The command concerns access to a community’s private life, not ordinary fairness toward every outsider; which means that distinction matters because justice remains due even where trust is limited. An intimate adviser receives plans, fears, and weak points, so hostility in such a position can do damage that open opposition cannot; therefore the verse gives observable grounds for caution: relentless harm, delight in suffering, and words that disclose deeper hatred. It therefore does not authorise suspicion based on ancestry or a broad label; in other words conduct is the evidence. In present institutions, the same rule applies to confidential records, strategy, pastoral care, and family matters; hence kindness may be broad, while sensitive trust must be earned. The final appeal to understanding makes judgment a duty rather than an impulse; this is why believers are expected to read clear signs without becoming unjust, and to protect what has been entrusted to them without turning every difference into an accusation.
