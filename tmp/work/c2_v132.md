**THE ADVICE OF TWO FATHERS**

The verse names the speakers before it names the speech. ***“This was the advice of Abraham”*** ***“as well as Jacob”*** ***“to his children, ˹saying˺, “Indeed, Allah has chosen”***. Two fathers. One advice. Delivered across the generations of the House. In the gloss of Ibn ʿAbbās the advice is the standing charge of the family. Abraham and Jacob both said it. Both said it at the end, when the saying costs something. The verse counts the advice as inheritance. It is not opinion. What passes from the fathers to the children in this line is a religion with a dying instruction attached. The grammar does the theology. The advice is singular even though the speakers are two. The reason both names sit on the sentence is that the path was walked twice. Once at the raising of the House. Once at the deathbed. The children of the House receive the faith with a timestamp. It is the last hour of the fathers who held it. The parallel command states the dying-instruction in its open form (3:102 — **“and do not die except in ˹a state of full˺ submission ˹to Him˺”**).

**THE LAST WORDS AS AN INHERITANCE**

The content of the advice is a choosing and a dying-instruction. ***“for you this faith”*** ***“so do not die except in ˹a state of full˺ submission.””***. For Ibn ʿAbbās the choice is the family's own measurement. The faith is chosen for the children. It is not invented by them. The father's last words hand the choosing over as property. Qatādah reads the dying-instruction as the summary of every instruction. Whatever else the fathers taught, the state of the final hour is the state they asked for. The verse turns a deathbed into a curriculum. The advice is the whole religion compressed into its last required form. The difference between advice given at leisure and advice given at death is the weight the verse puts on it. The dying man's speech is his ledger read aloud. The reason the faith is named as chosen is that the choosing settles every later argument. The children do not have to discover the religion. They only have to die in it. What follows is that the submission named here is not a mood. It is a state. The state has a name and a Lord.

**DYING IN THE STATE OF SUBMISSION**

The verse's final clause is a deadline written as a hope. Do not die except in the submission. The report of Ibn ʿAbbās leaves the fathers' words standing over the whole family. The clause completes the covenant's circle. The House is built by submitters. It is inherited by people asked to die submitters. The point is that the verse places the proof of the religion at its very end. The last breath is the exam. The advice is the exam preparation. For the modern reader the clause is comfort and instruction at once. This age hides death from view. It therefore loses the deadline that organises a life. The verse puts the deadline back at the centre of the family's speech. The verse of the last hour states the same horizon in its open form (29:57 — **“Every soul will taste death, then to Us you will ˹all˺ be returned”**). The advice of the two fathers is not nostalgia. It is logistics. The children will die. The fathers want the state ready. The reason the verse keeps both names on the charge is that two witnesses are needed for a last will. The submission is asked for as the final form of a life. The life is what the House was built to hold. The advice is singular because the faith is singular. Two fathers agree at the end of two different lives. The agreement is the inheritance. The children receive a chosen faith and a final instruction. The choosing settles what to hold. The dying-instruction settles how to end holding it. The two charges fit together like a hand and a glove. The deathbed is not a place of new ideas. It is a place of kept ones. The verse dates the advice to the last hour on purpose. The dating gives the words their weight. For the reader the weight transfers. The faith is still chosen. The hour is still coming. The state is still asked for. The advice of the fathers is therefore a standing charge. It is read again at every deathbed in the line. The charge names the faith. The charge names the state. The two names are the inheritance entire.
