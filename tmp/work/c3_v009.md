**THE CERTAIN GATHERING**

***“Our Lord! You will certainly gather all humanity”*** establishes the certain gathering. The prayer of the grounded believers moves from the heart’s present condition to the future resurrection. They affirm that Allah will gather all humanity for a Day about which there is no doubt. The world contains scattered peoples, hidden deeds, unequal outcomes, and unresolved injustices. The gathering brings every person before the One who knows the entire record. This is Yawm al-Qiyāmah, the Day of Resurrection, and also the Day of Judgement. Gathering indicates that death is not disappearance. Every generation, nation, ruler, victim, believer, and rejecter will be brought together. No person will be lost in the passage of time, and no action will be omitted because it was private or forgotten by people. The statement about which there is no doubt is both a creed and a moral warning. Confidence in the Hereafter corrects the illusion that worldly life is the final reality. If there were no resurrection, injustice could remain permanently unanswered and righteousness could be irrational whenever it requires sacrifice. The promise of the Day gives moral meaning to patience, repentance, charity, and restraint. The believer does not know the time of the Day, but he knows that it will come. The unknown date is not a reason to dismiss the appointment; it is a reason to prepare without delay. Allah’s promise is true because He is perfect in knowledge, power, and truthfulness. (45:24 — **“And they argue, “There is nothing beyond our worldly life. We die; others are born.”**) A transmitted reading from Ibn ʿAbbās includes resurrection, reckoning, the Scale, the crossing, Paradise, and the Fire within the promised gathering.

**NO DOUBT ABOUT THE APPOINTMENT**

***“for the ˹promised˺ Day—about which there is no”*** deepens no doubt about the appointment. Human beings promise and fail through forgetfulness, weakness, death, or changing circumstances. Allah is not subject to any of these limitations. What He promises, whether reward, warning, resurrection, or victory for truth, is certain. The believer’s confidence is therefore not optimism based on worldly appearances. It is trust in the promise of Allah. A righteous person may die without seeing justice, while a tyrant may die apparently successful. The final judgement has not been cancelled; it has been postponed to the appointed Day. The previous verse asked Allah not to let hearts deviate after guidance. This verse asks the heart to remain oriented toward the final meeting. Remembering the resurrection strengthens sincerity: the servant no longer lives only for immediate praise, gain, or revenge. He knows that the true audience is Allah and the true completion of every matter is before Him. The prayer also joins personal steadfastness to universal accountability. It is not enough to ask for one’s own guidance while forgetting that all humanity will be judged. The believer asks for a good end and hopes that others too will be guided before the gathering. The Day is a source of fear for the wrongdoer and hope for the oppressed. Allah’s justice does not mean that every person receives identical circumstances; it means that every person is judged with perfect knowledge of circumstances, capacity, intention, and action. (18:47 — **“˹Beware of˺ the Day We will blow the mountains away,”**)

**THE PROMISE CANNOT FAIL**

***“doubt. Surely Allah does not break His promise.””*** clarifies the promise cannot fail. No good deed is lost and no injustice is overlooked. At the same time, the believer does not depend only on his deeds. He seeks Allah’s mercy, as the surrounding verses show. The settled confidence of the promise includes both the warning to the rebellious and the hope offered to those who repent and do good. The promised gathering is like a court summons already issued to every household: differing travel times do not cancel the appointed hearing. Today, delayed justice does not mean a forgotten account; every person still moves toward the gathering Allah has fixed. Because the certain gathering opens into no doubt about the appointment, the promise cannot fail joins the certain gathering to no doubt about the appointment. Since the promise cannot fail develops the certain gathering, no doubt about the appointment carries the transition. The link with the previous prayer matters, which means the promise cannot fail answers no doubt about the appointment. No doubt about the appointment gives practical form to the certain gathering; the link with the previous prayer sets its horizon. The certain gathering stays bound to the promise cannot fail because no doubt about the appointment locates the certain gathering within choices shaped by the certain gathering. The link with the previous prayer carries the certain gathering into conduct shaped by the promise cannot fail.
