**PROOFS THAT CHANGE NO MINDS**

A policy of stubbornness receives its diagnosis. ***“Even if you were to bring every proof to the People of the Book, they would not accept your direction ˹of prayer˺”***. The verse removes the burden of proof from the Prophet's shoulders. The proofs have already done their work. The recognition of the House and the traits was filed in their own shelves long before the turn. The description of the awaited messenger was written in the material they guarded. The proof itself was acknowledged (26:197 — **“Was it not sufficient proof for the deniers that it has been recognized by the knowledgeable among the Children of Israel”**). The knowledgeable recognised. The proof was sufficient. What follows is that the refusal downstream of recognition is not an information problem. It is a posture problem. ʿIkrimah, Mujāhid, and al-Ḍaḥḥāk read the refusal here as the partisanship of the market-stall. Each party will not accept the qiblah of the other. The effect of the verse is to stop the Prophet from exhausting himself over a verdict already reached.

**THREE REFUSALS AND ONE PRICE**

The verse then stacks the refusals so the pattern is unmistakable. ***“nor would you accept theirs”*** ***“nor would any of them accept the direction ˹of prayer˺ of another.”***. Your direction is refused. Their own rival's direction is refused. The third clause shows the disease has no owner. The seal of bias sits on the heart before the argument begins. Ibn ʿAbbās marks the clause as a description of the scene rather than a command. The clause describes how the ground lies so that the planting can go where the soil is open. The point is that refusals of this kind predate the discussion. They survive every proof because they were never produced by a shortage of proofs. The difference between an honest question and a sealed verdict is the willingness to accept an answer. The sealed verdict is recognizable by its allergy to evidence. The clause therefore becomes a diagnostic tool. Watch what a claim does with proof. The posture tells the truth before the words finish. A sealed verdict announces itself by the way it handles the exhibit. The diagnostic therefore costs nothing to use and never misreports.

**KNOWLEDGE OVERRULED**

The closing conditional names the price of overriding the known. ***“And if you were to follow their desires after ˹all˺ the knowledge”*** ***“that has come to you”*** ***“then you would certainly be one of the wrongdoers”***. The knowledge has arrived. The desires remain. Following the second after the first is named as wrongdoing with no softening. The same word against the desires was filed against the claimants of exclusive guidance (2:120 — **“that has come to you, there would be none to protect or help you against Allah”**). No protector. No helper. The sentence of the wrongdoer is handed down at the end of the line. For the modern reader the conditional is a school policy and a household policy. Knowledge is given to be obeyed, not traded for the comfort of the room. The markets of direction keep their stalls open late, and every stall sells its own east. The verse forbids the trade that overrides the known. The price is named in advance. Wrongdoing. And the absence of anyone who can protect the trader from the owner of the horizons.

**WHEN THE ARGUMENT ENDS**

There is a quiet mercy hidden inside the diagnosis. Once the refusal is recognised as pre-decisional, the faithful disputant is released from the spiral of re-argument. The verse does not ask the Prophet to win the sealed verdict. It asks him to keep his own account clean and to keep the knowledge un-traded. The effect of the release is enormous in practice. Energy that would have been spent on the unmoveable is redirected toward the teachable. The reason the closing conditional is addressed to the Prophet is that he is the one most exposed to the pressure of the room. The desires of the loudest can bend a speaker even after the knowledge has arrived. The verse installs the rule against the bending. For the modern reader the diagnostic has a workday form. The committee that will not accept any direction, the forum that re-litigates settled facts, the family that refuses every proof on principle — these are the sealed verdicts of today. The verse names the posture and frees the worker to walk on. The knowledge stands. The trader stands warned. The owner of the horizons keeps the register.
