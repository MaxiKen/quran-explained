**THE MESSENGER PRAYED FOR**

The verse puts a name-less request in the builders' mouths. ***“Our Lord! Raise from among them a messenger”***. From them. From the valley's own households. And to them. Back into the same households. In the answering of Ibn ʿAbbās each phrase finds its man. The raising is Muhammad ﷺ, the one who prayed at the standing-place of Abraham and settled the House. The reciting of the revelations is his reciting of the book. The teaching of the Book and the wisdom is his teaching of the book and of the rulings and the law. The point lands quickly. The prayer of the builders includes the school the building will host. The House and the curriculum were commissioned in the same sentence. The request is plural in both directions because the mercy of the teaching runs along family lines. The parallel verse states the same commission from the sender's side (2:151 — **“Since We have sent you a messenger from among yourselves—reciting to you Our revelations, purifying you, teaching you the Book and wisdom,”**). The messenger's work is named before the messenger is named.

**FOUR VERBS OF THE TEACHING**

The commission has four verbs and the tradition keeps them in order. Recite. Teach the Book. Teach the wisdom. Purify. ***“who will recite to them Your revelations, teach them the Book”*** ***“and wisdom, and purify them.”*** In the transmission of Ibn ʿAbbās the reciting reaches its distance. The truth sounded across the Hijaz and the lands with the mission of Muhammad ﷺ. The rest of the commission stayed in the households the prayer was made for. The same gloss leaves the cleaning to the heart itself. The purifying is the work of the teaching on the one who receives it. Four verbs together define a school. Reading. Ruling. Shaping. Cleansing. The difference between information and revelation is exactly the fourth verb. A messenger who only recites delivers sounds. A messenger who purifies delivers people. The reason wisdom sits beside the Book is that the reading needs a ruling mind to become practice. What follows is that the prayer's answer is not a scripture dropped from the sky. It is a teacher raised from the family and set among the people of the house. The believers' own verse names the raising as their favour (3:164 — **“Indeed, Allah has done the believers a ˹great˺ favour by raising a messenger from among them—reciting to them His revelations, purifying them,”**).

**THE PRAYER'S ANSWER**

The verse closes the commission with the names that make it reliable. ***“Indeed, You ˹alone˺ are the Almighty, All-Wise.””*** The Almighty makes the raising certain. The All-Wise places the raising where it belongs. Ibn ʿAbbās's account puts the son of ʿAbd al-Muṭṭalib at the place where Abraham and Ishmael had prayed. The prayer was answered in his mouth and his mission. The builders' request and the sender's promise meet in one figure. The closing names hold both halves of the meeting. The power that raised the messenger. The wisdom that taught him what to teach. For the modern reader the commission still describes the only school the House was built to host. A recitation read aloud. A book explained. A wisdom that judges. A purity that lands in the chest. This age has many teachers and few purifiers. The verse keeps the fourth verb in the curriculum. The effect of the ending is a prayer answered but not finished. The commission runs until the reciting stops. The reason the verse closes on the two names is that the school needed a power behind it and a wisdom inside it. The commission outlives the commission's first audience. The messenger died. The reciting did not. The teaching moved from the valley to the world. The purifying moved with it. Four verbs still describe the school. Nothing has been added. Nothing has been taken away. The reading aloud still opens the work. The explaining still follows the reading. The wisdom still judges the practice. The cleaning still lands in the chest. A modern reader can test any religious school by the four verbs. Where the reciting stops, the school is closed. Where the purifying stops, the school is decoration. The commission's return address is still in force. Raised from among them. Sent back to them. The teaching runs in both directions. The prayer of the builders was answered in a person. The person left a curriculum. The curriculum is still taking students. The two names behind the commission still hold. The power raises. The wisdom places. The school continues.
