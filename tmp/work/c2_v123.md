**THE DAY OF NO EXCHANGES**

The verse turns the address a second time. ***“And guard yourselves against the Day”***. The occasion is named in the next breath: ***“when no soul will be of any help to another.”*** The report of Ibn ʿAbbās is direct about the span. The verse was revealed about everyone up to the Day of Resurrection. The address keeps its historical audience. The meaning leaves the address behind. It settles on every soul that will ever be audited. So the Day here is not an event on a family calendar. It is the name of the accounting itself. Mujāhid reads the opening as the guarding of one's own Day. The discipline is the one the book's caller teaches those who call morning and evening. The reason the guarding comes first is simple. The accounting will be exact whether or not anyone prepared for it. The effect is a Day named by its refusals. The refusals are one refusal wearing three coats. Nothing moves between the accounts.

**THREE REFUSALS IN ONE BREATH**

The verse names the refusals one by one. First, the refusal of help. No help passes from soul to soul. Second, the refusal of price: ***“No ransom will be taken, no intercession accepted”***. The parallel address states the same block in its own shape (2:48 — **“Guard yourselves against the Day on which no soul will be of help to another. No intercession will be accepted,”**). Third, the refusal of every remaining remedy: ***“and no help will be given”***. The block is total. The parallel of the ransom states it harder still (2:48 — **“no ransom taken, and no help will be given”**). In the record of Ibn Abī Ḥātim the report of Ibn ʿAbbās marks the verse as running to the end of the days. No generation folds into another's account. Every ordinary hope of the guilty closes in one line. No borrowed merit. No rigged verdict. No adjournment. The difference between this accounting and the world's courts is not severity. It is completeness. The kindness and the severity of the verse are the same statement read twice. The wronged are also on this ledger. The swiftness finally serves them.

**THE ACCOUNT THAT STANDS ALONE**

The verse closes where it opened. The guarding is personal because the accounting is personal. Not the family's earning. Not the crowd's. The horizon note from Ibn ʿAbbās makes the same point from the other direction. The verse runs to the Day of Resurrection precisely so that no one can hide inside a generation. The Day is neither collective nor negotiable. There is a strange privacy about the end. The nearest people to a person on that Day are the very people the verse just named. The only currency is what the hands and hearts have actually made. For the modern reader this age offers many soft ledgers. Reputations. Narratives. The comfort of being on the right side of history. The verse answers all of them with one accounting shape. The earned stays with the earner. The reader is being taught to stand alone on the account now. The standing is still cheap. The openings for making are still open. The reason the verse keeps returning to the Day is that the remembering is the preparation. The account stands alone. The getting ready happens here. The guarding has a shape and the shape is attention. Attention to the Day named in the first line. Attention to the refusals named in the middle. Attention to the account named at the end. The soul learns to stand alone early. Then the standing at the end costs nothing. That is the wisdom of the verse's order. The guard is worn now. The account is opened later. Between the two lies the only stretch of time the person controls. The earned remains. The given-back remains. Everything else in the sentence is subtraction. No help. No ransom. No intercession. The subtraction is not cruelty. It is clarity. The courts of the world blur the ledger with favours and faces. This court does neither. The reason the verse can promise no injustice on the Day is that the Day has no back rooms. The ruling is public. The paying is exact. The mindfulness named at the opening is the only currency that gains value before the account is opened. Spend it now.
