**LIFE DEATH AND PERFECT SIGHT**

***“is Allah who gives life and causes death. And Allah is All-Seeing of what you do”*** The rejection of faithless counsel establishes how the community should process grief. The believer's losses processed at the creed's own desk. The term written (145), the places appointed (154), the ownership confessed. Sahih Muslim preserves the Prophet's instruction not to surrender to if only after a loss, but to affirm Allah's decree because counterfactual obsession opens a door for Satan. The report supplies an exact operating principle for the verse. The counterfactual court dissolved at its first convening. While the next verse completes the re-pricing. The deaths the formula dreads audited against the forgiveness they purchase. His forgiveness and mercy are far better than whatever wealth those who stay behind accumulate. Imagine a family repeating that a traveller would have lived at home, although death does not wait for an address. In connection with the counsel of disbelief rejected, Ibn ʿAbbās rejects the hypocritical claim that remaining in Madinah could prevent death and locates life and death under Allah’s complete sight. The forbidden statement turns a painful event into a false claim of control; which means staying home is imagined to guarantee life, while travel or battle is treated as the independent maker of death. Allah answers by locating both life and death under His rule; therefore human choices still matter, but they do not own the appointed term. The claim also harms the speaker because repeated counterfactual blame settles as agony in the heart; in other words grief that could lead to prayer and patient care instead becomes an endless trial of an unreal past. In current life, bereaved families may hear cruel suggestions that one ordinary decision alone created a death; hence responsible causes should be faced where they exist, but no person should claim mastery over what only Allah gives and takes. His perfect sight includes the journey, the intention, the means, and the end, so judgment does not depend on the survivor’s speculation, because the practical demand of travel and battle under divine decree follows from it.
