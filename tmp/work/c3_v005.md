**COMPLETE DIVINE KNOWLEDGE**

***“Surely nothing on earth or in”*** establishes complete divine knowledge. This verse establishes a foundational attribute of Allah: nothing in the heavens or on earth is hidden from Him. His knowledge encompasses the visible and invisible, the public and private, the past and future, the smallest movement and the largest event. Nothing is lost in the vastness of creation and nothing is obscured by darkness, distance, or secrecy. The verse follows the discussion of revelation and rejection. People may conceal their motives, distort scripture, or deny signs, but their outward claims do not conceal the truth from Allah. He knows what a person says, what he means, what he understands, and what he deliberately refuses to acknowledge. Human knowledge depends on observation, memory, teachers, instruments, and time. Allah’s knowledge depends on nothing. He knew creation before bringing it into existence, knows it while it exists, and knows its final outcome. His knowledge does not increase because He learns something new, nor does it diminish through forgetfulness. This is part of the perfection of tawḥīd. Allah is not a limited observer within the universe. He is the Creator and Lord of the universe, and created reality cannot conceal itself from its Creator. The Qur’an therefore repeatedly joins knowledge to authority and judgement: the One who knows every deed is the One before whom every deed will be accounted for. People are often able to manage appearances. A person may speak religiously while seeking status, appear generous while desiring praise, or condemn injustice while benefiting from it privately. (6:59 — **“With Him are the keys of the unseen—no one knows them except Him.”**)

**KNOWLEDGE WITHOUT DEPENDENCE**

***“the heavens is hidden from Allah”*** deepens knowledge without dependence. Allah knows the hidden motive. The Prophet ﷺ said that actions are judged by intentions, and this hadith is recorded in al-Bukhārī and Muslim. This does not mean that people should claim to know the unseen contents of another person’s heart. Human beings judge what is apparent and leave ultimate judgement to Allah. It does mean that each servant must examine his own heart and remember that Allah is not deceived by performance. The verse is also a source of comfort. An oppressed person whose pain is ignored by institutions, or whose testimony is hidden by powerful people, is not unseen by Allah. Every injury, lost opportunity, and silent act of endurance is known. The absence of immediate justice does not mean the absence of divine knowledge. Likewise, the quiet good deed that no one praises is preserved with Allah. A private prayer, an unnoticed act of charity, or restraint from a sin may be more valuable than public achievement. The believer works for Allah’s sight rather than the unstable approval of people. Because Allah knows everything, no one can excuse deliberate wrongdoing by saying, No one saw. The Qur’an’s moral law reaches private conduct as well as public life. A trustworthy person is trustworthy when unobserved; a truthful person remains truthful when lying would be profitable. Awareness of divine knowledge should produce murāqabah, the vigilant awareness that Allah observes the servant. (20:7 — **“Whether you speak openly ˹or not˺,”**)

**THE INNER LIFE MATTERS**

This awareness is not paranoia. It is a steady spiritual presence that encourages sincerity and protects from hidden corruption. Think of hidden motives as writing sealed in an envelope to every human observer: no seal or distance hides them from Allah. Today, private messages, hidden accounts, and carefully managed reputations remain fully open to divine knowledge. Because complete divine knowledge opens into knowledge without dependence, the inner life matters joins complete divine knowledge to knowledge without dependence. Since the inner life matters develops complete divine knowledge, knowledge without dependence carries the transition. Comfort for the oppressed matters, which means the inner life matters answers knowledge without dependence. Knowledge without dependence gives practical form to complete divine knowledge; comfort for the oppressed sets its horizon. Complete divine knowledge stays bound to the inner life matters because knowledge without dependence supplies the human setting of complete divine knowledge. Comfort for the oppressed carries complete divine knowledge into conduct shaped by the inner life matters. Every concealed motive remains fully present to the One who judges.
