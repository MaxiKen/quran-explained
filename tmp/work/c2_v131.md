**TWO WORDS OF THE SUBMISSION**

The verse is one of the shortest in the Book. It works like a hinge. ***“When his Lord ordered him”***. The command itself is barely spoken. ***““Submit ˹to My Will˺,” he responded”***. The answer arrives before the sentence ends. Ibn ʿAbbās compresses the whole theology into the reply: *"A Muslim boy to a Muslim father; he said: 'I submit to the Lord of all worlds.'"* The submission here is not a decision made after deliberation. It is an answer given while the command is still in the air. So the verse measures the religion by its response time. The reason the order names His Lord rather than a set of rules is that the command has a face. The submitting is to the face. The first half of the verse is the pattern of the Book in miniature. An order. A Lord. A person who says the word before the echo has faded. The parallel states the same word as the entry itself (6:161 — **“Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Surely my Lord has guided me to the Straight Path, a perfect way, the faith of Abraham, the upright,”**).

**THE REPLY THAT NAMES THE LORD**

The answer inside the verse is a full creed in six words. ***““I submit to the Lord of all worlds.””***. In the report of Ibn ʿAbbās the response is a formula that travels. The father says it. The son says it. The saying itself is the inheritance. The same transmission names the two speakers of the scene. A Muslim boy answering a Muslim father. The verse holds two generations in one breath and gives both the same word. The effect is that the creed arrives as a reply. It does not arrive as a thesis. The Lord is named inside the submitting. The submitting knows whose it is. The difference between a surrender without a name and a submission to the Lord of the worlds is the difference between despair and religion. The reason the verse puts the response in the past tense is worth noticing. He responded. The answering has already happened. The verse reads it back as a completed act. Every later order of the same Lord is met the same way. The first word. Then the life. The summons to the community states the same word as its entry rule (2:208 — **“O believers! Enter into Islam wholeheartedly and do not follow Satan’s footsteps. Surely he is your sworn enemy”**).

**THE RELIGION WITHOUT A CENTURY**

The verse closes on a name that outlives every clock. The Lord of all worlds. The report of Ibn ʿAbbās leaves the response standing where it was given. At the moment of the order. The early tradition marks it as the first submission of the first family. The point is that the religion in this verse has no century. The order and the answer are the same in every age. The Lord of the worlds does not age out of His own lordship. For the modern reader the portability is the challenge. This age rewrites its answers by the decade and calls the rewriting maturity. The verse answers an order with a word that has never needed updating. The parallel's command closes the same circle. The first to submit. The rest told to do the same. What follows is that the submission is not a mood of surrender. It is a named act with a named Lord. The naming is what keeps the act from becoming mere resignation. The reason the verse is short is that the answer is short. The religion was never meant to take longer than the responding. The response time is the verse's quiet measurement. The order is still in the air. The answer has already landed. A faith that waits for the echo is a different faith from this one. The naming of the Lord inside the answer keeps the submission from drifting. It is not surrender to the weather. It is submitting to the Lord of all worlds. The father-son pair of the report shows the word passing across a table. The passing is the whole method of the religion. One generation says it. The next repeats it. The repeating is not laziness. It is inheritance. For a reader now the six words stand at the door of every decision. The door is the same in every century. The order arrives. The answer names the Lord. The life follows the answer. The shortness of the verse is its own instruction. Do not decorate the answer. Give it.
