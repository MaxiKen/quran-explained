It does not void elsewhere in the Qur’an’s charter. The two verses adjudicate the same rule from two sides. Every era’s people judged by the revelation binding in their era. And once the final sending arrives and reaches a person , the binding revelation is this one. The verse’s sentence operating exactly at that boundary, neither before it nor beyond it. Nor is the sentence tribal. Islam in its clause is the substance. The submission with its creed and covenant. Not the demographic label. The label-holder without the substance stands warned by the whole argument , and the substance-seeker is met by the Ways own openness. Whoever submits themselves to Allah and does good will have their reward with their Lord . The sentence published, the chapter turns to its hardest file. Those who held the substance and abandoned it. How will Allah guide a people who disbelieved after they had believed.
