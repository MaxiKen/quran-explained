The three descriptions identify different effects of the same revelation; which means it explains matters plainly to people in general, guides those willing to follow, and warns the mindful in a way that deepens their guard. The difference is not a defect in the message; therefore it lies in the response of the hearer. The lesson then continues beyond one battle, teaching every mindful community to read setbacks through revelation rather than through panic, pride, or the verdict of an enemy. Explanation becomes guidance when its clear account is allowed to govern the next decision.
