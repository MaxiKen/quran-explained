**SATAN’S SLIP THROUGH EARLIER EARNINGS**

***“Indeed, those ˹believers˺ who fled on the day when the two”*** Indeed, those ˹believers˺ who fled on the day when the two armies met were made to slip by Satan because of their misdeeds closes the flight-file with its causation-verdict. And the verdict's anatomy is the passage's most careful jurisprudence. Inna lladhina tawallaw minkum yawma ltaqa l-jam'an. Those of you who turned away on the day the two hosts met. The file's subjects named as minkum. Of you. Believers. The runners of the passes. Innama stazallahumu sh-shaytan bi-ba'di ma kasabu. Satan only caused them to slip by some of what they had earned. The causation-chain the verdict's doctrine. The flight not filed as hypocrisy, nor as decree's raw imposition, but as istizlal. The enemy's engineered slip. Purchased, the bi-clause insists, by prior earnings. Some of what they had earned. The day's collapse rooted in the season's accumulated faults (the spoils-love the hill documented. The small disobediences that thin the heart's garrison before the great hour tests it). The sin-to-sin mechanics this Book publishes as standing law. Satan's purchase on the believer being never arbitrary. The footholds sold to him, transaction by prior transaction, in the quiet seasons before the fields. (3:154 — **“while others were disturbed by evil thoughts about Allah—the”**)

**FLIGHT ON THE DAY OF COLLISION**

***“armies met were made to slip by Satan because of their misdeeds.”*** But Allah has pardoned them. Surely Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Forbearing delivers the file's closing entries. Wa laqad afa llahu anhum. And Allah has certainly pardoned them. The afw's second publication. The runners' amnesty stated as accomplished fact in the eternal text. The community's worst hour closed, by revelation's own hand, at clemency (the pardoned never to be re-prosecuted by the community's tongues. Ibn Umar's cited usage of this clause standing as the jurisprudence). Inna llaha ghafurun halim. Surely Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Forbearing. The seal's pairing the file's exact medicine. Al-Ghafur over the flight's guilt, and al-Halim. The Forbearing. Over its process. The Lord who did not seize the runners at the passes, nor the earners at their earlier earnings, but bore, scheduled, and pardoned. The hilm-name being the verse's deepest teaching about the whole causation-chain. The same forbearance that let the faults accumulate unpunished held the punishment off the collapse they purchased. Mercy operating at both ends of the mechanism. (3:156 — **“O believers! Do not be like the unfaithful”**) Flight on the day of collision has present-day force because past sins can create openings for later collapse, yet sincere repentance prevents one failure from becoming a permanent identity.

**PARDON JOINED TO FORBEARANCE**

***“But Allah has pardoned them. Surely Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Forbearing”*** This verdict guides a believer examining a personal lapse. The verse's chain read backward as self-audit. The great collapse traced to the small earnings (the garrison thinned before the wall broke). Whence the discipline. The minor faults serviced at their season precisely because they are the enemy's future footholds. And the chain read forward as the community's mercy-manual. The fallen brother's file processed at the verse's own order. The causation understood (the slip engineered, the purchase prior), the pardon published, the names guarded. While the passage now turns to the whisperers' counter-theology of death. The next warning rejects people who blame a brother's death on travel or absence from home. Imagine a healed fracture that remains vulnerable at one point but grows strong through careful recovery. Regarding satan’s slip through earlier earnings, Ibn ʿAbbās connects the retreat at Uhud to Satan’s exploitation of earlier disobedience and then stresses Allah’s pardon joined to forbearance. The verse traces an outward retreat to an earlier inward vulnerability; which means satan made them slip through some of what they had earned, which means neglected wrongs can weaken a person before a greater test arrives. This does not give Satan independent control or remove human responsibility; therefore his opening was connected to their deeds. The announced pardon then prevents the diagnosis from becoming hopelessness; in other words allah knew the flight, named its cause, and still forgave. His forbearance explains why punishment did not seize them at the first failure and why time for repair remained; hence a believer should examine smaller habits before a crisis exposes their weight. Private compromise, delayed repentance, or repeated neglect may affect public courage later; this is why the proper response is not to declare a fallen believer permanently false. It is to close the opening through repentance, rebuild obedience, and trust the pardon Allah has made known.
