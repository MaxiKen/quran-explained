**RECITATION THAT BEGINS REFORM**

***“Indeed, Allah has done the believers a”*** Indeed, Allah has done the believers a ˹great˺ favour by raising a messenger from among them turns the final account-passage to its foundation-stone. And the turning's logic is the passage's studied mercy. After the census's degrees and before the wound's final accounting, revelation re-anchors the community in the favour beneath all its files. Laqad manna llahu alal-mu'minina idh ba'atha fihim rasulan min anfusihim. Allah has surely conferred favour on the believers in raising among them a messenger from their own selves. The mann-word. The conferral. (3:163 — **“They ˹each˺ have varying degrees in the sight of Allah.”**) ***“˹great˺ favour by raising a messenger from among”*** The sending itself the summit-grant. And the min-anfusihim clause the grant's nearness-mercy. The clause carrying, at this passage's station, its specific consolation. The community bleeding from its worst week is reminded that its wounded commander. The one bled at their failure, calling at their backs, lenient past their desertion. Is himself the favour. The week's whole grief transacted around the grant's own person. Uhud re-read, one more time, as a season inside the mercy, never outside it.

**FORMATION THROUGH BOOK AND WISDOM**

***“them—reciting to them His revelations, purifying them,”*** Reciting to them His revelations, purifying them, and teaching them the Book and wisdom itemises the favour's operations. The three-fold curriculum. The recitation (the words delivered exact), the tazkiyah, and the Book-and-wisdom. The operations' listing at this station being the final account’s frame. The community's failures (the hill, the passes) were failures inside a curriculum. Students' lapses under a Teacher still teaching. The file processed as formation, never as abandonment. ***“and teaching them the Book and wisdom. For”*** For indeed they had previously been clearly astray closes the favour-verse with the before-photograph. Wa in kanu min qablu lafi dalalin mubin. Though they had been, before, in manifest error. And the photograph's function is gratitude's calibration. The community's self-audit (the passage's whole business) conducted against the right baseline. Not the angels' record (the ranks' failures measured from perfection would crush) but their own before. The Jahiliyyah's pit. From which the favour's operations had carried them to a community that, at its worst week, still held its Prophet alive, its ranks re-formable, its pardon published, and its next morning's march musterable. (3:165 — **“Why is it when you suffered casualties ˹at Uḥud˺—although”**) The book and wisdom taught remains urgent today because teachers serve people best by reciting revelation, cultivating character, and joining knowledge to practical wisdom.

**CLEAR ERROR LEFT BEHIND**

***“indeed they had previously been clearly astray”*** The distance from the pit the favour's true measure. A reader shaped by a great favour from among themselves and the verse carries its permanent audit-discipline. Every era's and every soul's failures weighed with the before-photograph in the other hand. The mann remembered at the wound's own table. While the accounting now closes at its cause. Why is it when you suffered casualties. You protested 'How could this be.' Say. It is because of your disobedience. Imagine a teacher from the students own community who reads the lesson, cleans the practice, and explains its wise use. Concerning a great favour from among themselves, Ibn ʿAbbās identifies the favour as an Arab Messenger of known lineage who recites revelation, purifies from idolatry, and teaches lawful and unlawful wisdom. The favour is described through a complete work of formation; which means recitation gives the community Allah’s words, purification removes the beliefs and habits that corrupt life, and teaching joins the Book to wisdom in practice. None of the three can be safely discarded; therefore recitation without moral change leaves knowledge unfruitful, while zeal without the Book loses its measure. Wisdom shows how revealed truth is carried into judgment and conduct; in other words the Messenger being from among the believers also made his language, lineage, and daily example known to them, leaving no remote or unknowable teacher between the message and its first hearers. Remembering their earlier error protects this education from pride; hence a community lifted by revelation must not speak as though it educated itself. Present teachers serve the same pattern only in a limited way: convey accurately, cultivate character, and show sound use; this is why the favour remains Allah’s, and every benefit from it should return as gratitude and faithful learning.
