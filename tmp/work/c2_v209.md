**SLIPPING IS OFTEN GRADUAL**

A slip is not the same as a final fall. The Qur’an repeatedly calls those who sin to repent and repair. Allah’s might is joined elsewhere to His forgiveness, and His wisdom includes giving servants opportunities to return. The person who notices his deviation should stop, seek refuge from Satan, confess the wrong, restore rights, and resume the path. Repentance should not be delayed because a person feels unworthy. Satan wants the sin followed by despair. Allah wants the servant to return. The warning of this verse and the mercy of the next passages should be read together. Believers should not take pleasure in another person’s slip. They advise, protect, and encourage return while maintaining necessary boundaries. Teachers should explain proof clearly, families should support those trying to change, and institutions should create routes for correction rather than rewarding concealment. This does not mean ignoring harm. Justice may require accountability, and protecting victims comes first. But accountability should aim at truth and reform, not permanent humiliation. Allah is both Mighty and Wise, and believers seek to reflect those qualities in their response.
