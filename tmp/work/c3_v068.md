**THE CLAIM ESTABLISHED BY FOLLOWING**

***“Indeed, those who have the best claim to Abraham”*** Indeed, those who have the best claim to Abraham are his followers, this Prophet, and the believers awards the estate the verdict of the surrounding passage cleared. And the awards criterion is its doctrine. Awla, the best claim, the nearest title. Assigned not by blood (the contests assumed currency) but by following. His followers. Those of his own age and after who walked his actual way. The inclining, the submitting, the association of nothing. Then this Prophet. Muhammad, peace be upon him, named as the successions centre. The restorer of the patriarchs House and qiblah , his law the hanif-religions completion. And the believers his community. The practising muslims of every land enrolled in the patriarchs line by the only registrar the verse recognises. Resemblance in religion. The award executes, formally, the rule this argument fixed at its foundation. My covenant is not extended to the wrongdoers. The covenant travels by conduct. And the Prophet stated the awards principle as standing doctrine. Every prophet has guardians from among the prophets. My guardian among them is my father, the friend of my Lord. And he recited this verse. With the wider rule sounded across the Sunnah. Whoever is slowed by his deeds is not sped by his lineage . (3:67 — **“Abraham was neither a Jew nor a Christian;”**) Reading the claim established by following beside the prophet and the believers makes divine guardianship answerable within the claim established by following. Considering the claim established by following together with the prophet and the believers shows why the claim established by following must govern action affecting divine guardianship.

**THE PROPHET AND THE BELIEVERS**

***“are his followers, this Prophet, and the believers.”*** And Allah is the Guardian of those who believe seals the award with the wilayah-clause. And its placement over this estate is the verse’s finest stroke, the wording shows. The contested inheritance of Ibrahim was never, at its heart, a genealogical trophy but a relationship. Ibrahim was the khalil the intimate friend of Allah. To claim his estate is to claim proximity to his Lord. And the closing clause names where that proximity actually resides. Allah is the Wali Guardian, Patron, intimate Protector of those who believe. The believers of the verse’s award-list holding, in this clause, the estates true substance. The Friends friendship, extended to whoever carries the Friends religion. While the blood-claimants without the following hold documents to an intimacy they never entered. (3:69 — **“Some of the People of the Book wish to mislead you ˹believers˺.”**) Today, the prophet and the believers matters because admiration for Abraham becomes credible only when a person follows his worship, trust, and rejection of idols. Reading the prophet and the believers beside divine guardianship makes the claim established by following answerable within the prophet and the believers. Considering the prophet and the believers together with divine guardianship shows why the prophet and the believers must govern action affecting the claim established by following. In the prophet and the believers, belief is tested when divine guardianship guides choices about the claim established by following. The prophet and the believers: therefore the claim established by following joins divine guardianship before Allah.

**DIVINE GUARDIANSHIP**

***“And Allah is the Guardian of those who believe”*** The verse’s permanent account, the context shows, faces its own award-holders first. The community named in the grant the believers. Holds the Ibrahim-estate by the grants own criterion. Following. And the title lapses where the following lapses. The Muslim who claims the patriarch while abandoning the patriarchs substance. The tawhid uncompromised, the submission at first command , the readiness to stake the dearest. Has re-entered the very contest this verse closed, on the losing sides terms. Claiming by label what is awarded only by likeness. The estate settled, the chapter turns to the disinherited claimants response. The campaign to mislead , the rejected signs , the mixed and hidden truth. The file of those who, losing the patriarch, moved to unsettle his awarded heirs. Reading divine guardianship beside the claim established by following makes the prophet and the believers answerable within divine guardianship. Considering divine guardianship together with the claim established by following shows why divine guardianship must govern action affecting the prophet and the believers. In divine guardianship, belief is tested when the claim established by following guides choices about the prophet and the believers. Divine guardianship: therefore the prophet and the believers joins the claim established by following before Allah. Since the prophet and the believers prepares for divine guardianship, its consequence appears when the claim established by following governs a real decision. The claim established by following has this consequence because the prophet and the believers follows it: divine guardianship becomes visible within the claim established by following. Since divine guardianship tests the claim established by following, the reader must carry the prophet and the believers into a real decision. The claim established by following therefore uses the prophet and the believers to judge divine guardianship. The claim established by following therefore moves toward the prophet and the believers, making divine guardianship an accountable concern. Imagine an inheritance awarded to the child who keeps the founder’s trust rather than the stranger who only carries his surname. This comparison clarifies divine guardianship without reducing the prophet and the believers to the image. On the claim established by following, Ibn ʿAbbās identifies Abraham’s true heirs as his faithful contemporaries, Muhammad, and believers who follow the same revealed religion under Allah’s protection.
