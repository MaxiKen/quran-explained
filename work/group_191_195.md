## Verse 195

The *hijrah* — the migration — is the first: the *hajaru* — they migrated — leaving the land of shirk for the land of faith — leaving behind loved ones, brethren, friends and neighbors (Ibn Kathir); the *ukhriju* — they were expelled — is the second: the *min diyarihim* — from their homes — the Mushriks tormented them and forced them to migrate; the *udhu fi sabil* — they were harmed in My cause — is the third: "for their only wrong, to the people, was that they believed in Allah Alone" (Ibn Kathir; 60:1 parallel); the *qatalu wa qutilu* — they fought and were killed — is the fourth: the *qitāl* — the fighting — the *qatl* — the death. The verse's *sabili* — My cause — is the frame: the *hijrah* — the migration — the *ukhrij* — the expulsion — the *adha* — the harm — the *qitāl* — the fight — the *qatl* — the death — all in the *sabil* — the cause — of the divine.

"I will certainly forgive their sins and admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow, as a reward from Allah" — *la'ukaffiranna 'anhum sayyi'atihim wa la'udkhilannahum jannatin tajri min tahtiha al-anhar* — I will certainly expiate their evil deeds and admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow. The *takfir* — the expiation — is the first: the *la'ukaffiranna* — I will certainly expiate — the *sayyi'at* — the evil deeds; the *dukhul* — the admission — is the second: the *la'udkhilanna* — I will certainly admit — the *jannat* — the Gardens — the *tajri min tahtiha al-anhar* — under which rivers flow. Ibn Kathir: "I will expiate from them their evil deeds and admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow (in Paradise)." Al-Jalalayn: "I shall surely absolve of their evil deeds, concealing these with forgiveness, and I shall admit them to Gardens underneath which rivers flow" — a *thawab* — a reward — a verbal noun reaffirming the import of *la-ukaffiranna* — from God (with the shift of person). The verse's *takfir* — the expiation — and the *dukhul* — the admission — are the two: the *sayyi'at* — the sins — expiated — the *jannat* — the Gardens — the *anhar* — the rivers — the reward — of the *hijrah* — the migration's reward.

"And with Allah is the finest reward!" — *wa Allahu 'indahu husnu al-thawab* — and Allah has with Him the finest reward. The *husn* — the finest, the best — is the divine: the *'indahu* — with Him — the *husn al-thawab* — the best reward — the *husn* — the excellence — of the *thawab* — the reward. Ibn Kathir: "and with Allah is the best of rewards." Al-Jalalayn: "with Him is the fairest reward — the fairest requital." The verse's *husn al-thawab* — the finest reward — is the best: the *'inda Allah* — with Allah — the *husn* — the finest — the *thawab* — the reward — the best — of the rewards.

The verse's context: the acceptance (195) of the *du'a'* (191–194) — the *istijabah* — the response — the *'amal* — the work — the *dhakaran aw untha* — the male and the female — the *hijrah* — the migration — the *ukhrij* — the expulsion — the *adha* — the harm — the *qitāl* — the fight — the *qatl* — the death — the *takfir* and the *dukhul* — the expiation and the admission — the *husn al-thawab* — the finest reward. Ibn Kathir: "Allah Accepts the Supplication of Men of Understanding."

For the reader, the verse is the acceptance of the supplication and the doctrine of the equal reward. The believer is taught: the *istajaba* — the response — of the *rabb* — the Lord — to the *du'a'* — the supplication — the *la udī'u 'amal' — the not-wasting — the *'amal* — the work — the *'amil* — the worker — the *dhakaran aw untha* — male or female — the *ba'dukum min ba'd* — the one from the other — the equality — of the reward — the occasion of Umm Salamah — the *hijrah* — the migration — of the women — mentioned. And the *hajaru* — the migrated — the *ukhriju* — the expelled — the *udhu fi sabil* — the harmed — the *qatalu* — the fought — the *qutilu* — the killed — the description of the faithful; and the *takfir* — the expiation — the *sayyi'at* — the sins — the *dukhul* — the admission — the *jannat* — the Gardens — the *anhar* — the rivers — the *thawab* — the reward — from the divine — the *husn al-thawab* — the finest. The believer's program: the *hijrah* — the migration — from the *shirk* — the *ukhrij* — the expulsion — the *adha* — the harm — the *sabil* — the cause — the *qitāl* — the fighting — the *qatl* — the martyrdom — the *takfir* — the expiation — the *jannah* — the Garden — the *husn al-thawab* — the finest reward — the promise — of the divine — accepted.
