- Al-Jalalayn, tafsir of 2:106 — "cause it to be forgotten" as "erase from your heart"; the alternative reading *nunsi'ha* (postpone); "better" = more beneficial, easier to implement, or with greater reward. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-106-sora-2.html
