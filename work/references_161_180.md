- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:161–162 — those who disbelieve and remain in that state until death; the eternal curse until the Day of Resurrection and after that in the Fire of Jahannam; the punishment neither lightened nor reprieved — continuous and eternal; the permissibility of cursing the disbelievers as a whole (with the scholars' distinction regarding cursing a specific disbeliever whose end is unknown). https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-161-sora-2.html
- Ibn Kathir (Abridged), tafsir of 2:163 — Allah is the only deity, no partners or equals; the Sustainer; ar-Rahman ar-Rahim; the report on Allah's Greatest Name in 2:163 and 2:255. https://quran.com/en/2:163/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:164 — the signs: creation, night and day, ships, rain reviving the dead earth, living creatures, winds veering, clouds held between sky and earth; proofs for people of understanding (cf. Ma'arif on 42:33 — the stillness of the ships). https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-164-sora-2.html
- Tafhim al-Qur'an (Maududi), commentary on 2:164–167 — the signs for those who use their reason; the definition of setting up a rival to God; those followed disown their followers; the angels' declaration of innocence (cf. 28:63); the followers' wish for a second chance and its falsity (they would return to what they were prohibited from, cf. 6:28); Allah showing them their deeds as regrets. https://islamicstudies.info/tafheem.php?sura=2&verse=164&to=167 and https://www.alim.org/tafsir/ibn-kathir/2/
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:168–169 — the order to eat lawful and pure things; the prohibition of following the footsteps of Shaitan; Qatadah and as-Suddi: every act of disobedience is among the footsteps of Shaitan; Ibn Abbas: any angry vow or oath; the man of the long journey with unlawful food, drink and clothes — how can his supplication be accepted (Muslim, at-Tirmidhi); the Bahirah, Sa'ibah, Wasilah, Ham of the Jahiliyyah; Shaitan commands the evil, the shamelessness, and saying about Allah without knowledge — includes every innovator and disbeliever. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-168-sora-2.html and https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/2/168/
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:170–171 — Ibn Ishaq from Ibn Abbas: revealed about a group of Jews whom the Messenger called to Islam; "We follow what we found our forefathers following"; the parable of the wandering animals; deaf (do not hear the truth), dumb (do not utter it), blind (do not recognize its path); sound understanding and guidance lacking. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-170-sora-2.html and https://quran.com/al-baqarah/171/tafsirs
- Ibn Kathir (Abridged), tafsir of 2:173 — the four prohibitions; dead animals (strangulation, blow, fall, goring, being partly eaten by a wild animal); the prohibition includes swine fat; offerings to other than Allah; the permission in emergency (mudtar) without willful disobedience and without transgression, when the lawful is not available; the hadith of 'Abbad ibn Shurahbil regarding the garden (Ibn Majah); Sa'id ibn Jubayr and Masruq on the necessity. https://quran.com/2:173/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi), tafsir of 2:173 — the extent of the prohibitions (dead animal, blood, swine flesh, and what is slaughtered for other than Allah); the consensus on the slaughter in anyone's name other than Allah; the conditions of the mudtar (life in danger, no lawful alternative, certainty of benefit, no desire, minimum needed); the distinction between the exception and the making lawful — the forbidden remains forbidden, the sin is forgiven. https://quran.com/en/2:173/tafsirs/en-tafsir-maarif-ul-quran
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:174–176 — concealing what Allah sent down of the Book and purchasing a small gain (the joys of this earthly life); Ibn Abbas: revealed about Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf, Huyyay ibn Akhtab and their like; they consumed nothing but fire; Allah will not speak to them nor purify them; "That is because Allah has sent down the Book in truth" — the concealment as the trading of guidance for misguidance and forgiveness for punishment; the perseverance toward the Fire; the difference over the Book (Al-Jalalayn: believing in parts and disbelieving in others) as schism. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-174-sora-2.html and https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-176-sora-2.html
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:177 — facing the east or the west does not necessitate righteousness unless legislated by Allah; the faith (Allah, the Last Day, the angels, the Books, the prophets); giving wealth in spite of love for it to the kinsfolk, orphans, the poor, the wayfarer, the askers, and for freeing slaves; establishing the prayer and paying the zakah; keeping the covenant; the patient in suffering, adversity and battle; those are the truthful and the pious. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-177-sora-2.html
- Ibn Kathir (Abridged), tafsir of 2:178 — the wisdom behind the law of equality; the Children of Israel not allowed to pardon in return for blood money; the Muslim Ummah allowed the penalty or the pardon and the diyah (Ibn 'Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr, Muqatil, ar-Rabi' bin Anas); "sought in a good manner" and "paid to him respectfully" — the claimant without harassment, the payer without harm or resistance. https://quran.com/al-baqarah/178/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi), tafsir of 2:177–179 — qisas literally has the meaning of likeness; the law negated the pre-Islamic demand of killing an innocent free man for a slave or a man for a woman; the killer is the one killed; the concession and mercy of the blood-money and pardon; the transgression after that (false case, post-pardon re-opening) severely punished; "there is life in the qisas" — the deterrent; the verse addressed to people of reason. https://quran.com/en/2:178/tafsirs/en-tafsir-maarif-ul-quran and https://islamicstudies.info/quran/maarif.php?sura=2&verse=177&to=182
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:180 — the command to include parents and relatives in the will was obligatory before the inheritance verses; abrogated by 4:7 (Ibn 'Abbas via Ibn Abi Hatim; Qatadah, as-Suddi, Muqatil, Tawus, Ibrahim an-Nakha'i, Shurayh, ad-Dahhak, az-Zuhri); no will for a deserving heir; the will for qualifying (non-inheriting) relatives recommended up to a third; the will should observe justice (no injustice against the qualified inheritors, no extravagance or stinginess); Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas' hadith regarding the bequest of the third. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-180-sora-2.html
