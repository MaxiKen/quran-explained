## References

- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim* (Al-Mubarakpuri abridgement), on 3:21–22 — "Chastising the Jews for Their Disbelief and for Killing the Prophets and Righteous People": the People of the Book's denials; prophets killed "without cause"; "kibr is refusing the truth and degrading people" (the Prophet's statement); the sarcasm of "announce to them a painful torment"; the loss of deeds in this world and the Hereafter and the absence of helpers. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-21-sora-3.html
- Al-Jalalayn on 3:21 — the reported tradition of forty-three prophets killed and one hundred and seventy devout worshippers each killed in turn; the "good tidings" as sarcastic ridicule; the grammatical reading of *fa-bashshirhum*. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-21-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:23–25 — "Chastising the People of the Book for Not Referring to the Book of Allah for Judgment": the call to the Torah and Injil where Allah commanded them to follow Muhammad; the false claim that the Fire touches them only for a few days (a day per thousand years, seven days in the tradition); the self-deception of their invented creed; "How will it be when We gather them" — the questioning of the liars and the killers; the full payment and no injustice. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-23-sora-3.html
- Al-Jalalayn on 3:23 — the occasion: two Jews fornicated, asked the Prophet to adjudicate, he ruled stoning, they refused; the Torah was consulted, the same verdict found, the two stoned, the Jews became wrathful. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-23-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir + Al-Jalalayn on 3:26 – "Encouraging Gratitude": all sovereignty is Allah's; "You are the Giver, You are the Taker"; the refutation of those who thought they could decide for Allah (43:31–32, 6:124, 17:21); the transfer of prophethood from the Children of Israel to the Final Prophet; the Ummah's reach east and west; Al-Jalalayn: the hypocrites' "How preposterous!" at the promise of Persia and Byzantium; "in Your hand is good, that is, as well as evil" (reading); You are able to do all things. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-26-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:27 — the night entering into the day and the day into the night (the lengths exchanged, the seasons of the year); the living from the dead and the dead from the living; the provision without limit. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-26-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:28 — "The Prohibition of Supporting the Disbelievers": the prohibition of taking the disbelievers as comrades instead of the believers; "whoever does that… Allah will discard him"; the cross-references 60:1, 4:144, 5:51, 8:73; the exception *tuqyah* — outward friendship in fear, never inward; Abu ad-Darda's "we smile… although our hearts curse them" (al-Bukhari); Al-Hasan: "The Tuqyah is allowed until the Day of Resurrection"; Al-Jalalayn on the safeguard "in words but not in your hearts"; "warns you against Himself" — His anger and severe torment; "to Allah is the final return." https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-28-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:29–30 — "Allah Knows What the Hearts Conceal": the secrets and apparent matters; nothing, not even the weight of an atom, escapes His observation (75:13); the Day the deeds are brought before the servant; the wish of the great distance (43:38); "warns you against Himself" — against His punishment; Ra'uf — Al-Hasan al-Basri: "Allah is so kind with them that He warns them against Himself"; Maududi (note 27): "out of sheer goodwill." https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-29-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:31–32 — "Allah's Love is Attained by Following the Messenger": the verse judges against those who claim love without following the Shari'ah; "Whoever commits an act that does not conform with our matter, then it will be rejected of him" (Sahih); Al-Hasan al-Basri and the Salaf on the test of the claim; "what you will earn is much more than what you sought in loving Him"; the obedience command; "defiance of the Messenger's way constitutes Kufr"; even the claim of love does not avail without following the Final Messenger. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-31-sora-3.html
- Al-Jalalayn on 3:31 — the occasion: "We only worship idols out of our love for God that they might bring us close to Him"; the following and the reward; the forgiveness of previous sins for the one who follows. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-31-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:33–34 — "The Chosen Ones Among the People of the Earth": Adam (created with His Hand, breathed into him, angels prostrated, names taught, Paradise granted, then sent down out of wisdom); Nuh (first Messenger to the earth, his long call, the drowning); the household of Ibrahim (including the master of mankind and Final Prophet); the household of 'Imran (father of Maryam, mother of 'Isa); "Offspring, one of the other." https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-33-sora-3.html
- Maududi, *Tafhim al-Qur'an*, on 3:33 (notes 29–30) — the beginning of the second discourse, 9 A.H.; the delegation from Najran (seventy-three towns and villages; ~120,000 armed men; three chiefs — 'aqib, sayyid, usquf; Ibn Hisham and Ibn Ishaq cited); the alternatives of Islam or dhimma; 'Imran as the father of Musa and Harun, Amram in the Bible. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-33-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:35–36 — "The Story of Maryam's Birth": Hannah bint Faqudh; the bird feeding its chick; the supplication and the pregnancy; the vow of the *muharrar* for the service of Bayt al-Maqdis; 'Imran died while she was still pregnant; "the male is not like the female" in strength and service; the naming of the newborn on the day of birth (the Prophet's naming of Ibrahim; Anas's brother 'Abdullah; the 'aqiqah hadith — Ahmad, Sunan, sahih per at-Tirmidhi); the refuge for Maryam and her offspring ('Isa) from Shaytan; the hadith (Abd ar-Razzaq, Abu Hurayrah) that no newborn is touched by Satan except Maryam and her son. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-35-sora-3.html
- Al-Jalalayn on 3:35–36 — Hanna in old age; the consecration "liberated and delivered from the distractions of this world for the service of Your Holy House in Jerusalem"; 'Imran's death during the pregnancy. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-35-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir + Al-Jalalayn on 3:37 — "Maryam Grows Up; Her Honor is with Allah": the goodly acceptance; her excellent growth and character; the companionship of the righteous; Zakariyya's guardianship (husband of her maternal aunt per Ibn Ishaq and Ibn Jarir; the hadith "I saw John and 'Isa, who are maternal cousins"; the hadith "The maternal aunt is just like the mother" — Two Sahihs, the case of 'Amarah bint Hamzah); the mihrab and the out-of-season fruits (Mujahid, 'Ikrimah, Sa'id bin Jubayr, Ad-Dahhak, Qatadah, as-Suddi and others); the casting of quills at the River Jordan (Al-Jalalayn — twenty-nine priests, Zakariyya's quill floated); Maryam's reply — "It is from Allah… without limit." https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-37-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:38–40 — "The Supplication of Zakariyya, and the Good News of Yahya's Birth": the trigger (the out-of-season provision), the old prophet with feeble bones and gray hair, the barren wife, the secret call; "grant me from You a good offspring"; the angels' call while he stood in prayer in the mihrab; the name Yahya — "he lives" (Qatadah — filled with life by faith); "believing in the Word from Allah" = 'Isa (Ibn 'Abbas, Al-Hasan, Qatadah, 'Ikrimah, Mujahid, as-Suddi, ar-Rabi', Ad-Dahhak and others); *sayyid* (wise, noble, pious — the collected readings); *hasur* — immune from illegal sexual relations, not celibate (contra the reading); "a prophet from among the righteous" as the second, better good news (cf. 28:7); the "how" and the answer "Thus Allah does what He wills." https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-38-sora-3.html
