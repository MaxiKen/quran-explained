## References

- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim* (Al-Mubarakpuri abridgement), on 3:1–4 — "Which was revealed in Al-Madinah": the first eighty-three ayat relate to the delegation from Najran; the tafsir of Alif-Lam-Mim (cf. 2:1) and of the Ayat al-Kursi names (2:255); "sent down the Book with truth" — no doubt it is from Allah; "confirming what came before it" — the previous divinely revealed Books and the glad tidings of Muhammad's prophethood; the Torah and the Gospel; the Furqan (the Criterion, the Decisive Authority); the warning against those who reject the ayat. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-3-sora-3.html
- Al-Jalalayn on 3:1–4 — the Qur'an enveloped by the truth, with veracity in what it announces, confirming the previous Books; the Torah and the Gospel revealed in order; the Furqan as the guidance and the Criterion. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-3-sora-3.html
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi) on 3:1–6 — the opening of the surah; the Ever-Living, the One Who sustains all that exists; the revelation in truth with no doubt or suspicion. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-3-sora-3.html
- Tanwir al-Miqbas (Ibn 'Abbas) on 3:3–4 — Gabriel brought the Scripture with the truth, confirming the divine Oneness; the Torah revealed to Moses, the Gospel to Jesus. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-3-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:5–6 — nothing on earth or in the heavens is hidden from Allah; the shaping in the wombs as He wills; the hadith of the creation's stages (nutfah, 'alaqah, mudghah; the angel records the provision, deeds, term, and misery or happiness; the soul is breathed). https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-6-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:7 — "The Mutashabihat and Muhkamat Ayat": the muhkamat are the entirely clear foundations of the Book (the rulings, the allowed, the prohibited, the limits, the obligations); the mutashabihat are not entirely clear (abrogated ayat, parables, oaths, what is believed but not implemented); the deviant-hearted follow the mutashabih to alter its meanings; the hadith (al-Bukhari, Muslim, Abu Dawud, from 'A'ishah): "When you see those who seek the ambiguous verses in it, they are those whom Allah has named. Beware of them!"; the four types of tafsir (Ibn 'Abbas); "all of it is from our Lord" — both kinds are true and each testifies to the truth of the other. https://salimhannure.wordpress.com/2015/06/25/the-mutashabihat-and-muhkamat-ayat-tafsir-ibn-kathir/
- Ibn Kathir on 3:8–9 — "Do not deviate our hearts from the guidance after You allowed them to acquire it. Do not make us like those who follow the Mutashabih…"; "grant us mercy from You"; "Our Lord, Verily, it is You Who will gather mankind together on the Day about which there is no doubt." https://salimhannure.wordpress.com/2015/06/25/the-mutashabihat-and-muhkamat-ayat-tafsir-ibn-kathir/
- Ibn Kathir on 3:10–12 — the wealth and children of the disbelievers will not avail them against Allah (cf. 3:116, 2:24 — the fuel of the Fire); the people of Pharaoh and those before them rejected the signs and were seized for their sins; "Say to those who disbelieve: 'You will be defeated and gathered together to Hell.'" https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-11-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir on 3:13 — "In the two armies" — the two camps that met in combat at Badr; the Muslims fighting in the cause; the idolaters of Quraysh in disbelief; the disbelievers saw the Muslims as twice their number (the illusion that Allah made a factor of the victory); Ibn 'Abbas (Tanwir): 313 believers against Abu Sufyan's 950; Al-Jalalayn: the disbelievers numbered nearly a thousand; "Allah supports with His victory whom He wills"; a lesson for the people of insight. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-13-sora-3.html
- Ibn Kathir (and the classical notes) on 3:14–15 — the enjoyment of the worldly desires: women, children, heaped treasures of gold and silver, fine horses, cattle, and tilled land; the pleasures of the worldly life; "but with Allah is the finest destination"; the better news: Gardens with rivers beneath, staying forever, pure spouses, and Allah's pleasure; Allah is All-Seeing of the servants. https://legacy.quran.com/3/13-19
- Ibn Kathir on 3:16–17 — the mindful's description: "Our Lord, we have believed, so forgive us our sins and protect us from the punishment of the Fire"; the patient (al-sabirin), the truthful (al-sadiqin), the devoutly obedient (al-qanitin), the spenders (al-munfiqin), and the seekers of forgiveness before the dawns (al-mustaghfirin bi al-as-har). https://legacy.quran.com/3/13-19
- Ibn Kathir on 3:18 — "The Testimony of Tawhid": Allah bears witness — made clear through proofs and signs; the angels and those having knowledge bear witness; "this ayah emphasizes the great virtue of those who have knowledge"; "(He) maintains His creation in justice"; Al-Jalalayn: the testimony of the prophets and the believers through conviction and words. https://quran.com/ali-imran/19/tafsirs/en-tafisr-ibn-kathir
- Ibn Kathir on 3:19–20 — "Truly, the religion with Allah is Islam" (and the cross-reference 3:85: whoever seeks a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted of him); the People of the Scripture did not differ except out of rivalry after knowledge came to them; "say: I have submitted myself to Allah, and so have those who follow me"; "say to those given the Scripture and the illiterates (Arab pagans): Do you also submit yourselves?"; if they turn away, your duty is only to convey the Message — their reckoning is with Allah. https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/3/19/
- Maududi, *Tafhim al-Qur'an* on 3:19 (notes on Islam) — the religion with Allah is the surrender (Islam); the contention that the Qur'an confirms the original revealed fragments of the Torah and the Gospel, not the later human interpolations. https://www.islamicstudies.info/tafheem.php?sura=3&verse=1&to=6
