- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:121 — the address applied to the Messenger with the ruling covering his entire Ummah; the meaning of true recitation (Yatlunahu Haqqan Tilawatih): Ibn Abbas via as-Suddi from Abu Malik — make lawful what it makes lawful, prohibit what it makes unlawful, recite as it was revealed, do not change the words, do not interpret with other than its actual interpretation; the ones who adhered to the earlier Books will believe in what was sent to the Prophet. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-121-sora-2.html
- Al-Jalalayn, tafsir of 2:121 — "with true recitation" = reciting it as it was revealed. https://qurano.com/en/2-al-baqara/verse-121/
- Qur'an Gallery, "Abraham's Trials for Leadership (2:124)" — Ibn Kathir's principle: divine honour and leadership earned through obedience in trials; "My covenant does not include the wrongdoers" severs entitlement based on bloodline; the appointment as Imam (leader) for mankind. https://qurangallery.app/by-ayah/abrahams-trials-leadership-surah-al-baqarah-2-124-divine-covenant
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:125 — "The Virtue of Allah's House": mathaba as a place of resort (hearts eager, never bored — the answer to Ibrahim's supplication, cf. 14:40); aman as safety from enemies and armed conflict (cf. 3:96–97); the Maqam as the stone Ibrahim stood on while building; Umar's suggestion and the revelation of the verse (Sahih Muslim, the long hadith); the Prophet praying two rak'ahs at the Maqam. https://quran.com/al-baqarah/125/tafsirs/169
- Ma'arif al-Qur'an (Mufti Muhammad Shafi), tafsir of 2:125 — history of the Kaaba; Ibn Kathir from Mujahid: Ibrahim was in Syria when the site was indicated to him; the Maqam stone with the print of his foot (al-Bukhari); Anas saw the mark; Ibn Abbas: the whole Haram as "Maqam" for the two rak'ahs (accepted by most fuqaha); the Prophet reciting the verse and praying two rak'ahs with the Kaaba between him and the Maqam (Sahih Muslim); the Wajib/Sunnah rulings. https://previous.quran.com/2:125/tafsirs/en-tafsir-maarif-ul-quran
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:127 — "Allah accepted Ibrahim's supplication": the raising of the foundations, the stone brought by Ishmael, both praying "Our Lord! - Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:130–134 — "who turns away from the religion of Ibrahim" means abandoning his path, way and method (cf. 6:75–79 on Abraham's search for the Lord); 2:132: the submission enjoined by Ibrahim and Ya'qub — "perform righteous deeds during your lifetime and remain on this path so Allah will grant you the favour of dying upon it; one dies upon the path he lived and is resurrected according to what he died on"; 2:134 — "a nation who has passed away" = existed before you; for them what they earned, for you what you earned. https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/2/ and https://quran.com/en/sapi-betina/132/tafsirs
