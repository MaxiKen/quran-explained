## References

- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir al-Qur'an al-'Azim*, commentary on 2:62 — the identity of the Sabians: quoted views include al-Tabari's uncertainty, Mujahid's (via Wahb ibn Munabbih) view that they are between Jews and Christians with no specific religion, the view that they read the Zabur, and the view that they are star-worshippers. Quran.com: https://quran.com/en/bakara/62/tafsirs
- Qur'an Gallery, "Sabeans/Sabians in Islamic Identity" — the three Qur'anic mentions (2:62, 5:69, 22:17), modern identification with the Mandaeans, and the principle that salvation rests on true faith and deeds rather than group labels. https://qurangallery.app/topics/sabeans-sabians-islamic-identity-quran
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:65–66 — the Sabbath-breakers: town by the sea identified as Aylah (Eilat); the fish coming openly on the Sabbath and the nets set before it; Ibn Abbas via al-'Awfi on the young becoming monkeys and the old becoming swine; Ibn Abi Hatim and Ibn Abbas on the transformed perishing without offspring; Qatadah on the transformation; the punishment as a deterrent (*nakal*) for present and future generations. https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/2/65/
- AbdurRahman.org, "Breaking the Sabbath" — the narrative of the Sabbath-breaking town: the fish appearing on the Sabbath, the deceit of nets and enclosures set before it, and the interpretation of the transformation. https://abdurrahman.org/2014/01/27/breachingsabbath/
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:67–71 — the cow: Ibn Abi Hatim from 'Ubaydah al-Salmani on the wealthy man murdered by his nephew, the command to sacrifice a cow, the escalating questions, the final specification of the unworked cow, the price of the cow, and the striking of the dead man. https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/2/67/
- al-Sa'di, *Tafsir al-Sa'di* on 2:67–71 — had the people not asked so many questions, any cow would have sufficed; their questioning made the matter harder for them. https://ilfp.wordpress.com/2010/04/27/51/
- Ibn Kathir, *Tafsir*, commentary on 2:78–79 — the unlettered (*ummiyyun*) and the meaning of *amani*; Ibn Abbas via al-Dahhak on *amani* as "false statements they utter"; Az-Zuhri and Ibn Abbas on the People of the Book who altered the Book and wrote one with their own hands; al-Dahhak on the woe for the lies written and the wealth unjustly acquired. https://www.alim.org/quran/tafsir/ibn-kathir/surah/2/78/
- SurahQuran.com, *Tafsir* on 2:79 (English) — those who write the Book with their own hands and trade it for a little price, with the woe pronounced upon what they wrote and what they earned. https://surahquran.com/tafsir-english-aya-79-sora-2.html
