<<<VERSE 4:15>>>
"As for those of your women who commit illegal intercourse—call four witnesses from among yourselves. If they testify, confine the offenders to their homes until they die or Allah ordains a different way for them." This verse sets out the early ruling for women who commit illegal sexual intercourse: four witnesses are called, and if they testify, the offenders are confined to their homes until death or until Allah ordains another way. This ruling was later abrogated.

### Phrase by phrase

"Those of your women who commit illegal intercourse" refers to women who commit illegal sexual intercourse.

**"Call four witnesses from among yourselves"** commands calling four witnesses.

**"If they testify"** means if the witnesses testify.

**"Confine the offenders to their homes"** means confining them to houses.

**"Until they die"** means until death comes to them.

**"Or Allah ordains a different way for them"** refers to the abrogation of this ruling later.

### The abrogated ruling

At the beginning of Islam, if a woman committed adultery as stipulated by sufficient proof, she was confined to her home without leave until she died. Ibn Abbas said the early ruling was confinement, until Allah sent down Surat An-Nur (chapter 24), which replaced it with flogging for fornication or stoning to death for adultery (later). Similar reports came from Ikrimah, Sa'id bin Jubayr, Al-Hasan, Ata Al-Khurasani, Abu Salih, Qatadah, Zayd bin Aslam and Ad-Dahhak, and this is a matter agreed upon. Imam Ahmad recorded that Ubadah bin As-Samit said that when revelation descended upon the Prophet his face showed signs of strain, and one day Allah sent down a revelation and the Prophet said, "Take from me: Allah has made another way for them. The married with the married, the unmarried with the unmarried. The married receives a hundred lashes and stoning to death, while the unmarried receives a hundred lashes then banishment for a year." Muslim and the collectors of the Sunan recorded Ubadah bin As-Samit saying the Prophet said, "Take from me, take from me. Allah has made another way for them: the unmarried receives a hundred lashes and banishment for one year, while the married receives a hundred lashes and stoning to death." At-Tirmidhi said "Hasan Sahih." The verse cites 24:2 (An-Nur). The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ad-Dahhak, Mujahid, Sa'id bin Jubayr, and others. Collectors include At-Tirmidhi and al-Bukhari. Ahmad, Madinah, Māʿiz, Mālik, torah, two sahihs, Ubadah bin As-Samit, Sunan, and related terms appear. The scholars and terms mentioned include Ahmad, Ad-Dahhak, hadith, Ibn Abbas, lut, madinah, Māʿiz, Mālik, Mujahid, An-Nur, Sa'id bin Jubayr, Sunan, torah, two sahihs, Ubadah bin As-Samit.

### In short

- The verse sets out the early ruling of confinement for women who commit illegal intercourse.
- Four witnesses are required.
- The ruling was later abrogated by Surat An-Nur.
- The Prophet announced the new ruling of flogging and stoning.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.

<<<VERSE 4:16>>>
"And the two among you who commit this sin—discipline them. If they repent and mend their ways, relieve them. Surely Allah is ever Accepting of Repentance, Most Merciful." This verse commands disciplining the two who commit this sin, and relieving them if they repent and mend their ways, for Allah is ever accepting of repentance, most merciful.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And the two among you who commit this sin"** refers to the two who commit this sin.

**"Discipline them"** commands punishing them.

**"If they repent and mend their ways"** means if they repent and do righteous deeds.

**"Relieve them"** means leave them alone.

**"Surely Allah is ever Accepting of Repentance, Most Merciful"** affirms Allah's acceptance of repentance and mercy.

### Repentance and mercy

The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ad-Dahhak, Mujahid, Sa'id bin Jubayr, and others. Collectors include At-Tirmidhi. The verse involves fiqh, hadith, Abu Bakr, Abu Hurairah, Ahmad, Ad-Dahhak, Ibn Abbas, Khuzaimah ibn Thabit, lut, Mujahid, Al-Munkadir, Sa'id bin Jubayr, al-Shāfi, Sunan, At-Targhib, two sahihs, Ubadah bin As-Samit, and related terms. The numbers 15 and 16 appear. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abu Bakr, Abu Hurairah, Ahmad, Ad-Dahhak, fiqh, hadith, Ibn Abbas, Khuzaimah ibn Thabit, lut, Mujahid, Al-Munkadir, Sa'id bin Jubayr, al-Shāfi, Sunan, At-Targhib, two sahihs, Ubadah bin As-Samit.

### In short

- The verse commands disciplining the two who commit this sin.
- If they repent and mend their ways, they are relieved.
- Allah is accepting of repentance and most merciful.
- The verse follows the previous ruling.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
