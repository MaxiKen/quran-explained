<<<VERSE 4:7>>>
"For men there is a share in what their parents and close relatives leave, and for women there is a share in what their parents and close relatives leave—whether it is little or much. These are obligatory shares." This verse establishes that both men and women have a fixed share in the inheritance left by parents and close relatives, whether the property be small or large. It is a legal share ordained by Allah, and it follows the preceding verses on orphans and property.

### Phrase by phrase

"For men there is a share" means men have a portion of the inheritance.

"In what their parents and close relatives leave" refers to what parents and near relatives leave behind.

**"And for women there is a share"** means women also have a portion, just as men do.

**"Whether it is little or much"** means the share applies regardless of the amount.

**"These are obligatory shares"** means these are fixed legal portions ordained by Allah.
