<<<VERSE 4:34>>>
"Men are the caretakers of women, as men have been provisioned by Allah over women and tasked with supporting them financially. And righteous women are devoutly obedient and, when alone, protective of what Allah has entrusted them with. And if you sense ill-conduct from your women, advise them first, if they persist, do not share their beds, but if they still persist, then discipline them gently. But if they change their ways, do not be unjust to them. Surely Allah is Most High, All-Great." This verse states that men are caretakers and maintainers of women because Allah provisioned men over women and men support them financially. Righteous women are obedient and protective. If ill-conduct is sensed, advise, then abandon beds, then discipline gently. If they change ways, do not be unjust. Allah is Most High, All-Great.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Men are the caretakers of women"** means men are responsible for women.

**"As men have been provisioned by Allah over women"** means Allah made men excel over women for certain tasks.

**"And tasked with supporting them financially"** means men spend from their means on women.

**"And righteous women are devoutly obedient"** means righteous women are obedient to their husbands.

**"And, when alone, protective of what Allah has entrusted them with"** means they guard in the husband's absence what Allah orders them to guard.

**"And if you sense ill-conduct from your women, advise them first"** commands admonishing them.

**"If they persist, do not share their beds"** commands abandoning them in their beds.

**"But if they still persist, then discipline them gently"** commands beating them gently.

**"But if they change their ways, do not be unjust to them"** forbids seeking a means against them if they return to obedience.

**"Surely Allah is Most High, All-Great"** affirms Allah's highness and greatness.

### Caretaking and discipline

The man is responsible for the woman, her maintainer, caretaker and leader who disciplines her if she deviates. Prophethood and other important positions of leadership are exclusive to men. The Prophet said, "People who appoint a woman to be their leader, will never achieve success." Al-Bukhari recorded this. Men have a degree of responsibility over women (2:228). The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Al-Hasan Al-Basri, As-Suddi, and others. Collectors include Al-Bukhari and Ibn Jarir. The verse cites 2:228 and 2:233. It involves qiblah, sai, say, sunnah, and related terms. The numbers 34 and 11 appear here. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abdur-Rahman bin Awf, Abu Bakr, Abu Hurayrah, Ahmad, al-Bahr, al-Baihaqi, hadith, hajj, Al-Hasan Al-Basri, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Sa'd, Jabir, jerusalem, job, lot, makkah, al-Maqdis, al-Muhit, Muslim, qiblah, sai, say, As-Suddi, sunnah, unseen.

### In short

- Men are caretakers and maintainers of women.
- Righteous women are obedient and protective.
- Ill-conduct is addressed by advising, abandoning beds, then gentle discipline.
- If they change ways, do not be unjust.
- Allah is Most High, All-Great.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.

<<<VERSE 4:35>>>
"If you anticipate a split between them, appoint a mediator from his family and another from hers. If they desire reconciliation, Allah will restore harmony between them. Surely Allah is All-Knowing, All-Aware." This verse commands appointing mediators from both families if a split is anticipated, and promises that if they desire reconciliation, Allah will restore harmony. He is All-Knowing, All-Aware.

### Phrase by phrase

**"If you anticipate a split between them"** refers to fear of discord between spouses.

**"Appoint a mediator from his family and another from hers"** commands appointing arbitrators from both families.

**"If they desire reconciliation"** means if they want reconciliation.

**"Allah will restore harmony between them"** promises Allah will restore harmony.

**"Surely Allah is All-Knowing, All-Aware"** affirms Allah's knowledge and awareness.

### Mediation and reconciliation

The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, and others. Collectors include Ibn Jarir. The verse involves fiqh, say, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abu Hanifah, Abu Umar bin Abdul-Barr, al-Baihaqi, al-Basri, baynihimā, fiqh, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Abi, Ibn al-Rabi, Ibn Salamah, Ibn al-Shahnah, lot, ruh, al-Salmani, say, al-Tarablusi, unseen.

### In short

- Appoint mediators from both families if a split is anticipated.
- If they desire reconciliation, Allah restores harmony.
