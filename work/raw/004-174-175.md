<<<VERSE 4:174>>>
The proof and the light: "O humanity! There has come to you conclusive evidence from your Lord. And We have sent down to you a brilliant light" (4:174). After refuting the beliefs that ascribed godhood to Jesus, the passage opens outward with a universal address.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O humanity! There has come to you conclusive evidence from your Lord"** announces a plain, unequivocal proof that eradicates all possibility of excuse and every evil doubt. One reading names the proof itself: a messenger from your Lord, Muḥammad ﷺ, the definitive argument come against the people of Makkah. Ibn ʿAbbās said the Holy Prophet ﷺ was called the burhān, the proof, for his blessed person, his noble morals, his miracles, and his receiving the very revelation of the Book of Allah. Rūḥ al-Maʿānī carries the same reading. Beyond these open proofs no other proof is needed. His person is, in itself, proof personified.

**"And We have sent down to you a brilliant light"** names, as Ibn Jurayj and others said, the Qur'ān, a clear light directing to the Truth, a scripture expounding the lawful and the unlawful, which falsehood cannot touch from any angle, a revelation from the Wise and Praised One. Verse 15 of sūrat al-Mā'idah says the like: "There has come to you from Allah a light and a clear Book." A question arises there, for the conjunction demands dissimilarity and light and Book seem one. The recorded answer: difference in expression is enough, the meanings remaining the same. And if the light is taken as the Holy Prophet ﷺ and the Book as the Qur'ān, that too is correct. It does not make him light in the literal sense, for it goes nothing against his being physically human.

### The call like sunrise

So loud and clear is a divine call to human beings that darkness is wiped out and life's realities lit to their utmost. Its arguments fortify it so well that denial cannot stand against it. Ridicule the deniers may offer, but logic they cannot. When God makes the sun rise, light and darkness separate, and the same divine power runs through the call of truth: after it, truth and untruth stand so clearly parted that no one in his senses finds it hard to understand. To see the sun, though, a man must open his eyes, and to receive guidance from the call, he must pay full attention. One who does not heed it stays deprived even while the call is being made.

The inward reading hears two lamps lit for the servant: one before him, the sunnah's lamp, which is the proof itself, and one within, the heart's lamp of faith and shining light. Happy is the servant who walks between these two lamps. One breath with the Friend is worth the two worlds, and one vision of the Friend is given gratis to a hundred thousand spirits. The grateful soul answers: in gratitude I send my spirit to You, one whiff of union doing what a hundred spirits never could.

### In short

- The proof come from the Lord is either the Prophet ﷺ himself, morals, miracles and revelation in one person, or the book-borne argument he carries.
- The light sent down is the Qur'ān, clear, untouched by falsehood, expounding the lawful and the unlawful.
- Like sunrise, the call parts truth from untruth, but eyes must be opened to see it.

<<<VERSE 4:175>>>
The holding and the guiding: "As for those who believe in Allah and hold fast to Him, He will admit them into His mercy and grace and guide them to Himself through the Straight Path" (4:175). The verse maps the whole journey of the believer in one line.

### Phrase by phrase

**"those who believe in Allah and hold fast to Him"** means, in one reading, His worship and reliance on Him for everything at all. Ibn Jurayj said it means they believe in Allah and hold fast to the Qur'ān. Another reading adds Muḥammad ﷺ and the Qur'ān to the object of belief, and reads the holding fast as holding to Allah's divine Oneness.

**"He will admit them into His mercy and grace"** means He grants them His mercy and admits them into Paradise, rewards and ranks increased and multiplied, favor and bounty from Him, honor added.

**"and guide them to Himself through the Straight Path"** opens a clear way with no wickedness in it and no deviation, namely the religion of Islam. It describes the believers in this life and the Hereafter: in this life their footing is straight and safe in creed and in action alike, confirmed in faith, and in the Hereafter they walk the path of Allah that leads to the gardens of Paradise.

### The grip that outlasts the deceiver

Holding on firmly is necessary, because in this world of trial Satan pursues man, deceiving him in many ways and turning him from the truth. Unless a man resolves to support the truth and fight Satan's deceptions, Satan will induce him into his fold. Yet man is not left alone in the trial. Those who wish to tread the path receive divine guidance at every turn, and by God's help they reach their destination. When a man gives all importance to the truth alone, God engenders in him the ability to persevere on the path of pure truth, and so saves him from deviating onto paths that lead astray.

The inward reading divides the labor rightly. The servant contributes faith and the grip of holding fast, as servanthood demands. The Exalted Lord contributes bounty and mercy, in the attribute of loving kindness. Their guidance is to see that every recompense found and every generosity seen flows from His bounty and mercy, never from their own faith or grip. As the Prophet ﷺ said:

> There is none of you who will be saved by his deeds.

They said: not even you, O Messenger of God? He said:

> Not even I, unless God envelops me with His mercy.

### In short

- Belief plus the grip, on Allah, on His Oneness, on the Qur'ān, draws mercy, grace and raised ranks.
- The Straight Path is Islam itself, safe in creed and action now, and the road to the gardens later.
- Deeds do not save anyone by themselves, for the recompense is bounty and mercy from first to last.
