Once Hārūn's (Aaron's) explanation had calmed him, Mūsā (Moses) returned to the Tablets: "When Moses’ anger subsided, he took up the Tablets whose text contained guidance and mercy for those who stand in awe of their Lord". He had cast them down out of jealousy for Allah and anger for His sake, because his people had worshipped the calf. Now that his anger at his people had died down, he picked them up again.

### Phrase by phrase

**"When Moses’ anger subsided"**: when his anger with his people abated and he grew calm.

**"he took up the Tablets"**: the Tablets of the Torah that he had thrown down in anger.

**"whose text"**: literally "in their copy", meaning what was inscribed on them. The Arabic word nuskhah means a piece of writing copied from a book or from other writings. One explanation takes it as what remained on the Tablets. Another takes it as what was given back to him of the two Tablets.

**"contained guidance and mercy"**: guidance away from error and a clear explanation of the truth, and mercy that shields from punishment.

**"for those who stand in awe of their Lord"**: those who fear Him and keep in mind the outcome He will bring. In the Arabic, a small particle, lām, is placed before "their Lord", the object of awe, because that object comes ahead of its verb.

### What happened to the broken Tablets?

Several scholars of tafsīr said that when Mūsā threw the Tablets to the ground, they shattered, and he gathered up the pieces afterwards. He found guidance and mercy in what was inscribed on them, but the detailed rulings of the Law were lost, or so they said. They also claimed that the broken pieces were kept in the treasuries of certain Israelite kings until the rise of the Islamic state. Only Allah knows whether these reports are true.

Other reports say that the Tablets broke when Mūsā set them aside in haste, and that Allah then gave him the Torah written on some other material.

### Who benefits from Allah's Book?

Allah's Book is a book of guidance and grace. It is the best guide for a person in this world and a sure source of Allah's grace in the Hereafter. Yet it benefits only those who fear Allah and keep thinking about how He will decide their fate. These are the real seekers of truth. When the truth comes before them, they accept it, with no inner complexes holding them back.

For such people Allah becomes the centre of their hopes and fears, and their lives and wealth are devoted to Him. Their fear of Allah awakens their awareness and lifts every barrier from their eyes, so they do not miss His signs when these appear. In short, they live in fear of their Lord, not in contentment and complacency.

### In short

- When his anger calmed, Mūsā took up the Tablets he had cast down for Allah's sake.
- What was written on them held guidance from error and mercy from punishment.
- Some reports say the Tablets shattered and part of the Law was lost, but only Allah knows if this is true.
- Allah's Book benefits those who stand in awe of their Lord, for that fear opens their eyes to the truth.
