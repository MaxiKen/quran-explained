<<<VERSE 4:29>>>
"O believers! Do not devour one another's wealth illegally, but rather trade by mutual consent. And do not kill each other or yourselves. Surely Allah is ever Merciful to you." This verse forbids devouring one another's wealth illegally, permits trade by mutual consent, forbids killing each other or oneself, and affirms Allah's mercy.

### Phrase by phrase

"Do not devour one another's wealth illegally" forbids unlawful acquisition of property.

**"But rather trade by mutual consent"** permits trade by mutual consent.

**"And do not kill each other or yourselves"** forbids killing.

**"Surely Allah is ever Merciful to you"** affirms Allah's mercy.

### Unlawful wealth and killing

The verse prohibits illegally acquiring each other's property using dishonest methods such as riba, gambling and other wicked methods. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and others. Collectors include Abu Dawud, Al-Bukhari's, An-Nasai, At-Tirmidhi, Ibn Jarir, al-Hakim. The verse cites 2:188, 24:61, 25:68, 25:70. It involves riba, jumuah, tayammum, shariah, say, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abdullah bin Amr, Abdullah bin Mas'ud, Abi Bakrah, Abu Hayyan, Abu Hurayrah, Abu Said, adam, Ahmad, Anas bin Malik, al-Bahr, Al-Farisi, hadith, Ibn Abbas, al-Isfahani, jahannam, job, jumuah, al-Khudhri, kufr, al-Mazhari, al-Muhit, Mujahid, Al-Mundhiri, Qatadah, riba, As-Salasil, say, shariah, Al-Targhib, tayammum, Thabit bin Ad-Dahhak, Two Sahihs.

### In short

- Devouring wealth illegally is forbidden.
- Trade by mutual consent is permitted.
- Killing each other or oneself is forbidden.
- Allah is merciful.
