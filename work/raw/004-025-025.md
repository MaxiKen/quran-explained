<<<VERSE 4:25>>>
"But if any of you cannot afford to marry a free believing woman, then let him marry a believing bondwoman possessed by one of you. Allah knows best the state of your faith and theirs. You are from one another. So marry them with the permission of their owners, giving them their dowry in fairness, if they are chaste, neither promiscuous nor having secret affairs. If they commit indecency after marriage, they receive half the punishment of free women. This is for those of you who fear falling into sin. But if you are patient, it is better for you. And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful." This verse permits marrying a believing bondwoman when one cannot afford a free believing woman, requires the owner's permission and a fair dowry, requires chastity, gives half the punishment for indecency after marriage, and says patience is better.

### Phrase by phrase

**"But if any of you cannot afford to marry a free believing woman"** refers to those who lack the means.

**"Then let him marry a believing bondwoman possessed by one of you"** permits marrying a believing slave girl.

**"Allah knows best the state of your faith and theirs"** affirms Allah's knowledge.

**"You are from one another"** means you are one from another.

**"So marry them with the permission of their owners"** requires the owner's permission.

**"Giving them their dowry in fairness"** requires paying the dowry fairly.

**"If they are chaste, neither promiscuous nor having secret affairs"** requires chastity.

**"If they commit indecency after marriage, they receive half the punishment of free women"** gives half the punishment.

**"This is for those of you who fear falling into sin"** is for those who fear harm in religion or body.

**"But if you are patient, it is better for you"** says patience is better.

**"And Allah is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful"** affirms Allah's forgiveness and mercy.
