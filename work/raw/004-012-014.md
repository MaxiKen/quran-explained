<<<VERSE 4:12>>>
"You will inherit half of what your wives leave if they are childless. But if they have children, then your share is one-fourth of the estate—after the fulfilment of bequests and debts. And your wives will inherit one-fourth of what you leave if you are childless. But if you have children, then your wives will receive one-eighth of your estate—after the fulfilment of bequests and debts. And if a man or a woman leaves neither parents nor children but only a brother or a sister from their mother's side, they will each inherit one-sixth, but if they are more than one, they all will share one-third of the estate—after the fulfilment of bequests and debts without harm to the heirs. This is a commandment from Allah. And Allah is All-Knowing, Most Forbearing." This verse sets out the inheritance shares of spouses and of siblings from the mother's side (kalalah), after bequests and debts, and declares them a commandment from Allah.

### Phrase by phrase

**"You will inherit half of what your wives leave if they are childless"** gives the husband half when the wife leaves no child.

**"But if they have children, then your share is one-fourth"** gives the husband one-fourth when there are children.

**"And your wives will inherit one-fourth of what you leave if you are childless"** gives the wife one-fourth when the husband leaves no child.

**"But if you have children, then your wives will receive one-eighth"** gives the wife one-eighth when there are children.

**"And if a man or a woman leaves neither parents nor children but only a brother or a sister from their mother's side"** introduces the kalalah case.

**"They will each inherit one-sixth"** gives one-sixth to a single sibling from the mother's side.

**"But if they are more than one, they all will share one-third"** gives one-third to multiple siblings from the mother's side.

**"After the fulfilment of bequests and debts"** means debts and bequests are paid first.

**"This is a commandment from Allah"** declares these shares ordained by Allah.

**"And Allah is All-Knowing, Most Forbearing"** affirms Allah's knowledge and forbearance.

### Spousal shares and kalalah

The husband gets half of what his wife leaves if she has no child, and one-fourth if she has a child. The wife gets one-fourth of what her husband leaves if he has no child, and one-eighth if he has a child. Payment of debts comes before fulfilling the will, then the will, then inheritance, and there is consensus on this among scholars. The rule applies to grandchildren as well. If there is more than one wife, they all share the fourth or eighth. Kalalah means the man or woman whose inheritance is in question leaves neither ascendants nor descendants, but has a brother or sister from the mother's side. Each gets one-sixth, and if more than two, they share one-third. The verse cites 30:21. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ibn Mas, Ibn Abi, Al-Hakam, Al-Qurtubi, al-Alusi, al-Jassas, al-Ma'ani, al-Hidayah, Al-Nis, Salaf, As-Siddiq, Abu Bakr, Abi Waqqas, Anṣārī, and others. Collectors include Ibn Jarir. The verse involves hajj, mahr, ruh, say, shariah, ummah, Kalālah, al-Islām, opening, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abi Waqqas, Abu Bakr, al-Alusi, Anṣārī, hadith, hajj, Al-Hakam, al-Hidayah, Ibn Abi, Ibn Mas, al-Islām, al-Jassas, Kalālah, al-Ma'ani, Al-Madinah, mahr, Al-Nis, opening, Al-Qurtubi, ruh, Salaf, say, shariah, As-Siddiq, ummah.
