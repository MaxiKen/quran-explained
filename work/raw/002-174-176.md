<<<VERSE 2:174>>>
The earlier verses dealt with unlawful things that are tangible. The verses that follow take up intangible deeds that are unlawful, evil deeds inner and outer, beginning with the religious scholars of the Jews who sold false verdicts and distorted the Torah for those who paid them. "Indeed, those who hide Allah's revelations, trading them for a fleeting gain consume nothing but fire into their bellies. Allah will neither speak to them on the Day of Judgment, nor will He purify them. And they will suffer a painful punishment". Al-Kalbī, reporting from Abū Ṣāliḥ from Ibn ʿAbbās, said the verse was revealed about the leaders and doctors of the Jews, who received gifts and surpluses from ordinary Jews and had hoped the new prophet would be sent from among them. When he was sent from among others they feared losing their income and their leadership, so they altered the traits of Muḥammad ﷺ in the Torah and told people that the prophet to be sent at the end of time would look like this, and did not resemble the prophet in Makkah. Ordinary people compared the altered description with Muḥammad ﷺ, found it different, and did not believe in him. Another report names Kaʿb ibn al-Ashraf, Ḥuyayy ibn Akhṭab and Judayy ibn Akhṭab as the people concerned.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Indeed, those who hide Allah's revelations"**. The Jews, and the Christians likewise, who concealed what their Books said in description of Muḥammad ﷺ, all of which testified to his truth as a Messenger and Prophet, and more generally the signs of truth and prophethood revealed in the Scriptures.

**"trading them for a fleeting gain"**. They took a small price in exchange, the joys and delights of this earthly life: leadership, status, money, and the gifts and honour the Arabs gave them. They feared that if they announced what they knew about Muḥammad ﷺ the people would abandon them and follow him, so they hid the truth to keep the little they were getting and sold their souls for that small profit. They preferred it to guidance, to following the truth and believing in the Messenger ﷺ, and so profited only failure and loss in this life and the next. In this world Allah made the truth about His Messenger ﷺ known anyway by clear signs and unequivocal proofs, and the very people the Jews feared would follow him did follow him and became his supporters against them, so that they earned anger on top of the wrath they had already incurred.

**"consume nothing but fire into their bellies."**. Whatever they eat in return for hiding the truth will become a raging fire in their stomachs on the Day of Resurrection, or, on another reading, they eat nothing but the unlawful, and the Fire is their journey's end. Allah says similarly: "Indeed, those who unjustly consume orphans' wealth ˹in fact˺ consume nothing but fire into their bellies. And they will be burned in a blazing Hell" (4:10), and an authentic hadith says that those who eat or drink from gold or silver vessels are filling their stomachs with the fire of Jahannam.

**"Allah will neither speak to them on the Day of Judgment, nor will He purify them."**. Because Allah is furious with them for concealing the truth, He will not speak to them kindly or with words that bring them joy, nor look at them, nor cleanse them of the filth of their sins by forgiving them, nor praise or commend them in any way. They will hear only what grieves them.

**"And they will suffer a painful punishment"**. That is the Fire, a torment that reaches into their hearts.

### A warning to the scholars of this Ummah

There is no doubt that people who conceal the contents of the Book of Allah and collect insignificant worldly gains for this breach of trust are simply eating fire. On the Day of Qiyāmah Allah will not speak to them affectionately or purify them by forgiving their sins, and their punishment will be terrible. In this there is a veiled warning to the ʿulamāʾ, the religious scholars of the community of the Last Prophet ﷺ, to keep away from such practices and never fall short in disclosing the true injunctions of Allah for any material reason or interest of their own. Whoever changes an injunction of the Sharīʿah out of greed for worldly gain should know that what he consumes is like embers of fire stored in his stomach, because that is the end of such deeds. Some perceptive scholars have said that unlawful wealth is in reality the very fire of Hell, even though we do not sense it as such in this life, for after death a person's deeds appear in the form of fire.

When superstitious beliefs about prohibitions have been accepted by the masses as religion and held sacred, the scholars of the community often lack the courage to declare openly that these beliefs have nothing to do with true religion, fearing to cut themselves off from the public to whom they owe their popularity. They may revel in compromises that bring them wealth and honour, but in Allah's eyes they are the worst of offenders. Hiding the truth for worldly considerations is not among the offences Allah overlooks in the Hereafter. It is a crime that deprives a person of His mercy.

In the devotional reading, the scholars are commanded to spread the evidentiary proofs of knowledge, while the friends of Allah are commanded to preserve the trusts of the secret. If the scholars conceal the proofs of knowledge they will be bridled with a bridle of fire, and if the friends divulge even a sliver of the secret they are at once distanced from the secrets and stripped of the lights they were given. To each there is a limit, and to each a command.

### In short

- The verse condemns the Jewish scholars who altered and hid the Torah's description of the Prophet ﷺ to keep their gifts and leadership (Ibn ʿAbbās via al-Kalbī, naming Kaʿb ibn al-Ashraf and the sons of Akhṭab).
- What they earned by concealment is fire in their bellies (cf. 4:10), and on the Day of Judgement Allah will not speak to them, purify them or praise them.
- It is a warning to the scholars of every age, including this Ummah, never to hide or bend Allah's rulings for money or popularity, for unlawful wealth is itself the fire of Hell.
- Inwardly, scholars must publish their proofs while the friends of Allah must guard their secrets, each within his limit.

<<<VERSE 2:175>>>
Verse 175 describes the bargain those concealers made. "They are the ones who trade guidance for misguidance and forgiveness for punishment. How persistent are they in pursuit of the Fire". In their mortal life they abandoned guidance and adopted error, and in the Hereafter they will miss forgiveness and deserve punishment.

### Phrase by phrase

**"They are the ones who trade guidance for misguidance"**. The guidance was to announce the Prophet's ﷺ description found in their Books, the news of his prophethood and the glad tidings of his coming proclaimed by earlier prophets, and then to believe in him and follow him. Instead they preferred misguidance, denying and rejecting him and concealing his description. They bought disbelief at the price of faith and Judaism at the price of Islam, taking error in place of guidance in this world.

**"and forgiveness for punishment."**. Concealment and the sins accompanying it lead them towards torment. Without that concealment, pardon would have awaited them in the Hereafter. They chose what leads to the Fire instead of what leads to Paradise.

**"How persistent are they in pursuit of the Fire"**. How bold they are to plunge into the Fire, and how great their endurance seems. The statement is meant to provoke amazement in the believers at the indifference with which the disbelievers commit sins that must bring them the Fire, as if they did not realise what suffering it contains, for in reality they will have no patience whatever to endure it. Those who see them in their severe and painful torment will be amazed that they can bear such punishment. Other readings ask what made them so bold as to enter the Fire, or how deeply they indulge in the works of the people of the Fire. We seek refuge with Allah from such an end.

### What does the exchange mean inwardly?

In the devotional reading, those who prefer the other to the unseen (ghayb), the creation to the Real, and the lower self (nafs) to intimacy with Allah, how hard are their hearts, how shameless what they love and seek, how cheap their worth, and how disgraceful in the eyes of those who can see.

### In short

- The concealers of truth exchanged guidance for misguidance by rejecting the Prophet ﷺ they recognised, and forfeited the forgiveness awaiting them for punishment.
- Their boldness in sinning their way to the Fire is held up as a cause of amazement, since they could never endure it.
- Inwardly, this is the hardness of a heart that prefers creation to the Creator and the self to intimacy with Him.

<<<VERSE 2:176>>>
Verse 176 gives the reason for the punishment just described. "That is because Allah has revealed the Book in truth. And surely those who differ regarding it are totally engrossed in opposition". All of this befalls them because they chose to stray from the Book of Allah, which is so clear and true.

### Phrase by phrase

**"That is because Allah has revealed the Book in truth."**. The torment of eating fire and what follows it is deserved because Allah sent down the Books to His Messenger Muḥammad ﷺ and to the prophets before him, bringing Gabriel with the Qur'an as He had sent the Torah, and these revelations establish the truth and expose falsehood. Their Books commanded them to announce the truth and spread knowledge, since what Allah reveals in truth must be made clear and not hidden, yet they defied that knowledge and rejected it. The Final Messenger ﷺ called them to Allah, commanded righteousness and forbade evil, and they rejected him, denied him and hid what they knew about him, thereby mocking the signs Allah had revealed to His messengers.

**"And surely those who differ regarding it are totally engrossed in opposition"**. Those who dispute about the Book, believing in parts of it and disbelieving in and concealing other parts, in particular the Jews who contravened what it said of the traits of Muḥammad ﷺ and then hid them, are in schism far removed from the truth and in deep disagreement with guidance. Some say the verse refers to the idolaters, who differed over the Qur'an, some calling it poetry, some sorcery and some divination. Either way, people in such intransigence can expect a matching punishment.

### The road away from return

Worse than those who merely fail to accept the truth are those who, when it is presented to them, start meaningless discussions instead. They develop a propensity to divisiveness and in the end stray so far from the truth that no chance of return remains. In the devotional reading, Allah revealed the Book in truth and made its decree come to pass in sincerity, bringing each party to what He had prepared for them and establishing them on the way He fashioned for them.

### In short

- The punishment follows from the fact that Allah's Books were revealed in truth and commanded to be proclaimed, and these people hid and mocked them instead.
- Those who pick and choose within the Book, or dispute its nature as the idolaters did, have drifted into schism far from the truth.
- Quarrelling with the truth rather than accepting it leads step by step to a point of no return.
