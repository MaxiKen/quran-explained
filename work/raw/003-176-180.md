- What they hoarded, gold and silver, will be hung and tied round their necks like a collar of fire on Resurrection Day, explained as the bald-headed poisonous snake saying, "I am your wealth, I am your treasure".
- To Allah belongs the inheritance and heritage of heavens and earth after their inhabitants pass, and He is Well-Acquainted with generosity, miserliness, intentions and what hearts conceal.
