<<<VERSE 4:43>>>
"O believers! Do not approach prayer while intoxicated until you are aware of what you say, nor in a state of full impurity—unless you merely pass through the mosque—until you have bathed. But if you are ill, on a journey, or have relieved yourselves, or been intimate with your wives and cannot find water, then purify yourselves with clean earth, wiping your faces and hands. And Allah is Ever-Pardoning, All-Forgiving." This verse forbids approaching prayer while intoxicated until one knows what one is saying, and while sexually impure except when passing through, until bathing. It permits tayammum with clean earth when water is unavailable due to illness, travel, or other causes. And affirms Allah is Ever-Pardoning, All-Forgiving.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O believers! Do not approach prayer while intoxicated"** forbids approaching prayer while drunk.

**"Until you are aware of what you say"** means until one knows what one is saying.

**"Nor in a state of full impurity"** forbids attending while sexually impure.

**"Unless you merely pass through the mosque"** permits passing through.

**"Until you have bathed"** requires bathing.

**"But if you are ill, on a journey, or have relieved yourselves, or been intimate with your wives and cannot find water"** lists excuses.

**"Then purify yourselves with clean earth"** commands tayammum.

**"Wiping your faces and hands"** describes the wiping.

**"And Allah is Ever-Pardoning, All-Forgiving"** affirms Allah's pardon and forgiveness.

### Intoxication and purity

Prayer while drunk is forbidden, for one does not know the meaning of what he is saying in that state, and attending the Masjids while sexually impure is forbidden, except when one is merely passing through the Masjid from one door to another. This Ayah was revealed before alcohol consumption was completely prohibited. In that Hadith, the Messenger of Allah recited this Ayah to Umar, who said, "O Allah! Explain the ruling about Khamr (intoxicants) for us in a plain manner." When this Ayah was revealed, the Prophet recited it to Umar, who still said, "O Allah! Explain the ruling about Khamr (intoxicants) for us in a plain manner." After that, they would not drink alcohol close to the time of prayer. When Allah's statement, "O you who believe! alcoholic drinks, gambling, Al-Ansab, and Al-Azlam are an abomination of Shayatan's handiwork. So avoid that in order that you may be successful" (5:90), until, "So, will you not then abstain" (5:91) was revealed, Umar said, "We abstain, we abstain." In another narration, when the Ayah in Surat An-Nisa' was revealed, "O you who believe! Do not approach Salah while you are in a druken state until you know what you are saying," at the time of prayer, the Messenger of Allah would have someone proclaim. "Let not any drunk approach the prayer." This is the wording collected by Abu Dawud. Sa'd said that four Ayat were revealed concerning him. A man from the Ansar once made some food and invited some Muhajirin and Ansar men to it, and they ate and drank until they became intoxicated. They then boasted about their status. Afterwards a man held a camel's bone and injured Sa'd's nose, leaving a scar ever since. This occurred before Al-Khamr was prohibited, and Allah later revealed, "O you who believe! Approach not AsSalat (the prayer) when you are in a drunken state." Muslim recorded this Hadith, and the collectors of the Sunan recorded it, with the exception of Ibn Majah. Ali bin Abi Talib said that Abdur-Rahman bin Awf made some food to which he invited them and served some alcohol to drink. When they became intoxicated, and the time for prayer came, they asked someone to lead them in prayer. He recited 'Say, O disbelievers! I do not worship that which you worship, but we worship that which you worship.' Allah then revealed something. The verse cites 2:219, 2:237, 5:90, 5:91, 33:49, 33:72, 39:53. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, A'ishah, Abu Hurayrah, and others. Collectors include Abu Dawud, Al-Bukhari, An-Nasa'i, At-Tirmidhi, Ibn Jarir, Ibn Majah. The verse involves ghusl, salah, say, shariah, taqwa, tayammum, wudu, and related terms. ### In short

109 and 708

- Do not approach prayer while intoxicated.
- Do not attend while sexually impure except when passing through.
- Bathe when possible. Use tayammum when water is unavailable.
- Allah is Ever-Pardoning, All-Forgiving.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
