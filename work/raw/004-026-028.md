<<<VERSE 4:26>>>
"It is Allah's Will to make things clear to you, guide you to the noble ways of those before you, and turn to you in mercy. For Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise." This verse states that Allah wishes to make clear what is lawful and unlawful, to show the ways of those before, and to accept repentance. He is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

### Phrase by phrase

**"It is Allah's Will to make things clear to you"** means Allah wishes to make clear what is lawful and unlawful.

**"Guide you to the noble ways of those before you"** means showing the righteous ways and how to adhere to His commandments.

**"And turn to you in mercy"** means accepting repentance from sin and error.

**"For Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise"** affirms Allah's knowledge and wisdom in commands and decrees.

### Clarity and mercy

The verse explains to believers what He has allowed and prohibited. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and others. The verse involves sacred law, halal, haram, say, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include halal, haram, sacred law, say, Tawus, the path, Waki.

### In short

- Allah wishes to make clear what is lawful and unlawful.
- He guides to the ways of those before.
- He accepts repentance.
- He is All-Knowing, All-Wise.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
