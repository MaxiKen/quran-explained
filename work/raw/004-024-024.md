<<<VERSE 4:24>>>
"Also forbidden are married women—except female captives in your possession. This is Allah's commandment to you. Lawful to you are all beyond these—as long as you seek them with your wealth in a legal marriage, not in fornication. Give those you have consummated marriage with their due dowries. It is permissible to be mutually gracious regarding the set dowry. Surely Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise." This verse forbids married women except female captives in one's possession, declares this Allah's commandment, makes all others lawful in legal marriage, commands giving due dowries, permits mutual graciousness regarding the dowry, and affirms Allah's knowledge and wisdom.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Also forbidden are married women"** forbids marrying women already married.

**"Except female captives in your possession"** excepts female captives acquired through war, after making sure they are not pregnant.

**"This is Allah's commandment to you"** declares this prohibition ordained by Allah.

**"Lawful to you are all beyond these"** makes all other women lawful.

**"As long as you seek them with your wealth in a legal marriage, not in fornication"** requires seeking them with wealth in legal marriage, not fornication.

**"Give those you have consummated marriage with their due dowries"** commands paying the dowry.

**"It is permissible to be mutually gracious regarding the set dowry"** permits mutual agreement to give more or less.

**"Surely Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise"** affirms Allah's knowledge and wisdom.

### Female captives and lawful marriage

The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, and others. Collectors include An-Nasa'i, At-Tirmidhi, Ibn Jarir, al-Bukhari. The verse cites 2:229. It was reported that Abu Sa'id Al-Khudri said they captured women from Awtas who were already married and disliked having relations with them because they had husbands. They asked the Prophet, and this verse was revealed, so they had relations with them. This is the wording collected by At-Tirmidhi, An-Nasa'i, Ibn Jarir and Muslim. Allah's statement "Thus has Allah ordained for you" means adhere to Allah's Book and do not transgress His set limits. "All others are lawful" means women other than the prohibited types, as Ata and others stated. The verse involves fiqh, hadith, hudud, iddah, jihad, mahr, nikah, shariah, sunnah, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abi Talib, Abu Bakr, Abu Sa'id, Ahmad, fiqh, hadith, Al-Hadrami, halal, al-Harb, hidayah, hudud, Ibn Abbas, iddah, al-Islam, al-Jassas, jihad, Al-Khudri, al-Ma'ani, Ma'bad Al-Juhani, mahr, makkah, Mujahid, Muslim, nikah, Al-Nis, Ar-Rabi, ruh, say, shariah, sunnah, two sahihs, ʿuhūd, ummah.

### In short

- Married women are forbidden except female captives.
- All others are lawful in legal marriage.
- Dowries must be paid.
- Mutual graciousness regarding dowry is permitted.
- Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
