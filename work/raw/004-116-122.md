<<<VERSE 4:116>>>
The unforgiven and the forgiven: "Surely Allah does not forgive associating ˹others˺ with Him ˹in worship˺, but forgives anything else of whoever He wills. Indeed, whoever associates ˹others˺ with Allah has clearly gone far astray" (4:116). The mention of jihād in the preceding verses took in all forces hostile to Islam, yet the actual description was limited to the Jews and the hypocrites, while the pagans, far greater in number, stood outside it. The passage now turns to their beliefs and the punishment they will face, a coherence of its own in this place, for the thief of the earlier episode became an apostate, and the lasting punishment he earned now becomes evident.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Surely Allah does not forgive associating ˹others˺ with Him ˹in worship˺"** states the one sin that stands outside pardon. The verse has appeared before in this sūrah, at verse 48, in the same words save for the ending. There the ending read that whoever ascribes a partner to Allah has designed a great sin, and here it reads that he has gone far astray. The reason for the difference lies in the addressees. The earlier verse spoke to the Jewish people of the Book. Through the Torah they knew the truth of Allah's Oneness, the falsity of shirk and the veracity of the Prophet's ﷺ mission, and yet they entered shirk regardless, acting as though this were the Torah's own teaching, a total forgery and a false accusation. That earned the word of the great sin. The present verse speaks to the pagans of Makkah, who had no background of Book or Prophet, though the rational arguments for the Oneness of Allah were all too clear, and taking stones crafted by their own hands as objects of worship was ineffectual and false even to ordinary common sense. That earned the word of going far astray.

**"but forgives anything else of whoever He wills"** keeps every other sin at the door of His mercy, forgiven to whoever is fit for it, out of His mercy and grace, even where the sinner dies without repentance.

**"Indeed, whoever associates ˹others˺ with Allah has clearly gone far astray"** totals the loss: other than the true path, away from guidance and righteousness, the self destroyed in this life and the Hereafter, and contentment lost in both.

### The permanence of the punishment

Some people doubt the proportion here. The crime of kufr and shirk is committed within the limited time of one life, so why should its punishment be limitless and permanent? The answer lies in the doer's stance. The kāfir and the mushrik simply does not regard what he does as a crime. He takes it as good and proper, worth his while, accompanied by the firm intention to remain so always. Holding that position to the last breath, he has committed a permanent crime to the full extent of his choice and volition, and the punishment for it must be permanent. The scholars count three kinds of injustice. The first Allah will never forgive, and it is shirk. The second could be forgiven, and it is falling short in the rights of Allah. The third meets retribution, and it is the violation of the rights that Allah's creatures hold over one another.

The reality of shirk is equality. Shirk in its reality is the placing of any created being beside Allah in worship, or in love, or in reverence. The words of the disbelievers on arrival into Jahannam are recorded in the Book: "By Allah, we were in obvious error when we had equated you with Allah, the Lord of all the worlds" (26:97). Even the polytheists did not believe their idols were the Creator and Master of the universe. It was under other erroneous assumptions that they regarded them as equal in worship, love and reverence, and this was the shirk that caused their entry into the Fire, as Fatḥ al-Mulhim notes. In short, whoever sets any created being level with Allah in His particular attributes, Creator, Provider, Absolute Master, Knower of the Seen and the Unseen, has committed shirk.

The wider teaching sharpens the edge. Opposition to the call of truth and victimisation of its followers are unforgivable, for while other sins flow from human weakness, opposing the call flows from insolence and haughtiness, and such rebellion is not forgiven unless a person confesses his mistakes and abandons the stance. The call brought in its pure and original form is a divine mission under the special succour of Allah, and to oppose it is to stand against Allah Himself. The believer has his roots in Allah and rejoins them whenever he turns back, while whoever attaches himself to anyone besides Allah is deprived of the true base in the universe. Good actions rooted in polytheism have only surface value, nullified by the slightest jolt and swept away in this world, whereas the good action based in monotheism unfolds its results in the Hereafter.

The inward reading strips the matter to the atom. To acknowledge anything other than Him, imagining even an atom as a cause of origination, is the very essence of shirk. No pardon is possible for it, while pardon remains possible for other things. Whoever seeks access to Allah through what he imagines is joining partners to Him without realizing it. No, He is Allah, the One.

### In short

- Shirk alone lies outside forgiveness, and the earlier verse 48 named it the great sin for the People of the Book who knew better, while this verse names straying for the pagans who crafted their own gods.
- The punishment is permanent because the crime is permanent, held by firm intention to the last breath.
- Three kinds of injustice divide all sin, never forgiven, forgivable, and retributed.
- Shirk is equality given to a created being, in worship, in love, in reverence, or in the attributes of Lordship.
- Even an atom imagined as a cause beside Him is the essence of the unforgiven.

<<<VERSE 4:117>>>
The idols and their instructor: "Instead of Allah, they only invoke female gods and they ˹actually˺ invoke none but a rebellious Satan" (4:117). The verse looks at what the pagans worship and finds the worshipper of stones to be, in reality, the worshipper of the devil.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Instead of Allah, they only invoke female gods"** describes the idols with feminine names, al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā, and Manāt with them, which can neither benefit nor harm anyone. Juwaybir reported from aḍ-Ḍaḥḥāk the claim behind them: the idolators said that the angels are Allah's daughters and that they worshipped them only so that they would bring them closer to Allah. Thus the angels became gods to them, the shapes were carved as girls, and the verdict followed that these idols resembled the daughters of Allah being worshipped. The Qur'ān answers them: "Have you then considered al-Lāt and al-ʿUzzā" (53:19), The angels are called females in their reckoning, though angels are but servants of the Most Gracious, and a kinship has been invented between Him and the Jinn (37:158). The gloss names the worshippers as the people of Makkah, who took lifeless idols in place of Allah.

**"and they ˹actually˺ invoke none but a rebellious Satan"** names the true object of the worship. This worship was Shayṭān's command to them, made to look fair and beautiful in their eyes, so that the worship paid him in reality. Allah said: "Did I not command you, O Children of Adam, that you should not worship Shaytan" (36:60), and the angels shall proclaim on the Day of Resurrection about the idolators who worshipped them: "Nay, but they used to worship the Jinn, most of them were believers in them" (34:41). By obeying him in this worship of female idols, they pray to a rebel intense in his rebellion.

### The enemy of every soul

The teaching beneath names that enemy. Man's real challenge in this world is from Satan, who has no real power, yet lures human beings with fanciful promises to fulfil wishful, imaginary desires, making them deviate from the straight path. His way of misguiding runs by two main types, superstition and interference in creation. Faith in superstition means expecting from something results that have no relation to it, as when a person believes, on self-styled suppositions, that someone other than Allah controls the affairs of the world, or immerses himself in worldly pursuits and hopes by wishful thinking to achieve the Hereafter without having worked for it.

The inward reading sees the mechanism. Lifeless things were set up and given names, and the delusion plunged ahead. The errors of conjecture were made the support, and so the truth was left far behind.

### In short

- The female idols, al-Lāt, al-ʿUzzā and Manāt, were daughters pretended for Allah, and the angels made into girls of stone.
- Behind the stones stands the rebel who made the worship seem fair, so the worshipper of idols is the worshipper of Shayṭān.
- Satan's lure runs by superstition and interference, promise without power, hope without work.

<<<VERSE 4:118>>>
The claim of the cursed one: "I will surely take hold of a certain number of Your servants" (4:118). The verse lets the outcast speak, and his boast sets the proportions of the peril.

### Phrase by phrase

**"cursed by Allah"** means expelled and banished from His mercy and His grace, removed from all good, distanced by the Real and sent away by His distancing.

**"I will surely take hold of a certain number of Your servants"** is his oath before his Lord, the taking of an appointed portion, a fixed and known share. Muqātil ibn Ḥayyān counted the share: of every one thousand, a single one for Paradise, the remaining nine hundred and ninety-nine for the Fire. One rendering prints it in figures, 999 of every thousand destined for the Fire, and adds that what Iblīs imprints in a person becomes that person's obligation and command.

The inward reading keeps the boast in proportion. None other than Iblīs is what they pray to, Iblīs whom the Real has distanced from His mercy. Yet the grip of God turns even Iblīs about, according to what the Creator desires. Did even an atom of self-sufficiency in him admit affirming, divinity would have an associate in him. It does not. Only the Real brings about in created beings their states and circumstances, and only He creates what follows the whisperings in the being led astray, the Ḍalālan. He is the Guide and the One who leads astray, the One of free disposal over all. He creates the power of expectations, the āmālan, in human hearts after the whisperings, and He makes their ugly actions, the aʿmālan, seem beautiful in their eyes.

### In short

- The curse is banishment from mercy, and the boast is a fixed share of servants.
- The share is fearsome in the arithmetic given, one of a thousand escaping.
- Even the outcast is turned in the grip of God, for guidance, misguidance and the heart's expectations are created by the Real alone.

<<<VERSE 4:119>>>
The programme of corruption: "I will certainly mislead them and delude them with empty hopes. Also, I will order them and they will slit the ears of cattle and alter Allah's creation" (4:119). The verse lists the rebel's four projects, and each one found takers.

### Phrase by phrase

**"I will certainly mislead them and delude them with empty hopes"** covers the inner work: leading them from the true path, tempting them to feign repentance, arousing false hopes, encouraging delay and procrastination with righteous deeds, and casting into hearts the thought of life enduring, of no rising and no reckoning to come, so that the misguidance itself turns attractive.

**"I will order them and they will slit the ears of cattle"** names the first outward corruption, the slitting that designated the animals as Bahīrah, Sāʾibah and Waṣīlah, as Qatādah and as-Suddī stated, the she-camels called bahāʾir among them.

**"and alter Allah's creation"** names the second. Al-Ḥasan ibn Abī al-Ḥasan al-Baṣrī read it as tattooing. In his Ṣaḥīḥ, Muslim recorded the prohibition of tattooing the face, one wording of which runs the curse of Allah on whoever does it. The Ṣaḥīḥ also records from Ibn Masʿūd the word:

> May Allah curse those who have tattoos and those who do it, who pluck their facial hairs and the one who does it for them, and those who make spaces between their teeth for the purpose of beauty, changing what Allah has created.

And why, he asked, should he withhold his curse from those the Prophet ﷺ had already cursed, the Book of Allah itself commanding it, citing the word "And whatsoever the Messenger gives you, take it, and whatsoever he forbids you, abstain from it" (59:7). Another reading takes the alteration further still, the changing of Allah's religion, the substituting of His religion with unbelief, making lawful what Allah has made unlawful and unlawful what He has made lawful.

**"And whoever takes Satan as a guardian instead of Allah, has certainly suffered a tremendous loss"** totals the account. The word walī, guardian, marks the following and the obeying, and the loss is manifest: this life and the Hereafter both gone, a loss that cannot be compensated or restored, ending in the Fire made perpetual.

### The division of the work

The inward reading assigns the work exactly. Misguidance is consigned to Iblīs only as a secondary cause. Otherwise, who is Iblīs? His job is the instilling of disquiet, no more. In the tracks of that disquieting, the Exalted Lord creates the misguidance, for guidance and misguidance, felicity and wretchedness, come from God: "Whomsoever God guides, he is guided, and whomsoever He misguides, you will not find for him a guiding friend" (18:17).

### In short

- The empty hope, the deferred repentance, the slit ears and the altered creation are his four projects, and all found takers.
- Tattooing, plucking and teeth-spacing drew the curse of Ibn Masʿūd, on the authority of the Messenger ﷺ and the command of the Book.
- Reading the alteration as the corruption of religion extends it to every lawful turned unlawful.
- The guardian taken instead of Allah costs both worlds, a loss nothing restores.
- Even here the disquiet is his, but the misguidance created after it belongs to the Real.

<<<VERSE 4:120>>>
The promiser unmasked: "Satan only makes them ˹false˺ promises and deludes them with ˹empty˺ hopes. Truly Satan promises them nothing but delusion" (4:120). The verse reads the fine print of every promise the rebel ever made.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Satan only makes them ˹false˺ promises"** lists the goods promised: long life, the attainment of worldly hopes, the assurance that there is no resurrection and no reckoning, even the whisper that there is no Paradise and no Hell. Deceit is the manner of his promising, and his supporters are tempted into believing themselves winners in this life and the Hereafter.

**"and deludes them with ˹empty˺ hopes"** exposes the goods as hopes with no reality, mere wishes inspired and held.

**"Truly Satan promises them nothing but delusion"** is the verdict: falsehood and lies, nothing else in the package. The end of the transaction is written in the Book: "And Shaytan will say when the matter has been decided: Verily, Allah promised you a promise of truth. And I too promised you, but I betrayed you. I had no authority over you" (14:22), and the passage runs on to the painful torment of the wrongdoers.

The inward reading sees the decree beneath. For those whose allotment is being led astray in the present state, punishment has been decreed in the end. What appears, appears by His deliberative power, and no sliver of being astray or being guided belongs to any other. To fully grasp the truth of Allah's Oneness is a rare and precious thing, and those who have mastered it are few.

### In short

- The promises run from long life to the denial of the Rising, and the deliverer of them owns nothing he promises.
- On the decided Day he confesses the betrayal and admits he had no authority.
- Delusion is the whole of the package, and the mastery of Oneness remains the rare escape from it.

<<<VERSE 4:121>>>
The address at the end of the road: "It is they who will have Hell as their home, and they will find no escape from it" (4:121). The verse hands the story to its setting.

### Phrase by phrase

**"It is they who will have Hell as their home"** names the people, those who liked and preferred what Shayṭān was promising and assuring them of, and names the home, the destination and abode on the Day of Resurrection.

**"and they will find no escape from it"** closes every exit: not able to avoid, avert, evade or elude the Fire, no escape and no sanctuary, no alternative to it.

### In short

- Hell is home to those who preferred the promises of the deluder.
- No exit, no refuge and no alternative awaits them there.

<<<VERSE 4:122>>>
The other address: "And those who believe and do good, We will soon admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow, to stay there for ever and ever. Allah's promise is ˹always˺ true. And whose word is more truthful than Allah's" (4:122). After the home of the misled, the verse opens the home of the guided.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And those who believe and do good"** describes the people. Truthful in heart and obedient in limb, they performed the righteous acts commanded and left off the evil they were prohibited from doing.

**"We will soon admit them into Gardens under which rivers flow"** describes the welcome. The rivers of Paradise, of wine, water, milk and honey, flow beneath its rooms and habitations, and its palaces overlook the streams. The plenty obeys its people, for the rivers are made to flow wherever their people think of them flowing.

**"to stay there for ever and ever"** fixes the term: without end or removal, never to die, never to be moved out.

**"Allah's promise is ˹always˺ true"** vouches for the whole: a true promise from Allah, which shall come to pass, promised and fulfilled in truth.

**"And whose word is more truthful than Allah's"** asks the closing question, and the answer is none, for no one is more truthful in statement and narration than Allah, and there is no deity worthy of worship nor Lord except Him. The proclamation of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ ran in his speech:

> The most truthful speech is Allah's Speech, and the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad. The worst matters are the newly invented in religion, every newly invented matter is an innovation, and every innovation is a heresy, and every heresy is in the Fire.

### The second way of Satan

The teaching of the passage names that second way, the interference in the divine scheme. Allah has created man with an innate desire to turn all his attention to Him. To interfere is to divert the attention toward things other than Allah, or to push acquisition through self-devised ways instead of the natural course He set. In this manner Satan interferes with man's observance of Allah's divine plan for the entire universe.

The inward reading reads the promise from the divine side. Those made fortunate by decree and speech were helped at the time they were brought into existence by generosity and power, and then surely what had been promised in reward was brought about through what they were given in a place of good return.

### In short

- Truthful hearts and obedient limbs receive the Gardens, where the rivers run wherever their people think.
- The stay is for ever and ever, and the promise binding it is true and fulfilled.
- No word is more truthful than Allah's, and the Prophet's ﷺ proclamation stands on that word.
- The second way of Satan is interference with the innate attention to Allah, and the fortunate were helped from their coming into existence.
