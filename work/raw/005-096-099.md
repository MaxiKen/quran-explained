<<<VERSE 5:96>>>
"It is lawful for you to hunt and eat seafood, as a provision for you and for travellers. But hunting on land is forbidden to you while on pilgrimage. Be mindful of Allah to Whom you all will be gathered"

This verse makes lawful for the believers the hunting and eating of sea game, as a provision for them and for travellers. It forbids hunting on land while they are on pilgrimage, and it ends by urging them to fear Allah, before whom they will be gathered.

### Phrase by phrase

**"It is lawful for you to hunt and eat seafood, as a provision for you and for travellers."** The material explains that sea game is lawful, and it names two kinds. The first is what is taken from the water alive, and the second is what the water throws ashore dead, and which is lawful to eat when salted. Abu Bakr as-Siddiq, Zayd ibn Thabit, Abdullah ibn Amr, Ikrimah, Mujahid, As-Suddi and Al-Hasan al-Basri are reported to have said similar things. The material cites a report from Jabir ibn Abdullah, who said that an army of three hundred men under Abu Ubaydah ibn al-Jarrah ate from a huge fish that the sea had cast ashore for eighteen days, and that two of its ribs were set in the ground and a camel passed beneath them. The report is recorded in the two sahihs.

**"But hunting on land is forbidden to you while on pilgrimage."** The material says that the hunting of land game is forbidden in ihram. Whoever hunts while in ihram must pay the expiation, along with the sin if he does it deliberately, as the earlier verse 5:95 set out. The material says the meat of game killed by mistake is not lawful to eat, and that the game killed for a pilgrim is not lawful to him in particular.

**"And if you are a pilgrim, the game is lawful when another hunts it."** The material gives two reports. As-Sa'b ibn Jaththamah gave a zebra to the Prophet ﷺ, and he returned it, saying that they were in ihram. In another report, Abu Qatadah hunted a zebra while not in ihram, and the Prophet ﷺ asked whether anyone had pointed at it or helped kill it, and when they said no, he said to eat. The material says these reports are in the two sahihs.

**"Be mindful of Allah to Whom you all will be gathered."** The material says that the verse ends with the reminder of return to Allah, and that the believers must obey what He commands and avoid what He forbids.

### The water of the sea

The material says that Ibn Abbas, Sa'id ibn Jubayr and Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib explained the first part of the verse as the fresh game taken from the sea and the second part as the salted food, and the material notes that Ibn Abbas read the water game as what is taken alive from the water. Malik ibn Anas recorded that Abu Hurayrah said a man asked the Prophet ﷺ whether a believer at sea could use the sea water for ablution, since their fresh water runs short and they grow thirsty. The Prophet ﷺ said that its water is pure and its dead are lawful. Ash-Shafi'i and Ahmad ibn Hanbal recorded it with the four Sunan compilers, and Al-Bukhari, At-Tirmidhi and Ibn Hibban graded it sahih. The material says that Ibn Jarir, At-Tabari, read the verse as the broad permission for the people of the sea.

### In short

- Sea game is lawful as food for the believers and for travellers.
- The hunting of land game is forbidden in the state of ihram, and the expiation is due for it.
- The believers must fear Allah, to whom they will return.

<<<VERSE 5:97>>>
"Allah has made the Ka'bah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary of well-being for all people, along with the sacred months, the sacrificial animals, and the offerings decorated with garlands. All this so you may know that Allah knows whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth, and that He has perfect knowledge of everything"

This verse says that Allah made the Ka'bah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary of well-being for all people, along with the sacred months, the sacrificial animals and the garlanded offerings. It says that all this is so that the believers may know that Allah knows what is in the heavens and on the earth, and has perfect knowledge of everything.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Allah has made the Ka'bah, the Sacred House, a sanctuary of well-being for all people."** The material explains that the word qiyam means that on which the stability and survival of something depends. The Ka'bah is the cause of the stability and tranquility of the people. The material says that as long as people turn towards it in prayer and the hajj continues, the world will remain safe, and that if no one made the hajj or turned towards it, the world would face a great punishment. Ata' is reported to have called the Ka'bah the deep sea that holds the world. The material says that this link is known through the spirit and through the long experience of peace in Makkah, and that the cause of this is known only through the words of the Creator.

**"Along with the sacred months, the sacrificial animals, and the offerings decorated with garlands."** The material says that these four things are a sanctuary and a sign for the Arabs, who regarded them as sacred even in the time of ignorance. The Ka'bah and the sacred area were made a haram, where hunting and the cutting of trees and grass are forbidden. The sacred months are the months when fighting is forbidden. The offerings and the garlanded animals were symbols of the sanctity of the pilgrimage.

**"All this so you may know that Allah knows whatever is in the heavens and whatever is on the earth."** The material says this knowledge is the purpose of the sanctuary. The One who made these things for the benefit of mankind knows the good of their lives, and He will count their deeds and reward them.

**"And that He has perfect knowledge of everything."** The material says that none of the deeds or affairs of the people escape the observation of Allah, and He will repay each one for good or evil.

### The sanctuary and peace

The material says that peace in the world comes from laws and their enforcement, and that the Ka'bah is a symbol and a means of peace, since in the sacred area even the strong cannot oppress the weak. The material reflects that the rites are signs of the trial of man, not sources of holiness in themselves, and that they remind the believer that God sees all his actions.

### In short

- The Ka'bah, the sacred months, the sacrificial animals and the garlanded offerings are a sanctuary for mankind.
- The purpose is to show that Allah has perfect knowledge of the heavens and the earth.
- The sanctuary is a sign of peace, and the rites are a sign of trial, not of holiness in themselves.

<<<VERSE 5:98>>>
"Know that Allah is severe in punishment and that He is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful"

This verse tells the believers that Allah is severe in punishment, and that He is also All-Forgiving and Most Merciful.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Know that Allah is severe in punishment."** The material says that the laws of lawful and unlawful are based on wisdom, and that whoever obeys them receives good, while whoever opposes them receives the worst of curse and punishment. The warning in this phrase is that Allah's punishment is severe.

**"And that He is All-Forgiving, Most Merciful."** The material says that a sin committed through forgetfulness or heedlessness is not punished at once, and that the door of forgiveness stays open for the one who repents and feels shame for what he did.

### In short

- Allah's punishment is severe for those who transgress His limits.
- His forgiveness is open to those who repent, and He is Most Merciful.
- The verse pairs warning and mercy so that the believers keep to the limits and do not despair.

<<<VERSE 5:99>>>
"The Messenger's duty is only to deliver the message. And Allah fully knows what you reveal and what you conceal"

This verse says that the Messenger's duty is only to deliver the message, and that Allah fully knows what people reveal and what they conceal.

### Phrase by phrase

**"The Messenger's duty is only to deliver the message."** The material explains that the Prophet ﷺ is to convey the commands of Allah to the people. Whether they accept or reject them, the gain or loss is theirs, and their rejection does not harm the Messenger.

**"And Allah fully knows what you reveal and what you conceal."** The material says that Allah cannot be deceived, since He knows what people do openly and in secret. The material says the verse was read in the sense that what is shown publicly and what is hidden of good and evil are both known to Him.

### The message and the knowledge of Allah

The material says that the duty of the Messenger is to convey the injunctions of Allah to the people He created. Whether they accept or reject them, the profit or loss of that choice falls on them alone, and their disbelief does no harm to the Messenger. The material adds that Allah cannot be deceived, since He knows all that people do openly and in secret. In the wider sense, the verse 5:99 is a reminder to the believers that their inner state is known to Him as well as their outward acts.

### In short

- The Messenger's duty is to deliver the message, not to compel belief.
- The gain and loss of acceptance or rejection belong to the people.
- Allah knows what is revealed and concealed.
