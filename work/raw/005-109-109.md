<<<VERSE 5:109>>>
"˹Consider˺ the Day Allah will gather the messengers and say, “What response did you receive?” They will reply, “We have no knowledge ˹compared to You˺! You ˹alone˺ are indeed the Knower of all unseen.”"

This verse calls the believers to consider the Day when Allah will gather the messengers and ask them what response they received from their peoples. The messengers will answer that they have no knowledge, and that Allah alone knows all that is unseen.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Consider the Day Allah will gather the messengers."** The material explains that the Day is the Day of Resurrection, and that the question is first put to the prophets, so that the whole creation sees that no one is exempt from reckoning. The material says the verse opens with an ominous image, since all of mankind will stand on the open ground, and the prophets are named because they will be asked first.

**"And say, What response did you receive?"** The material says the question is about how the peoples received the call to Allah and His religion. Did they follow the command or did they refuse and oppose it? The question is aimed at the peoples, though it is addressed to the prophets, who will be the first to testify to the good or bad deeds of their communities.

**"They will reply, We have no knowledge compared to You!"** The material explains that the answer is out of respect for the majesty of Allah. The messengers' knowledge reached only the outward conduct of their peoples, and not the secrets of their hearts. Mujahid, Al-Hasan al-Basri and As-Suddi are reported to have said the answer comes from the horror of that Day. Ibn Abbas is reported by Ali ibn Abi Talhah to have explained that the messengers said their knowledge is nothing compared to the encompassing knowledge of Allah. Ibn Jarir and Ibn Abi Hatim recorded these explanations.

**"You alone are indeed the Knower of all unseen."** The material says that the knowledge of the unseen belongs to Allah alone. Faith is a secret of the heart, and no one can know it with certainty without a revelation from Allah.

### The removal of a doubt

The material raises the question of how the prophets could say they have no knowledge of the faith of their peoples, since many believed at their hands and many opposed them. The material says that Imam Razi, as reported in Al-Bahr Al-Muhit, answered that the knowledge of certainty is one thing, and conjecture is another. The judgement of faith in this world rests on outward deeds, and the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah is the custodian of the secrets of the hearts. On the Day of Resurrection, judgement will be based on certainty, and not on conjecture.

The material cites 36:65, which says that on that Day a seal will be set on the mouths, and that the hands and feet will bear witness to what was done. It also cites 7:6, which says that Allah will question those to whom the Book was sent and the messengers, and 15:92-93, which says that He will call all of them to account for what they did.

### The prophets and their affection for people

The material says that the prophets could have mentioned the apparent response of their peoples. Instead they said nothing about what they knew and left the matter to the knowledge of Allah. The material says this is a sign of their affection for their peoples, since prophets do not speak against their communities unless they must. They used the legitimate excuse of not having certain knowledge.

### The five questions

The material reports a hadith recorded by Tirmidhi, in which the Prophet ﷺ said that no son of Adam will move a step on the Day of Resurrection until he is asked five questions: about his age and how he used it, about his youth and how he spent it, about his wealth and where he got it and how he spent it, and about what he did with his knowledge. The material says that the Prophet ﷺ told his Ummah how they would be tested, so that they might prepare their answers.

### In short

- On the Day of Resurrection, Allah will ask the messengers how their peoples responded.
- The messengers will reply that they have no knowledge, and that only Allah knows the unseen.
- The answer shows the affection of the prophets for their peoples, and it leaves judgement to certain knowledge.
