<<<VERSE 4:17>>>
"Allah only accepts the repentance of those who commit evil ignorantly or recklessly then repent soon after—Allah will pardon them. And Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise." This verse states that Allah accepts the repentance of those who do evil in ignorance and foolishness and repent soon afterwards, and He will pardon them. It follows the discussion of limits and disobedience.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Allah only accepts the repentance of those who commit evil ignorantly or recklessly"** means those who do evil in ignorance and foolishness.

**"Then repent soon after"** means repenting soon afterwards, even just before seeing the angel of death.

**"Allah will pardon them"** means He will forgive them.

**"And Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise"** affirms Allah's knowledge and wisdom.

### Repentance accepted until death

Every person who disobeys Allah, even willfully, is ignorant while committing the act of disobedience. Mujahid and others said every person who disobeys Allah by mistake or intentionally is ignorant until he refrains from the sin. Qatadah said the Companions used to say every sin the servant commits, he commits out of ignorance. Abdur-Razzaq narrated that Ma'mar said Qatadah said the Companions agreed that every sin committed by intention or otherwise is committed in ignorance. Ibn Jurayj said a narrator narrated that Mujahid said every person who disobeys Allah is ignorant while committing the act. Ata bin Abi Rabah said something similar. Abu Salih said Ibn Abbas commented it is because of one's ignorance that he commits the error. Ali bin Abi Talhah reported Ibn Abbas saying "and repent soon afterwards" means until just before he looks at the angel of death. Ad-Dahhak said everything before death is "soon afterwards." Al-Hasan Al-Basri said it means just before his last breath leaves his throat. Ikrimah said all of this life is "soon afterwards." Imam Ahmad recorded that Ibn Umar said the Messenger said, "Allah accepts the repentance of the servant as long as the soul does not reach the throat." At-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah also collected it. At-Tirmidhi said "Hasan Gharib." By mistake Ibn Majah mentioned Abdullah bin Amr as narrator, but the correct narrator is Abdullah bin Umar bin Al-Khattab. The verse cites 6:158, 40:84 and 66:8. It was commented on by Mujahid, Qatadah, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Abbas, Ad-Dahhak, Al-Hasan Al-Basri, Ikrimah, Ali bin Abi Talhah, Abu Salih, and others. Collectors include At-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah. The verse involves tawbah, shirk, ummah, adam, musa, aaron, yusuf, pharaoh, salah, zakah, and related terms. One reading adds a spiritual reading: to perceive maturity means decency and integrity, generosity and preservation of honour, keeping company with elders and striving to look to the good and perform acts of devotion. The one who is mature (rashid) is the one guided to his Lord. The numbers 89 and 972 appear here. The scholars and terms mentioned include aaron, Abu Dharr, Abu Hayyan, adam, Ahmad, al-Bahr, Ad-Dahhak, al-Ghazali, hadith, Al-Hasan Al-Basri, Holy Qur'an, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Marduwayh, Al-Khattab, al-Muhit, Mujahid, musa, pharaoh, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, salah, say, shirk, tawbah, ummah, yusuf, zakah.

### In short

- Allah accepts repentance of those who do evil in ignorance and repent soon after.
- Repentance is accepted until the soul reaches the throat.
- Allah will pardon them.
- The discussion adds the views of Mujahid, Qatadah and others.
- One reading adds a spiritual reading.

<<<VERSE 4:18>>>
"However, repentance is not accepted from those who knowingly persist in sin until they start dying, and then cry, 'Now I repent!' nor those who die as disbelievers. For them We have prepared a painful punishment." This verse states that repentance is not accepted from those who persist in sin until death approaches and then cry "Now I repent," nor from those who die as disbelievers. For them a painful punishment is prepared.

### Phrase by phrase

"Repentance is not accepted from those who knowingly persist in sin" refers to those who continue in sin.

"Until they start dying" means until death faces them.

**"And then cry, 'Now I repent!'"** is the late repentance not accepted.

**"Nor those who die as disbelievers"** refers to those who die in disbelief.

**"For them We have prepared a painful punishment"** is the punishment.

### Warning against late repentance

The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ad-Dahhak, Mujahid, and others. Collectors include At-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah. The verse cites 6:158, 40:84 and 66:8. It involves shariah, shirk, tawbah, ummah, and related terms. One reading adds a spiritual reading: whoever has been given charge over others and acts unjustly has the Lord as his adversary. The numbers 89 and 972 appear here. The scholars and terms mentioned include aaron, Abu Dharr, Abu Hayyan, adam, Ahmad, al-Bahr, Ad-Dahhak, david, al-Ghazali, hadith, Al-Hasan Al-Basri, Holy Qur'an, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Marduwayh, Al-Khattab, al-Muhit, Mujahid, musa, pharaoh, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, salah, say, shariah, shirk, tawbah, ummah, yusuf, zakah.

### In short

- Repentance is not accepted from those who persist in sin until death.
- Nor from those who die as disbelievers.
- A painful punishment is prepared for them.
- The verse follows the previous one.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
