<<<VERSE 4:37>>>
"Those who are stingy, promote stinginess among people, and withhold Allah's bounties. We have prepared for the disbelievers a humiliating punishment." This verse chastises stingy behavior of those who refuse to spend their money for what Allah ordered, assist in the spread of stingy behavior, and hide what Allah bestowed upon them of His bounties. Allah has prepared for the disbelievers a disgraceful torment.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Those who are stingy"** refers to misers who refuse to spend.

**"Promote stinginess among people"** means they assist in the spread of stingy behavior.

**"And withhold Allah's bounties"** means they hide what Allah bestowed upon them.

**"We have prepared for the disbelievers a humiliating punishment"** is the punishment.

### Censure of stinginess

The verse chastises stingy behavior. The Messenger of Allah said, "What disease is more serious than being stingy?" He also said, "Beware of being stingy, for it destroyed those who were before you, as it encouraged them to cut their relations and they did, and it encouraged them to commit sin and they did." The miser is ungrateful for Allah's favor. Similarly, Allah said, "Verily, man is ungrateful to his Lord. And to that he bears witness" (100:6-7). The verse cites 100:6-7 and 100:8. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ibn Zayd, al-Kalbi, and others. Collectors include al-Bukhari. The verse involves ihsan, kafir, kufr, shirk, and related terms. The number 37 appears here. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abu Hurairah, Abu Said, hadith, helpers, Ibn Abbas, Ibn al-Ashraf, Ibn Zayd, ihsan, kafir, al-Kalbi, kufr, madinah, Salaf, say, shirk, siwāhi.

### In short

- Stingy behavior is chastised.
- Misers assist in spreading stinginess.
- They hide Allah's bounties.
- A humiliating punishment is prepared for disbelievers.
