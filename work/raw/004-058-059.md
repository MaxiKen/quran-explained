<<<VERSE 4:58>>>
"Indeed, Allah commands you to return trusts to their rightful owners. And when you judge between people, judge with fairness. What a noble commandment from Allah to you! Surely Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing." This verse commands returning trusts to those to whom they are due, judging between men with justice, and affirms that this is an excellent teaching from Allah. He is Ever All-Hearer, All-Seer.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Indeed, Allah commands you to return trusts to their rightful owners"** commands returning trusts.

**"And when you judge between people, judge with fairness"** commands judging with justice.

**"What a noble commandment from Allah to you!"** affirms the excellence of the teaching.

**"Surely Allah is All-Hearing, All-Seeing"** affirms Allah hears and sees.

### Returning trusts and judging with justice

Allah commands that the trusts be returned to their rightful owners. Al-Hasan narrated that Samurah said that the Messenger of Allah said, "Return the trust to those who entrusted you, and do not betray those who betrayed you." This Hadith was recorded by Imam Ahmad and the collectors of Sunan. This command covers every matter which one is expected to look after, such as Allah's rights on His servants: praying, Zakah, fasting, penalties for sins, vows and so forth. It also includes the rights of the servants toward one another, such as what they entrust each other with, including cases that are not recorded or documented. Allah commands that all types of trusts be fulfilled. Those who do not implement this command in this life will have it extracted from them on the Day of Resurrection. It is recorded in the Sahih that the Messenger of Allah said, "The rights will be rendered back to those to whom they are due, and even the sheep that does not have horns will take revenge from the horned sheep." Ibn Jarir recorded that Ibn Jurayj said about this Ayah that it was revealed concerning 'Uthman bin Talhah. The Messenger of Allah took the key of the Ka'bah from him and entered it on the Day of the victory of Makkah. When the Prophet went out, he was reciting this Ayah. He then called 'Uthman and gave the key back to him. Ibn Jarir also narrated that 'Umar bin Al-Khattab said, "When the Messenger of Allah went out of the Ka'bah, he was reciting this Ayah." The verse cites 2:283, 12:54, 26:193, 28:26, 33:72, 81:21. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Abu Hurayrah, and others. Collectors include Ibn Jarir and al-Bukhari. The verse involves fard, hajj, haram, hijrah, kufr, mubah, nafl, salah, sawm, shariah, shirk, sunnah, tawhid, zakah, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include al-Abbas, Abu Dharr, Abu Hurairah, Abu Talib, al-Baihaqi, al-Basri, bukhari and muslim, egypt, fard, gabriel, hadith, al-Hajabi, hajj, haram, hijrah, Holy Qur'an, Ibn Abbas, Ibn Abd, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Sa'd, Ibn Talhah, al-Iman, job, joseph, kabah, Al-Khattab, kufr, lot, makkah, al-Mazhari, moses, mubah, Muhammad bin Al-Hanafiyyah, al-Mustafa, al-Muttalib, nafl, opening, qiyamah, quraysh, salah, Samurah, sawm, say, Shahr bin Hawshab, shariah, shirk, Shu'ayb, Sunan, sunnah, tawhid, the path, The Qur'an, Umar bin Al-Khattab, Uthman, Uthman ibn Talhah, wajib, Worlds, zakah. <<<VERSE 4:59>>>
"O believers! Obey Allah and obey the Messenger and those in authority among you. Should you disagree on anything, then refer it to Allah and His Messenger, if you truly believe in Allah and the Last Day. This is the best and fairest resolution." This verse commands believers to obey Allah, the Messenger, and those in authority among them. If they differ in anything, they should refer it to Allah and His Messenger, if they believe in Allah and the Last Day. This is better and more suitable.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O believers! Obey Allah"** commands obeying Allah.

**"And obey the Messenger"** commands obeying the Messenger.

**"And those in authority among you"** commands obeying those in authority.

**"Should you disagree on anything"** refers to differing in anything.

**"Then refer it to Allah and His Messenger"** commands referring it to Allah and His Messenger.

**"If you truly believe in Allah and the Last Day"** makes it conditional on belief.

**"This is the best and fairest resolution"** states this is better and more suitable.
