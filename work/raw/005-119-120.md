<<<VERSE 5:119>>>
"Allah will declare, “This is the Day when ˹only˺ the faithful will benefit from their faithfulness. Theirs are Gardens under which rivers flow, to stay there for ever and ever. Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him. That is the ultimate triumph.”"

This verse describes the declaration that Allah will make on the Day of Judgement: that this is the Day on which only the faithful will benefit from their faithfulness. They will have gardens with rivers flowing beneath them, where they will dwell forever, and Allah will be pleased with them and they with Him. This is the ultimate triumph.

### Phrase by phrase

**"This is the Day when only the faithful will benefit from their faithfulness."** In verse 119 Allah answers Isa after he refers the end of the disbelieving Christians to the will of his Lord. The material reports that Ad-Dahhak said Ibn Abbas explained the Day as the Day on which tawhid benefits those who believed in it. The material says that the truth of the truthful will profit them on that Day, and that the truthful include the prophets and the believers who delivered their messages and were loyal.

**"Theirs are Gardens under which rivers flow, to stay there for ever and ever."** The material explains that the gardens are in paradise, and that the believers will abide in them forever and will never be removed from them. The material says the rivers are rivers of water, milk, wine and honey, as the commentary notes. This phrase is the reward of the faithful.

**"Allah is pleased with them and they are pleased with Him."** The material says that Allah is pleased with them for their faith and their works, and that they are pleased with Him for His reward and the honour He has given them. A hadith is cited in the material that after the believers have entered paradise, Allah says that His pleasure is the greatest blessing, and that He will never again be displeased with them. The material cites 9:72, which says that the good pleasure of Allah is the greatest bliss.

**"That is the ultimate triumph."** The material says that this is the great success, beyond which there is no greater success. The material cites 37:61, which calls for the workers to work for this, and 83:26, which calls those who want to strive to strive for this.

### In short

- On the Day of Judgement only the faithful will benefit from their faithfulness.
- Their reward is gardens with rivers flowing, where they will dwell forever.
- Allah's pleasure with them, and theirs with Him, is the ultimate triumph.

<<<VERSE 5:120>>>
"To Allah ˹alone˺ belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth and everything within. And He is Most Capable of everything"

This verse says that the kingdom of the heavens and the earth, and everything in them, belongs to Allah alone, and that He has power over everything.

### Phrase by phrase

**"To Allah alone belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth and everything within."** The material explains that Allah created everything, owns everything, and controls its affairs. The material says that everything and everyone is in His domain and under His power and will. The material says the stores of the heavens and earth, such as the rain, the lands, the vegetation and the fruits, belong to Him, and so does all that is in them.

**"And He is Most Capable of everything."** The material says that Allah is able to do all things, and that there is none like Him, no rival, no ancestor, no son, no wife, and no lord or god besides Him. The material notes that the power to reward the truthful and punish the liar belongs to Him alone.

### The last Surah

The material reports, through a chain that runs through Ibn Wahb, that Abdullah ibn Amr said the last Surah to be sent down was Surat Al-Ma'idah.

### The lesson of the Day

The material notes that according to the Qur'an and the Sunnah, truth and falsehood cover both words and deeds, and that a person who prays with care in public and in private is called a true servant of Allah. The material says that Allah Ta'ala speaks of the Day of Judgement as the Day of truth, and that this world was made as a test, so that man may show that his claims to faith are true. A man may claim a place in paradise on the strength of false hopes, but on the Day of Judgement only truth will be of benefit.

### In short

- The kingdom of the heavens and the earth belongs to Allah alone.
- He created all things, controls them, and has power over everything.
- The test of this world is whether the claim of faith is true.
