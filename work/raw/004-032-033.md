<<<VERSE 4:32>>>
"And do not crave what Allah has given some of you over others. Men will be rewarded according to their deeds and women equally according to theirs. Rather, ask Allah for His bounties. Surely Allah has perfect knowledge of all things." This verse forbids craving what Allah gave some over others, states that men and women are rewarded according to their deeds, commands asking Allah for His bounties, and affirms Allah's perfect knowledge.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And do not crave what Allah has given some of you over others"** forbids wishing for what others were endowed with.

**"Men will be rewarded according to their deeds"** means men earn reward for their works.

**"And women equally according to theirs"** means women also earn reward for their works.

**"Rather, ask Allah for His bounties"** commands asking Allah for His favor.

**"Surely Allah has perfect knowledge of all things"** affirms Allah's knowledge.

### Do not wish for others' endowments

Umm Salamah asked the Prophet why men go to battle and women do not, and women earn half the inheritance men get. Allah sent down this verse. At-Tirmidhi also recorded it. Ibn Jarir said each person will earn his wages for his works, reward if good, punishment if evil. Al-Walibi reported from Ibn Abbas that this verse is about inheritance, indicating each person gets his due share. Allah then directed servants to what benefits them: do not wish for what others were endowed with, for this is a decision that will come to pass and wishing does not alter its decree. However, ask Me of My favor and I will grant it. Allah knows who deserves this life and gives riches or poverty accordingly, and who deserves the Hereafter and directs him to righteousness. The verse cites 33:6. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Umm Salamah, and others. Collectors include At-Tirmidhi and Ibn Jarir. The verse involves say, jihad, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abū Bakr, Ahmad, Anṣārī, al-Bahr, hadith, Ibn Abbas, al-Islām, jihad, Kattānī, lot, al-Mishit, say, Umm Salama, Umm Salamah, Umm Salmah, wa-s’alū.

### In short

- Do not crave what Allah gave some over others.
- Men and women are rewarded according to their deeds.
- Ask Allah for His bounties.
- Allah has perfect knowledge.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.

<<<VERSE 4:33>>>
"And We have appointed heirs to what has been left by parents and next of kin. As for those you have made a pledge to, give them their share. Surely Allah is a Witness over all things." This verse states that heirs are appointed to what parents and next of kin leave, and that those to whom a pledge was made should be given their share. Allah is a Witness over all things.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And We have appointed heirs to what has been left by parents and next of kin"** means heirs are appointed to inheritance.

**"As for those you have made a pledge to, give them their share"** commands giving the share to those pledged.

**"Surely Allah is a Witness over all things"** affirms Allah's witness.

### Heirs and pledges

The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ad-Dahhak, and others. Collectors include Al-Bukhari and Ibn Jarir. The verse cites 33:6 and 33:23. It involves jihad, and related terms. The number 875 appears here. The scholars and terms mentioned include ansar, al-Bahr, Ad-Dahhak, emigrants, Godḍ, Ibn Abbas, jihad, lot, Al-Madinah, al-Mishit, al-Musayyab, Umm Salmah.

### In short

- Heirs are appointed to what parents and next of kin leave.
- Those pledged should be given their share.
- Allah is a Witness over all things.
- The verse follows the previous one.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
