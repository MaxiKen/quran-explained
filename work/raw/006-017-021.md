<<<VERSE 6:17>>>
The verse states that Allah alone brings harm and benefit. "If Allah touches you with harm, none can undo it except Him. And if He touches you with a blessing, He is Most Capable of everything." Harm and good both come from Allah. No one can remove what He sends, and no one can withhold what He gives. This is a basic article of faith.

### Phrase by phrase

**"If Allah touches you with harm"** means affliction, hardship, or penury. No one can remove it but Allah.

**"none can undo it except Him"** means no one can lift the harm but Allah.

**"And if He touches you with a blessing"** means good fortune, blessing, or wealth. No one can stop Allah's grace.

**"He is Most Capable of everything"** means Allah has power over all things. Whatever mercy Allah grants, none can withhold it, and whatever He withholds, none can grant it thereafter (35:2).

Allah is the Irresistible above His servants. He is the All-Wise and Well-Acquainted with all things. He places everything in its rightful place.

Imam al-Baghawi reported from Abdullah ibn Abbas that the Prophet ﷺ gave advice about remembering Allah, asking only Allah, and acting patiently. If all those created combined to bring a benefit in which Allah kept no share, they could not do it. If they combined to inflict a harm not in your lot, they could not do it. There is great good and barakah in patience. The help of Allah is with patience. This hadith appears in Tirmidhi and the Musnad of Ahmad with sound chains.

The verse is a revolutionary belief. It made Muslims shed dependence on the created and rely on the Creator alone. Yet the Ummah has started going off course, calling on created beings in distress instead of Allah. Praying through the mediation of prophets and men of Allah is permissible. But direct calling on a created being is rebellion. May Allah keep Muslims on the straight path.

Terms: al-Baghawi, barakah, lot, migration, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, say, ummah.

### The mystical reading

The mystical reading says the wellspring of pain and remedy is one. The origin of blessing and trial is one. The rising place of unbelief and faith is one. In togetherness they share one color, and in dispersion they show many colors. On your two cheeks are both unbelief and faith. In your two lips are both pain and remedy. [DS 810].

### In short

- Allah alone brings harm and benefit.
- No one can remove what He sends or withhold what He gives.
- He is the Irresistible, All-Wise, and All-Aware.
- Patience brings the help of Allah.
- The verse calls Muslims to rely on the Creator alone.

<<<VERSE 6:18>>>
The verse affirms Allah's supreme dominion. "He reigns supreme over His creation. And He is the All-Wise, All-Aware." He is the Vanquisher over His servants. Nothing can overpower or defeat Him. Everything is subservient to Him. He is above His servants as is appropriate for Him. He is the All-Wise in His creating, handling of matters, and institution of laws. He is the All-Aware and nothing is hidden from Him.

### Phrase by phrase

**"He reigns supreme over His creation"** means Allah is the Irresistible, the All-Conquering, the Vanquisher over His servants. He is powerful and dominant. Nothing can overpower or defeat Him. Everything is subservient to Him. The mastery of Allah prevails over His servants in the absolute sense. No human being comes out successful in everything they do.

**"And He is the All-Wise"** means His is the wisdom in creating, handling of matters, and institution of laws. Everything He does is essential wisdom. Perfection in knowledge and power are the sole attributes of Almighty Allah and belong only to Him.

**"All-Aware"** means He is the One who knows everything. He knows the innermost thoughts and outward actions of His servants. Nothing is hidden from Him.

One commentary says He breaks the pleasures of the servants.

One commentary says no human being controls the universe, so no human can cause events alone. God alone controls the factors.

Terms: 18, migration, al-Qahir, al-Hakim, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, remembrance, say, ummah, unseen.

### The mystical reading

The mystical reading says He is the Severe over His servants. He breaks the pleasures of the servants.

### In short

- Allah reigns supreme over His creation.
- Nothing can overpower or defeat Him.
- He is the All-Wise and the All-Aware.
- Perfection in knowledge and power belong to Him alone.
- Everyone remains under His power and control.

<<<VERSE 6:19>>>
The verse challenges the idolaters to name the greatest witness and establishes the Quran as a warning. "Ask them, O Prophet, 'Who is the best witness?' Say, 'Allah is! He is a Witness between me and you. And this Quran has been revealed to me so that, with it, I may warn you and whoever it reaches. Do you pagans testify that there are other gods besides Allah?' Then say, 'I will never testify to this!' And say, 'There is only One God. And I totally reject whatever idols you associate with Him.'"

### Phrase by phrase

**"Ask them, O Prophet, 'Who is the best witness?'"** means: what thing is the most great in witness? The answer is Allah. He is the greatest and most honoured witness. He is a witness between you and me. He knows what I have brought to you and how you respond.

**"Allah is! He is a Witness between me and you"** means Allah knows what I brought you and what you will answer me with. One commentary says there is no bearing witness more truthful than the Real's own bearing witness to what He witnessed at the first. That is His words, "God bears witness that there is no god but He" (3:18). This is the Real's bearing witness that the Real is the Real. Abū ʿAbdallāh Qurashī said this is a teaching for the servants and a right guidance for the seekers. Put aside the path of resistance, lest you fall into the pit like the abandoned Iblis.

One commentary says God bears witness to His oneness, His unity, and His self-sufficiency. Others assent to the truth of that to which He bears witness concerning Himself. He Himself bore witness to His lordship, greatness, and oneness, for no one else was worthy. The creatures cannot reach the core of His majesty. Jaʿfar ibn MuḤammad said the bearing witness of people is built on four pillars: following commands, avoiding prohibitions, contentment, and approval. People's bearing witness is of three sorts: the common people emerge from associationism, the elect enter contemplation, and the elect of the elect receive the breeze of companionship. The self-purifier sees all from Him. The recognizer sees all in Him. The tawhid-voicer sees all as He.

**"And this Quran has been revealed to me so that, with it, I may warn you and whoever it reaches"** means the Quran is a warner for all who hear of it. One commentary says Gabriel brought down this Quran. One commentary says it was revealed to me that I may warn you, people of Makkah, and whomever it may reach among men and jinn. One commentary says the Prophet ﷺ is the Last Prophet and the Quran will keep reaching until the Day of Qiyamah. It will continue to be recited and taught right through the Day of Qiyamah. Sayyidna Said ibn Jubayr said: to whomever the Quran has reached, he has become like one who has visited Muhammad ﷺ. Another hadith says: to whomever the Quran has reached, I am his warner. The Prophet ﷺ asked his Companions emphatically to convey his instructions and teachings to people, even if it be one ayah. Abdullah ibn Masud reports that the Prophet ﷺ praised those who convey his sayings to the Ummah.

Ar-Rabi' bin Anas said those who follow the Messenger ought to call to what he called to. Allah said next: "But those of the sects that reject it, the Fire will be their promised meeting place" (11:17).

**"Do you pagans testify that there are other gods besides Allah?"** is the question put to the idolaters. "I will never testify to this" is the Prophet's answer. Similarly, in another ayah, Allah said: "Then if they testify, testify not you with them" (6:150).

**"There is only One God. And I totally reject whatever idols you associate with Him"** means He is one God, the only one worthy of worship, who has no partners, sharers, or associates in His pristine Divinity. I am free from all that you associate as partners with Him.

The event of revelation: a deputation of the people of Makkah came to the Prophet ﷺ and asked for a witness. Thereupon the verse was revealed. The witness of Allah refers to those miracles and signs which Allah made manifest to confirm the veracity of the prophethood of the Prophet ﷺ. Addressing the people of Makkah, it was said: Do you stand to witness against Allah by saying that there are other gods along with Allah? If that is so, you are responsible for your fate. As for me, I bear no such witness.

Tawhid is the point. The path of the Quran is the path of oneness. Migration and the description of the Ummah are part of the good news the previous prophets gave. The verse is about the best witness and the warning to all who hear.

Terms: ʿAbdallāh, Abū ʿAbdallāh, gabriel, hadith, Jaʿfar, Jaʿfar ibn MuḤammad, makkah, migration, MuḤammad, qiyamah, Qurashī, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, say, tawhid, the path, ummah.

### The mystical reading

The mystical reading says God bears witness to His oneness. Others assent to the truth of that to which He bears witness. The self-purifier sees all from Him. The recognizer sees all in Him. The tawhid-voicer sees all as He. Say "God," then leave them (6:91).

### In short

- Allah is the greatest and most truthful witness.
- The Quran was revealed to warn all who hear it.
- The Prophet ﷺ bears no witness to other gods.
- There is only One God, and the Prophet is innocent of shirk.
- The verse refutes every claim of partners with Allah.

<<<VERSE 6:20>>>
The verse says the People of the Book recognize the Prophet as they recognize their own children. "Those to whom We gave the Scripture recognize him to be a true prophet as they recognize their own children. Those who have ruined themselves will never believe."

### Phrase by phrase

**"Those to whom We gave the Scripture"** means those to whom We gave the knowledge of the Torah and the Injil. One commentary names Abdullah ibn Salam and his followers, and contrasts them with Ka'b ibn al-Ashraf and his followers. One commentary says the Jews who were given the Torah and the Christians who were given the Gospel recognize the Prophet Muhammad completely, just as they recognize their own children from other children. One commentary says those to whom We have given the Scripture recognize him by the descriptions of him in their Scripture as they recognize their sons.

**"recognize him to be a true prophet as they recognize their own children"** means the People of the Book know him well. This is because they received good news from the previous Messengers about the coming of Muhammad ﷺ. The Torah and Injil carry a full description of the physical features of the Prophet ﷺ, his home city, his post-emigration place of residence, his habits and morals and achievements, all in such detail that it leaves no room for doubt. In fact, the Torah and Injil carry even the detailed description of his noble Sahabah. Therefore, there was no possibility that anyone who recited the Torah and Injil and believed in them would be unable to recognize the Prophet ﷺ.

The simile is of children, not of parents recognizing children, because the recognition of children by parents is most detailed and certain. Children remain before parents day and night under all conditions. So parents recognize their children better than children recognize their parents.

Sayyidna Abdullah ibn Salam belonged to the Jewish faith, then he became a Muslim. Sayyidna Faruq al-A'zam asked him why the People of the Book recognize the Prophet as their own sons. Abdullah ibn Salam said: We know the Holy Prophet ﷺ from his qualities and features revealed by Allah Almighty through the Torah. This knowledge is certain and absolute.

Sayyidna Zayd ibn Sa'nah, who first belonged to the People of the Book, recognized the Prophet ﷺ through the Torah and the Injil. He tested one quality: his forbearance dominates his anger. He found it true and became a Muslim.

**"Those who have ruined themselves will never believe"** means those who have brought loss to themselves by disbelief will not believe. They have lost their own souls.

One commentary says people do not accept the truth because of the fear of some immediate loss. The cost of acceptance is that man must give up status and ready-made advantages. When a man is not ready to make these sacrifices, he does not accept the truth. He pushes himself into eternal loss for instant gain. On the Day of Judgment, when all the veils are torn asunder, he will see that all supports except that of God were false. He will contradict his own statements. The edifice of false beliefs will be destroyed.

Terms: Abdullah ibn Salam, al-A'zam, Ibn al-Ashraf, Ibn Salam, injil, migration, al-Mustafa, Not, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, say, torah, ummah.

### The mystical reading

The mystical reading says reality is familiar to man. It speaks the same silent language throughout the universe. The Jews and Christians had heralded the advent of the last Prophet. In spite of things being so clear, people do not accept the truth because of the fear of some immediate loss.

### In short

- The People of the Book recognize the Prophet as they recognize their own children.
- The Torah and Injil describe him in detail.
- Abdullah ibn Salam and Zayd ibn Sa'nah recognized him and became Muslim.
- Those who ruin themselves by disbelief will not believe.
- Refusal of truth is often driven by fear of losing status or advantage.

<<<VERSE 6:21>>>
The verse asks who could be more unjust. "Who does more wrong than those who fabricate lies against Allah or deny His signs? Indeed, the wrongdoers will never succeed."

### Phrase by phrase

**"Who does more wrong than those who fabricate lies against Allah"** means lying about Allah is a great wrong. One commentary says the greatest wrongdoer attributes a partner to Allah or denies Allah's words. One commentary says who does greater wrong than he who invents a lie against Allah by associating other gods with Him. One commentary says none does greater evil than he who ascribes an associate to God.

**"or deny His signs"** means the wrongdoer denies Allah's proofs and refuses the clear signs.

**"Indeed, the wrongdoers will never succeed"** means both of these people will never acquire success. One commentary says the wrongdoers, the disbelievers and idolaters, will not be successful, will not escape or be safe from Allah's torment. One commentary says the evildoers shall not prosper on account of this. One commentary says those who do wrong by ascribing partners to Allah and denying His verses will never succeed if they do not repent.

One commentary says those who are not ready to make the sacrifices required by the truth push themselves into eternal loss. In this world of trial they find explanations and justifications in their support. But on the Day of Judgment, when all the veils are torn asunder, they will see that all supports except that of God were false, and they will have no alternative but to contradict their own statements. They will become witnesses against their own falsity. The edifice of false beliefs they constructed will be so totally destroyed that it will seem never to have existed.

The number 20 marks this verse. Say to them: the wrongdoers will not succeed. Not in this world in the true sense, and not in the Hereafter. The Ummah must convey the message. The migration and the path of the Prophet ﷺ are the path of truth. The Injil and the Torah point to al-Mustafa. The wrongdoer is the one who denies the signs. The People of the Book recognize him. Abdullah ibn Salam and al-A'zam knew him. Ibn al-Ashraf refused. Ar-Rabi' bin Anas said to call to what the Messenger called to. Say the truth and leave them.

Terms: Abdullah ibn Salam, al-A'zam, injil, migration, al-Mustafa, Not, Ar-Rabi' bin Anas, say, torah, ummah.

### The mystical reading

The mystical reading says whenever a man makes entities other than God the centre of his attention, a magical halo gradually forms around them. He indulges in wishful thinking. But on the Day of Judgment, when all the veils are torn asunder, he will see that all supports except that of God were absolutely false. He will contradict his own statements. He will become a witness against his own falsity.

### In short

- Fabricating lies against Allah is the greatest wrong.
- Denying Allah's signs is also a great wrong.
- The wrongdoers will never succeed.
- They will face Allah's torment.
- Repentance is the only way out.
