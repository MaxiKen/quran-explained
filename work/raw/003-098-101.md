<<<VERSE 3:98>>>
The address returns to the People of the Book with a witness in the room: "Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “O People of the Book! Why do you deny the revelations of Allah, when Allah is a Witness to what you do?”" They had learned the truth about the Messenger from the previous Prophets, who brought glad tidings of his coming. Refusing him therefore happens before the One who watched them receive that knowledge.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “O People of the Book! Why do you deny the revelations of Allah"**: why do you reject the Ayat of Allah, refuse the truth and reject what the Messenger ﷺ was sent with, Muhammad and the Qur'an? Why do you disbelieve in God’s verses, the Qur’ān, and reject the proof of the truth of your prophet-hood, including the proofs that came in the Torah and the Gospel?

**"when Allah is a Witness to what you do?”"**: Allah is Witness over what you do, of disbelief, by concealing the Truth and engaging in sins. He watches over everything you do, witnessing it, and will reward you accordingly. He is not unaware, and He will hold them responsible for their actions, on "The Day whereon neither wealth nor sons will avail" (26:88).

### What they already knew

Truth had reached them before refusal. Earlier Prophets and honourable Messengers had announced an unlettered Arab Hashimi Prophet from Makkah, master of Adam's children, Final Prophet and Messenger of the Lord of heavens and earth. Defiance therefore rejects a Messenger they were ordered to announce, not an unknown claimant. Allah witnesses their conduct and will hold them responsible.

### When truth puts a self-made religion at risk

When truth's call arrives pure, self-styled religion faces a threat to its interests. Its servants resist a new messenger with fallacious objections meant to turn hearers away. Basics receive little stress inside their system, hair-splitting over religion's relative aspects matters more, and virtue becomes full adherence to a man-made religious form. Rabbit meat may be shunned because saints did not consume it, alongside legalised illegal things. Facing al-Bayt al-Maqdis with compass precision can become paramount while daily activities remain unoriented toward God.

Sacrifice, not religious form, determines virtue. God's true servant offers love as his gift to his Lord, values no material thing above Him, accepts prestige loss for recognising truth, and spends wealth for God's cause. Cherished ambitions sacrificed at that price mark the virtuous God-worshipper.

### Called by the law, barred by decree

One inward reading sees a pilgrimage paradox. The law calls the People of Scripture and makes pilgrimage obligatory for them, yet true reality, ḥaqīqa, and divine subjugation, qahr, bar them from it. They are summoned legally and by command, madʿuwūna sharʿan wa-amran, while being banished by decree and subjugation, maṬrūdūna ḥukman wa-qahran.

### In short

- Denial happens before Allah's witnessing eye, despite the glad tidings of the unlettered Prophet already conveyed by earlier prophets.
- He will hold them responsible on a Day when wealth and sons do not avail (26:88).
- The purity of the truth threatens self-made religion, whose virtue is sacrifice, not form.

<<<VERSE 3:99>>>
Refusal becomes obstruction: "Say, “O People of the Book! Why do you turn the believers away from the Way of Allah—striving to make it ˹appear˺ crooked, while you are witnesses ˹to its truth˺? And Allah is never unaware of what you do.”" They do not merely decline the path. They bar believers from it, call its straightness crooked, and do so while their own Book testifies that Islam is the upright religion pleasing to God.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Say, “O People of the Book! Why do you turn the believers away from the Way of Allah"**: why do you stop those who have believed from the path of Allah, bar believers causing them to turn away from God's way, His religion, by denying the truth of the Prophet and concealing His graces? Why do you stop people who have faith from the Religion of Allah, wanting to turn its people from guidance to misguidance?

**"striving to make it ˹appear˺ crooked"**: desiring to make it crooked, inclining away from the truth, wanting to twist it from the truth to falsehood. The word is the verbal noun meaning "made crooked."

**"while you are witnesses ˹to its truth˺?"**: while you yourselves are witnesses, while your own knowledge says Islam is the upright religion pleasing to God, as your Book states. Its truth confirms what your Scriptures contain.

**"And Allah is never unaware of what you do.”"**: God is not heedless of what you do, in the way of unbelief and mendacity. Allah is not oblivious to your disbelief: concealing the Truth and committing transgressions. Instead, He gives you respite until your appointed time and then requites you. They will be repaid accordingly.

### Whom they tried to turn

This verse was revealed about those who invited ʿAmmar [Ibn Yasir] and his fellow Muslims to join their religion: Judaism. The obstruction was aimed first at believers in Allah, Muhammad and the Qur'an, to drive them back from the Religion of Allah and His worship.

An inward question sharpens the absurdity. How can one who is barred himself bar another? Surely in this is a secret belonging to the divine Lordship.

### In short

- The People of the Book bar believers from Allah's way and call it crooked, though their Book witnesses Islam's uprightness.
- ʿAmmar and his fellow Muslims were among those invited back to Judaism.
- Allah is never unaware, and respite lasts only until the appointed time.

<<<VERSE 3:100>>>
The warning is redirected to the believers themselves: "O believers! If you were to yield to a group of those who were given the Scripture, they would turn you back from belief to disbelief". The danger is not argument. It is suggestion, memory and tribal pride, used by people who envy the favour given to the believers in the sending of the Messenger.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O believers! If you were to yield to a group of those who were given the Scripture"**: O you who believe! If you obey a group of those who were given the Scripture, Jews and Christians, in what they say and accept their opinion in what they claim. If you obey a party of those who have received the Scripture, the Torah.

**"they would turn you back from belief to disbelief"**: they would render you disbelievers after you have believed, make you disbelievers in Allah and Muhammad after your belief in Allah and the message of Muhammad. The return comes through their envy and their misguidance.

### The envy behind obedience

Allah warns His believing servants against obeying the People of the Book, who envy the believers for the favour that Allah gave them by sending His Messenger ﷺ. Similarly, Allah said: "Many of the People of the Scripture (Jews and Christians) wish that they could turn you away as disbelievers after you have believed, out of their own envy" (2:109).

### Shammas ibn Qays and the poems of war

A particular event stands behind the warning. Shammas ibn Qays, a Jew with chronic malice against Muslims, saw the Ansar tribes Aws and Khazraj sitting together amiably. Malevolence drove him to sow discord. He arranged for a man to remind both tribes of their long pre-Islamic wars and the self-congratulating poems each side had composed about tribal pride. Recitation should happen while they sat together.

Poems heated the gathering. Charges and counter-charges rose until a fresh war's place and time were nearly set. News reached the Holy Prophet ﷺ, who came and rebuked them: he was present amidst them, after Islam had united them in friendship, so was this sheer ignorance and a wish to revert to kufr?

Warning entered their hearts. They recognised a Satanic slip, embraced each other, wept and repented. These verses were revealed against that background. This event appears in Ruh al-Ma'ani as narrated by Ibn Ishaq, while others narrate it from Zaid ibn Aslam.

Another narration through Ayyub from Ikrimah, transmitted by al-Mu'ammil ibn Isma'il, pictures a Jew in a mixed assembly reciting one tribe's war poetry until rival claims, battle cries for Aws and Khazraj, and armed lines formed.

### Caution, taqwa, and the keeper within

Trial never leaves this world. Satan's temptations create a standing risk that belief will turn to unbelief, and death may arrive while the spirit of faith is absent and amends are almost impossible. A person must therefore become his own keeper.

Taqwa is religion's mainspring: an unshakeable sense, held until death, that every action is accountable to God. This is the true and straight path. Turning from that path usually rearranges religious priorities. Secondary matters get made fundamental and are stressed as though they deserved the place of God-fearing concern for the Hereafter. Different groups stress different secondary aspects, and such alterations breed differences, serious schisms and eventually sects. Taqwa keeps attention on the one God, without it, trivial matters come to the forefront, disunity follows, and disunity carries calamity in both worlds. Unity becomes natural only when all emphasis rests on taqwa.

Estrangement also spreads socially. Anyone who becomes part of the estranged circle or follows God's enemy into ruinous companionship will be cast down to the same level.

### In short

- Obeying an envious group of the People of the Book returns believers to disbelief (2:109).
- Shammas ibn Qays engineered the recital of pre-Islamic war poems until Aws and Khazraj nearly set a time and place for fresh war.
- The keeper against Satan's turning is taqwa, unshakeable accountability, which alone makes unity natural.

<<<VERSE 3:101>>>
The defence against that turning is named: "How can you disbelieve when Allah’s revelations are recited to you and His Messenger is in your midst? Whoever holds firmly to Allah is surely guided to the Straight Path". Disbelief is far from a community receiving revelation day and night, with the Messenger reciting and conveying it. The verse was revealed while swords were being drawn inside that very community.

### Phrase by phrase

**"How can you disbelieve when Allah’s revelations are recited to you and His Messenger is in your midst?"**: amazement and rebuke frame the question. How could you disbelieve in Allah after faith when the biggest reason for firm faith stands with you? Allah's verses are recited to you while Messenger Muhammad clarifies them.

**"Whoever holds firmly to Allah is surely guided to the Straight Path"**: whoever depends upon Allah, then he is indeed guided to the right path. He who holdeth fast to Allah, he who adheres to Allah's Religion and His Book, is directed along a clear, solid way, which is Islam, it is also said this means: he has become firm on this way. Whoever holds on firmly to the Book of Allah and His Messenger's way of life, Allah has guided them to the right path.

### Two tribes with swords, and a voice raised between them

The Prophet, Allah bless him and give him peace, stood between two armies, read this verse and raised his voice. Hearing stopped talk and produced listening. Once recitation ended, weapons dropped and embraces and crying followed.

Zayd ibn Aslam names the instigator as Shas ibn Qays, an elderly Jew formed in the pre-Islamic period, a great disbeliever with extreme hate and intense envy toward Muslims. Seeing Aws and Khazraj companions gathered in friendly reconciliation after former enmity infuriated him. "The people of Banu Qaylah have united in this land," he said.

Ibn 'Abbas places swords in the story: mention of the pre-Islamic feud led Aws and Khazraj to brandish weapons at each other. News reached the Messenger of Allah ﷺ, he went to them, and revelation ran from this verse through "And hold fast, all of you together, to the cable of Allah, and do not separate" (3:103). Another chain has talk turning to anger, arms taken and two groups marching toward each other, before revelation came down through "He did save you from it".

This verse was also revealed about Mu'adh [Ibn Jabal] and his companions.

### Amazement, covenants and the most amazing faith

"And what is the matter with you that you believe not in Allah! While the Messenger invites you to believe in your Lord; and He has indeed taken your covenant, if you are real believers" (57:8). A Hadith states that one day, the Prophet said to his Companions, "Who among the faithful believers do you consider has the most amazing faith?" They said, "The angels." He said, "Why would they not believe, since they are with their Lord?" They mentioned the Prophets, and the Prophet ﷺ said, "Why would they not believe while the revelation is sent down to them?" They said, "Then, we are." He said, "Why would not you believe when I am among you?" They asked, "Who has the most amazing faith?" The Prophet said, "A people who will come after you and who will find only books that they will believe in."

Trust and reliance in Allah form the basis for right guidance, avoidance of wickedness, acquisition of truth and achievement of righteous aims.

Iʿtisam, holding firmly to Allah, means staying totally committed to iman. It denotes affirming His Being and Attributes, remaining staunchly faithful to His ordinances, and refusing to be lured into supporting any adversary. Such a person "is surely guided to the straight path", and being on that path is the key to all the good and benefit promised by Allah's Word.

### Holding fast, found rather than achieved

Disbelief's shade should not cross a heart lit by rising suns of deeper knowledge, ʿirfān. When day comes, night slips away. Holding fast is nevertheless received, not self-manufactured. Guidance from God in the beginning, bidāya, produces holding in the end, nihāya, holding does not create guidance, hidāya. Sincerity defines the true state: taking oneself to Him, persevering in flight toward Him and continually appealing for His help. Remove separation's covering from the innermost self and no atom seems to belong to, or come from, anything other than God. Those who hold fast are those held fast by Him. The leader of first and last said, "I seek refuge in You from You." Self-powered holding without effacement of strength and power lands in idolatry, shirk, while its owner remains unaware.

### In short

- Disbelief is amazing where Allah's verses, commands and prohibitions are recited and His Messenger is present (57:8).
- The Prophet read this verse standing between Aws and Khazraj until weapons dropped and enemies embraced, up to (3:103).
- Holding fast means affirming Allah's Being and Attributes without supporting any adversary, and inwardly it is found from God rather than achieved by the self.
