### What amr and shura mean

Word amr is applied for several shades in Arabic. In common usage it refers to any saying or doing of great importance. > O master! Pardon their shortcomings toward your rightful due and your work, and pass over it. As for their shortcomings toward My rightful due, you be their intercessor and ask for forgiveness from Me.

So pardon them, and ask forgiveness for them. So pardon them is an allusion to togetherness, for it is a decree. One who decrees in reality is God, and Messenger follows. Ask forgiveness for them is an allusion to dispersion, which is station of abasement and servanthood. Lord's custom with prophets and friends moves between the two states. Togetherness at one time. Dispersion at another. Togetherness without dispersion is disbelief, and dispersion without togetherness is associationism. Togetherness is Haqiqah itself, and dispersion is path of servanthood. When these two traits come together in someone, he is on road of Sunnah, sunnah, and congregation and walks straight on Tariqah, tariqah, and Shariah, shariah.

> And consult with them in the affair. O master! The states of the travelers in this road are diverse. One falls short, so ask pardon for him. One repents, so ask forgiveness for him. One is obedient, so consult with him.

And when thou art resolved, trust in God. Surely God loves those who trust. Resoluteness has a reality, basis of reality is rightness of what is desired. Togetherness of heart is basis of solidity in religion, jealousy in affair, and straightness in present moment. Resoluteness is of three sorts. One sort is resoluteness of repentance, second resoluteness of service, third resoluteness of Haqiqah. All three are built on trust. Trust has a root, a precondition, and a fruit. Its root is certainty. Its precondition is faith, alluded to in His words, "And in God put your trust, if you have faith" [5:23]. Its fruit is Real's love, in His words, "Surely God loves those who trust." Master Abu Ali, Abū ʿAlī, Daqqaq, Daqqāq, begins the three levels of trust saying cited from, "Trust has three levels. First is trust, " and the verse cited there is (9:72), numbering that citation in the frame.

### In short

- By Allah's Rahmah the Prophet was gentle, not severe in speech or hard in heart, or people would have scattered from around him.
- After Uhud he must pardon Companions' shortcomings toward him, ask forgiveness for shortcomings toward Allah, and consult them in affairs to win hearts and model the practice.
- Consultation is Sunnah where no categorical Qur'an or Hadith injunction exists, from people wise and God-fearing and, for Islamic Law, faqih, while settled commands such as Salah, Zakah and Hajj are not consulted about.
- His consultations at Badr, Uhud, Khandaq, Hudaybiyyah and Ifk decided real outcomes, majority does not helplessly bind an Amir after consultation, and the singular "you have taken a decision" gives final decision to the executive.
- Once decided, trust is placed in Allah alone, not in counsel, plans or means, and Allah loves those who trust in Him.
