### Phrase by phrase

**"O believers!"** — O you who believe in Muhammad and the Qur'an, addressed first as faith before being assigned endurance.
**"Patiently endure"** — be patient in obedience, afflictions, refraining from disobedience, obligations and misfortunes, and endure in Islam through comfort and calamity until death.
**"persevere"** — vie in patience, be more patient, outdo disbelievers in patience and determination so they are not more patient than you.
**"stand on guard"** — Rabitu, be steadfast and ready, secure frontiers, tie horses, and also await prayer after prayer in worship.
**"and be mindful of Allah"** — have Taqwa of Allah in all affairs and situations, obeying commands and staying away from prohibitions.
**"so you may be successful"** — in order that you may succeed and prosper in this life and the Hereafter, win Paradise and be saved from Fire and punishment.

### In short

- The surah closes with Sabr, Musabirah, Murabatah and Taqwa, patience in duties, against sins and in distress, outlasting enemies, and guarding frontiers or prayers.
- A day and night of Ribat surpasses a month of fasting and night prayer, and the dead guard's deeds, provision and safety continue until resurrection.
- Taqwa is the essence making endurance accepted, and success means Paradise, deliverance from the Fire and meeting Allah without fear.
