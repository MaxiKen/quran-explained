<<<VERSE 2:254>>>
The opportunity to give belongs to the present life: "Donate from what We have provided for you before the arrival of a Day when there will be no bargaining, friendship, or intercession." Wealth comes from Allah, and using it for Him stores up a reward that cannot be purchased after death. The verse calls believers to act while ability and time remain.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O believers! Donate from what We have provided for you"** Those who believe in Allah and follow His Messenger ﷺ are addressed. They should give from the permissible kinds of wealth He has granted them, including what is due as alms. Spending in the path of righteousness recognises both the gift and its Giver.

**"before the arrival of a Day when there will be no bargaining, friendship, or intercession."** This is the Day of Resurrection. No transaction will buy a missing good deed or ransom a person from judgment. Friends cannot lend deeds that were never performed, and family connection does not itself rescue someone. Intercession, shafā'ah, cannot operate independently of Allah's permission.

**"Those who disbelieve are ˹truly˺ the wrongdoers"** Denying Allah or the obligations He imposes is injustice. Another explanation identifies assigning partners to Him. The strongest wrong is meeting Him in unbelief. The wording must not be reversed into a claim that every person who does any wrong is therefore a disbeliever.

### Why will worldly resources not suffice then?

Even a ransom equal to the earth filled with gold cannot secure escape. The discussion connects the failure of family reliance with (23:101), where the blowing of the Trumpet is followed by the absence of useful kinship claims and ordinary questioning of one another.

The denial of intercession is explained especially concerning disbelievers, and also as denial of any intercession without Allah's leave. The account adds that release is not obtained through someone's independent influence unless Allah Himself forgives. It therefore rejects reliance on a human intermediary as though that person could command the judgment.

Two grammatical readings give different noun endings in the words for commerce, friendship and intercession. The nominative forms are bayʿun, khullatun and shafā‘atun. The other reading gives lā bayʿa, lā khullata and lā shafā‘ata. Both express the same central denial of these as freely available means of rescue. The variation does not restore a market in which salvation can be bought.

Ibn Abi Hatim records 'Ata' bin Dinar thanking "Allah Who said" that the disbelievers are the wrongdoers. He emphasised that the verse does not say that the wrongdoers are the disbelievers. That distinction preserves the force of the warning without extending it into an accusation the verse does not make.

### How does this connect with earlier duties?

Many instructions in the surah can feel burdensome because they require giving up something valued. Love of life and possessions can also become motives for sin. Obedience trains the believer not to make either attachment the final rule of conduct.

The earlier command to fight concerns the giving of effort and life. The good loan concerns giving wealth. Talut's story developed the first theme, while this command returns to the second. Worship and dealings with people often depend upon expenditure, so the following discussion gives it greater detail.

The connection is practical: the time to perform the deed still exists. The Hereafter is not another setting where the missed act can simply be bought. The reminder should therefore produce action, not only admiration for generosity in other people's stories.

### What sacrifice is included beyond money?

A broader reading includes every sacrifice for Allah's cause: committing one's life, relinquishing material interests and accepting the cost of following His guidance. A claim accepted only while convenient does not demonstrate the same sincerity as a commitment maintained through sacrifice.

Sincerity makes possible a deeper relationship with the goal. The reflection describes it as light leading towards Paradise, while refusal to pay any cost leaves the person in darkness. This is not a claim that a cash payment mechanically buys knowledge. It concerns the earnestness expressed in giving something up for the truth.

The inward admonition is to use the ability Allah grants before strength diminishes and the allotted time ends. Opportunity itself is help from Him. Waiting until action becomes impossible is not a substitute for gratitude while it remains possible.

### In short

- Give from Allah's lawful provision while the opportunity to act remains.
- Money, friendship and independent intercession cannot purchase rescue at the final judgment.
- The warning about unbelief must not be reversed into declaring every wrongdoer a disbeliever.
- Sacrifice shows sincerity through wealth, effort and the interests surrendered for Allah's cause.
