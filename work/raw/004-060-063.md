<<<VERSE 4:60>>>
"Have you, O Prophet, not seen those who claim they believe in what has been revealed to you and what was revealed before you? They seek the judgment of false judges, which they were commanded to reject. And Satan only desires to lead them farther away." This verse asks about those who claim to believe in what was revealed to the Prophet and before him, yet seek judgment from Taghut, which they were commanded to reject. Satan wishes to lead them far astray.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Have you, O Prophet, not seen those who claim they believe in what has been revealed to you and what was revealed before you?"** asks about those who claim belief.

**"They seek the judgment of false judges"** means they wish to go for judgment to Taghut.

**"Which they were commanded to reject"** means they were ordered to reject them.

**"And Satan only desires to lead them farther away"** means Satan wishes to lead them far astray.

### Referring to Taghut

Allah, indeed, rebukes those who claim to believe, in what Allah has revealed to His Messenger. And to the previous prophets. Yet they turn to other than the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of His Messenger for judgment in their disputes. It was reported that the occasion behind revealing this Ayah was that a man from the Ansar and a Jew had a dispute, and the Jew said, "Let us refer to Muhammad to judge between us." However, the Muslim man said, "Let us refer to Ka'b bin Al-Ashraf (a Jew) to judge between us." It was also reported that the Ayah was revealed about some hypocrites who pretended to be Muslims, yet they sought the judgment of Jahiliyyah. Further reasons were also reported as the occasion for the Ayah. Yet the Ayah has a general meaning, as it chastises all who refrain from referring to the Qur'an and Sunnah for rulings and prefer the judgment of whatever they chose of falsehood, which befits the description of Taghut here. The verse cites 5:52, 24:51, 31:21. It was commented on by Ibn Abi, al-Sha'bi, and others. Collectors include At-Tabarani. The verse involves kufr, ruh, say, sunnah, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include Abu Hatim, Abu Salih, ansar, Al-Ashraf, Al-Aslami, dawud, gabriel, Ibn Abi, Ibn al-Ashraf, Ibn al-Khattab, ishaq, al-Khattab, kufr, madinah, ruh, say, al-Sha'bi, sunnah, al-Tamimi, The Jew, the path, torah.

### In short

- They claim belief but seek judgment from Taghut.
- They were commanded to reject Taghut.
- Satan wishes to lead them astray.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abi and others.
- The verse follows the previous passage.
