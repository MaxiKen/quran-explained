<<<VERSE 5:112>>>
"˹Remember˺ when the disciples asked, “O Jesus, son of Mary! Would your Lord be willing to send down to us a table spread with food from heaven?” Jesus answered, “Fear Allah if you are ˹truly˺ believers.”"

This verse recalls the request of the disciples of Isa, son of Mary, that a table spread with food should come down to them from heaven. Isa answers that they should fear Allah if they are truly believers.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Remember when the disciples asked, O Jesus, son of Mary! Would your Lord be willing to send down to us a table spread with food from heaven?"** The material explains that the table is called the ma'idah, the name from which this Surah takes its name. The disciples are named as the Hawariyun. Some said that they asked for it because they were poor and in need, so that they could eat from it each day and be more able to worship. The material says the request came from the disciples and was accepted as a clear proof for the prophethood of Isa.

**"Jesus answered, Fear Allah if you are truly believers."** The material says that Isa told them to fear Allah, and not to ask for this, since it may become a trial for them. He told them to trust Allah for their provision. The material says that the request for a miracle is not fitting for a believer, who should seek sustenance through the means that Allah has set.

### In short

- The disciples asked Isa for a table spread with food from heaven.
- Isa told them to fear Allah and to trust Him for their provision.
- A believer should not test Allah with a demand for a miracle.

<<<VERSE 5:113>>>
"They said, “We ˹only˺ wish to eat from it to reassure our hearts, to verify you are indeed truthful to us, and to become its witnesses.”"

This verse gives the reason of the disciples for their request: they wish to eat from the table to reassure their hearts, to know that Isa has told them the truth, and to become witnesses to it.

### Phrase by phrase

**"We only wish to eat from it to reassure our hearts."** The material explains that the disciples wanted their faith to grow when they saw the food come down from heaven as sustenance for them.

**"To verify you are indeed truthful to us, and to become its witnesses."** The material says that they wanted to know that Isa had spoken the truth about his message, and that they would testify that the table was a sign from Allah, a proof that Isa was a prophet. The material says that they wished to attest to the truth of what he brought.

### In short

- The disciples wanted their hearts reassured.
- They wanted to know that Isa had told them the truth.
- They wanted to become witnesses that the table was a sign from Allah.

<<<VERSE 5:114>>>
"Jesus, son of Mary, prayed, “O Allah, our Lord! Send us from heaven a table spread with food as a feast for us, the first and last of us, and as a sign from You. Provide for us! You are indeed the Best Provider.”"

This verse gives the prayer of Isa, son of Mary, asking Allah to send down a table of food from heaven, as a feast for the first and last of his people, and as a sign from Him, and to provide for them, since He is the Best Provider.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Jesus, son of Mary, prayed, O Allah, our Lord! Send us from heaven a table spread with food."** The material explains that Isa made the supplication to Allah, and that the table is the ma'idah. The material says that some held that the people already had some food with them, and what was asked for was the blessing of food from heaven.

**"As a feast for us, the first and last of us."** The material says that the feast is for the people of the time of Isa, and for those who come after them. As-Suddi said that the day on which the table came down would be a day of celebration, which they would consider sacred. Sufyan ath-Thawri said it was a day of prayer. The material notes that the day is said to have been a Sunday.

**"And as a sign from You."** The material says that the sign is a proof for the believer and an argument against the one who disbelieves, and that it shows that Allah is able to do all things and accepts the supplication of Isa.

**"Provide for us! You are indeed the Best Provider."** The material says that Isa asked for a food that needed no labour, and that Allah is the best of those who provide.

### In short

- Isa prayed to Allah to send a table of food from heaven.
- The feast was for the first and last of his people, and was a sign from Allah.
- Isa asked for provision, and called Allah the Best Provider.

<<<VERSE 5:115>>>
"Allah answered, “I am sending it down to you. But whoever among you denies afterwards will be subjected to a torment I have never inflicted on anyone of My creation.”"

This verse gives the answer of Allah to the prayer of Isa. He will send the table down, and whoever among them denies after that will receive a torment unlike any that He has inflicted on any of His creation.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Allah answered, I am sending it down to you."** The material explains that Allah accepted the prayer, and sent the table down to the disciples, as the apparent wording states. The material reports from Ibn Abbas that the angels brought the table down, with seven fish and seven loaves, and that the people ate from it, and that the last group ate as the first did. Ishaq ibn Abdullah reported that the table was sent down with seven loaves and seven fish, and that when some stole food from it, saying it might not come down tomorrow, the table went up again.

**"But whoever among you denies afterwards will be subjected to a torment I have never inflicted on anyone of My creation."** The material says the torment is unique, since the sign was an overwhelming proof, and any denial after it is only from stubbornness. The material cites 4:145 for the hypocrites, who will be in the lowest depths of the Fire, and 40:46 for the people of Pharaoh, who will be made to enter the severest torment. Abdullah ibn Amr is reported to have said that the three who receive the severest torment on the Day of Resurrection are the hypocrites, the people of the table who disbelieved, and the people of Pharaoh.

### The gratitude for a great blessing

The material says that when a blessing is unique, gratitude for it must be greater, and that the punishment for ingratitude is also unique. The majority of the people of knowledge held that the table did come down. It is reported from Ammar ibn Yasir, in a hadith recorded by Tirmidhi, that the table came down with bread and meat, and that some of the people broke the trust by storing food for the next day, and so were transformed into monkeys and swine. The material says that the people ate as they wished, but storing food for the next day was forbidden.

### In short

- Allah answered the prayer and sent the table down.
- Whoever denies after the sign will suffer a torment unlike any given to others.
- Ingratitude for a great blessing brings a great punishment.
