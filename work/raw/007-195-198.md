<<<VERSE 7:195>>>
Allah now shows how helpless the idols are, and how far their worshippers surpass them: "Do they have feet to walk with? Or hands to hold with? Or eyes to see with? Or ears to hear with?" Then the Prophet ﷺ throws down a challenge: "Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Call upon your associate-gods and conspire against me without delay". The questions deny that the idols have any of these things. The idolaters have them all, so why worship what is less complete than themselves?

### Phrase by phrase

**"Do they have feet to walk with?"** Can they walk toward any good? The question is a denial: they have no feet.

**"Or hands to hold with?"** Hands with which to give and take, to act or to strike. The word for hands, aydin, is the plural of yad.

**"Or eyes to see with?"** Can they see your worship of them?

**"Or ears to hear with?"** Can they hear your call? If these idols can do none of these things, how can you worship them, hoping they will bring you benefit or protect you from harm?

**"Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Call upon your associate-gods"** Say, O Muhammad, to the idolaters of Makkah: call on all your partners together and seek your gods' help against me.

**"and conspire against me without delay"** Plot against me all together, do whatever you will to destroy me, and give me no respite, not even for a moment. Put all your effort into it, for I am not concerned with you.

### Who made the same challenge before?

Hūd (Hud) answered his people in the same way. They told him: "All we can say is that some of our gods have possessed you with evil.” He said, “I call Allah to witness, and you too bear witness, that I ˹totally˺ reject whatever you associate with Him ˹in worship˺. So let all of you plot against me without delay. I have put my trust in Allah—my Lord and your Lord. There is no living creature that is not completely under His control. Surely my Lord’s Way is perfect justice" (11:54-56).

### Why do people worship what cannot walk or see?

It is astonishing that, in a world with so many arguments for one Lord, people make polytheism their religion. Through the wonderful harmony between two human beings, a child is born. Some then believe this happened through the blessing of some living or dead saint. Some credit it to imagined deities. Some say it came from the blind interplay of material forces. Others think the beautiful baby was the reward of their own efforts.

Behind the idols of stone or metal that polytheists set up lies a "philosophy". The idols are said to be outward forms in which the god they stand for has become incarnate, so worshipping them amounts to worshipping that god. Among ordinary people, though, polytheism takes the form of regarding the idols themselves as holy. These idols cannot walk, talk, see or hear. Yet superstitious people imagine that they will be of use to them and meet their needs.

### In short

- The idols have no feet, hands, eyes or ears, so their worshippers are more complete than they are.
- The Prophet ﷺ challenged the idolaters to call on all their gods and plot against him at once, as Hūd challenged his people.
- People credit a child's birth to saints, deities, blind forces or themselves, and treat helpless idols as holy.

<<<VERSE 7:196>>>
The Prophet ﷺ explains why he does not fear them: "“Indeed, my Protector is Allah Who has revealed this Book. For He ˹alone˺ protects the righteous". Verse 196 gives the ground of his confidence. Allah, who revealed the Qur'an to him, is his protector and helper, so their hostility does not frighten him in the least.

### Phrase by phrase

**"“Indeed, my Protector is Allah"** The word rendered "protector" also means helper. It is the One in charge of his affairs, who keeps him safe so that he needs no one besides Him. Allah's support is enough for him. Allah is his supporter, in whom he trusts and with whom he takes refuge. In this world and the next He guards him, as He will guard every righteous believer who comes after. So he fears nothing from their idols.

**"Who has revealed this Book."** The Book is the Holy Qur'an. Allah sent Jibrīl (Gabriel) to him with the Scripture, as guidance for people.

**"For He ˹alone˺ protects the righteous"** He takes charge of the righteous, protecting them, keeping them safe and helping them. These are those among His creation who do good. According to Ibn ʿAbbās, "the righteous" here means all who set up no one as equal to Allah, prophets and faithful Muslims alike.

### Why mention the revelation of the Book?

Of all Allah's attributes, this verse names in particular His revealing the Book to the Prophet ﷺ. The only reason for the idolaters' hostility was that he called them to the message of the Qur'an. So he could be sure that Allah, who had revealed it, would help and protect him.

Ibrāhīm al-Khalīl, the Friend of Allah, declared the same reliance. He told his people: "Have you ˹really˺ considered what you have been worshipping—you and your ancestors? They are ˹all˺ enemies to me, except the Lord of all worlds. ˹He is˺ the One Who created me, and He ˹alone˺ guides me" (26:75-78). He also said to his father and his people: "I am totally free of whatever ˹gods˺ you worship except the One Who originated me, and He will surely guide me.” And he left this enduring declaration among his descendants, so they may ˹always˺ turn back ˹to Allah˺" (43:26-28).

### Can enemies harm the righteous?

The closing words give a general rule. Allah helps and protects not only His messengers, who hold the highest rank, but every Muslim who acts righteously. So no one's opposition or hostility can harm a true Muslim in the real sense. Very often he is made to triumph over his enemies in this world. If, for some good reason, he does not prevail and seems defeated, even that does not damage his real aim. His failure in this world is in fact success, because the aim of his life is to seek Allah's pleasure and obey Him in everything. A setback that comes from Allah only brings him closer to that aim.

### In short

- The Prophet ﷺ did not fear the idolaters, since Allah, who revealed the Qur'an to him, was his protector.
- The revelation is mentioned because the Qur'an's message was the cause of their hostility.
- Allah protects all the righteous, so even an apparent defeat is, for a true Muslim, a step toward Allah's pleasure.
<<<END>>>

<<<VERSE 7:197>>>
The Prophet ﷺ goes on: "But those ˹false gods˺ you call besides Him can neither help you nor even themselves.”" This repeats what was said earlier (7:192), but this time as a direct address to the idolaters. Since their gods can do nothing, why should he be concerned with them?

### Phrase by phrase

**"But those ˹false gods˺ you call besides Him"**: the idols you call on and worship besides Allah, O idolaters.

**"can neither help you"** They have no power to benefit or harm you.

**"nor even themselves.”"** They cannot even stop what is done to them. They are utterly incapable of doing anything. So how can you call on them instead of calling on Allah?

### What counts as an idol?

This is not limited to the familiar kinds of idol. It is the state of everything given the status of an object of worship. That includes one's native land and community, and personalities, living or dead, who attract feelings that belong only to Allah, the One. All these fall into the same category, since giving them such feelings amounts to treating them as divine.

What is the reality of all these things? None has power of its own. None made its own legs, hands or eyes. Everyone with legs was given them, and if a leg is taken away he cannot make another. Everyone with hands was given them, and if a hand is lost he cannot make another. Everyone with eyes received them as a gift, and if an eye is lost he cannot make himself a new one.

Allah alone is humankind's Sustainer and Saviour. In this world He is unseen, but in the Hereafter He will reveal Himself. Then people will see with their own eyes that Allah alone provided for and sustained them. Only their foolishness led them to credit this to imagined deities. Those partners cannot even look after themselves, so what can they do for others? Allah alone helps His servants in this world, and He will help His faithful servants in the world to come.

### In short

- The false gods cannot help their worshippers, or even protect themselves.
- Anything given worship-like devotion, whether land, community or a person, is helpless in the same way, since none made its own limbs.
- Allah alone sustains and helps, and in the Hereafter everyone will see it plainly.
<<<END>>>

<<<VERSE 7:198>>>
The idols look as if they see, but they are blind: "If you ˹idolaters˺ call them to guidance, they cannot hear. And you ˹O Prophet˺ may see them facing towards you, but they cannot see". The idolaters used to make statues in the shape of people and animals, with hands, feet and eyes. Yet these were only objects, lifeless and unable to move.

### Phrase by phrase

**"If you ˹idolaters˺ call them to guidance, they cannot hear."** Call the idols you serve in place of Allah to steadfastness and the right path, and they will not hear the call. One explanation takes the address as being to the Muslims calling the idols to the truth. Either way, the idols neither hear nor answer, because they are not living but dead. The point was made before, with the verse quoted under 7:193 (35:14).

**"And you ˹O Prophet˺ may see them facing towards you,"** You see the idols, O Muhammad, staring at you with their eyes open and painted on, looking toward you as a person looks.

**"but they cannot see"** They perceive nothing, because they are not living but dead. They are objects that cannot see!

### Why does the verse speak of idols as if they were people?

The idols have eyes that stare as if they could see, though they are solid. So the verse speaks of them as though they had minds. It says tarāhum, the form used for rational beings, and not tarāhā, the form used for things. This is because they are made in human shape, with eyes drawn on them.

### In short

- Idols called to guidance cannot hear, because they are lifeless.
- Their carved and painted eyes seem to look at the Prophet ﷺ, but they see nothing.
- The verse speaks of them as if they had minds only because they were shaped like people.
