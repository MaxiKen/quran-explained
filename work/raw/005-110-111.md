<<<VERSE 5:110>>>
"And ˹on Judgment Day˺ Allah will say, “O Jesus, son of Mary! Remember My favour upon you and your mother: how I supported you with the holy spirit so you spoke to people in ˹your˺ infancy and adulthood. How I taught you writing, wisdom, the Torah, and the Gospel. How you moulded a bird from clay, by My Will, and breathed into it and it became a ˹real˺ bird, by My Will. How you healed the blind and the lepers, by My Will. How you brought the dead to life, by My Will. How I prevented the Children of Israel from harming you when you came to them with clear proofs and the disbelievers among them said, “This is nothing but pure magic.”"

This verse recalls the favours that Allah gave to Isa, son of Mary, on the Day of Judgement. Allah reminds him of how the holy spirit supported him, how he spoke to people in infancy and in adulthood, how he was taught the Book and the Torah and the Gospel, and how he gave life to clay birds, healed the blind and the lepers, and raised the dead, all by the will of Allah. It also recalls how Allah protected him from the Children of Israel, who called his clear proofs pure magic.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And on Judgment Day Allah will say, O Jesus, son of Mary! Remember My favour upon you and your mother."** The material explains that the favour is spoken of in the past tense, as something that is already settled. The material says that the favours were given to Isa, and that he will be reminded of them on the Day of Resurrection. The mother is named along with him, since she was chosen and given the purity that was attributed to her by the unjust.

**"How I supported you with the holy spirit so you spoke to people in your infancy and adulthood."** The material explains that the holy spirit is Jibril, and that the speaking in the cradle was a miracle of Isa, who called the people to Allah as a child and as an adult. The material says that the speech in the cradle was a testimony to the purity of his mother and to his own worship of Allah.

**"How I taught you writing, wisdom, the Torah, and the Gospel."** The material says that the Torah was revealed to Musa, son of Imran, who spoke to Allah directly, and that Isa was taught the Book, the wisdom, the Torah and the Injil. The material notes the sacred law and its benefits as part of the wisdom.

**"How you moulded a bird from clay, by My Will, and breathed into it and it became a real bird, by My Will."** The material explains that the bird came to life by the permission of Allah, and that this was a sign of His power. The material says the miracle is explained as an example of the will of Allah, and not as something that Isa could do by himself.

**"How you healed the blind and the lepers, by My Will. How you brought the dead to life, by My Will."** The material says that these healings and the raising of the dead were by the permission of Allah, and that Isa called on Allah for them. The material notes that these were mentioned in the earlier Surah of Al Imran as well.

**"How I prevented the Children of Israel from harming you when you came to them with clear proofs."** The material explains that the Children of Israel rejected Isa, called him a magician, and tried to kill him by crucifixion. Allah saved him, raised him to Himself, and protected him from their harm.

**"And the disbelievers among them said, This is nothing but pure magic."** The material says that the disbelievers called the miracles plain sorcery. A variant reading gives the word for sorcerer, and so the material notes that they called Isa himself a manifest sorcerer.

### In short

- Allah reminds Isa of the favours that He gave him, including the holy spirit, his speech, the Book and the Gospel.
- The miracles of the clay bird, the healing and the raising of the dead were by the will of Allah.
- Allah protected him from the Children of Israel, who called his miracles magic.

<<<VERSE 5:111>>>
"And how I inspired the disciples, “Believe in Me and My messenger!” They declared, “We believe and bear witness that we fully submit ˹to Allah˺.”"

This verse recalls that Allah inspired the disciples to believe in Him and in His messenger, and that they answered that they believed, bore witness, and submitted to Allah.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And how I inspired the disciples, Believe in Me and My messenger!"** The material explains that the word inspired is the same word used for the inspiration given to the mother of Musa, and to the bee. The material says that Allah placed in the hearts of the disciples, the Hawariyyun, the faith in Him and in His messenger. Al-Hasan al-Basri is reported to have said that Allah inspired them, and As-Suddi that He put it in their hearts. The material says this was a favour, since Allah gave Isa disciples and companions.

**"They declared, We believe and bear witness that we fully submit to Allah."** The material explains that the disciples answered that they believed, and that they were Muslims, which means that they submitted. The material says that this shows the disciples of Isa were a sign of his truth, and that they were among the helpers of the messenger.

### In short

- Allah inspired the disciples to believe in Him and in His messenger.
- The disciples answered that they believed and submitted to Allah.
- This was a favour to Isa, who had companions to help him.
