<<<VERSE 4:36>>>
"Worship Allah alone and associate none with Him. And be kind to parents, relatives, orphans, the poor, near and distant neighbours, close friends, needy travellers, and those bondspeople in your possession. Surely Allah does not like whoever is arrogant, boastful." This verse commands worship of Allah alone, kindness to parents, relatives, orphans, the poor, neighbours near and far, close friends, needy travellers, and bondspeople, and states that Allah does not like the arrogant and boastful.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Worship Allah alone"** commands worshipping Allah alone without partners.

**"And associate none with Him"** forbids associating partners with Him.

**"And be kind to parents"** commands dutifulness to parents.

**"Relatives, orphans, the poor"** commands kindness to relatives, orphans, and the poor.

**"Near and distant neighbours"** commands kindness to neighbours near and far.

**"Close friends"** commands kindness to close friends.

**"Needy travellers"** commands kindness to wayfarers.

**"And those bondspeople in your possession"** commands kindness to bondspeople.

**"Surely Allah does not like whoever is arrogant, boastful"** states Allah does not like the arrogant and boastful.

### Worship and kindness

The verse orders that worship be given to Allah Alone without partners. He is the only Creator and Sustainer, sending His favors and bounties on His creation in every situation and instance. Therefore He deserves to be singled out, without associating anything or anyone from His creation with Him in worship. The Prophet said to Mu'adh, "Do you know what Allah's right on His servants is?" Mu'adh replied, "Allah and His Messenger know better." He said, "That they should worship Him and should not worship any others with Him." The Prophet then said, "Do you know what the right of the servants on Allah is if they do this? He should not punish them." Dutifulness to parents follows, because Allah made parents the cause of the servants coming into existence, after they did not exist. In many places Allah joins the order to worship Him with being dutiful to parents. For example, He said, "give thanks to Me and to your parents" (31:14), and, "And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him. And that you be dutiful to your parents" (17:23). After Allah ordained being dutiful to parents, He ordained kind treatment of relatives, males and females. A Hadith states, "Charity given to the poor is Sadaqah, while charity given to relatives is both Sadaqah and Silah (keeping the relations)." The verse then mentions orphans, because they lost their caretakers who would spend on them. So the verse commands that the orphans be treated with kindness and compassion. The verse then mentions Al-Masakin (the poor) who have various needs and cannot find what sustains these needs. Therefore, the verse commands they should be helped in acquiring their needs in a sufficient manner that will end their inadequacy. The verse cites 2:83, 7:54, 9:60, 16:90, 17:23, 19:32, 31:14. It was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Mujahid, Qatadah, and others. Collectors include Abu Dawud, Al-Bukhari, An-Nasa'i, At-Tirmidhi, Ibn Jarir. ### In short

- Worship Allah alone and associate none with Him.
- Be kind to parents, relatives, orphans, the poor, neighbours, friends, travellers, and bondspeople.
- Allah does not like the arrogant and boastful.
- The verse joins worship with dutifulness to parents.
- The discussion adds the views of Ibn Abbas and others.
