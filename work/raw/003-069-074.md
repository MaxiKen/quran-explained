<<<VERSE 3:69>>>
A wish is exposed and turned back on those who held it: "Some of the People of the Book wish to mislead you ˹believers˺. They mislead none but themselves, yet they fail to perceive it". The Jews envy the faithful and want them misguided, as they had been misguided themselves. The punishment of the attempt falls on the attempters, while they remain unaware of it.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Some of the People of the Book wish to mislead you ˹believers˺"**: a party from Scripture's people longs to push you away from Islam. Jewish and Christian scholars want believers diverted from Allah-guided truth.

**"They mislead none but themselves, yet they fail to perceive it"**: straying's burden lands on guides whose audience will not follow them. Attempts deepen their own misguidance and carry consequences they cannot see. A second possibility is that Allah's informing His Prophet escapes their perception.

### The invitation after Uhud

The occasion was an invitation to named Companions. After the Battle of Uhud, Kaʿb al-Ashraf invited ʿAmmār ibn Yāsir, Muʿādh ibn Jabal and Hudhayfa ibn al-Yamān to join Judaism and leave Islam. The verse was revealed about ʿAmmār, Muʿādh and Hudhayfa when the Jews called them to embrace their religion.

Misery tries to reproduce itself inwardly. People ruined by discord, tribulation and temptation wish their condition on everyone. Believers were their target, but God would perfect His light and return the evil effects to the actors.

### In short

- Envious scholars wish the believers misguided and only deepen their own misguidance, unaware.
- The named targets were Muʿādh, Hudhayfa and ʿAmmār, invited to Judaism after Uhud.
- Discord wishes its own condition on everyone, but Allah perfects His light instead.

<<<VERSE 3:70>>>
The accusation moves from wish to contradiction: "O People of the Book! Why do you reject the signs of Allah while you bear witness ˹to their truth˺". They know for certain that Allah's verses are true and authentic, and they bear witness to them. Their disbelief targets what their own books confirm.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O People of the Book! Why do you reject the signs of Allah"**: why disbelieve in the revelations of Allah, Muhammad and the Qur'an, and in the verses in Torah and Injīl proving Muhammad's prophethood? To deny his prophethood is to declare those verses false, which is infidelity.

**"while you bear witness ˹to their truth˺"**: Scripture itself teaches you that Muhammad is a prophet sent by Allah. His prophecy's authenticity had your witness before the mission, and confirmation of these signs comes from you.

### Knowledge aggravates, but never licenses

The witness and knowledge named in these two verses do not mean that disbelief would be acceptable without them. Kufr, disbelief, is intrinsically abominable to practise and is prohibited under all conditions. Attestation and knowledge are mentioned to increase the impact of reproach. You disavow what you know, and the reproach lands because the knowledge is there.

### In short

- The signs rejected include their own Torah and Injīl verses proving Muhammad's prophethood.
- Witnessing the truth beforehand makes denial a disavowal of knowledge, not an error of ignorance.
- Disbelief is forbidden in every condition, and knowledge only deepens the rebuke.
<<<END>>>

<<<VERSE 3:71>>>
The second reproach names the method: "O People of the Book! Why do you mix the truth with falsehood and hide the truth knowingly". Truth and falsehood are first blended, by distortion and false explanation, and then the truth left clear, such as the description of the Prophet in their books, is hidden by people who know exactly what they are doing.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O People of the Book! Why do you mix the truth with falsehood"**: why do you confound, mix, truth with falsehood by distorting and falsifying Scripture? The real statement, the prophecy of Muhammad's prophethood, is confused with the fictitious, interpolated statements and false exegesis. Muhammad's traits are confounded with those assigned to the anti-Christ.

**"and hide the truth knowingly"**: conceal Muhammad's description and traits as found in Scripture, despite knowing your conduct, knowing the truth, and knowing what is false.

### Forsaking as a judgement, and the double life

Regarding Muḥammad's affair, concealment persists despite knowledge that he is a truthful Prophet. Nothing explains it inwardly except divine forsaking, ḥukm al-khidhlān, and deprivation's decision, qaḍiyyat al-ḥirmān, descending on concealers.

Hypocrites are also exposed among them. They wanted to forestall trouble with the Muslims while avoiding conflict with their brothers among the disbelievers. So they conspired to pretend agreement publicly with the Messenger ﷺ and the Muslims while secretly remaining true among themselves to their corrupt beliefs.

### In short

- Mixing is done by interpolation and false explanation; hiding is done by concealing the Prophet's description, knowingly.
- The traits of the anti-Christ are even confounded with Muhammad's traits to complete the confusion.
- Concealment is read as divine forsaking, and its social form is a public agreement that hides private corruption.
<<<END>>>

<<<VERSE 3:72>>>
A staged conversion is described from the inside: "A group among the People of the Book said ˹to one another˺, “Believe in what has been revealed to the believers in the morning and reject it in the evening, so they may abandon their faith". The plan was cooked up to manufacture doubt. A learned group would enter Islam at dawn and leave it by night, hoping weak believers would conclude that the learned had found a defect.

### Phrase by phrase

**"A group among the People of the Book said ˹to one another˺, “Believe in what has been revealed to the believers in the morning"**: a party from Scripture's people told some among them to believe in the Qur'an, revealed to believers, at dawn prayer's opening.

**"and reject it in the evening"**: and disbelieve in it at the end of the day, at the end thereof, at the Ẓuhr prayer in the qiblah version.

**"so they may abandon their faith"**: so that they, the believers, may turn back from Muhammad's religion. The ignorant would say, "They reverted to their old religion because they uncovered some shortcomings in the Islamic religion." Or: "These Jews are knowledgeable, and they could only have turned away after accepting it because they know it to be false." Or, in the plotters' own summary, "These people know better about Allah's Book and they have turned back."

### Twelve rabbis and a script

Al-Ḥasan and as-Suddī give the conspiracy a cast: twelve rabbis from Khaybar's Jews and the town of Uraynah. Morning would bring pretended entry into Muhammad's religion; evening would bring declared disbelief. Their alternative line announced scriptural research and scholarly consultation proving Muhammad not genuine, clearly a lying falsehood. Doubt among his Companions would follow, because People of the Book seemed more knowledgeable. Revelation warned the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and believers.

According to Ibn Abī Najīḥ, Mujāhid pictured Jews at dawn prayer beside the Prophet ﷺ, then unbelieving by day's end. Brief adherence was meant to look like investigation that uncovered defects in a briefly followed religion.

### The qiblah version of the same trick

Mujāhid, Muqātil and al-Kalbī connect revelation with the qiblah's change toward the Kaʿbah. Resistance to being opposed led Kaʿb ibn al-Ashraf and companions to tell lowly people to accept Muhammad's Kaʿbah revelation and pray toward it in the morning, then disbelieve in the Kaʿbah and restore the former qiblah that night. Observers might declare that People of the Book knew better and return to their qiblah and religion.

The plan had a rule of sincerity kept for its own side. The feigned declaration of belief in Islam was reserved to hoodwink Muslims only. True belief would be affirmed before none but those who follow their own creed: sincerity with the latter, expediency with the former.

By exposing it, God showed that their hypocrisy benefited them in neither world. In this world Allah informed His Prophet and the believers of it. In the hereafter it deprived them of sincere devotion toward God.

### In short

- Twelve rabbis of Khaybar and Uraynah scripted a dawn conversion and an evening apostasy to make Muslims suspect a discovered defect.
- The same staged trick is reported around the change of qiblah toward the Kaʿbah, driven by Kaʿb ibn al-Ashraf.
- Exposure cancels the trick in this world, and the absence of sincere devotion cancels it in the next.
<<<END>>>

<<<VERSE 3:73>>>
The plotters' private instruction follows: "And only believe those who follow your religion.” Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Surely, ˹the only˺ true guidance is Allah’s guidance.” ˹They also said,˺ “Do not believe that someone will receive ˹revealed˺ knowledge similar to yours or argue against you before your Lord.” Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Indeed, all bounty is in the Hands of Allah—He grants it to whoever He wills. And Allah is All-Bountiful, All-Knowing.”" Knowledge is to be hoarded inside the community and denied to outsiders, because outsiders given the like of it would become equals, or better.

### Phrase by phrase

**"And only believe those who follow your religion.”"**: secret knowledge should be trusted only to followers of your religion. Belief or acceptance was limited to a Jew following Judaism and its Jerusalem qiblah. Muslims were to be denied exposure that might create faith or furnish proof. Li-man's lām is extra, yielding "follow and accord with your religion".

**"Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Surely, ˹the only˺ true guidance is Allah’s guidance.”"**: God's guidance is Islam; all else is error. Its parenthetical placement does not reduce its force. Islam is Allah's religion, and the Kaʿbah is His qiblah.

**"˹They also said,˺ “Do not believe that someone will receive ˹revealed˺ knowledge similar to yours or argue against you before your Lord.”"**: the Book, wisdom and virtues they received should be admitted as given to nobody unless religion is followed. Ahad, "anyone", precedes the exception, producing exclusion before identification. Another construal guards against believers disputing and prevailing before their Lord on Resurrection Day from a supposedly sounder religion. A rebuking hamza in a variant demands denial that another could receive its like and be affirmed.

**"Say, ˹O Prophet,˺ “Indeed, all bounty is in the Hands of Allah—He grants it to whoever He wills. And Allah is All-Bountiful, All-Knowing.”"**: bounty lies in Allah's Hand and goes to whomever He wills. He suffices His creatures' needs, embraces bounty amply, and knows recipients and deserving people. Prophethood, Islam and Abraham's Qiblah are Muhammad and his Companions' portion.

### Why the secret had to be kept

Their rationale is superiority lost. If Muslims learn the secret, equality follows; indeed, belief could make them better still, or proof could be used against their teachers before the Lord in this life and the Hereafter. Allah nevertheless guides faithful hearts to perfect faith through clear verses, plain proofs and unequivocal evidence sent down to His servant and Messenger Muhammad ﷺ. This happens even though the description of Muhammad ﷺ is hidden, the unlettered Prophet already present in the Books received from earlier prophets. Giving and taking belong to His control. He gives faith, knowledge and sound comprehension to whomever He wills, and He misguides whomever He wills by blinding sight and mind, sealing his heart and hearing and stamping his eyes closed, with perfect wisdom and unequivocal proofs.

Their motive is envy, envy that Muslims were given the Scripture, or envy of their supremacy in religious argument. The tricks fail for the same reason. Guidance is in Allah's control, so He can make whoever He will steadfast on it, and no trick, plan or enticement can wean such a person away.

### A second address: the caress of the believers

Some explainers take part of this address as God's speech to the Muslims, caressing the folk of recognition and faith and reminding them of His favour in the religion of submission. It then has two paths: one toward the common faithful of this community, and one toward the recognisers and the elect among the folk of the Ṭarīqah. In the first respect it runs: "O assembly of submitters! Do not suppose or hold firm that anyone has been given what has been given to you." Submission is unmatched as religion, the Qur'an as book and Muḥammad as prophet, each given to you. Ramadan is unmatched as a month and Friday as a day, and both belong to you. Shari'ahs fall before your shariah and compacts before your compact. Covenant holds perfection of religion and Shariah, together with beauty of Tariqah and Haqiqah: "Today I have perfected your religion for..." (5:3).

Gratitude is then commanded, and with it a boundary: "Do not have faith in any but those who follow your religion." Friendship belongs only to sharers of religion, brotherhood only to the faithful, and distance must be kept from the irreligious and the estranged. This is what He says here too: "Do not lean upon those who do..." (11:113).

### In short

- Secret knowledge is to be kept from outsiders lest they equal or surpass its owners and use it as proof before the Lord.
- True guidance is Allah's guidance alone, Islam, and bounty is in His hand to give without asking any community's permission.
- Some explainers also hear the verse caressing the believers: no religion, book, prophet, month or day like theirs was given to anyone (5:3, 11:113).

<<<VERSE 3:74>>>
The answer ends with selection: "He chooses whoever He wills to receive His mercy. And Allah is the Lord of infinite bounty". Guidance, prophethood and favour are not a community's inheritance to guard. They are gifts distributed by a bounty that singles out whom it wills, and envy misunderstands the nature, timing and wisdom of grace.

### Phrase by phrase

**"He chooses whoever He wills to receive His mercy"**: Muhammad and his Companions are singled out for His religion and mercy. Selection from creation brings guidance, prophethood and numerous favours to whomever He wishes.

**"And Allah is the Lord of infinite bounty"**: Allah is of bounty abounding. He is generous toward Muhammad by bestowing on him prophethood and Islam. He has endowed you, O believers, with tremendous virtue, honouring your Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over all other prophets and directing you to the best Shari'ah there is.

### Mercy distributed in kinds

Blessings are singled out by kind. Provisions go to one group. Another receives character traits, another worship, and another desire. Outward success belongs elsewhere, while secret cores are realised by some and encountered by others. Still others receive outer skins as a gift. God says, and His words are truth: "If you count God's blessings, you will..." (14:34).

The recipients remain grouped obscurely, with nobody specified, so hope can grow in the hopeful and fear not harden in the fearful. No servant states surpass hope and fear in worship and obedience, and the Lord of the Worlds praises servants in these states: "They hope for His mercy and fear..." (17:57).

Utmost striving changes nothing at the final step. Complete obedience and every condition of servanthood still end in deliverance by God's mercy. A prophetic narration says:

> "No one will enter the Garden by his deeds." It was said to him, "Not even you, O Messenger of God!" He said, "Not even I, unless God envelops me with His mercy." 

Even at the limit of devotion, mercy does the delivering. However hard servants strive in obedience and perform all the conditions of servanthood, in the end it is God's mercy that will deliver them. It has been narrated from the Prophet: "No one will enter the Garden by his deeds." It was said to him, "Not even you, O Messenger of God!" He said, "Not even I, unless God envelops me with His mercy."

### In short

- Allah selects Muhammad and his Companions, and whomever He wills, for His mercy, and His bounty has no limit.
- Mercy is distributed in many kinds, material, moral, inward and secret, beyond counting (14:34).
- Being left unspecified feeds hope and fear together, and even perfect obedience is finally delivered by mercy, not by deeds.
