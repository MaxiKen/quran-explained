<<<VERSE 4:11>>>
"Allah commands you regarding your children: the share of the male will be twice that of the female. If you leave only two or more females, their share is two-thirds of the estate. But if there is only one female, her share will be one-half. Each parent is entitled to one-sixth if you leave offspring. But if you are childless and your parents are the only heirs, then your mother will receive one-third. But if you leave siblings, then your mother will receive one-sixth—after the fulfilment of bequests and debts. Be fair to your parents and children, as you do not fully know who is more beneficial to you. This is an obligation from Allah. Surely Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise." This verse sets out the rules of inheritance: the male receives twice the share of the female. Two or more daughters receive two-thirds. One daughter receives one-half. Each parent receives one-sixth if there are children. If childless, the mother receives one-third. If there are siblings, the mother receives one-sixth. All this is after bequests and debts. It ends by saying these fixed shares are an obligation from Allah, who is All-Knowing, All-Wise.

### Phrase by phrase

**"Allah commands you regarding your children"** introduces the inheritance rules for children.

**"The share of the male will be twice that of the female"** means the male gets a portion equal to that of two females.

**"If you leave only two or more females, their share is two-thirds of the estate"** gives two-thirds to two or more daughters.

**"But if there is only one female, her share will be one-half"** gives one-half to a single daughter.

**"Each parent is entitled to one-sixth if you leave offspring"** gives each parent one-sixth when there are children.

**"But if you are childless and your parents are the only heirs, then your mother will receive one-third"** gives the mother one-third when there are no children.

**"But if you leave siblings, then your mother will receive one-sixth"** reduces the mother's share to one-sixth when siblings exist.

**"After the fulfilment of bequests and debts"** means bequests and debts are paid first.

**"Be fair to your parents and children, as you do not fully know who is more beneficial to you"** reminds that one does not know which relative is more beneficial.

**"This is an obligation from Allah"** declares these fixed shares are ordained by Allah.

**"Surely Allah is All-Knowing, All-Wise"** affirms Allah's knowledge and wisdom.

### The reason for revealing the verse

Al-Bukhari recorded that Jabir bin Abdullah said the Prophet came visiting him on foot with Abu Bakr at Banu Salamah's dwellings and found him unconscious. He performed ablution and poured water on him, and Jabir regained consciousness and asked what to do with his money. This verse was later revealed. Muslim and An-Nasa'i also recorded it, and the remainder of the six compilers collected it. Ahmad recorded from Jabir that the wife of Sa'd bin Ar-Rabi' came to the Prophet and said her husband was killed as a martyr at Uhud and his uncle took their money and left nothing for his two daughters, who would not be married without money. The Prophet said Allah will decide, and later the verse was revealed and the Prophet sent word to the uncle commanding him to give two-thirds to Sa'd's two daughters and one-eighth to their mother, and whatever is left is his. Abu Dawud, At-Tirmidhi and Ibn Majah collected this hadith. The first hadith from Jabir was about the case of the last verse in the surah (4:176), for at that time Jabir had sisters and did not have daughters, parents or offspring to inherit from him. Yet it was mentioned here as Al-Bukhari did.

### Learning the shares

This verse, the following, and the last verse in this surah contain the knowledge of Al-Fara'id (inheritance). The knowledge of Al-Fara'id is derived from these three verses and from the hadiths on this subject which explain them. Learning this knowledge is encouraged, especially the specific things mentioned in the verses. Ibn Uyaynah said knowledge of Al-Fara'id was called half of knowledge, because it affects all people. The verse was commented on by Ibn Abbas, Ibn Jurayj, Ibn Uyaynah, Qatadah, Ar-Rabi, Jabir, Abu Bakr, Sa'd, and others. The verse cites 35:32 and 4:176. Collectors include Abu Dawud, Al-Bukhari, An-Nasa'i, At-Tirmidhi, Ibn Majah. Al-Hasan graded the relevant hadith. The verse involves fard, qiyas, salaf, sunnah, shariah, say, hadith, uhud, lot, al-Nuqsan, ʿaṣaba, and related terms. The scholars and terms mentioned include IbnAbHatim and 4176. ### In short

- The verse sets out fixed inheritance shares for children and parents.
- The male receives twice the female's share.
- Bequests and debts are paid first.
- These shares are an obligation from Allah.
- The discussion adds the stories of Jabir and Sa'd bin Ar-Rabi'.
