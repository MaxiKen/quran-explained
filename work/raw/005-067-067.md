<<<VERSE 5:67>>>
"O Messenger! Convey everything revealed to you from your Lord. If you do not, then you have not delivered His message. Allah will ˹certainly˺ protect you from the people. Indeed, Allah does not guide the people who disbelieve"

This verse commands the Prophet ﷺ to convey all that has been revealed to him, and it warns that failing to convey even part of it would mean he had not delivered the message at all. It then promises that Allah will protect him from the people, and it ends by saying that Allah does not guide the disbelievers.

### Phrase by phrase

**"O Messenger! Convey everything revealed to you from your Lord."** The material explains that the address uses the title Messenger. The command is to convey the whole of the revelation, without withholding any part. The material notes that the Prophet ﷺ fulfilled this command in the best way. Aisha is reported to have said that whoever says Muhammad hid any part of the revelation is lying, and Al-Bukhari recorded this report.

**"If you do not, then you have not delivered His message."** The material says that to hide even one verse would mean that the message was not delivered. Ibn 'Abbas explained that if the Prophet ﷺ hid only one verse revealed to him, then he had not conveyed the message of his Lord. The material says the Prophet ﷺ knew the gravity of this, and it notes that Az-Zuhri said the message comes from Allah, the Messenger is its deliverer, and the believers' duty is to submit to it.

**"Allah will certainly protect you from the people."** The material explains that Allah's protection means He will aid and support the Prophet ﷺ and give him victory over his enemies. The verse removes his fear and grief, because none of them can touch him with harm.

**"Indeed, Allah does not guide the people who disbelieve."** The material explains that the Prophet's task is to convey, and that guidance belongs to Allah, who guides whom He wills. The material cites 2:272, which says that the guidance of the people is not upon the Prophet, and 13:40, which says that his duty is only to convey, and the reckoning is with Allah.

### The protection before the verse

The material gives reports that the Prophet ﷺ was guarded before this verse was revealed. Aisha reported that on one night the Prophet ﷺ wished that a pious man of his companions would guard him. They heard the sound of arms, and Sa'd bin Abi Waqqas came to guard him. The Prophet ﷺ slept after that. This is recorded in the two sahihs. Another report says this happened in Madinah, after the hijrah and after the marriage of Aisha. Ibn Abi Hatim recorded that the Prophet ﷺ was guarded until this verse was revealed, and then he came out and said to the people that they should leave, since Allah would protect him. At-Tirmidhi recorded it, and Ibn Jarir and Al-Hakim recorded it too. The material gives a report from al-Hasan, in which the Prophet ﷺ said that the charge of conveying the message frightened him, because the people would reject and oppose him, but that when this verse came, he was at peace.

### The farewell sermon

The material explains that the Prophet ﷺ fulfilled the command at the farewell hajj. He addressed a great gathering of his Companions, and asked them whether he had conveyed the message. They said that he had, and that he had fulfilled the trust and given sincere advice. He raised his finger toward the sky and then toward them and said, O Allah, did I convey? Jabir bin Abdullah reported this in Muslim's collection. The material says that those present were to convey the message to those who were absent, including those yet to be born. Abu Sa'id al-Khudri and Mu'adh are also reported to have passed on what they had heard before their deaths, so that they would not carry the trust unfulfilled.

The material notes that the sermon was the constitution of Islam and the last will of the Prophet ﷺ, and that it was given before the Companions at the hajj.

### The lesson for the preacher

The material explains the position of the preacher of the truth. In Arabia the call to pure religion threatened the position of those who held religious authority, and the Prophet ﷺ met the worst opposition. The preacher has two ways open to him. He can take the worldly consequences into account, in which case the work of conveying God's word becomes impossible. Or he can disregard those consequences and trust in God, who promises to help those who devote themselves to preaching. The reaction of the people is natural, but it has a limit set by God's law of trial. The opponents cannot stop the work or prevent its completion, and the acceptance of the message is the responsibility of the hearers.

### In short

- The Prophet ﷺ was commanded to convey the whole of the revelation, and to hide even one part would mean he had not delivered the message.
- Allah promised to protect him from the people, and the guarding stopped after this verse came.
- The farewell sermon shows the Prophet ﷺ fulfilling the command, and the believers are asked to carry the message to those who are absent.
