## Sūrah al-Aʿlā 87:1

**Expanded Commentary**

The sūrah's first movement is the movement of the glorification, and the movement runs from this verse to the fifth, where the acts of the Lord complete the reason of the command. The command is the door of the sūrah, and the door opens on the acts.

**The Two Positions of the Most High**

The word the Most High stands in the verse in a position that the exegetes record in two ways, and the two ways are the two readings of the verse. The first reading takes the word to qualify the Lord, and the reading is the reading of the majority, and it reads the verse as the simple command to glorify the Lord, the Lord who is the Most High. **The Manner of the Glorification**

The second reading of the verse, the reading that takes the Most High to qualify the Name, gives the command its manner, and the manner is the manner that the exegetes record in two directions. The first direction is the direction of the separation: the glorification of the Name is the glorification that keeps the Name apart from every other thing, and the separation is what the command requires. The second direction is the direction of the revealed names: the glorification is the praise of God with the names that God has revealed, and the revealed names are the names of the Book, and the Book names them in its standing verse: "Call upon Allah or call upon the Most Compassionate—whichever you call, He has the Most Beautiful Names" (17:110). The two directions of the manner are the two sides of the command, and the command is the command of the manner as well as the command of the act.

**The Place in the Prayer**

The tradition records the place of the sūrah's verse in the prayer, and the place is the place of the prostration. The report states that when the verse of the sūrah of the Human — "So glorify the Name of your Lord, the Greatest" (56:74) — was revealed, the Prophet ﷺ told his Companions to enact it in the bowing, and when the verse of this sūrah was revealed, he told them to enact it in the prostration. **The Name of the Sūrah**

The sūrah takes its name from the word of its first verse, and the name is the name of the attribute: al-Aʿlā, the Most High. The naming of the sūrah by the attribute is the Book's way of placing the sūrah under the attribute it states, and the sūrah states the attribute in its first word. The attribute is the attribute of the height above the creation, and the sūrah's statement of the attribute is the statement of the Lord above the acts it will name. The sūrah that opens on the attribute carries the attribute through its verses: the acts of the Lord are the acts of the Most High, and the encouragement of the Lord is the encouragement of the Most High, and the contrast of the sūrah is the contrast that the Most High states. The name of the sūrah is the name of the attribute, and the attribute is the sūrah's first word.

**The Structure of the Sūrah**

The sūrah moves in five movements, and the first verse is the door of the first movement. The first movement is the movement of the glorification, and it runs from this verse to the fifth, where the acts of the Lord complete the reason of the command. The second movement is the movement of the encouragement, and it runs from the sixth to the ninth, where the promises of the Lord are stated to the Prophet ﷺ. The third movement is the movement of the contrast, and it runs from the tenth to the fifteenth, where the two classes are set against each other. The fifth movement is the movement of the connection, and it runs from the eighteenth to the nineteenth, where the sūrah is connected to the earlier scriptures. The sūrah's structure is the structure of the five movements, and the first verse is the door of the first.
